Written by: Adam Chase

作者：亚当·蔡斯

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

🎬 The hallway, Ross is running up the stairs. Note: This show continues where the last one left off.

🎬 走廊，罗斯正跑上楼梯。注意：本节目在上一个节目结束的地方继续播放。

Ross Geller: Chandler!!! Chandler!!! (He opens the door to the apartment but is stopped by the chain; Chandler and Monica quickly stop making out and try to get dressed.) Chandler, I saw what you were doing through the window! Chandler, I saw what you were doing to my sister! Now get out here!

钱德勒！！！钱德勒！！！（他打开了公寓的门，但被锁链挡住了；钱德勒和莫妮卡很快停止做爱，试着穿衣服。）钱德勒，我从窗户看到你在做什么！钱德勒，我看到你在对我妹妹做什么！快出去！

（对莫妮卡）哇！听着，我们跑得很好。是什么？四个月？五个月？我的意思是，这比大多数人一生中拥有的都多！所以，再见，保重，再见！（他吻了她，开始爬出阳台窗户。）

你在干什么？！

哦，我要去吃羊肉。

来吧，钱德勒，来吧，我能对付罗斯。（他们走到门口。罗斯正试图把手伸进去解开锁链；莫妮卡把他的手往后推。）（对罗斯）等等！（她开门。）嘿，罗斯，怎么了，兄弟？

(Ross spots Chandler and starts chasing him around the kitchen table. Chandler runs and hides behind Monica.)

（罗斯发现了钱德勒，开始围着厨房桌子追他。钱德勒跑过去，躲在莫妮卡后面。）

你到底在干什么？！！

Rachel Green: (running from the guy's apartment with Joey in tow) Hey, what's-what's going on?!

（带着乔伊从那家伙的公寓跑出来）嘿，怎么了？！

Chandler Bing: Well, I think, I think Ross knows about me and Monica.

嗯，我想，我想罗斯知道我和莫妮卡的事。

Joey Tribbiani: (panicking) Dude! He's right there!

（惊慌失措）老兄！他就在那儿！

Ross Geller: (To Chandler) I thought you were my best friend, this is my sister! My best friend and my sister! I-I cannot believe this!

（对钱德勒）我以为你是我最好的朋友，这是我妹妹！我最好的朋友和我妹妹！我真不敢相信！

Chandler Bing: Look, we're not just messing around! I love her. Okay, I'm in love with her.

听着，我们不只是闹着玩！我爱她。好吧，我爱她。

我很抱歉你不得不这样发现。我很抱歉，但这是真的，我也爱他。

(There's a brief pause.)

（有一个短暂的停顿。）

Ross Geller: (happily) My best friend and my sister! I cannot believe this. (He hugs them both.) (To Joey and Rachel) You guys probably wanna get some hugs in too, huh? Big news!

（高兴地）我最好的朋友和我妹妹！我真不敢相信。（他拥抱了他们两个。）（对乔伊和瑞秋）你们可能也想拥抱一下，嗯？大新闻！

Rachel Green: Awww, no, it's okay, we've actually known for a while.

哦，不，没关系，我们其实已经知道一段时间了。

(There's another pause as Ross gets angry again.)

（罗斯再次生气时，又停了一下。）

Ross Geller: What? What? What?! You guys knew? (Joey and Rachel backup against the door.) You all knew and you didn't tell me?!!

什么？什么？什么？！你们知道吗？（乔伊和瑞秋靠在门上。）你们都知道，但你们没告诉我？！！

Rachel Green: Well, Ross, we were worried about you. We didn't know how you were going to react.

罗斯，我们很担心你。我们不知道你会有什么反应。

(Pause.)

（停顿。）

Ross Geller: (happily again) You were worried about me? You didn't know how I was going to react? (He hugs them both.)

（又高兴了）你担心我？你不知道我会有什么反应？（他拥抱了他们两个。）

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, all right, whew! What do you say we all clear out of here and let these two lovebirds get back down to business? (Ross turns and glares at him.) Hey-hey-hey, I-I-I'm just talking here, he-he's the one doing your sister.

好吧，好吧，呼！你说我们都离开这里，让这两个恋人回去干正事怎么样？（罗斯转过身来瞪着他。）嘿，嘿，嘿，我-我-我只是在说，他是在干你妹妹。

Chandler Bing: That's not funny.

那不好笑。

Joey Tribbiani: That's not funny at all!

一点也不好笑！

(They all get up and leave.)

（他们都站起来离开了。）
