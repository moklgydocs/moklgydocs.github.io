🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Rachel is trying to pry more information about Chandler and Monica from Joey who's sitting on the couch and busy downing a pizza.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，瑞秋正试图从坐在沙发上忙着吃比萨饼的乔伊那里打听更多关于钱德勒和莫妮卡的信息。

Rachel Green: Come on Joey!!!

来吧，乔伊！！！

Joey Tribbiani: Rach, I told you everything I knew last night! Look, it's not that big of a deal, so Monica and Chandler are doing it.

瑞秋，我昨晚把我知道的都告诉你了！听着，这没什么大不了的，所以莫妮卡和钱德勒在做。

Rachel Green: I can't believe you would say that!

我真不敢相信你会这么说！

Joey Tribbiani: Sorry. Monica and Chandler are making love.

对不起。莫妮卡和钱德勒在做爱。

Rachel Green: No! I mean come on! This is a huge deal! (She sits next to him on the couch.) Fine I want—I need more details, who-who initiated the first kiss?

不！我是说来吧！这是一个巨大的交易！（她坐在他旁边的沙发上）好吧，我想知道更多的细节，初吻是谁发起的？

Joey Tribbiani: (thinks) I don't know.

（想）我不知道。

Rachel Green: Is he romantic with her?

他和她浪漫吗？

Joey Tribbiani: I don't know.

我不知道。

Rachel Green: Are they in love?

他们恋爱了吗？

Joey Tribbiani: (thinks) I don't know.

（想）我不知道。

Rachel Green: You don't know anything.

你什么都不知道。

Joey Tribbiani: Ohh, I know one thing!

哦，我知道一件事！

Rachel Green: What?

什么？

Joey Tribbiani: They did it right there on the couch.

他们就在沙发上做的。

(He points to where she's sitting and she jumps up quickly.)

（他指着她坐的地方，她迅速跳了起来。）

Opening Credits

🎬 Central Perk, everyone is there as Ross enters.

🎬 中央公园，罗斯进来时，所有人都在那里。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey Ross!

嘿，罗斯！

Phoebe Buffay: Hey!

嘿！

Ross Geller: (disinterested) Hey-yeah. (He hurries up to the counter.) Hey Gunther, can I have a scone please? (To the gang.) Wanna hear some good news? Someone I know is getting married! Yeah! And weddings are happy occasions! Oh, by-the-by it's my ex-wife Emily!

（无私地）嘿，是的。（他急忙走向柜台）嘿，冈瑟，能给我一个烤饼吗？（对帮派）想听些好消息吗？我认识的人要结婚了！是 啊婚礼是快乐的时刻！哦，对了，是我前妻艾米丽！

All: What? Oh!

什么？哦

Chandler Bing: Sorry man.

对不起，伙计。

Gunther: Here's your scone.

这是你的烤饼。

Ross Geller: Oh, thanks Gunther. (He takes it, hands the plate it's on to Rachel, sets it down on the table, and proceeds to pound it into oblivion while saying.) STUPID BRITISH SNACK FOOD!!!!!!!

哦，谢谢你，冈瑟。（他接过盘子，把盘子递给瑞秋，把它放在桌子上，一边说着，一边把它捣成一团抛在脑后。）愚蠢的英国快餐！！！！！！！

Chandler Bing: Did they teach you that in your anger management class?

他们在你的愤怒管理课上教过你吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Hey, you know what might help you deal with it? Think of it this way, you and Emily are in the past and you can't be mad about the past. So are you still mad about the Louisiana Purchase?

嘿，你知道什么能帮你解决吗？这样想吧，你和艾米丽都在过去了，你不能为过去发疯。那么你还在为购买路易斯安那州而生气吗？

Rachel Green: Pheebs, I don’t think anyone's mad about that.

菲比，我想没人会为此生气。

Phoebe Buffay: Exactly! Because it's in the past!

没错！因为它已经过去了！

Joey Tribbiani: (eyeing the flattened scone) Anybody gonna eat that?

（盯着扁平的烤饼）有人要吃吗？

🎬 Chandler's office, Monica and him are at a party his office is throwing.

🎬 钱德勒的办公室，莫妮卡和他在办公室举办的派对上

Monica Geller: Look at us all dressed up for the big office party! By the way, what are we celebrating?

看我们都打扮好去参加大型办公室派对！顺便问一下，我们在庆祝什么？

Chandler Bing: Oh, we had a lot of liquor left over from the Christmas party.

哦，我们在圣诞晚会上喝了很多酒。

Monica Geller: I think this is so cool because none of our friends are here and we can be a real couple. We don't have to hide.

我觉得这很酷，因为我们的朋友都不在这里，我们可以成为一对真正的夫妻。我们不必躲起来。

Chandler Bing: I know, I can do this. (He takes her hand.)

我知道，我能做到。（他握住她的手。）

Monica Geller: Ooh, and I can do this. (She kisses him on the cheek.)

哦，我能做到。（她吻了他的脸颊。）

(They both stand real close together.)

（他们俩站得很近。）

Both: We can't do that. (They separate.)

我们不能那样做。（他们分开了。）

(Chandler's boss (Doug) walks up.)

（钱德勒的老板（道格）走了过来。）

Doug: Hey Bing! (Slaps him on his ass.) (Sees Monica) Wo-ho-ho, who's the pretty lady and what the hell is she doing with you?

嘿，宾！（拍他的屁股）（见莫妮卡）哇呵呵，谁是那个漂亮的女人，她到底在跟你干什么？

Chandler Bing: I asked myself that very question, sir. Uh, (Points to Monica) this is Monica. (Points to his boss.) This is my boss, Doug. Doug this is Monica.

我问自己这个问题，先生。嗯，（指着莫妮卡）这是莫妮卡。（指着他的老板）这是我的老板，道格。道格，我是莫妮卡。

Monica Geller: Hi, nice to meet you!

嗨，很高兴认识你！

Doug: Hi! And this is my wife Kara.

嗨！这是我妻子卡拉。

Kara: Nice to meet you Monica. Bing! (Slaps Chandler on his butt.)

很高兴认识你，莫妮卡。宾！（打了钱德勒一巴掌。）

Doug: Say uh, Bing, did you hear about the new law firm we got working for us?

喂，宾，你听说我们为我们工作的新律师事务所了吗？

Chandler Bing: No, sir.

没有，先生。

Doug: Yeah, Dewey, Cheatum, and Howe.

是的，杜威、夏特姆和豪。

(Chandler does a fake laugh.)

（钱德勒假装笑了。）

Doug: Come on honey, let's go drink our body weight. (They walk off leaving Chandler and Monica alone.)

来吧，亲爱的，我们去喝我们的体重。（他们走开了，只留下钱德勒和莫妮卡一个人。）

Monica Geller: What was that?

那是什么？

Chandler Bing: What?

什么？

Monica Geller: That noise you just made?

你刚才发出的噪音？

Chandler Bing: Oh, that was my work laugh.

哦，那是我的工作。

Monica Geller: Really? Your work laugh?

真的吗？你的作品笑了吗？

Chandler Bing: Oh, believe me, to survive this party, you're gonna have to come up with one too.

哦，相信我，要想在这个派对上活下来，你也得想出一个。

Monica Geller: All right, check me out.

好吧，看看我。

Chandler Bing: Okay.

好的。

(She walks up to where Doug is finishing another joke to another group.)

（她走到道格正在给另一组人讲另一个笑话的地方。）

Doug: …says $30 Father; same as in town.

……说30美元，爸爸；和城里一样。

(Monica does a fake laugh. For the laughs, you'll have to see the episode. I can't describe them.)

（莫妮卡假装笑。至于笑，你必须看这一集。我无法描述它们。）

🎬 Central Perk, Monica is buying a muffin as Chandler runs in.

🎬 中央公园，莫妮卡正在买松饼，钱德勒跑了进来。

Chandler Bing: Hey! Everybody at work loved you last night!

嘿！昨晚工作的每个人都爱你！

Monica Geller: Really?

真的吗？

Chandler Bing: And! They like me more just because I was with ya! I think you repaired a lot of the damage from when they met Joey. And Doug wants us to play tennis with them. He's never even talked to me outside of work. Except for that time when we bumped into each other at that strip club. (She glares at him.) Strip church. Anyway, I'm gonna go try and find a racquet.

还有！他们更喜欢我只是因为我和你在一起！我想你们修复了他们遇到乔伊时造成的很多损坏。道格想让我们和他们一起打网球。他甚至从来没有在工作之外跟我说过话。除了那次我们在脱衣舞俱乐部碰面。（她怒视着他）脱光了教堂。不管怎样，我要去找一个网拍。

Monica Geller: Hey, I thought you already had one.

嘿，我还以为你已经有了呢。

Chandler Bing: Oh I used too, but then Joey thought it would be fun to go to Central Park and hit rocks at…bigger rocks. (He starts to leave and stops an entering Rachel.) Hey Rach, do you have a tennis racquet?

哦，我也用过，但后来乔伊觉得去中央公园，在更大的石头上撞石头会很有趣。（他开始离开，阻止了一个进入的瑞秋）嘿，瑞秋，你有网球拍吗？

Rachel Green: Oh umm, y'know I lent it to Joey and I never actually got it back.

哦，嗯，你知道我把它借给乔伊了，但我从来没有把它还回来。

Chandler Bing: Okay, good luck with that. (Exits.)

好吧，祝你好运。（退出。）

Rachel Green: (To Monica) Hey!

（对莫妮卡）嘿！

Monica Geller: Hi!

嗨！

Rachel Green: What's up?!

怎么了？！

Monica Geller: What are you doing here? I thought you had to do inventory all day.

你在这里干什么？我以为你一整天都得清点存货。

Rachel Green: Well yeah, I do, but I decided to take a long lunch and spend some time with my friend Monica. Y'know I-I feel that we don't talk anymore. How are you? What is new with you?

是的，我喜欢，但我决定吃一顿长午餐，和我的朋友莫妮卡在一起。我觉得我们再也不说话了。你好吗？你有什么新鲜事？

Monica Geller: Uhh, not much. Uh, work's good.

嗯，不多。嗯，工作很好。

Rachel Green: Oh y'know what, we don’t have to talk about work. We can talk about anything!

哦，你知道吗，我们不必谈论工作。我们什么都可以谈！

Monica Geller: Okay. Umm…

好的。嗯…

Rachel Green: Hey! Y'know what? Let's talk about relationships!

嘿！你知道吗？让我们谈谈人际关系！

Monica Geller: Okay, what's going on with you?

好吧，你怎么了？

Rachel Green: Nothing! You go!

没什么！你走！

Monica Geller: Well, I-I—there was this guy at the bank that I thought was cute umm, but I don't anymore.

嗯，我，我，银行里有个家伙，我觉得他很可爱，嗯，但我不再喜欢了。

Rachel Green: Wow that's uh, juicy. Umm, (checks watch) y'know what though Mon, I actually do have a lot of work to do so if-if—are you sure there's just not anything else?

哇，真是多汁啊。嗯，（检查手表）你知道吗，虽然Mon，我确实有很多工作要做，如果你确定没有其他事情的话？

Monica Geller: Yes, I'm sure! Rachel is there something that you want to talk me about?

是的，我肯定！瑞秋，你有什么想跟我说的吗？

Rachel Green: No! (Gets up to leave.) (Under her breath.) If there was I wouldn't tell you.

不！（站起来准备离开）（屏住呼吸）如果有，我不会告诉你。

🎬 Chandler, Joey, and Ross's, Phoebe is settling a dispute between the chick and the duck.

🎬 钱德勒、乔伊和罗斯家，菲比正在解决小鸡和小鸭之间的争端。

(The duck quacks.)

（鸭子嘎嘎地叫。）

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, then what happened?

好吧，那发生了什么事？

(The duck flaps its wings frantically.)

（鸭子疯狂地拍打着翅膀。）

Phoebe Buffay: (gasps) Ohh. Uh-huh. Uh-huh. (The chick clucks.) You'll get your turn!

（喘息）哦。嗯哼。嗯哼。（小鸡咯咯地叫）轮到你了！

Ross Geller: (entering from the bathroom) Hey Pheebs, what's going on?

（从浴室进来）嘿，菲比，怎么了？

Phoebe Buffay: Nothing! (Picks up and sets the chick down on the floor.) (To the chick.) This is not over!

没什么！（把小鸡抱起来放在地板上。）（对小鸡。）这还没有结束！

Ross Geller: No! No! No!

不！不不

Phoebe Buffay: What?!

什么？！

Ross Geller: I was up all night writing this really nasty letter to Emily! It was perfect and now it's all covered in-in… (The duck quacks.) Actually, thanks!

我整晚都在给艾米丽写这封非常讨厌的信！它是完美的，现在它的所有覆盖在…（鸭子嘎嘎）事实上，谢谢！

Joey Tribbiani: (entering from his room) All right! Everybody ready to go to the movies?

（从他的房间进来）好吧！大家准备好去看电影了吗？

Ross Geller: Uh actually, I think I'm gonna skip it.

嗯，事实上，我想我会跳过它。

Joey Tribbiani: Really?

真的吗？

Ross Geller: Yeah, I'm gonna stay and read my book. I just wanna be alone right now.

是的，我要留下来读我的书。我现在只想一个人呆着。

Joey Tribbiani: Oh. Are you sure you don't want to come? Tom Hanks, Meg Ryan, they get mail and stuff.

哦。你确定你不想来吗？汤姆·汉克斯，梅格·瑞安，他们收到邮件和其他东西。

Ross Geller: That's okay, Joe.

没关系，乔。

Joey Tribbiani: All right, let's go Pheebs.

好吧，我们走吧，菲比。

Ross Geller: (licks the envelope and encounters a foreign substance on the glue.) Oh God!

（舔了舔信封，发现胶水上有异物。）哦，天哪！

🎬 A tennis court somewhere in the city of New York, it's the doubles match-up of a century Chandler and Monica versus Doug and Kara.

🎬 纽约某地的一个网球场，这是一场世纪钱德勒和莫妮卡对道格和卡拉的双打比赛。

Doug: Bing!

宾！

(Hits the ball towards Chandler who returns it back to him. He then hits the ball at Monica who slams it and it bounces off Kara's leg.)

（将球打向钱德勒，钱德勒将球还给他。然后，钱德勒将球打向莫妮卡，莫妮卡猛击，球从卡拉的腿上弹了回来。）

Kara: Oww!!

噢！！

Monica Geller: Game!

游戏！

Doug: Well, I gotta tell ya Bing; that partner of yours is a real tiger. (To his wife.) Are you all right sweethart?

嗯，我得告诉亚冰；你的那个搭档真是一只老虎。（对妻子）你没事吧，甜心？

Kara: (out of breath and mouths) I'm not all right.

（上气不接下气地说）我不舒服。

Doug: We're, we're just gonna get a little sip of water.

我们，我们只是想喝点水。

(They both walk off the court.)

（他们都走下了球场。）

Monica Geller: Am I on fire today or what?! Those birds are browned, basted, and ready to be carved!

我今天着火了还是怎么了？！那些鸟都是棕色的，涂了油，准备雕刻！

Chandler Bing: Okay, easy Martina. I think we should let them win the next game.

好的，放松点，玛蒂娜。我认为我们应该让他们赢下一场比赛。

Monica Geller: I'm sorry, I don't understand what you just said.

对不起，我不明白你刚才说的话。

Chandler Bing: Let them win one.

让他们赢一场吧。

Monica Geller: Are you crazy?! We own those two! I mean look at 'um, he can't breath and she's popping pills.

你疯了吗？！我们拥有那两个！我的意思是看，他不能呼吸，而她在吃药。

Chandler Bing: You're not even giving them a chance!

你甚至没有给他们机会！

Monica Geller: They have racquets don't they?!

他们有球拍，不是吗？！

Doug: Uh Bing, I think we're gonna make this the last game.

嗯，宾，我想我们会把这当成最后一场比赛。

Chandler Bing: Oh yes, sir! Put me out of my misery. Are you sure you never played pro? (Does his work laugh.) (To Monica) Please let them win!

哦，是的，先生！把我从痛苦中解救出来。你确定你从未打过职业比赛吗？（他的作品笑了。）（对莫妮卡）请让他们赢！

Monica Geller: I'll take it down to 95% but that's the best I can do.

我会降到95%，但这是我能做的最好的了。

(She serves to Doug who returns it to Chandler. As it bounces over his head Chandler swings and misses.)

（她将球传给道格，道格将球还给钱德勒。球弹过他的头，钱德勒挥棒未击中。）

Chandler Bing: Oopsey, missed it!

哎呀，错过了！

Monica Geller: I got it! (She hits a forehand smash that bounces right in between Doug and Kara and scores a point.)

我明白了！（她正手扣杀，正好在道格和卡拉之间反弹，并得到一分。）

Doug: Nice shot.

好球。

(Chandler glares at her and she shrugs her shoulders. Monica serves again; and Kara returns it.)

（钱德勒瞪了她一眼，她耸了耸肩。莫妮卡又发球了，卡拉还了。）

Monica Geller: I got it!!

我明白了！！

(Chandler cuts in front of her and hits the ball high and long.)

（钱德勒从她前面切入，把球打得又高又长。）

Chandler Bing: Long! (Gives Monica the Work Laugh.)

再见！（莫妮卡笑了。）

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Monica and Chandler are returning from the game.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋的，莫妮卡和钱德勒从游戏中回来。

Monica Geller: I can't believe you let them win!

真不敢相信你让他们赢了！

Chandler Bing: Yeah, at least you hid your feelings well about it. (Removes a smashed racquet from his bag.)

是啊，至少你对这件事隐瞒得很好。（从他的包中取出一个破碎的网拍。）

Monica Geller: I was frustrated.

我很沮丧。

Chandler Bing: It was my racquet.

是我的网拍。

Monica Geller: I was frustrated with you!

我对你很失望！

Chandler Bing: If we hadn't lost the game they never would've invented us to dinner tomorrow night.

如果我们没有输掉比赛，他们就不会让我们明晚去吃饭。

Monica Geller: Y'know what really bothers me? Is—it's how-how different you act around them! I mean y'know the throwing the tennis games, the fake laugh, the "I'll see you around, Bing!" "Not if I see you first, Doug!" (Mocks the fake laugh.) I gotta tell you, I don't like Work Chandler. Okay? The guy's a suck-up.

你知道什么真正困扰着我吗？这是因为你在他们周围的行为有多不一样！我的意思是你知道扔网球比赛，假笑声，还有“我会在附近等你的，宾！”“如果我先见到你，道格就不会了！”（嘲笑假笑声）我得告诉你，我不喜欢工作，钱德勒。可以这家伙是个讨厌鬼。

Chandler Bing: Okay y'know what, because you said that, I'm not putting out tonight.

好吧，你知道吗，因为你说了，我今晚不出去。

🎬 Chandler, Joey, and Ross's, the next morning the girls are there with Joey.

🎬 钱德勒、乔伊和罗斯家，第二天早上女孩们和乔伊在一起。

Monica Geller: I'm telling you, something's wrong! My brother does not stay out all night.

我告诉你，有点不对劲！我弟弟不是整夜都呆在外面。

Joey Tribbiani: Maybe we should check the trash chute.

也许我们应该检查一下垃圾槽。

Rachel Green: Ross couldn't fit down the trash chute.

罗斯装不下垃圾槽。

Joey Tribbiani: That's right, he almost could. Which is exactly how I got stuck there.

没错，他几乎可以。这正是我被困在那里的原因。

(Ross enters.)

（罗斯走了进来。）

Phoebe Buffay: Hey!

嘿！

Rachel Green: There he is!

他来了！

Monica Geller: Oh my God! (She goes to hug him, stops short, and hits him on the shoulder.) Where the hell have you been?!!

哦，我的天啊！（她去拥抱他，突然停下来，打了他的肩膀。）你到底到哪里去了？！！

Ross Geller: Just, y'know out.
