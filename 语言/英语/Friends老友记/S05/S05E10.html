The One With The Inappropriate Sister

有不合适妹妹的那个

Written By: Shana Goldberg-Meehan

沙娜·戈德伯格·米汉

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

With Help From: Aaron Miller

在艾伦·米勒的帮助下

🎬 Chandler, Joey, and Ross's apartment, Ross is cleaning out the fridge. Joey walks from his room. He looks like he just woke up.

🎬 钱德勒、乔伊和罗斯的公寓，罗斯正在清理冰箱。乔伊从他的房间走出来。他看起来好像刚醒来。

Joey Tribbiani: What are you doing?

你在干什么？

Ross Geller: I...reorganized the fridge. See, bottom shelf: meats and dairy. (There’s nothing on the shelf.) Middle shelf: fruits and vegetables. (There’s one lone tomato.) And top shelf: expired products. (The shelf is jammed packed.)

我…重新整理了冰箱。参见，底层货架：肉类和乳制品。（架子上什么也没有。）中间的架子：水果和蔬菜。（只有一个西红柿）和顶层货架：过期产品。（架子上塞满了东西。）

Joey Tribbiani: Why are you doing this?

你为什么要这么做？

Ross Geller: Because I am bored...Out of my mind. I’ve already been to the bank, post office, and the dry cleaners.

因为我很无聊……我疯了。我已经去过银行、邮局和干洗店了。

Joey Tribbiani: Dude, you just described seven days worth of stuff. You’ve got to spread it out a little, you know. Haven’t you ever been unemployed?

伙计，你刚刚描述了七天的价值。你知道，你得把它摊开一点。你没有失业过吗？

Ross Geller: Hey, I am not unemployed. I’m on sabbatical!

嘿，我没有失业。我在休假！

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, don’t get religious on me, ok. (Ross looks a little confused.) A guy in your position needs to be a little better at relaxing. You know. Why do you think we have the comfortable chairs? Huh...come here…sit down. (Ross sits down.) Ready? (Joey flips the foot rest up.) Ahh!! (He reclines the chair fully.) Ohh, yeah!! Huh?!

嘿，别对我信教，好吗。（罗斯看起来有点困惑。）像你这样的人需要更善于放松。你知道的。你认为我们为什么有舒适的椅子？嗯……过来……坐下。（罗斯坐下）准备好了吗？（乔伊把脚架翻了起来。）啊！！（他把椅子完全倾斜）哦，耶！！呵呵？！

Ross Geller: So what, we just sit?

那又怎样，我们就坐着？

Joey Tribbiani: Ohh, no, no. We’re not going to just sit. (Joey sits down and hits the speed dial button on the phone.) Shhh. (It begins to ring.)

哦，不，不。我们不会只是坐着。（乔伊坐下来，按下电话上的快速拨号键。）嘘。（开始响了。）

Chandler Bing: (Answering the phone at work) Hello, Chandler Bing.

（上班时接电话）你好，钱德勒·宾。

Joey Tribbiani: (In a high pitched female voice) Hello Mr. Bing...I love you.

（用高音的女声）你好，宾先生……我爱你。

Chandler Bing: (Angrily) Alright, whoever this is, stop calling me! (Ross and Joey laugh silently.) It’s been six months! It’s not funny!

（愤怒地）好吧，不管这是谁，别再给我打电话了！（罗斯和乔伊默默地笑着）已经六个月了！一点也不好笑！

Joey Tribbiani: But, I love you.

但是，我爱你。

Chandler Bing: Leave me alone! For the love of God, leave me alone!!! (Joey hangs up.)

离我远点！看在上帝的份上，别管我！！！（乔伊挂断了电话。）

Joey Tribbiani: And that’s Wednesday. (He reclines in his chair.) Ohh.

那是星期三。（他斜靠在椅子上。）哦。

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Central Perk, Chandler, Ross, Joey, Monica, and Rachel are there. Phoebe walks in ringing a bell.

🎬 中央公园、钱德勒、罗斯、乔伊、莫妮卡和瑞秋在那里。菲比按了门铃走了进来。

Phoebe Buffay: Hey you guys, guess what?

嘿，你们猜怎么着？

Chandler Bing: The British are coming?

英国人来了？

Phoebe Buffay: Ohh, you and your ways. (She shakes the bell at him and sits down.) Since it’s Christmastime. I’m going to be one of those people collection donations.

哦，你和你的方式。（她朝他摇了摇钟，坐了下来。）因为现在是圣诞节。我将成为募捐的人之一。

All: Ohh.

哦。

Phoebe Buffay: (Excitedly) Yeah, I already have my bell and later on...I get my bucket.

（兴奋地）是的，我已经有了我的铃铛，后来……我拿到了我的水桶。

Chandler Bing: Ohh.

哦。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, yeah, I’m going to be out there spreading joy to the people. I mean, last year, I spread a little joy but not really enough. So this year, I’m going to do the whole city.

是的，是的，我要出去向人们传播快乐。我的意思是，去年，我传播了一点快乐，但还不够。所以今年，我要做整个城市。

Monica Geller: You know, I knew a girl in high school who did that. She was very popular. (Chandler laughs.)

你知道，我在高中认识一个女孩，她就是这么做的。她很受欢迎。（钱德勒笑了。）

Joey Tribbiani: So Pheebs, where are you doing all, your bell ringing?

菲比，你到底在哪儿，你的铃声响了？

Phoebe Buffay: Ohh, they gave me a great spot. Right by Macys. Yeah, they hardly ever give such a good spot to a rookie, but I’m the only one who can sing "Merry Christmas" in 25 languages. (She smirks.) I lied.

哦，他们给了我一个好位置。就在梅西百货旁边。是的，他们很少给新人这样好的位置，但我是唯一能用25种语言唱“圣诞快乐”的人。（她笑了笑）我撒谎了。

Rachel Green: Oh my god. Ok you guys, there’s Danny. Watch. Just watch this. (He walks past the couch to the counter.) See?! Still pretending he’s not interested. Ohh, he’s coming over. Just pretend like we don’t know him. We’ve forgotten who he is.

我的天啊。好了，伙计们，丹尼来了。看。看这个。（他走过沙发走向柜台）看到了吗？！仍然假装他不感兴趣。哦，他过来了。假装我们不认识他。我们已经忘记他是谁了。

Danny: Hey guys.

嘿，伙计们。

All: Hey Danny.

嗨，丹尼。

Monica Geller: Danny? You know Rachel? She’s nice. She’s not bad to look at, right?

丹尼？你认识瑞秋吗？她很好。她看起来不错，对吧？

Rachel Green: Thanks, Mon.

谢谢，妈妈。

Danny: Well, of course.

当然可以。

Monica Geller: Do you want to go out on a date with her?

你想和她出去约会吗？

Rachel Green: Monica!!!

莫妮卡！！！

Danny: (Looking at Monica) Absolutely! Is Friday okay?

（看着莫妮卡）当然！星期五可以吗？

Monica Geller: Friday’s perfect...She can’t wait.

星期五很好……她等不及了。

Danny: (To Monica) On the date, I will be able to talk to her directly? (To Rachel) See ya Friday. (He walks out.)

（对莫妮卡）约会的时候，我能直接和她谈谈吗？（对瑞秋）星期五见。（他走了出去。）

Rachel Green: (Somewhat angrily) Okay. What the hell was that? You know what? Don’t answer me. (Giggling) I have a date with Danny.

（有点生气）好的。那到底是什么？你知道吗？不要回答我。（咯咯笑）我和丹尼有个约会。

🎬 Estelle's (Joey's Agent) Office, Joey is there.

🎬 埃斯特尔（乔伊的经纪人）办公室，乔伊在那里。

Joey Tribbiani: How could I not get the part? The play was about a 29-year-old Italian actor from Queens.

我怎么可能得不到这个角色？该剧讲述的是一位来自皇后区的29岁意大利演员。

Estelle: Well, Telia Shire suddenly became available.

嗯，泰利亚郡突然变得可用了。

Joey Tribbiani: She’s a woman!

她是个女人！

Estelle: What can I say? She nailed it.

我能说什么？她成功了。

Joey Tribbiani: (Very discouraged) Okay, is there anything else?

（非常沮丧）好吧，还有别的事吗？

Estelle: Well, you’re just going to say no again but...gay porn.

嗯，你只是想再说一次不，但是……同性恋色情。

🎬 In front of Macys, Phoebe is collecting donations and ringing her bell.

🎬 在梅西面前，菲比正在募捐并敲钟。

Phoebe Buffay: Happy Holidays. Feliz Navidad. Allo, and Merry Christmas. (A man put some change in her bucket.) Ohh thank you sir. Here's some joy. (She waves her hand up and down as if she is spreading joy.)

节日快乐。费利兹·纳维达。再见，圣诞快乐。（一个男人在她的桶里放了一些零钱。）哦，谢谢你，先生。这里有一些快乐。（她上下挥舞着她的手，好像在传播喜悦。）

Monica Geller: (Walking in from off screen.) Phoebe!

（从银幕下走进来）菲比！

Phoebe Buffay: Hey!

嘿！

Monica Geller: I just wanted to see how it's going.

我只是想看看进展如何。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, it's going okay.

嗯，一切都很好。

Monica Geller: (Taking out her wallet.) Well good, here let me help you out.

（拿出钱包）好的，我来帮你。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, thanks!

哦，谢谢！

Monica Geller: Yeah!

是啊！

Phoebe Buffay: Wow!

哇！

(Monica puts some change in Phoebe's bucket.)

（莫妮卡在菲比的桶里放了一些零钱。）

Phoebe Buffay: Is that a new Swede jacket? It looks really expensive.

那是一件新的瑞典夹克吗？看起来真的很贵。

Monica Geller: Yeah. I guess. (She puts more money in the bucket.)

是的。我想是吧。（她把更多的钱放进桶里。）

Phoebe Buffay: Just get your nails done?

把你的指甲修好？

Monica Geller: Yes Phoebe, but this is all I have. Okay? (She pours out the rest of her change purse into the bucket.)

是的，菲比，但这是我所有的。可以（她把剩下的零钱倒进桶里。）

Phoebe Buffay: Okay! Thanks! Happy Holidays, here's your joy. (She waves her arm and spreads her joy.)

好的！谢谢节日快乐，这是你的快乐。（她挥动手臂，尽情地欢乐。）

(A man walks up and puts a dollar bill in.)

（一名男子走上前，把一张一美元的钞票放进去。）

Phoebe Buffay: Thank you! And Happy Holidays.

谢谢你！节日快乐。

(The man starts to take some change out.)

（那人开始拿出一些零钱。）

Phoebe Buffay: Wait, you can't take the money out.

等等，你不能把钱拿出来。

The Man: I'm making change. I need change for the bus.

我在改变。我需要换公共汽车的零钱。

Phoebe Buffay: But, can't you leave the dollar? This money is for the poor.

但是，你不能留下美元吗？这笔钱是给穷人的。

The Man: I'm poor! I gotta take the bus!

我很穷！我要坐公共汽车！

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, Seasons Greetings and everything, but still…

好的，祝你节日快乐，一切顺利，但是…

The Man: Bite me, blondie! (The man storms off.)

咬我，金发女郎！（那人怒气冲冲地走了。）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, I'm going to give him something else besides joy, just… (She scowls at him.)

哦，我要给他除了快乐以外的东西，只是…（她对他怒目而视。）

🎬 Chandler, Joey, and Ross's, Joey is telling Ross how he didn't get the part.

🎬 钱德勒、乔伊和罗斯，乔伊告诉罗斯他怎么没得到这个角色。

Joey Tribbiani: That part was perfect for me! I can't believe I didn't get it!

那部分对我来说太完美了！真不敢相信我竟然没拿到！

Ross Geller: I'm sorry, man. Hey, y'know what you should do? You should make something happen for yourself. Y'know, like-like write a play. Write a movie! Huh? I mean, what about those Good Will Hunting guys?

对不起，伙计。嘿，你知道你该怎么做吗？你应该为自己做点什么。你知道，就像写剧本一样。写一部电影！嗯？我是说，那些好心人呢？

Joey Tribbiani: Come on Ross be realistic, y'know? If I did write something, what are the chances I could get those guys to star in it?

拜托，罗斯，现实点，你知道吗？如果我真的写了什么，我能让那些家伙成为明星的机会有多大？

(Ross just stares at him until he figures it out.)

（罗斯只是盯着他看，直到他明白过来。）

Joey Tribbiani: Wait a second, I could star in it!

等一下，我可以演！

Ross Geller: Or that.

或者那个。

Joey Tribbiani: I can't write! Y'know I mean I-I-I'm an actor, I don't have the discipline that takes, y'know? I can't do it.

我不会写字！你知道我是说我是个演员，我没有那种纪律，你知道吗？我做不到。

Ross Geller: I'll help you. Yeah, I'll make up a schedule and make sure you stick to it. And plus, it'll give me something to do.

我会帮你的。是的，我会制定一个时间表，确保你坚持下去。而且，这会给我一些事情做。

Joey Tribbiani: Really? You'd-you'd do that for me?!

真的吗？你会为我这么做？！

Ross Geller: Yeah!

是啊！

Joey Tribbiani: Thanks!

谢谢！

Ross Geller: (grabbing a notepad and sitting down) All right, we'll start off slow. The only thing you have to do tonight is come up with the name of your main character.

（抓起一个记事本坐下）好的，我们慢慢开始。今晚你唯一要做的就是说出你的主角的名字。

Joey Tribbiani: Done!

完成了！

Ross Geller: And it can't be Joey.

不可能是乔伊。

Joey Tribbiani: It's not.

不是。

Ross Geller: Or Joseph.

或者约瑟夫。

Joey Tribbiani: (disappointed) Oh.

（失望）哦。

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Rachel is returning. Monica enters from her room wearing nothing but a robe.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，瑞秋回来了。莫妮卡从她的房间里进来时只穿着长袍。

Monica Geller: Hey, what's up?

嘿，怎么了？

Rachel Green: I just saw Danny getting on the subway with a girl and he had his arm around her.

我刚看见丹尼和一个女孩上地铁，他用胳膊搂着她。

Monica Geller: Oh, honey, I'm sorry.

哦，亲爱的，对不起。

Rachel Green: Well, you should be, this is all your fault! You meddled in our relationship!!

你应该这样，这都是你的错！你干涉了我们的关系！！

Monica Geller: You had no relationship!!

你没有关系！！

Rachel Green: No, but I was doing my thing and everything was going according to the plan!

没有，但是我在做我的事情，一切都按照计划进行！

Monica Geller: Oh God, stop with the plan! So what, so what you saw him with a girl? Who cares?! That doesn't mean anything! Now look, you're going to go out on a date with Danny and you're going to be so charming he's gonna forget all about that stupid subway girl.

哦，天哪，别再计划了！那又怎样，你看到他和一个女孩在一起？谁在乎？！那没有任何意义！听着，你要和丹尼出去约会，你会很迷人，他会忘记那个愚蠢的地铁女孩。

Rachel Green: She was kinda stupid. You're right. All right, I'm just gonna go on the date. I'm gonna go on the date. That is the new plan.

她有点笨。你说得对。好吧，我就去约会。我要去约会了。这就是新计划。

(Rachel goes into her room and closes the door. Which allows Monica to let Chandler out of her room.)

（瑞秋走进她的房间，关上门，莫妮卡让钱德勒离开了她的房间。）

Monica Geller: Come on, hurry!

快点，快点！

(Chandler runs out the door and closes it behind him. After a short pause the door opens and Chandler comes rushing back through, grabs Monica, kisses her good-bye, and heads back out.)

（钱德勒跑出门，在他身后关上。稍停片刻，门开了，钱德勒冲了回来，抓住莫妮卡，吻了吻再见，然后走了出去。）

🎬 Chandler, Joey, and Ross's, Joey is trying to write his movie, Chandler is playing a game on the counter by trying to flip a ping pong ball with a spoon into a nearby bowl.

🎬 钱德勒、乔伊和罗斯，乔伊正在写他的电影，钱德勒正在柜台上玩游戏，试图用勺子把乒乓球抛到附近的碗里。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, how do you spell suspicious?

嘿，你怎么拼写可疑？

Chandler Bing: Why?

为什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Because I think this character is going to be suspicious about stuff.

因为我觉得这个角色会对一些东西产生怀疑。

(Chandler makes it into the bowl.)

（钱德勒把它放进碗里。）

Chandler Bing: Yes! Chandler Bing, 7! Chandler Bing, 0.

是的！钱德勒·宾，7岁！钱德勒·宾，0。

Joey Tribbiani: You're driving me crazy with that!

你的话快把我逼疯了！

Chandler Bing: Okay, I'll stop.

好吧，我停下来。

Joey Tribbiani: Don’t stop! Move the bowl further away! Ross could make that shot!

别停下来！把碗移远一点！罗斯能射到！

(Chandler slides the bowl to the far end of the counter. He tries again, but he hits the spoon to hard and the ball goes flying away.)

（钱德勒把碗滑到柜台的另一端。他再试了一次，但他使劲打勺子，球飞走了。）

Joey Tribbiani: Well, you suck! But at least you suck at a man's game now.

你真烂！但至少你现在在一个男人的游戏中很差劲。

Chandler Bing: You wanna play?

你想玩吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Chandler, I can't be playing games, Ross is gonna be home soon. And I have to write five whole pages if I'm gonna stick to his schedule.

钱德勒，我不能玩游戏，罗斯马上就要回家了。如果我要遵守他的时间表，我得写整整五页。

Chandler Bing: Well, so, play for the next 30 minutes and then write until he gets home.

那么，在接下来的30分钟里玩，然后写，直到他回家。

Joey Tribbiani: (jumping up) All right! But uh, listen, what do you say we crank it up a notch?

（跳起来）好吧！但是，呃，听着，我们把它提高一个档次怎么样？

Chandler Bing: I'm intrigued.

我很好奇。

Joey Tribbiani: All right, all we need is a little lighter fluid.

好吧，我们只需要一点清淡的液体。

Chandler Bing: Okay, but be careful okay, because I wanna get our security deposit back.

好吧，但是小心点，好吧，因为我想拿回我们的押金。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, I think we said good-bye to that when we invented hammer darts.

是的，我想我们在发明锤子飞镖的时候已经跟它说再见了。

Chandler Bing: Do you even remember which part of the wall is not spackle?

你还记得墙的哪一部分不是垃圾吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Uh yeah, right here. (He punches his fist through the wall next to the door.)

嗯，是的，就在这里。（他用拳头猛击门边的墙。）

🎬 In front of Macys, Phoebe is still ringing her bell. A guy puts some change into the bucket.

🎬 在梅西面前，菲比还在按铃。一个男人往桶里放了一些零钱。

Phoebe Buffay: Thank you, Happy Holidays.

谢谢你，节日快乐。

(Another woman walks up and throws something into the bucket.)

（另一个女人走上前，把东西扔进桶里。）

Phoebe Buffay: Now, that's trash. Young lady, you can't… (The lady ignores her and walks off.) Hey! Stop that young lady, she donated trash!

现在，那是垃圾。年轻女士，你不能…（女士不理她，走开了。）嘿！阻止那个年轻女士，她捐赠了垃圾！

(Another guy walks by and throws his light cigarette butt in the bucket.)

（另一个人走过，把他的烟头扔进桶里。）

Phoebe Buffay: Hey!! (The bucket starts smoking.) The charity's on fire! Help! (Yet another guy walks by carrying a cup, which Phoebe grabs.) Oh good! Thank you, I need that. 太好了！现在，我们可以去看游骑兵比赛了！（停顿）昨晚！

不，伙计，罗斯把票撕碎了！

这花了你一整天？！
