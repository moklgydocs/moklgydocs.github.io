你为什么一直看屏幕？

Phoebe Buffay: I'm not. I'm praying. (looking up) Please let the Knicks win... Thank you Thor! (Mike is standing up) Where... where are you going?

我不是。我在祈祷。（抬起头）请让尼克斯队获胜。。。谢谢你，托尔！（迈克站起来）哪里。。。你要去哪里？

Mike: Going go to the bathroom.

去洗手间。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, I think you should wait.

嗯，我想你应该等一下。

Mike: Why?

为什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, if you don't... if you don't hold it in, you don't get all the nutrients.

好吧，如果你不。。。如果你不坚持，你就得不到所有的营养。

Announcer: Knicks fans, please turn your attention to the big screen on the scoreboard. Someone has a special question to ask.

尼克斯队的球迷们，请把你们的注意力转向记分板上的大屏幕。有人有一个特别的问题要问。

(We see the screen where it says: "Mike will you marry me?" and then we see Phoebe and Mike on the screen. Phoebe stands up and kneels in front of Mike.)

（我们看到屏幕上写着：“迈克，你愿意嫁给我吗？”然后我们在屏幕上看到菲比和迈克。菲比站起来，跪在迈克面前。）

Phoebe Buffay: Mike Hannigan... will you marry me? (Mike looks bewildered)

迈克·汉尼根。。。你愿意嫁给我吗？（迈克看起来很困惑）

Announcer: Get a load of this... She's proposing to him. Guess we know who wears the pants in that family. (people are laughing, while Mike still seems bewildered)

看一看这个。。。她在向他求婚。我想我们知道那家谁当家了。（人们都在笑，而迈克似乎仍然困惑不解）

Phoebe Buffay: That's not very enlightened!

那不是很开明！

(There's booing around them, and Mike sinks in his chair, holding his hand in above his eyes, hoping no-one would recognize him)

（他们周围响起嘘声，迈克坐在椅子上，把手举过眼睛，希望没人认出他来）

Phoebe Buffay: Hey, hey! (shouting) Boo us? Boo you!

嘿，嘿！（大叫）嘘我们？嘘你！

🎬 Rachel and Joey's. Joey is in the kitchen and the telephone rings.

🎬 瑞秋和乔伊的。乔伊在厨房，电话铃响了。

Joey Tribbiani: (picks up the phone) Hello? Yeah, this is Joey Tribbiani... Oh, hi! Well, I'm glad you liked my letter... No my mommy and daddy aren't home right now... (looks puzzled) Okay, bye bye. (hangs up) (to himself) She was nice!

（拿起电话）喂？是的，这是乔伊·特里比亚尼。。。哦，嗨！很高兴你喜欢我的信。。。不，我妈妈和爸爸现在不在家。。。（看起来很困惑）好的，再见。（挂断）（自言自语）她很好！

(Rachel and Amy enter)

（瑞秋和艾米进来了）

Rachel Green: Joey, get Amy's bags, she is moving out!

乔伊，拿艾米的包，她要搬出去了！

Joey Tribbiani: Whoo-hoo! (and leaves for Rachel's room)

呼呼！（然后去瑞秋的房间）

Amy: You're kicking me out?

你要把我踢出去？

Rachel Green: You put holes in my baby's ears!

你在我孩子的耳朵上打洞了！

Amy: Yeah well, at least now people will know she is a girl!

是啊，至少现在人们会知道她是个女孩！

Rachel Green: (gasps) I can't believe I ever even tried to help you. You are so beyond help.

（喘息）我简直不敢相信我曾经试图帮助过你。你真是帮不上忙。

Amy: You know what? Ever since I got here, you have been nothing but negative.

你知道吗？自从我来到这里，你就一直很消极。

Rachel Green: Excuse me?

对不起？

Amy: You didn't want me to marry the old guy with the great apartment. Then, I tried to help your daughter to de-emphasize her flaws (frantically pointing at her nose) And suddenly I am the bad guy?

你不想让我嫁给一个公寓很好的老头子。然后，我试图帮你女儿淡化她的缺点（疯狂地指着她的鼻子），突然我成了坏人？

Rachel Green: (yelling) Joey, where are those bags?

（大叫）乔伊，那些包在哪里？

Joey Tribbiani: (Yelling from Rachel's room) She has a lot of crap!

（从瑞秋的房间里大叫）她有很多废话！

Amy: You know what? When I moved in here I thought: This is gonna be so great. Just us sisters, back together again like when we were kids, except without that stupid Jill... Oh! Who has gotten fat by the way...

你知道吗？当我搬到这里的时候，我想：这会很好。只有我们姐妹，像我们小时候一样重新在一起，除了没有那个愚蠢的吉尔。。。哦顺便说一句，谁长胖了。。。

Rachel Green: (doesn't believe what she's hearing) Seriously?

（不相信她听到的）真的吗？

Amy: hm-mmm... Mom said she gained like fifteen pounds.

嗯，嗯。。。妈妈说她体重增加了15磅。

Rachel Green: Hips or thighs?

臀部还是大腿？

Amy: Ass and face.

屁股和脸。

Rachel Green: (gasps) Oh! Oh my God! I thought she was on Atkins.

（喘息）哦！哦，我的上帝！我以为她在阿特金斯。

Amy: She was. Carbs found her... See, this is what I wanted. Two sisters, talking about real stuff.

她是。卡布斯找到她了。。。看，这就是我想要的。两姐妹，谈论真实的事情。

Rachel Green: (embarrassed) Oh, I can give you that.

（不好意思）哦，我可以给你。

Amy: You can?

你可以吗？

Rachel Green: Yeah. I just, I kept trying to make you a better person, but you're... you're already a pretty perfect version of what you are.

是的。我只是，我一直想让你变得更好，但是你。。。你已经是一个非常完美的版本了。

Amy: (touched) Thank you. I've got to admit, Emma does look cute.

（感动）谢谢你。我得承认，艾玛看起来确实很可爱。

Rachel Green: Did you just say Emma?

你刚才是说艾玛吗？

Amy: Ugh, I'm sorry... Ella.

啊，对不起。。。艾拉。

🎬 Mike and Phoebe at a restaurant.

🎬 迈克和菲比在餐厅

Phoebe Buffay: That woman at the game didn't know what she was talking about. Mike, obviously you have balls.

那个参加比赛的女人不知道她在说什么。迈克，很明显你很有胆量。

Mike: But please, let's just forget the whole thing.

但是拜托，让我们忘掉这一切吧。

Phoebe Buffay: (the waiter puts a piece of cake on the table) I would love it. Consider it forgotten... But just so you know... however and whenever you decide to propose, I promise I'll say yes. Whether... whether, you know, it is in a basketball game, or in sky writing, or you know, like some lame guy in a cheesy movie who hides it in the cake.

（服务员把一块蛋糕放在桌子上）我很喜欢。想想它被遗忘了…但是你要知道。。。不管你什么时候决定求婚，我保证我会答应的。是否无论是在篮球比赛中，还是在天空写作中，或者你知道，就像一部低俗电影中的瘸子把它藏在蛋糕里。

(Mike's face changes from happy to sad, and he looks at the cake, disappointed.)

（迈克的脸从高兴变为悲伤，失望地看着蛋糕。）

Phoebe Buffay: It's in the cake, isn't it?

它在蛋糕里，不是吗？

Mike: (puts on a fake smile) Where else would lame Mr. No Balls hide it? (he takes the ring from the cake, and cleans it with a napkin)

（假装微笑）跛脚的无球先生还能把它藏在哪里？（他从蛋糕上取下戒指，用餐巾擦干净）

Phoebe Buffay: What's the matter with me? How do I keep ruining this? I'm sorry, I'm sorry!

我怎么了？我怎么能一直毁了这一切？对不起，对不起！

Mike: No! It's my fault. I keep trying to propose in these stupid ways and all I wanna do is tell you that I love you and I want to spend the rest of my life with you.

不！这是我的错。我一直试图用这些愚蠢的方式求婚，我只想告诉你我爱你，我想和你共度余生。

(Phoebe has this weird, anxious, nervous look on her face)

（菲比脸上有一种奇怪、焦虑、紧张的表情）

Mike: I'm gonna do this now.

我现在就去做。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh my God!

哦，我的天啊！

(Mike starts to kneel in front of Phoebe.)

（迈克开始跪在菲比面前。）

Mike: Phoebe, I...

菲比，我。。。

Phoebe Buffay: Wait! Oh wait! (she takes off a ring that was already on her left ring finger. After that Mike starts to kneel again, but then...) Oh no! (She was wearing rings on all her fingers and her thumb, and takes all of these off.)

等等！哦，等等！（她摘下了一枚已经戴在左手无名指上的戒指。之后迈克又开始跪下，但是…）哦，不！（她所有的手指和拇指上都戴着戒指，然后把这些都取下来。）

Mike: Ready?

准备好了吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Uh-huh! (and now Mike kneels properly)

嗯哼！（现在迈克正确地跪下了）

Mike: Phoebe, I love you. There's no-one else in the world I would ask to marry me... three times. But I wanna take care of you, have babies with you, and grow old with you... Phoebe Buffay, will you marry me?

菲比，我爱你。世界上没有其他人会向我求婚。。。三次。但是我想照顾你，和你一起生孩子，和你一起变老。。。菲比·布菲，你愿意嫁给我吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Yes!

是的！

(Mike puts the ring on her finger)

（迈克把戒指戴在她的手指上）

Mike: I love you!

我爱你！

Phoebe Buffay: I love you more!

我更爱你！

Mike: Not possible! (they kiss, and then Mike says proudly...) She's gonna be Mrs. No Balls.

不可能！（他们接吻，然后迈克骄傲地说……）她将成为无球夫人。

(They kiss again, and Phoebe looks at the ring.)

（他们再次接吻，菲比看着戒指。）

没那么好。几乎是人们不想听他们的孩子很丑。
