The One with Phoebe's Wedding

菲比的婚礼

Written by: Robert Carlock & Dana Klein

作者：Robert Carlock&Dana Klein

Produced by: Robert Carlock & Wendy Knoller

罗伯特·卡洛克和温迪·诺勒

Transcribed by: Coffee Mug, Eleonora, Sebastiano & Vanessa

转录人：咖啡杯、Eleonora、Sebastiano&Vanessa

🎬 Central Perk. Joey's sitting on the couch and Phoebe enters.

🎬 中央公园。乔伊坐在沙发上，菲比进来。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, hey Joey.

哦，嘿，乔伊。

Joey Tribbiani: Uh, hey.

嗯，嘿。

Phoebe Buffay: Listen, I need to ask you something. Ok, you know how my step dad's in prison.

听着，我有件事要问你。好吧，你知道我继父是怎么进监狱的。

Joey Tribbiani: (afraid) Yeah.

（害怕）是的。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah. Well, uhm... listen he was supposed to get a weekend furlough, so he'd come to the wedding tomorrow, but he just called and... uhm... well, apparently stabbing Iceman in the exercise yard just couldn't wait till Monday.

是的。嗯，嗯。。。听着，他本来应该有周末休假，所以他明天会来参加婚礼，但他只是打电话来。。。嗯。。。很明显，在操场上刺伤冰人是迫不及待地要到星期一。

Joey Tribbiani: So he can't come?

那么他不能来了？

Phoebe Buffay: No, and so there's no one to walk me down the aisle and... well, I would just really love it if you would do it.

没有，所以没有人陪我走过过道。。。如果你愿意的话，我会非常喜欢的。

Joey Tribbiani: Seriously?

真的吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, you've... you know, sort of been like a dad to me. I mean, you've always, you know, looked out for me and shared your wisdom...

是的，你已经。。。你知道，对我来说有点像个父亲。我的意思是，你一直，你知道，照顾我，分享你的智慧。。。

Joey Tribbiani: I am pretty wisdomous.

我很有智慧。

Phoebe Buffay: So... what do you say?

那么。。。你怎么说？

Joey Tribbiani: Are you kidding? Phoebe, I would be honored. (they hug)

你在开玩笑吗？菲比，我很荣幸。（他们拥抱）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, thank you. I hope... I hope you know how much you mean to me.

哦，谢谢你。我希望。。。我希望你知道你对我有多重要。

Joey Tribbiani: (takes her hand) Listen, I hope... that you know... (has difficulty saying it) I don't want you to see your father cry, GO TO YOUR ROOM!

（握住她的手）听着，我希望。。。你知道。。。（很难说）我不想让你看到你父亲哭，回你的房间去！

（她的手机响了）哦，是我的婚礼策划人。她快把我逼疯了！（她回答）你好。。。嘿，好吧，别尖叫了！好啊大比目鱼。好吧，不管怎样，鲑鱼。我不，我不。。。这对我来说无关紧要！

（她戴着耳机进来，对着麦克风说话）嗯，这对我很重要！

为什么，我一定是在他们教导弹的那天参加了导弹训练。

减去12就行了。

好吧，那么。。。1800减12等于。。。一千七百。。。

（尖叫）六点钟！

好的。

好的。等等（她的手机响了）盖勒来了！不我说它必须在4点之前到达那里。再见。（她挂断电话）哦，做一个冰雕有多难？

冰雕？听起来真是太棒了！我告诉过你我只想要一个简单的婚礼。

Monica Geller: Please... honey, leave the details to me. Now I wanna make this day as special for you as I can. Now, ok, I was thinking that the harpist should wear white.

请。。。亲爱的，把细节留给我吧。现在我想让这一天对你来说尽可能的特别。好吧，我在想竖琴手应该穿白色的。

Phoebe Buffay: What harpist? My friend Marjorie is playing the steel drums.

什么竖琴手？我的朋友马乔里正在打钢鼓。

Monica Geller: Ooh... she backed out.

哦。。。她退出了。

Phoebe Buffay: She did? Why?

是吗？为什么？

Monica Geller: I made her. (Phoebe looks shocked) Steel drums don't really say "elegant wedding". Nor does Marjorie's overwhelming scent.

我创造了她。（菲比看起来很震惊）钢鼓并没有真正说“优雅的婚礼”。马乔里浓烈的气味也没有。

Phoebe Buffay: (looking angry) Hey! She will shower when Tibet is free.

（看起来很生气）嘿！西藏自由时，她会洗澡。

🎬 The wedding rehearsal dinner.

🎬 婚礼彩排晚宴

Chandler Bing: Hey!

嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Hey!

嘿！

Chandler Bing: You look great. I'm so glad we're having this rehearsal dinner, you know, I so rarely get to practice my meals before I eat them.

你看起来很棒。我很高兴我们有这个彩排晚宴，你知道，我很少在吃饭前练习。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, what did we say was your one gift to us?

好吧，我们说什么是你给我们的唯一礼物？

Chandler Bing: No stupid jokes. I thought that was for the actual wedding.

不要开愚蠢的玩笑。我以为那是为了真正的婚礼。

Phoebe Buffay: Rehearse it!

排练一下！

Ross Geller: Hi! (he kisses Phoebe)

嗨！（他亲吻菲比）

Mike: Thanks for coming you guys.

谢谢你们来。

Ross Geller: Oh, (he goes towards Mike in order to shake hands but Mike hugs him) hey, oh... I... I was-I was going for a hand shake.

哦，（他走向迈克想和他握手，但是迈克拥抱了他）嘿，哦。。。我我想和你握手。

Mike: Is that why your hand is pressed against my crotch?

这就是你的手被压在我胯部的原因吗？

Ross Geller: That is why!

这就是为什么！

Mike: Yeah.

是的。

Phoebe Buffay: So Rach.

瑞秋。

Rachel Green: Yeah.

是的。

Phoebe Buffay: Where is Emma?

艾玛在哪里？

Rachel Green: Oh, Monica made me send her to my mother's. Apparently babies and weddings don't mix.

哦，莫妮卡让我把她送到我妈妈家。很明显，婴儿和婚礼是不和谐的。

Monica Geller: (coming) Are you still crying about your damn baby? Pheebs, you gotta keep the line moving, remember, 20 seconds per person. Your see these clowns all the time! (she takes off)

（过来）你还在为你那该死的孩子哭泣吗？菲比，你得让队伍继续前进，记住，每人20秒。你经常看到这些小丑！（她起飞了）

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, you're Mike's parents, right?

嘿，你是迈克的父母，对吗？

Mike's mother: Yes, we are.

是的，我们是。

Joey Tribbiani: Ah, our little ones are growing up fast, uh?

啊，我们的孩子们长得很快，嗯？

Mike's father: How's that?

怎么样？

Joey Tribbiani: You know, on the one hand you're happy for them, but on the other hand it's hard to let go.

你知道，一方面你为他们感到高兴，但另一方面你很难放手。

Mike's father: Who in God's name are you?

看在上帝的份上，你是谁？

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, I'm not that fond of you either, ok buddy? But I'm just trying to be nice for the kids!

嘿，我也不是很喜欢你，好吗，伙计？但我只是想对孩子们好一点！

(cut to Ross, Chandler and Rachel)

（切换到罗斯、钱德勒和瑞秋）

Chandler Bing: (to Rachel) You know what I just realized? We have no idea what we're doing in the wedding tomorrow.

（对瑞秋）你知道我刚刚意识到什么吗？我们不知道明天的婚礼要做什么。

Ross Geller: Yeah, I thought we'd be groomsmen, but wouldn't they have asked us by now? When did they ask you to be their bridesmaid?

是的，我以为我们会是伴郎，但他们现在不会问我们了吗？他们什么时候请你做他们的伴娘的？

Rachel Green: Uh... November?

嗯。。。十一月？

Ross Geller: I wanna say it's not looking good.

我想说它看起来不太好。

嘿，菲比。。。

Phoebe Buffay: What's up?

怎么了？

Rachel Green: Uhm... you haven't told these guys what they're doing in the wedding yet.

嗯。。。你还没告诉这些家伙他们在婚礼上做什么。

Chandler Bing: Heh.

嘿。

Phoebe Buffay: Uhm... well, they're not in the wedding.

嗯。。。他们不在婚礼上。

Ross Geller: What? (Ross and Chandler don't know what to say, so there's an embarrassing long pause)

什么？（罗斯和钱德勒不知道该说什么，所以停顿了很长时间）

嗯，这真的很尴尬（盯着地板）哦，我可以走了！

Phoebe Buffay: I'm sorry you guys but, you know, Mike's got his brother and his friends from school so... you know, you were-you were... if it helps you, you were next in line, you just-you just missed the cut.

我很抱歉你们，但是，你们知道，迈克有他的兄弟和他的朋友在学校，所以。。。你知道，你是你是。。。如果这对你有帮助的话，你就是下一个，你只是错过了机会。

哦，伙计！

Chandler Bing: This is like figure skating team all over again. (Phoebe and Ross glare at him astonished) I mean synchronized swimming. (they continue to glare) I mean- I mean the balance beam. (to Ross) Help me!

这又像是花样滑冰队。（菲比和罗斯惊讶地瞪着他）我是说花样游泳。（他们继续怒目而视）我是说——我是说平衡木。（对罗斯）帮帮我！

Ross Geller: FOOTBALL!

足球！

Chandler Bing: Thank you.

谢谢你。

Monica Geller: (looking at Phoebe eating something) Pheebs, spit that out, that has pork in it.

（看着菲比吃东西）菲比，吐出来，里面有猪肉。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh! I though the pot stickers were supposed to be vegetarian!

哦！我还以为那些锅贴应该是素食主义者呢！

Monica Geller: Yeah, I changed them. I-I sent you a fax about it!

是的，我换了。我给你发了一份传真！

Phoebe Buffay: I don't have a fax machine.

我没有传真机。

Monica Geller: Ah, well then there are gonna be a few surprises!

啊，那么会有一些惊喜！

Ross Geller: I can't believe we're gonna be the only people that aren't in this wedding.

我不敢相信我们会是唯一不参加婚礼的人。

Chandler Bing: I know, I hate being left out of things.

我知道，我讨厌被冷落。

Ross Geller: And it's a wedding! It'd be weird if I'm not in it...

这是一场婚礼！如果我不在里面会很奇怪。。。

Mike: Hey guys, how is it going?

嘿，伙计们，最近怎么样？

好的。我们只是坐在这里。单独地什么也不做。这是我们明天的排练。

是的，听着，关于明天，我。。。我有个问题要问你。我刚发现我的一个伴郎有急事，不能来了。

发生什么事了？

谁在乎呢？

我会的！

（对钱德勒）好吧，如果菲比选择了，那就向迈克的下一个伴郎问好。

哦，我会的。但我需要一面镜子。。。因为他就是我！

求你了，你要下去了！

你越来越沮丧了！

花样滑冰队的人就是这么说的吗？

（几乎哭了）我不知道，我没有成功！（他们拥抱）

我打算娶她。

和演戏一样。

第二杆！

三击！你只能再得到一个，迈克！

什么，什么，什么，不，我不想那样做。

Phoebe Buffay: All right, I guess I'll have to find a new bridesmaid.

好吧，我想我得找个新伴娘了。

Ross Geller: I'll do it! (Monica approaches)

我会的！（莫妮卡走近）

Monica Geller: Ok, it's 2100 hours. (to Phoebe) Time for your toast. (Mike appears)

好的，现在是2100小时。（对菲比）该干杯了。（迈克出现了）

我有时间去洗手间吗？

你在2030年上过厕所。用你自己的时间小便，迈克！（给菲比和迈克）。现在，关于祝酒词，好吧，你要保持简短，没有什么比长篇大论更能扼杀排练晚宴了。可以你只要进去，做你的事，然后出去！

Mike: Is that what you say to Chandler?

这就是你对钱德勒说的吗？

(she reads a note). My friend Joey and I decided to fix each other up with friends so I, I... (Monica is twirling her hands in order to make Phoebe speed up her speech) oh I... hum... I gave it a lot of thought and I fixed him up with my friend Mary Ellen who couldn't be here tonight because... (Monica is tapping her watch with her finger) it's not important... she is in rehab. Anyway, so, ok, Joey said that he was fixing me up with his friend Mike, only he didn't have a friend Mike so he just brought, uhm, my Mike and, and (Monica clears her throat) but despite, you know... it got... it got good.
