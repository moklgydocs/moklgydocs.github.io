The One with the Home Study

🎬 Central Perk

🎬 中央公园

(Joey and Ross enter. Phoebe and Mike are sitting on the couch, reading a magazine.)

（乔伊和罗斯进来了。菲比和迈克坐在沙发上看杂志。）

Ross Geller: Hey you guys!

嘿，伙计们！

Phoebe Buffay: Hey!

嘿！

Ross Geller: Hey, what are you doing?

嘿，你在干什么？

Mike: Oh, figuring out our wedding plans.

哦，搞清楚我们的婚礼计划。

Chandler Bing: That's funny, we were doing the same thing!

真有趣，我们在做同样的事情！

Ross Geller: Yeah!

是啊！

Phoebe Buffay: It's really crazy! The hall, the dress, the food... I-I had no idea how expensive this stuff was!

真是疯了！大厅，衣服，食物。。。我不知道这东西有多贵！

Chandler Bing: Yeah it is really pricey. I mean, I freaked when I first heard the numbers.

是啊，真的很贵。我是说，当我第一次听到这些数字时，我吓了一跳。

Phoebe Buffay: So what did you two do about it?

你们两个怎么办？

Chandler Bing: It was pretty simple actually, I came up with a couple of cost-cutting solutions, wrote out a list and Monica told me to go to hell.

其实很简单，我想出了几个降低成本的办法，写了一张清单，莫妮卡让我见鬼去吧。

Ross Geller: There's no way around it Pheebs, you just gonna have to accept the fact that this is gonna cost you a lot of money.

没办法，菲比，你只需要接受这样一个事实，这会花你很多钱。

Mike: I heard that weddings are like a 40 billion dollar a year industry.

我听说婚礼就像一个每年400亿美元的产业。

Ross Geller: Yeah, and I'm responsible for just like half of that.

是的，我要对其中的一半负责。

Phoebe Buffay: But really, it does seem like this money could be put to better use?

但真的，看起来这笔钱可以更好地使用吗？

Mike: Are you serious?

你是认真的吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah! Now, how would you feel if we gave all the wedding money to charity and we just got married at City Hall?

是啊！现在，如果我们把所有的结婚钱都捐给慈善机构，而我们刚刚在市政厅结婚，你会怎么想？

Mike: I think it would make me wanna marry you even more. (he kisses her)

我想这会让我更想嫁给你。（他吻了她）

Ross Geller: I've got to say you guys, that's an incredible gesture!

我得说你们这些家伙，这是一个不可思议的手势！

Chandler Bing: (to Ross) Maybe you do that next time you get married!

（对罗斯）也许你下次结婚时会这么做！

Ross Geller: No, no, no. The next time it's gonna be a Hawaii at sunset. [pause] But maybe the time after that!

不，不，不。下次是日落时分的夏威夷。[停顿]但也许在那之后的某个时候！

钱德勒？

我承认香烟和杂志，但那盘磁带不是我的。

它不是我的！

（手里拿着录像机出去）好吧，我想我们永远都不知道那是谁的！

嗯，任何贡献，无论大小，都是值得感激的。

好吧，我想你会很感激这件事（她给了他一张支票）

嗯，这太慷慨了！

我们不想得到任何认可。这是完全匿名的。

完全匿名。来自两个善良的陌生人。

X先生和菲比·布菲。

如果你愿意，我们可以把你的名字写进我们的通讯。

没必要。

布菲的拼写是B-U-F-F-A-Y。

而且“X”的拼写是嗯。。。“迈克·汉尼根”。

对。我代表孩子们：非常感谢你们两位。

当然，我很高兴我们这么做了。感觉真好！

Mike: It does. It feels really good!

是的。感觉真的很好！

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, look! And we get these free t-shirts! (she takes a t-shirt which was on the counter)

哦，看！我们还有这些免费的t恤！（她拿了一件柜台上的t恤）

Charity guy: Oh, actually, that's the shirt I wore to the gym.

哦，事实上，那是我去健身房穿的衬衫。

Phoebe Buffay: Mhm... it's moist.

嗯。。。天气潮湿。

🎬 Joey and Rachel's apartment.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋的公寓

Ross Geller: (he enters) Hi!

（他进来）嗨！

Rachel Green: Hi! Emma will be up in a minute!

嗨！艾玛马上就上来！

Ross Geller: Oh, good!

哦，很好！

Rachel Green: Oh hey Ross... Listen, I heard about you and Charlie. I'm really sorry.

哦，嘿，罗斯。。。听着，我听说你和查理。我真的很抱歉。

哦，没关系。我肯定还有很多其他美丽的古生物学家。

Rachel Green: Absolutely.

当然。

Ross Geller: There was one! She's it! All the rest look like they should live under a bridge!

有一个！她就是！其他人看起来都应该住在桥下！

所以，嗯。。。你今天打算做什么？

Ross Geller: Well, I was thinking of taking Emma to the playground!

嗯，我想带艾玛去操场！

Rachel Green: Oh my God, what!?

哦，天哪，什么！？

Ross Geller: Like I said I was thinking of taking Emma to the museum of knives and fire!

就像我说的，我想带艾玛去刀火博物馆！

Rachel Green: Ok, look, Ross. I do not want Emma going to the playground.

好的，看，罗斯。我不想艾玛去操场。

Ross Geller: Be-caaauuuse...

是卡奥乌斯。。。

（心烦意乱）好吧，如果你一定要知道。。。我有一个创伤。。。秋千事件。。。我小时候。

Ross Geller: Seriously?

真的吗？

Rachel Green: Yes, I was 4 years old and I was on the swing and then all of a sudden my hair got tangled in the chain. And to get me out my mom had to-had to cut a big chunk of my hair! (crying) And it was uneven for weeks!

是的，我当时4岁，正在荡秋千，突然我的头发被链子缠住了。为了让我出去，我妈妈不得不剪掉我的一大块头发！（哭着）好几个星期都不平坦！

Ross Geller: (sarcastic) And you made it through that? I wonder who's gonna play you in the movie!

（讽刺地）你成功了吗？我不知道谁会在电影里扮演你！

Rachel Green: Ok, fine! You can make fun of me. I do not want Emma going there. And I was thinking Claire Danes.

好的，很好！你可以取笑我。我不想艾玛去那里。我在想克莱尔·丹尼斯。

Ross Geller: Look, I'm sorry to hear about your tragedy, ok? But the swings are perfectly safe, and besides Emma loves them. You know what, you should come with us and you'll see!

听着，听到你的悲剧我很难过，好吗？但是秋千是绝对安全的，而且爱玛也很喜欢。你知道吗，你应该和我们一起去看看！

Rachel Green: Ross, those things go like 40 miles an hour! Ok? When you're... and there is that moment when you are at the top, when you just don't know if you're gonna return back to earth!

罗斯，那些东西每小时跑40英里！好啊当你。。。有那么一刻，当你在巅峰时，你只是不知道你是否会回到地球！

Ross Geller: Space is filled with orbiting children. (pause) Look, please, just come on, you know, when you’ll see the look on Emma’s face, I swear you won’t regret it.

太空中到处都是绕轨道运行的孩子。（停顿）听着，求你了，来吧，你知道，当你看到艾玛脸上的表情时，我发誓你不会后悔的。

Rachel Green: All right!

好吧！

Ross Geller: Good, you don’t want to be one of those mothers who pass on their irrational fears on their children, do you?

很好，你不想成为那种把非理性恐惧传给孩子的母亲，是吗？

Rachel Green: Irrational, huh? All right, well, I’ll remember that the next time you freak out about a spider in your apartment!

不理智，嗯？好吧，下次你为你公寓里的蜘蛛发疯时，我会记得的！

Ross Geller: Oh, yeah, that’s the same, I am sure there are thirty different species of poisonous swings!

哦，是的，那是一样的，我肯定有三十种不同的有毒秋千！

耶！
