Written by: Adam Chase and Ira Ungerleider

几内亚比格抄写

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, everyone is looking at papers.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，每个人都在看报纸。

Joey Tribbiani: How could someone get a hold of your credit card number?

怎么会有人知道你的信用卡号码？

Monica Geller: I have no idea. But look how much they spent!

我不知道。但看看他们花了多少钱！

莫妮卡，你能冷静下来吗？信用卡的人说你只需要支付你买的东西。

Monica Geller: I know. It's just such reckless spending.

我知道。这就是如此不计后果的消费。

Ross Geller: I think when someone steals your credit card, they've kind of already thrown caution to the wind.

我想当有人偷了你的信用卡时，他们已经把警告抛到了九霄云外。

哇，真是个极客。他们花了69.95美元买了一把神奇的拖把。

是我。

马塞尔，别碰灯了！别驼背了！现在马塞尔，回来-（马塞尔跑向瑞秋的房间）过来，马塞尔-

哦，不，不在我的房间里！我去找他。

罗斯，你得对驼背做点什么。

什么？这只是一个阶段。

嗯，我们就是这么说乔伊的……

Ross Geller: Would you all relax? It's not that big a deal.

你们能放松一下吗？没什么大不了的。

（射门外）住手！马塞尔！坏猴子！

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Rachel Green: Let's just say my Curious George doll is no longer curious.

我们就说我好奇的乔治娃娃不再好奇了。

乔…乔…乔…斯大林？

斯大林……斯大林……我知道那个名字吗？听起来很熟悉。

Chandler Bing: Well, it does not ring a bell with me...

嗯，这对我来说没什么意义……

（写下来）乔·斯大林。你知道，那很好。

（乔伊显然想到了‘当然！’并把它写下来。）

约瑟夫·斯大林。我想你会记得的！

哦，是的！再见，小鸟，约瑟夫·斯大林主演。约瑟夫·斯大林是屋顶上的小提琴手。

嘿。

嘿。

嗨。（在电话中）嗨，嗯，是的，我是莫妮卡·盖勒。嗯，我想我要和你一起上几节课，我想知道是什么课。

你在干什么？

（使她安静）好的，太好了。谢谢。（挂断）我要去上课了。

什么，什么，这样你就可以和偷你信用卡的女人跳舞了？

Monica Geller: This woman's got my life, I should get to see who she is.

这个女人有我的生活，我应该看看她是谁。

Rachel Green: Go to the post office! I'm sure her picture's up! ...Okay, Monica, y'know what, honey, you're kinda losing it here! I mean, this is really becoming like a weird obsession thing.

去邮局！我敢肯定她的照片挂了……好吧，莫妮卡，你知道吗，亲爱的，你在这里有点迷失了！我的意思是，这真的变得像是一种奇怪的痴迷。

Phoebe Buffay: This is madness. It's madness, I tell you, for the love of God, Monica, don't do it!! ...Thank you.

这太疯狂了。这太疯狂了，我告诉你，看在上帝的份上，莫妮卡，别这么做……非常感谢。

🎬 A Tap Class, the girls are standing at the door.

🎬 一个踢踏班，女孩们站在门口。

Monica Geller: What d'you think?

你觉得怎么样？

Phoebe Buffay: Lotsa things.

很多东西。

(They go in and sit down.)

（他们进去坐下。）

Rachel Green: Which one do you think she is?

你认为她是哪一个？

(The teacher comes up to them.)

（老师向他们走来。）

Teacher: May I help you?

我能帮你吗？

Monica Geller: Oh, no thanks, we're just here to observe.

哦，不用了，谢谢，我们只是来观察的。

Teacher: You don't observe a dance class. You dance a dance class. Spare shoes are over there.

你不看舞蹈课。你在舞蹈课上跳舞。备用鞋在那边。

Rachel Green: What does she mean?

她是什么意思？

Phoebe Buffay: I think she means (Imitates) 'You dance a dance class'. Oh, c'mon, c'mon. (They put on some spare shoes)

我想她的意思是（模仿）“你在舞蹈课上跳舞”。哦，来吧，来吧。（他们穿上了一些备用鞋）

Monica Geller: Okay, d'y'see anybody you think could be me?

好的，你看到你认为可能是我的人了吗？

Teacher: (To the class) People! Last time there were some empty yoghurt containers lying around after class. Let's not have that happen again!

（对全班同学）大家好！上次课后有一些空的酸奶容器。别让这种事再发生了！

Rachel Green: She could be you.

她可能就是你。

(Music starts)

（音乐开始）

Teacher: Let's get started. Five, six, a-five six seven eight...

让我们开始吧。五，六，五六七八……

(Everyone starts to dance in unison. Monica flounders)

（大家齐声起舞，莫妮卡挣扎着）

Monica Geller: Okay, I'm not getting this!

好吧，我不明白！

Phoebe Buffay: (Dancing in a swirly, Phoebe kind of way) I'm totally getting it!

（像漩涡一样跳舞）我完全明白了！

Monica Geller: Did you ever feel like sometimes you are just so unbelievably uncoordinated?

你有没有觉得有时候你真是太不协调了？

(Rachel taps into view; she is in perfect sync with the rest of the class)

（瑞秋进入视野；她与班上其他同学完全同步）

Rachel Green: What? You just click when they click.

什么？当他们点击时，你只需点击。

Teacher: Alright people, now everyone grab a partner.

好了，各位，现在每个人都找一个搭档。

(The girls are unsure how to pair off. Phoebe settles it)

（女孩们不确定如何配对。菲比解决了这个问题）

Phoebe Buffay: Okay. And, my, dead, mother, says, you, are, it. I'm with Rachel.

好的。而且，我的，死了，妈妈，说，你，是，它。我和瑞秋在一起。

太好了。又是体育课了。

Phoebe and **Rachel Green: **Aww.

菲比和瑞秋·格林：啊。

没关系，你可以到前面来和我跳舞。

Monica Geller: Why don't I just take off my clothes and have a nightmare.

我为什么不脱掉衣服做个恶梦呢。

(She starts to walk very slowly toward the front of the room. The teacher grabs her hand and pulls her. Suddenly a woman bursts in)

（她开始慢慢地向教室前面走去。老师抓住她的手并拉住她。突然一个女人闯了进来）

Woman: It's okay, it's okay, I'm here, I'm here. Sorry I'm late, okay, here I am. Who's the new tense girl?

没事，没事，我在这里，我在这里。对不起，我迟到了，好吧，我来了。那个新来的紧张女孩是谁？

Teacher: She's your partner.

她是你的搭档。

Woman: Hi. I'm Monica.

嗨。我是莫妮卡。

Monica Geller: Oh. Monica! ...Hi. I'm Mo- ...nana.

哦。莫妮卡……你好我是莫…娜娜。

Woman: (Fake Monica) Monana?

（假装莫妮卡）莫娜娜？

Monica Geller: Yeah. It's Dutch.

是的。是荷兰语。

Fake **Monica Geller: **You're kidding! I-I spent three years in Amsterdam. (Asks her something in Dutch)

你在开玩笑！我在阿姆斯特丹呆了三年。（用荷兰语问她）

Monica Geller: Um, Pennsylvania Dutch.

嗯，宾夕法尼亚荷兰语。

Teacher: And we're dancing. A-five, six, seven, eight...

我们在跳舞。A-5，6，7，8……

🎬 中央公园，罗斯进来了。

（羞愧地）嗨。

Chandler and **Joey Tribbiani: **Hey.

钱德勒和乔伊·崔比安尼：嘿。

你去哪儿了？

在兽医那里。

她不会让你戴那种大塑料筒吧？

她说马塞尔的驼背不是一个阶段。显然他已经达到性成熟。

我知道。我知道。我的意思是，有一天，他是一个小东西，在你知道之前，他是一个我无法摆脱的小东西。

你没有办法留住他吗？

你怎么把猴子带进动物园？

不可能。你不可能这么做的。

Fake **Monica Geller: **Monana was very brave.

太疯狂了。我们告诉他们我们是615房间的枪手。结果发现波士顿凯尔特人队占据了整个六楼！

Fake **Monica Geller: **So once they caught on to the fact that we're, y'know, short and have breasts...

假扮莫妮卡·盖勒：所以一旦他们意识到我们，你知道，又矮又有乳房的事实……

Monica Geller: ...They threw us out! I was thrown out of a hotel! Me!

……他们把我们赶了出去！我被赶出了旅馆！我

去吧，莫娜！好吧，不仅仅是你们这些女士实现了梦想。我得去给不认识的人倒咖啡。别等了。（出口）

哦，对了，明天我们要去百老汇试镜。

请原谅我？

假莫妮卡·盖勒：有一个公开的呼吁猫。我在想，我们去那里，唱着回忆，完全愚弄我们自己。你说什么？

不。想想你在跟谁打交道。我是说，我不像你。我甚至不能站在踢踏舞教室前。

Fake **Monica Geller: **Well, that's just probably 'cause of your Amish background.

嗯，那可能是因为你有阿米什人的背景。

Monica Geller: What?

什么？

Fake **Monica Geller: **Well, you're Pennsylvania Dutch, right?

嗯，你是宾夕法尼亚州的达奇人，对吗？

对。直到我买了一个吹风机，我才被回避。

Fake **Monica Geller: **I-I used to be just like you. And then one day I saw a movie that changed my life. Did you ever see Dead Poets' Society?

我以前和你一样。然后有一天我看了一部改变我生活的电影。你见过已故诗人协会吗？

Monica Geller: Uh-huh.

嗯。

Fake **Monica Geller: **I thought that movie was so incredibly... boring. I mean, that thing at the end where the kid kills himself because he can't be in the play? What was that?! It's like, kid, wait a year, leave home, do some community theatre. I walked out of there and I thought, 'Now, that's two hours of my life that I'm never getting back.' And that thought scared me more than all the other crap I was afraid to do.
