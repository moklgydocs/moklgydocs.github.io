The One With the Candy Hearts

Written by: Bill Lawrence

作者：比尔·劳伦斯

Transcribed by: Mindy Mattingly Phillips

明迪·马丁利·菲利普斯

With Minor Adjustments by: Eric Aasen

Eric Aasen

🎬 Central Perk, Ross is eyeing a beautiful woman at the counter, and Joey and Chandler are egging him on to go talk to her. No pun intended. I mean it.

🎬 中央公园，罗斯盯着柜台上的一个漂亮女人，乔伊和钱德勒怂恿他去和她说话。不是故意的双关语。我是认真的。

Joey Tribbiani: I'm tellin' you Ross, she wants you.

我告诉你罗斯，她想要你。

Ross Geller: She barely knows me. We just live in the same building.

她几乎不认识我。我们只是住在同一栋楼里。

Chandler Bing: Any contact?

有联系吗？

Ross Geller: She lent me an egg once.

她曾经借给我一个鸡蛋。

Joey Tribbiani: You're in!

你进来了！

Ross Geller: Aw, right.

噢，对。

Woman: Hi, Ross.

嗨，罗斯。

Ross Geller: Hey. (stutters something incoherent)

嘿。（口吃一些不连贯的东西）

Chandler Bing: Come on, Ross, you gotta get back in the game here, ok? The Rachel thing's not happening, your ex-wife is a lesbian—I don't think we need a third...

来吧，罗斯，你得回到游戏中来，好吗？瑞秋的事没有发生，你的前妻是个女同性恋-我想我们不需要第三个……

Joey Tribbiani: Excuse me, could we get an egg over here, still in the shell? Thanks.

对不起，我们能在这里买一个鸡蛋吗，还是在蛋壳里？谢谢

Ross Geller: An egg?

鸡蛋？

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, you're gonna go up to her and say, "Here's your egg back, I'm returning your egg."

是的，你要走到她跟前说，“这是你的鸡蛋，我要还给你。”

Chandler Bing: I think it's winning.

我想它赢了。

Ross Geller: I think it's insane.

我觉得这太疯狂了。

Chandler Bing: She'll love it. Go with the egg, my friend.

她会喜欢的。跟鸡蛋一起去，我的朋友。

(Ross walks over to the woman, egg in hand.)

（罗斯走向那个女人，手里拿着鸡蛋。）

Joey Tribbiani: Think it'll work?

你觉得行吗？

Chandler Bing: No, it's suicide. The man's got an egg.

不，这是自杀。那人有一个鸡蛋。

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Central Perk, Monica, Rachel, Phoebe, Joey, Chandler are there. Ross is still talking to the beautiful woman.

🎬 中央公园、莫妮卡、瑞秋、菲比、乔伊、钱德勒都在那里。罗斯还在和美女说话。

Monica Geller: You can not do this.

你不能这么做。

Rachel Green: Do what, do what?

做什么，做什么？

Monica Geller: Roger wants to take her out tomorrow night.

罗杰想明天晚上带她出去。

Rachel Green: No! Phoebes! Don't you remember why you dumped the guy?

不！菲比！你不记得你为什么甩了那家伙吗？

Phoebe Buffay: 'Cause he was creepy, and mean, and a little frightening... alright, still, it's nice to have a date on Valentine's Day!

因为他令人毛骨悚然，卑鄙，还有点吓人……好吧，不过，在情人节有个约会真是太好了！

Monica Geller: But Phoebe, you can go out with a creepy guy any night of the year. I know I do.

但是菲比，你可以在一年中的任何晚上和一个令人毛骨悚然的家伙约会。我知道我知道。

Rachel Green: Well, what are you guys doing tomorrow night?

嗯，你们明晚要干什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Actually, tomorrow night kinda depends on how tonight goes.

事实上，明天晚上要看今晚怎么样了。

Chandler Bing: Oh, uh, listen, about tonight...

哦，呃，听着，关于今晚……

Joey Tribbiani: No, no, no, don't you dare bail on me. The only reason she's goin' out with me is because I said I could bring a friend for her friend.

不，不，不，你不敢保释我。她跟我出去的唯一原因是因为我说我可以带一个朋友给她的朋友。

Chandler Bing: Yes, I know, but her friend sounds like such a...

是的，我知道，但她的朋友听起来像是个……

Joey Tribbiani: Pathetic mess? I know, but—come on, man, she's needy, she's vulnerable. I'm thinkin', cha-ching! (Rachel throws a roll at Joey. He picks it up and eats it.) Thanks. Look, you have not been out with a woman since Janice. You're doin' this.

可怜的烂摊子？我知道，但别这样，伙计，她需要帮助，她很脆弱。我在想，茶青！（瑞秋朝乔伊扔了一个面包卷。乔伊捡起面包吃了。）谢谢。听着，自从Janice之后你就没有和女人出去过。你在这么做。

Ross Geller: Hi. She said yes.

嗨。她答应了。

Chandler Bing: Yes! Way to go, man! (Chandler and Ross hug. Something crunches in Ross' shirt pocket.) Still got the egg, huh?

是的！干得好，伙计！（钱德勒和罗斯拥抱。罗斯衬衫口袋里有东西嘎吱作响。）还有鸡蛋吗？

🎬 A Restaurant, Joey and Chandler are there, waiting for their dates to show up.

🎬 一家餐厅，乔伊和钱德勒在那里，等待他们的约会对象出现。

Joey Tribbiani: (Looking at himself in the reflection on a knife) How do I look?

（看着自己在刀子上的倒影）我看起来怎么样？

Chandler Bing: Oh, uh, I... don't... care. (Joey's date shows up) Ok, now, remember, no trading. You get the pretty one, I get the mess.

哦，呃，我……不要……照顾（乔伊的约会对象出现了）好吧，现在，记住，不要交易。你得到漂亮的，我得到混乱。

Lorraine: Hi, Joey. Well well, look what you brought. Very nice.

嗨，乔伊。好吧，看看你带来了什么。很不错的。

Chandler Bing: ...And what did you bring?

……你带来了什么？

Lorraine: She's checking the coats. Joey, I'm gonna go wash the cab smell off my hands. Will you get me a white Zinfandel, and a glass of red for Janice.

她在检查外套。乔伊，我要去洗掉我手上的出租车气味。请给我拿一杯白色的仙粉黛，给珍妮丝拿一杯红色的。

Chandler Bing: Janice?

珍妮丝？

(Lorraine leaves. Joey shakes his head as though to say, 'It can't be the same Janice.' Janice enters.)

（洛林离开了。乔伊摇摇头，好像在说，‘不可能是同一个珍妮丝。’珍妮丝进来了。）

Janice: Oh.... my.... God.

哦……。我的……。上帝

Chandler Bing: (angrily) Hey, it's Janice.

（愤怒地）嘿，我是珍妮丝。

🎬 The bathroom at the restaurant, Chandler and Joey are talking.

🎬 餐厅的浴室，钱德勒和乔伊在聊天。

Chandler Bing: Ok, I'm makin' a break for it, I'm goin' out the window.

好吧，我要休息一下，我要从窗户出去。

Joey Tribbiani: No, no, no, don't! I've been waitin' for like, forever to go out with Lorraine. Just calm down.

不，不，不，不要！我已经等了很久，永远都等着和洛林约会。冷静点。

Chandler Bing: Calm down? Calm down? You set me up with the woman that I've dumped twice in the last five months!

冷静点？冷静你陷害了我和我在过去五个月里甩了两次的女人！

Joey Tribbiani: (at the urinal) Can you stop yellin'? You're makin' me nervous, and I can't go when I'm nervous.

（在小便池旁）你能不能别喊了？你让我紧张，紧张的时候我不能去。

Chandler Bing: I'm sorry, I'm sorry, you're right. (gets up right behind Joey and yells in his ear) Come on, do it, do it, go, come on!!!

对不起，对不起，你说得对。（站在乔伊身后对着他的耳朵大叫）来吧，做吧，做吧，去吧，来吧！！！

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, the girls are all there, discussing their bad luck with men.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，女孩们都在那里，和男人谈论她们的厄运。

Rachel Green: Ok, ok, Roger was creepy, but he was nothing compared to Pete Carney.

好吧，好吧，罗杰令人毛骨悚然，但和皮特·卡尼相比，他算不了什么。

Monica Geller: Which one was Pete Carney?

哪一个是皮特·卡尼？

Rachel Green: Pete the Weeper? Remember that guy who used to cry every time we had sex. (imitating) "Was it good for you?"

哭泣的皮特？还记得那个每次我们做爱都会哭的家伙吗。（模仿）“这对你有好处吗？”

Monica Geller: Yeah, well, I'll take a little crying any day over Howard-the-"I-win"-guy. (imitating) "I win! I win!" I went out with the guy for two months—I didn't get to win once.

是啊，我随时都会为霍华德这个“我赢”的家伙而哭。（模仿）“我赢了！我赢了！”我和那个家伙约会了两个月，一次也没赢。

Rachel Green: How did we end up with these jerks? We're good people!

我们怎么会和这些混蛋在一起？我们是好人！

Monica Geller: I don't know. Maybe we're some kinda magnets.

我不知道。也许我们是磁铁。

Phoebe Buffay: I know I am. That's why I can't wear a digital watch.

我知道我是。这就是为什么我不能戴电子表。

Monica Geller: There's more beer, right?

还有啤酒，对吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh! You know my friend Abby who shaves her head? She said that if you want to break the bad boyfriend cycle, you can do like a cleansing ritual.

哦！你认识我的朋友艾比，她剃光头？她说如果你想打破坏男朋友的恶性循环，你可以做一个清洁仪式。

Rachel Green: Pheebes, this woman is voluntarily bald.

菲比，这个女人是自愿秃头的。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah. So, we can do it tomorrow night, you guys. It's Valentine's Day. It's perfect.

是的。所以，我们可以明天晚上做，伙计们。今天是情人节。太完美了。

Monica Geller: Ok, well, what kind of ritual?

好吧，那么，什么样的仪式？

Phoebe Buffay: Ok. We can, um, we can burn the stuff they gave us.

好的。我们可以，嗯，我们可以烧掉他们给我们的东西。

Rachel Green: Or?

还是？

Phoebe Buffay: Or...or we can chant and dance around naked, you know, with sticks.

或者…或者我们可以裸体唱歌跳舞，你知道，用棍子。

Monica Geller: Burning's good.

燃烧很好。

Rachel Green: Burning's good. Yeah, I got stuff to burn.

燃烧很好。是的，我有东西要烧。

🎬 The Restaurant, Joey, Lorraine, Chandler, and Janice are at the table. Joey and Lorraine are seated very close, Chandler and Janice have backed their chairs away from one another.

🎬 餐厅、乔伊、洛林、钱德勒和珍妮丝在桌旁。乔伊和洛林坐得很近，钱德勒和珍妮丝把椅子往后靠着彼此。

Lorraine: You know, ever since I was little, I've been able to pick up quarters with my toes.

你知道，从我很小的时候起，我就可以用脚趾捡起25美分的硬币。

Joey Tribbiani: Good for you. (jumps suddenly) Uh, quarters or rolls of quarters?

对你有好处。（突然跳起来）呃，四分之一硬币还是一卷四分之一硬币？

Janice: By the way, Chandler. I cut you out of all my pictures. So if you want, I have a bag with just your heads.

对了，钱德勒。我把你从我所有的照片中删掉了。如果你愿意，我有一个只有你脑袋的包。

Chandler Bing: That's OK.

没关系。

Janice: Oh, are you sure? Really? Because you know, you could make little puppets out of them, and you could use them in your theater of cruelty.

哦，你确定吗？真正地因为你知道，你可以用它们制作小木偶，你可以把它们用在你的残酷戏剧中。

(Lorraine whispers into Joey's ear.)

（洛林对着乔伊耳语。）

Joey Tribbiani: (to Lorraine) We can't do that.

（对洛林）我们不能那样做。

Chandler Bing: (disgusted) What? What can't you do?

（厌恶）什么？你不能做什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Uh, can I talk to you for a second, over there?

我能和你谈谈吗，在那边？

(Chandler and Joey leave the table.)

（钱德勒和乔伊离开了桌子。）

Joey Tribbiani: Uh, we might be leaving now.

呃，我们现在可能要走了。

Chandler Bing: Tell me it's "you and me" we.

告诉我我们是“你和我”。

Joey Tribbiani: She said she wants to slather my body with stuff and then lick it off. I'm not even sure what slathering is, but I definitely want to be a part of it.

她说她想在我身上涂上东西，然后舔掉。我甚至不确定slathering是什么，但我肯定想成为其中的一部分。

Chandler Bing: Ok, you can not do this to me.

好吧，你不能这样对我。

Joey Tribbiani: You're right, I'm sorry. You're right.

你说得对，对不起。你说得对。

Lorraine: (to waiter) Uh, can we have three chocolate mousses to go please?

（对侍者）嗯，我们可以要三份巧克力慕斯吗？

Joey Tribbiani: I'm outta here. Here's my credit card. Dinner's on me. I'm sorry, Chandler.

我要走了。这是我的信用卡。晚餐我请客。对不起，钱德勒。

Chandler Bing: I hope she throws up on you.

我希望她吐在你身上。

(Joey leaves with Lorraine. Chandler sits back down with Janice.)

（乔伊和洛林一起离开。钱德勒和珍妮丝坐了下来。）

Chandler Bing: So...

那么……

Janice: Just us.

只有我们。

Chandler Bing: Oh, what a crappy night!

哦，多么糟糕的夜晚！

Janice: Although, I have enjoyed the fact that, uh your shirt's been stickin' outta your zipper ever since you came back from the bathroom.

不过，我很高兴，自从你从浴室回来后，你的衬衫就从拉链上粘了下来。

Chandler Bing: Excuse me. (gets up, jumps up and down while he zips his zipper up... other patrons look at him) How ya doin'?

对不起。（站起来，跳上跳下，他拉上拉链……其他顾客看着他）你好吗？

Janice: So, do we have the best friends or what?

那么，我们有最好的朋友吗？

Chandler Bing: Joey's not a friend. He's...a stupid man who left us his credit card. Another drink? Some dessert? A big screen TV?

乔伊不是朋友。他…一个把信用卡留给我们的笨蛋。再来一杯？要甜点吗？大屏幕电视？

Janice: I will go for that drink.

我去喝一杯。

Chandler Bing: You got it. Good woman! (the waiter turns around, it's a man) Could we get a bottle of your most overpriced champagne?

明白了。好女人！（侍者转过身来，是个男人）我们能喝一瓶你们最贵的香槟吗？

Janice: Each.

每个。

Chandler Bing: That's right, each. Oh, and a uh Rob Roy. (to Janice) I've always wanted to know...

没错，每个人都是。哦，还有一个，呃，罗伯·罗伊。（对珍妮丝）我一直想知道……

🎬 Chandler's bedroom, Chandler wakes up, and finds someone else's hand on his chest. He rolls over and is shocked to see Janice there.

🎬 钱德勒的卧室，钱德勒醒来，发现有人的手放在他的胸前。他翻了个身，看到珍妮丝在那里，吓了一跳。

Janice: Happy Valentine's Day!

情人节快乐！

Commercial Break

商业休息

🎬 The Hallway, Chandler is trying to get Janice out of his apartment.

🎬 在走廊上，钱德勒正试图把珍妮丝从公寓里救出来。

Janice: Oh, I miss you already. Can you believe this happened?

哦，我已经想你了。你能相信这一切吗？

Chandler Bing: No... no! And yet it did. Good-bye, Janice.

不……不但事实确实如此。再见，珍妮丝。

Janice: Kiss me!

吻我！

(Janice kisses him. Monica comes out for the newspaper.}

（珍妮丝吻了他。莫妮卡出来看报纸。）

Monica Geller: Oh, Chandler, sorry.

哦，钱德勒，对不起。

(Janice turns around, Monica sees who it is.)

（珍妮丝转过身，莫妮卡看到了是谁。）

Monica Geller: Ohhh, Chandler, sorry! Hey, Janice.

哦，钱德勒，对不起！嘿，珍妮丝。

Janice: Hi, Monica.

嗨，莫妮卡。

Chandler Bing: Ok, well, this was very special.

好吧，这很特别。

Monica Geller: Rach, come see who's out here!

瑞秋，过来看看谁在这里！

(Rachel comes out.)

（瑞秋出来了。）

Rachel Green: Oh my god. Janice, hi!

哦，我的天啊。珍妮丝，嗨！

Chandler Bing: Janice is gonna go away now.

珍妮丝现在要走了。

Monica Geller: I'll be right back.

我马上回来。

(Joey enters from the stairs.)

（乔伊从楼梯上走了进来。）

Rachel Green: Oh, Joey, look who it is.

哦，乔伊，看看是谁。

Joey Tribbiani: (in disbelief) Whoa.

（难以置信）哇。

Chandler Bing: Oh, good, Joey's home now.

哦，太好了，乔伊现在回家了。

Janice: This is so fun. This is like a reunion in the hall.

这太有趣了。这就像在大厅里重聚一样。

(Monica comes out with her cordless phone.)

（莫妮卡拿出她的无绳电话。）

Monica Geller: Oh, hi, Ross. Yeah. There's someone I want you to say hi to. (to Chandler) He just happened to call.

哦，嗨，罗斯。是的。我想让你跟一个人打个招呼。（对钱德勒）他碰巧打电话来。

Janice: Hi, Ross. Yes, it's me. How did you know? (she laughs obnoxiously)

你好，罗斯。是的，是我。你怎么知道的？（她厌恶地笑）

🎬 A Chinese Restaurant, Ross is there with his date.

🎬 一家中国餐馆，罗斯和他的约会对象在那里。

Ross Geller: I'm just sayin' if dogs do experience jet lag, then, because of the whole um, seven dog years to one human year thing, then, when a dog flies from New York to Los Angeles, he doesn't just lose three hours, he loses like a week and a half.

我只是说，如果狗真的经历了时差，那么，因为整个嗯，七个狗年到一个人年的事情，那么，当狗从纽约飞到洛杉矶时，它不仅损失了三个小时，还损失了大约一周半。

(Ross starts to laugh, and then makes a face like 'Why did I just say that?' Ross' ex-wife, Carol, and her lesbian lover, Susan, enter the restaurant. Ross stares at them.)

（罗斯笑了起来，然后做了个鬼脸，像是‘我为什么这么说？’罗斯的前妻卡萝和她的女同性恋情人苏珊走进餐厅。罗斯盯着他们。）

Kristin: That's funny. Who are they?

真有趣。他们是谁？

Ross Geller: The blond woman is my ex-wife, and the woman touching her is her... close, personal friend.

那个金发女人是我的前妻，抚摸她的女人是她的…亲密的私人朋友。

Kristin: You mean they're lovers.

你是说他们是情侣。

Ross Geller: If you wanna put a label on it.

如果你想贴标签的话。

Kristin: Wow, uh, anything else I should know?

哇，嗯，还有什么我应该知道的吗？

Ross Geller: Nope, nope, that's it.

不，不，就是这样。

(Carol takes off her jacket, her pregnant belly is exposed.)

（卡萝脱下外套，露出怀孕的肚子。）

Ross Geller: Oh, and she's pregnant with my baby. I always forget that part. (to Carol and Susan) Helloo!

哦，她怀了我的孩子。我总是忘记那部分。（对卡萝和苏珊）嗨！

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, the girls are holding their boyfriend bonfire.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，女孩们正拿着他们男朋友的篝火。

Phoebe Buffay: Ok, so now we need, um sage branches and the sacramental wine.

好的，现在我们需要鼠尾草树枝和圣餐酒。

Monica Geller: All I have is, is oregano and a Fresca.

我只有牛至和佛雷斯卡。

Phoebe Buffay: Um, that's ok! (throws it in fire) Ok. All right. Now we need the semen of a righteous man.

嗯，没关系！（把它扔进火里）好的。好的。现在我们需要一个正直男人的精液。

Rachel Green: Ok, Pheebs, you know what, if we had that, we wouldn't be doing the ritual in the first place.

好吧，菲比，你知道吗，如果我们有这个，我们一开始就不会做这个仪式了。
