S01E08 The One Where Nana Dies Twice

The One Where Nana Dies Twice

娜娜死两次的故事

[Scene: Chandler's Office, Chandler is on a coffee break. Shelley enters.)
Shelley: Hey gorgeous, how's it going?

嘿，美女，最近怎么样？

Chandler Bing: Dehydrated Japanese noodles under fluorescent lights... does it get better than this?

荧光灯下的脱水日本面条……比这个好吗？

Shelley: Question. You're not dating anybody, are you, because I met somebody who would be perfect for you.

问题。你没有和任何人约会，是吗？因为我遇到了一个非常适合你的人。

Chandler Bing: Ah, y'see, perfect might be a problem. Had you said 'co-dependent', or 'self-destructive'...

啊，你看，完美可能是个问题。如果你说“共同依赖”或“自我毁灭”……

Shelley: Do you want a date Saturday?

星期六你想约会吗？

Chandler Bing: Yes please.

好的。

Shelley: Okay. He's cute, he's funny, he's-

好吧。他很可爱，很有趣，很有趣-

Chandler Bing: He's a he?

他是一个男人，他？

Shelley: Well yeah! ...Oh God. I- just- I thought- Good, Shelley. I'm just gonna go flush myself down the toilet now...(backs out of the room) Okay, goodbye...

嗯，是的！…哦，天啊。我-只是-我想-很好，雪莉。我现在要去冲厕所了…（从房间后面）好的，再见……

Opening Credits

期初学分

[Scene: Monica and Rachel's, everyone is there.)
Chandler Bing: ...Couldn't enjoy a cup of noodles after that. I mean, is that ridiculous? Can you believe she actually thought that?

……在那之后就不能吃一杯面条了。我是说，那可笑吗？你能相信她真的这么想吗？

Rachel Green: Um... yeah. Well, I mean, when I first met you, y'know, I thought maybe, possibly, you might be...

嗯…是的。嗯，我是说，当我第一次见到你的时候，我想也许，也许，你可能……

Chandler Bing: You did?

是吗？

Rachel Green: Yeah, but then you spent Phoebe's entire birthday party talking to my breasts, so then I figured maybe not.

是啊，但后来你在菲比的整个生日派对上都在跟我的胸部说话，所以我想也许不是。

Chandler Bing: Huh. Did, uh... any of the rest of you guys think that when you first met me?

嗯。你们其他人第一次见到我的时候有没有想过？

Monica Geller: I did.

是的。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, I think so, yeah.

是的，我想是的，是的。

Joey Tribbiani: Not me.

不是我。

Ross Geller: Nono, me neither. Although, uh, y'know, back in college, Susan Sallidor did.

不，我也不知道。虽然，嗯，你知道，在大学里，苏珊·萨利多做到了。

Chandler Bing: You're kidding! Did you tell her I wasn't?

你在开玩笑！你告诉她我不是吗？

Ross Geller: No. No, it's just 'cause, uh, I kinda wanted to go out with her too, so I told her, actually, you were seeing Bernie Spellman... who also liked her, so...

不，不，只是因为，呃，我也有点想和她出去，所以我告诉她，实际上，你在和伯尼·斯佩尔曼约会……他也喜欢她，所以……

(Joey congratulates Ross, sees Chandler's look and abruptly stops.)

（乔伊祝贺罗斯，看到钱德勒的表情，突然停了下来。）

Chandler Bing: Well, this is fascinating. So, uh, what is it about me?

嗯，这很迷人。那么，呃，我是怎么了？

Phoebe Buffay: I dunno, 'cause you're smart, you're funny...

我不知道，因为你很聪明，很有趣……

Chandler Bing: Ross is smart and funny, d'you ever think that about him?

罗斯既聪明又有趣，你有没有想过他？

All: Yeah! Right!

是的！对！

Chandler Bing: WHAT IS IT?!

什么事？！

Monica Geller: Okay, I-I d'know, you-you just- you have a quality.

好吧，我知道，你只是-你有一种品质。

All: Yes. Absolutely. A quality.

是的，绝对是。一种品质。

Chandler Bing: Oh, oh, a quality, good, because I was worried you guys were gonna be vague about this.

哦，哦，质量很好，因为我担心你们会对此含糊其辞。

(Phone rings; Monica gets it)

（电话铃响了，莫妮卡接到了）

Monica Geller: Hello? Hello? Oh! Rachel, it's Paolo calling from Rome.

喂？喂？哦！瑞秋，我是罗马来的保罗。

Rachel Green: Oh my God! Calling from Rome! (Takes phone) Bon giorno, caro mio.

哦，天哪！从罗马打来的电话！（拿起电话）邦吉奥尔诺，卡洛米奥。

Ross Geller: (to Joey) So he's calling from Rome. I could do that. Just gotta go to Rome.

（对乔伊）他是从罗马打来的。我可以这么做。只要去罗马就行了。

Rachel Green: Monica, your dad just beeped in, but can you make it quick? Talking to Rome. (Showing off to Phoebe and Chandler) I'm talking to Rome.

莫妮卡，你爸爸刚打电话进来，但你能快点吗？跟罗马说话。（向菲比和钱德勒炫耀）我在跟罗马说话。

Monica Geller: Hey dad, what's up? (Listens) Oh God. Ross, it's Nana.

嘿，爸爸，怎么了？（听着）哦，天哪，罗斯，我是娜娜。

[Scene: The Hospital, Mr. and Mrs. Geller are there, along with Aunt Lillian. Ross and Monica enter and everyone says hi and kisses.)
Ross Geller: So, uh, how's she doing?

那么，呃，她怎么样了？

Aunt Lillian: The doctor says it's a matter of hours.

医生说这只是几个小时的问题。

Monica Geller: How-how are you, Mom?

你好吗，妈妈？

Mrs. Geller: Me? I'm fine, fine. I'm glad you're here. ...What's with your hair?

我？我很好，很好。我很高兴你在这里……你的头发怎么了？

Monica Geller: What?

什么？

Mrs. Geller: What's different?

有什么不同？

Monica Geller: Nothing.

没什么。

Mrs. Geller: Oh, maybe that's it.

哦，也许就是这样。

(Monica strides over to Ross, who is making coffee, and talks to him aside.)

（莫妮卡大步走到正在煮咖啡的罗斯跟前，和他在一旁交谈。）

Monica Geller: She is unbelievable, our mother is...

她太不可思议了，我们的妈妈……

Ross Geller: Okay, relax, relax. We are gonna be here for a while, it looks like, and we still have boyfriends and your career to cover.

好的，放松，放松。我们会在这里呆一段时间，看起来，我们还有男朋友和你的事业要报道。

Monica Geller: Oh God!

哦，天哪！

(They hug.)

（他们拥抱。）

[Cut to the hospital, later. Everyone is talking about Nana.]

[稍后转到医院。每个人都在谈论娜娜。]

Monica Geller: The fuzzy little mints at the bottom of her purse.

钱包底部的毛茸茸的小薄荷糖。

Ross Geller: Oh! ...Yeah, they were gross. Oh, you know what I loved? Her Sweet 'n' Los. How she was always stealing them from- from restaurants.

哦！…是的，它们很恶心。哦，你知道我喜欢什么吗？她的甜心。她总是从餐馆偷东西。

Mr. Geller: Not just restaurants, from our house.

不仅仅是餐馆，从我们家。

(The nurse comes out of Nana's room.)

（护士走出娜娜的房间。）

Nurse: Mrs. Geller?

盖勒太太？

(Everyone stands up. Cut to Ross and Monica in Nana's room.)

（大家都站起来，切到娜娜房间里的罗斯和莫妮卡。）

Ross Geller: She looks so small.

她看起来很小。

Monica Geller: I know.

我知道。

Ross Geller: Well, at least she's with Pop-Pop and Aunt Phyllis now.

嗯，至少她现在和爸爸和菲利斯阿姨在一起了。

Monica Geller: G'bye, Nana. (She kisses her on the forehead.)

再见，娜娜。（她吻了吻她的前额。）

Ross Geller: Bye, Nana.

再见，娜娜。

(He goes to kiss her but she moves. Monica screams. Ross shouts and stares in disbelief. Monica runs out of the room.)

（他去吻她，但她动了。莫妮卡尖叫。罗斯不相信地大叫和凝视。莫妮卡跑出了房间。）

Monica Geller: Ross!

罗斯！

(Ross runs out too.)

（罗斯也跑了出去。）

Mrs. Geller: What is going on?!

怎么回事？！

Ross Geller: Y'know how-how the nurse said that-that Nana had passed? Well, she's not, quite..

你知道护士是怎么说娜娜去世的吗？嗯，她不是，完全。。

Mrs. Geller: What?

什么？

Ross Geller: She's not- past, she's present, she's back.

她没有过去，她现在，她回来了。

Aunt Lillian: (reentering) What's going on?

（重新进来）发生了什么事？

Mr. Geller: She may have died.

她可能已经死了。

Aunt Lillian: She may have died?

她可能已经死了？

Mr. Geller: We're looking into it.

我们正在调查。

(Monica returns with the nurse and they go into Nana's room.)

（莫妮卡带着护士回来，他们走进了娜娜的房间。）

Ross Geller: I, uh, I'll go see. (He goes in)

我，呃，我去看看。（他进去）

Nurse: This almost never happens!

这几乎从未发生过！

(Nana passes for the second time and the nurse pulls the blanket over her. Ross and Monica go to tell the family)

（娜娜第二次过世，护士把毯子盖在她身上。罗斯和莫妮卡去告诉家人）

Ross Geller: Now she's passed.

现在她过世了。

🎬 Central Perk, Chandler, Phoebe, Joey, and Rachel are there.

🎬 中央公园、钱德勒、菲比、乔伊和瑞秋在那里

Chandler Bing: I just have to know, okay. Is it my hair?

我只是想知道，好吧。是我的头发吗？

Rachel Green: (exasperated) Yes, Chandler, that's exactly what it is. It's your hair.

（恼怒地）是的，钱德勒，就是这样。是你的头发。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, you have homosexual hair.

是啊，你的头发是同性恋。

(Monica and Ross enter.)

（莫妮卡和罗斯进来了。）

Rachel Green: So, um, did she...

那么，嗯，她是不是……

Ross Geller: Twice.

两次。

Joey Tribbiani: Twice?

两次？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, that sucks!

哦，太糟糕了！

Joey Tribbiani: You guys okay?

你们还好吧？

Ross Geller: I dunno, it's weird. I mean, I know she's gone, but I just don't feel, uh...

我不知道，这很奇怪。我是说，我知道她走了，但我不觉得……

Phoebe Buffay: Maybe that's 'cause she's not really gone.

也许那是因为她还没有真的走了。

Ross Geller: Nono, she's gone.

不，她走了。

Monica Geller: We checked. A lot.

我们查过了。很多

Phoebe Buffay: Hm, I mean maybe no-one ever really goes. Ever since my mom died, every now and then, I get the feeling that she's like right here, y'know? (She circles her hand around her right shoulder. Chandler, sitting on her right, draws back nervously) Oh! And Debbie, my best friend from junior high- got struck by lightning on a miniature golf course- I always get this really strong Debbie vibe whenever I use one of those little yellow pencils, y'know? ...I miss her.

嗯，我是说也许从来没有人真正去。自从我妈妈死后，我时不时地感觉到她就在这里，你知道吗？（她把手绕在右肩上。坐在她右边的钱德勒紧张地往后缩）噢！还有黛比，我初中时最好的朋友——在一个迷你高尔夫球场上被闪电击中——每当我用一支黄色的小铅笔时，我总是有一种非常强烈的黛比感觉，你知道吗……我想念她。

Rachel Green: Aw. Hey, Pheebs, want this? (Gives her a pencil)

噢。嘿，菲比，要这个吗？（给她一支铅笔）

Phoebe Buffay: Thanks!

谢谢！

Rachel Green: Sure. I just sharpened her this morning.

当然可以。我今天早上刚磨了她一下。

Joey Tribbiani: Now, see, I don't believe any of that. I think once you're dead, you're dead! You're gone! You're worm food! (realises his tactlessness) ...So Chandler looks gay, huh?

现在，看，我一点都不相信。我想一旦你死了，你就死定了！你走了！你是虫子食物！（意识到他的不得体）…那么钱德勒看起来是同性恋，是吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Y'know, I dunno who this is, but it's not Debbie. (Hands back the pencil)

你知道，我不知道这是谁，但不是黛比。（把铅笔递回来）

🎬 Nana's house, Ross, Mrs. Geller and Aunt Lillian are going through clothes.

🎬 娜娜的房子、罗斯、盖勒太太和莉莲阿姨正在检查衣服。

Ross Geller: I thought it was gonna be a closed casket.

我还以为会是一个封闭的棺材呢。

Mrs. Geller: Well, that doesn't mean she can't look nice!

嗯，那并不意味着她看起来不好看！

(They open a cupboard which, amongst other things, contains a chest of drawers)

（他们打开一个橱柜，其中包括一个抽屉柜）

Mrs. Geller: Sweetie, you think you can get in there?

亲爱的，你觉得你能进去吗？

Ross Geller: (sarcastic) I don't see why not.

（讽刺地）我不明白为什么不。

(He tries pushing against the chest of drawers. Then he opens one of the drawers and climbs into the closet using that; he falls behind the chest of drawers with a shout.)

（他试着推抽屉。然后他打开其中一个抽屉，用它爬进壁橱；他大叫一声落在抽屉后面。）

Ross Geller: Here's my retainer!

这是我的固定器！

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Monica is talking to her father.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，莫妮卡正在和她父亲谈话。

Mr. Geller: I was just thinking. When my time comes-

我只是在想。当我的时代来临-

Monica Geller: Dad!

爸爸！

Mr. Geller: Listen to me! When my time comes, I wanna be buried at sea.

听我说！当我的时代来临时，我想被海葬。

Monica Geller: You what?

你什么？

Mr. Geller: I wanna be buried at sea, it looks like fun.

我想被埋葬在海里，看起来很有趣。

Monica Geller: Define fun.

定义乐趣。

Mr. Geller: C'mon, you'll make a day of it! You'll rent a boat, pack a lunch...

来吧，你会成功的！你会租一艘船，打包午餐……

Monica Geller: ...And then we throw your body in the water... Gee, that does sound fun.

……然后我们把你的尸体扔进水里……哎呀，听起来确实很有趣。

Mr. Geller: Everyone thinks they know me. Everyone says 'Jack Geller, so predictable'. Maybe after I'm gone, they'll say 'Buried at sea! Huh!'.

每个人都认为他们认识我。每个人都说‘杰克·盖勒，如此可预测’。也许我走后，他们会说‘埋在海里！哼。

Monica Geller: That's probably what they'll say.

他们可能会这么说。

Mr. Geller: I'd like that.

我喜欢。

🎬 Chandler's Office, Shelley is drinking coffee; Chandler enters.

🎬 钱德勒的办公室，雪莱正在喝咖啡；钱德勒进来。

Chandler Bing: Hey, gorgeous.

嘿，好极了。

Shelley: (sheepish) Hey. Look, I'm sorry about yesterday, I, um-

（不好意思）嘿。听着，昨天的事我很抱歉，我-

Chandler Bing: No, nono, don't- don't worry about it. Believe me, apparently other people have made the same mistake.

不，不，别担心。相信我，显然其他人也犯了同样的错误。

Shelley: Oh! Okay! Phew!

哦！可以呸！

Chandler Bing: So, uh... what do you think it is about me?

那么，呃……你觉得我怎么样？

Shelley: I dunno, uh... you just have a-a...

我不知道，嗯……你只是有一个……

Chandler Bing: ...Quality, right, great.

……质量，对，很好。

Shelley: Y'know, it's a shame, because you and Lowell would've made a great couple.

你知道，这真是太遗憾了，因为你和洛威尔会是一对很好的夫妻。

Chandler Bing: Lowell? Financial Services' Lowell, that's who you saw me with?

洛厄尔？金融服务公司的洛威尔，你看到我和他在一起？

Shelley: What? He's cute!

什么？他很可爱！

Chandler Bing: Well, yeah... 's'no Brian in Payroll.

嗯，是的工资单上没有布莱恩。

Shelley: Is Brian...?

布莱恩是……？

Chandler Bing: No! Uh, I d'know! The point is, if you were gonna set me up with someone, I'd like to think you'd set me up with someone like him.

不！嗯，我知道！关键是，如果你要陷害我，我想你会陷害我和他一样的人。

Shelley: Well, I think Brian's a little out of your league.

嗯，我觉得布莱恩有点不适合你。

Chandler Bing: Excuse me? You don't think I could get a Brian? Because I could get a Brian. Believe you me. ...I'm really not.

对不起？你认为我找不到布莱恩？因为我可以得到一个布莱恩。相信我……我真的不是。

🎬 Nana's Bedroom, Ross is holding a dress out from inside the closet.

🎬 娜娜的卧室，罗斯从衣柜里拿出一件衣服。

Ross Geller: (holding a dress out from inside the closet) This one?

（从衣柜里拿出一件衣服）这件？

Aunt Lillian: No.

没有。

Ross Geller: I have shown you everything we have. Unless you want your mother to spend eternity in a lemon yellow pant-suit, go with the burgundy.

我已经把我们所有的东西都给你看了。除非你想让你的母亲永远穿着柠檬黄的裤子，否则就选勃艮第吧。

Aunt Lillian: You know, whatever we pick, she would've told us it's the wrong one.

你知道，不管我们选什么，她都会告诉我们选错了。

Mrs. Geller: You're right. We'll go with the burgundy.

你说得对。我们要勃艮第葡萄酒。

Ross Geller: Oh! A fine choice. I'm coming out. (Starts to climb over the furniture)

哦！不错的选择。我要出来了。（开始爬过家具）

Aunt Lillian: Wait! We need shoes!

等等！我们需要鞋子！

(Ross falls back inside)

（罗斯倒在里面）

Ross Geller: Okay. Um, how about these? (Holds out a pair)

好的。嗯，这些怎么样？（伸出一对）

Mrs. Geller: That's really a day shoe.

那真是一双普通鞋。

Ross Geller: And where she's going everyone else'll be dressier?

她要去哪里其他人都会穿得更考究？

Aunt Lillian: Could we see something in a slimmer heel?

我们能看看更细的鞋跟吗？

Ross Geller: (forages around) Okay, I have nothing in an evening shoe in the burgundy. I can show you something in a silver that may work.

（四处觅食）好吧，我在勃艮第没有穿晚礼服。我可以给你看一些可能有用的银币。

Aunt Lillian: No, it really should be burgundy.

不，应该是勃艮第。

Mrs. Geller: Mm. Unless we go with a different dress?

嗯。除非我们换件衣服？

Ross Geller: No! Nonono, wait a sec. I may have something in the back.

不！诺诺，等一下。我后面可能有东西。

(He finds a shoebox (out of shot), pulls it down and opens it. It is full of Sweet 'n' Lo's.)

（他找到一个鞋盒（镜头外），把它拉下来打开，里面装满了甜蜜的n'Lo's。）

Ross Geller: Oh my God..

哦，我的天。。

Mrs. Geller: Is everything all right, dear?

亲爱的，一切都好吗？

Ross Geller: Yeah, just... just Nana stuff.

是的，只是……只是娜娜的东西。

(He reaches up higher and knocks down another shoebox lid. Sweet 'n' Lo's rain down on him)

（他站得更高，撞倒了另一个鞋盒盖。甜蜜的n'Lo雨落在他身上）

Commercial Break

商业休息

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Monica and Rachel are preparing to leave for the funeral.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，莫妮卡和瑞秋准备去参加葬礼。

Ross Geller: (entering) How we doing, you guys ready?

（进来）怎么样，你们准备好了吗？

Monica Geller: Mom already called this morning to remind me not to wear my hair up. Did you know my ears are not my best feature?

妈妈今天早上已经打电话提醒我不要梳头发了。你知道我的耳朵不是我最好的特征吗？

Ross Geller: Some days it's all I can think about.

有时候我只能想到这些。

Phoebe Buffay: (entering) Hi, sorry I'm late, I couldn't find my bearings.

（进来）嗨，对不起我迟到了，我找不到方向。

Rachel Green: Oh, you-you mean your earrings?

哦，你是说你的耳环？

Phoebe Buffay: What'd I say?

我说什么了？

Rachel Green: (sticking her foot out) Hm-m.

（伸出脚）嗯。
