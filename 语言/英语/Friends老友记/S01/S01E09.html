S01E09 The One Where Underdog Gets Away

The One Where Underdog Gets Away

失败者逃之夭夭

Written by: Jeff Greenstein & Jeff Strauss

作者：杰夫·格林斯坦和杰夫·施特劳斯

Transcribed by: Mindy Mattingly Phillips

明迪·马丁利·菲利普斯

With Minor Adjustments by: Dan Silverstein

丹·西尔弗斯坦

🎬 Central Perk, Rachel is confronting her boss, Terry.

🎬 中央公园，瑞秋面对她的老板特里。

Rachel Green: Terry, I, I, I know that I haven't worked here very long, but I was wondering, do you think it would be possible if I got a $100 advance in my salary?

泰瑞，我，我，我知道我在这里工作的时间不长，但我想知道，如果我能预支100美元的薪水，你觉得有可能吗？

Terry: An advance?

预付款？

Rachel Green: It's so that I can spend Thanksgiving with my family. See, every year we go skiing in Vail, and normally my father pays for my ticket, but I sort of started the whole independence thing, you know, which is actually why I took this job.

这样我就可以和家人一起过感恩节了。看，每年我们都去维尔滑雪，通常我父亲会为我买票，但我开始了整个独立的过程，你知道，这就是我接受这份工作的原因。

Terry: Rachel, Rachel, sweetheart. You're a terrible, terrible waitress. Really, really awful.

瑞秋，瑞秋，甜心。你是个糟糕透顶的女服务员。真的，真的很糟糕。

Rachel Green: Ok, I, I hear what you're sayin'. I'm with you. Um, but I, but I'm trying really hard. And I think I'm doing better. I really do. Does anybody need coffee? (everyone in the place raises their hand) Oh, look at that.

好吧，我，我听到你在说什么了。我支持你。嗯，但是我，但是我真的很努力。我想我做得更好了。我真的喜欢。有人需要咖啡吗？（在场的每个人都举手）哦，看那个。

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Central Perk, Rachel is approaching a customer.

🎬 中央公园，瑞秋正在接近一位顾客。

Rachel Green: Excuse me, sir. Hi, you come in here all time. I was just wondering, do you think there's a possibility that you could give me an advance on my tips?

对不起，先生。嗨，你总是来这里。我只是想知道，你认为有可能提前给我小费吗？

Guy: Huh?

嗯？

Rachel Green: Ok, ok, that's fine. Fine. Hey, I'm sorry about that spill before. (picks up the tip he leaves) Only $98.50 to go.

好的，好的，没关系。好的嘿，我很抱歉之前的泄漏。（拿起他留下的小费）还剩98.50美元。

(Monica enters.)

（莫妮卡进来了。）

Monica Geller: Hey. Ross, did you know Mom and Dad are going to Puerto Rico for Thanksgiving?

嘿。罗斯，你知道爸爸妈妈要去波多黎各过感恩节吗？

Ross Geller: No, they're not.

不，他们不是。

Monica Geller: Yes, they are. The Blymens invited them.

是的。布莱门斯夫妇邀请了他们。

Ross Geller: You're wrong.

你错了。

Monica Geller: I am not wrong.

我没有错。

Ross Geller: You're wrong.

你错了。

Monica Geller: No, I just talked to them.

没有，我刚和他们谈过。

Ross Geller: (getting up, upset) I'm calling Mom.

（站起来，心烦意乱）我在给妈妈打电话。

(Joey enters. His face looks abnormally colorful.)

（乔伊走了进来，他的脸看起来异常的五颜六色。）

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, hey.

嘿，嘿。

Chandler Bing: Hey.

嘿。

Phoebe Buffay: Hey.

嘿。

Chandler Bing: And this from the cry-for-help department. Are you wearing makeup?

这是呼救部的。你在化妆吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Yes, I am. As of today, I am officially Joey Tribbiani, actor slash model.

是的，我是。从今天起，我正式成为演员兼模特乔伊·特里比亚尼。

Chandler Bing: That's so funny, 'cause I was thinking you look more like Joey Tribbiani, man slash woman.

真有趣，因为我觉得你看起来更像乔伊·特里比亚尼，男人和女人。

Phoebe Buffay: What were you modeling for?

你为什么要当模特？

Joey Tribbiani: You know those posters for the city free clinic?

你知道城市免费诊所的海报吗？

Monica Geller: Oh, wow, so you're gonna be one of those "healthy, healthy, healthy guys"?

哦，哇，那么你会成为那种“健康、健康、健康的人”吗？

Phoebe Buffay: You know, the asthma guy was really cute.

你知道，那个哮喘的家伙真的很可爱。

Chandler Bing: Do you know which one you're gonna be?

你知道你会成为哪一个吗？

Joey Tribbiani: No, but I hear lyme disease is open, so... (crosses fingers)

没有，但我听说莱姆病是开放的，所以……（祈祷）

Chandler Bing: Good luck, man. I hope you get it.

祝你好运，伙计。我希望你能得到它。

Joey Tribbiani: Thanks.

谢谢。

(Ross comes back to the couch.)

（罗斯回到沙发上。）

Ross Geller: (to Monica) Well, you were right. How can they do this to us, huh? It's Thanksgiving.

（对莫妮卡）嗯，你是对的。他们怎么能这样对我们？今天是感恩节。

Monica Geller: Ok, I'll tell you what. How about I cook dinner at my place? I'll make it just like Mom's.

好吧，我告诉你吧。我在我家做饭怎么样？我会让它像妈妈的一样。

Ross Geller: Will you make the mashed potatoes with the lumps?

你会用土豆块做土豆泥吗？

Monica Geller: You know, they're not actually supposed to have... (Ross looks at her sheepishly) I'll work on the lumps. Joey, you're going home, right?

你知道，他们实际上不应该有……（罗斯羞怯地看着她）我来处理这些肿块。乔伊，你要回家了，对吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah.

是的。

Monica Geller: And I assume, Chandler, you are still boycotting all the pilgrim holidays.

我猜，钱德勒，你还在抵制所有的朝圣假期。

Chandler Bing: Yes, every single one of them.

是的，每一个都是。

Monica Geller: Phoebe, you're gonna be with your grandma?

菲比，你要和你奶奶在一起？

Phoebe Buffay: Yes, and her boyfriend. But we're celebrating Thanksgiving in December 'cause he is lunar.

是的，还有她的男朋友。但我们在十二月庆祝感恩节，因为他是农历的。

Monica Geller: So you're free Thursday, then.

那么你星期四有空。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah. Oh, can I come?

是的。哦，我能来吗？

Monica Geller: Yeah. Rach, are you thinking you're gonna make it to Vail?

是的。瑞秋，你想你能去维尔吗？

Rachel Green: Absolutely. Shoop, shoop, shoop. Only a hundred and two dollars to go.

当然。嘘，嘘，嘘。只剩下102美元了。

Chandler Bing: I thought it was $98.50.

我还以为是98.50美元呢。

Rachel Green: Yeah, well it was. I, I broke a cup.

是的，是的。一、 我打碎了一个杯子。

Ross Geller: Well, I'm off to Carol's.

我要去卡萝家了。

Phoebe Buffay: Ooh, ooh! Why don't we invite her?

哦，哦！我们为什么不邀请她呢？

Ross Geller: (mimicking) Ooh, ooh. Because she's my ex-wife, and will probably want to bring her, ooh, ooh, lesbian life partner.

（模仿）哦，哦。因为她是我的前妻，可能会想带她来，哦，哦，女同性恋生活伴侣。

🎬 Carol and Susan's apartment, Susan is there. Ross enters.

🎬 卡萝和苏珊的公寓，苏珊在那里。罗斯进来。

Ross Geller: Hi, is uh, is Carol here?

嗨，卡萝在吗？

Susan Bunch: No, she's at a faculty meeting.

不，她在参加一个教员会议。

Ross Geller: Oh, I uh, just came by to pick up my skull. Well, not mine, but...**Susan Bunch: **Come in.

哦，我，呃，只是来取我的头骨。嗯，不是我的，但是……苏珊：请进。

Ross Geller: Thanks. Yeah, Carol borrowed it for a class, and I have to get it back to the museum.

谢谢。是的，卡萝为了上课借了它，我得把它还给博物馆。

Susan Bunch: What's it look like?

它看起来像什么？

Ross Geller: Kinda like a big face without skin.

有点像一张没有皮肤的大脸。

Susan Bunch: Yes, I'm familiar with the concept. We can just look for it.

是的，我对这个概念很熟悉。我们可以去找它。

Ross Geller: Ok. (browsing the apartment) Wow, you guys sure have a lot of books about bein' a lesbian.

好的。（浏览公寓）哇，你们肯定有很多关于做女同性恋的书。

Susan Bunch: Well, you know, you have to take a course. Otherwise, they don't let you do it.

嗯，你知道，你得上一门课。否则，他们不会让你这么做。

Ross Geller: (picking up a book) Hey, hey, Yertle the Turtle. A classic.

（拿起一本书）嘿，嘿，乌龟耶特尔。经典。

Susan Bunch: Actually, I'm reading it to the baby.

事实上，我正在给宝宝读。

Ross Geller: The uh, the baby that hasn't been born yet? Wouldn't that mean you're... crazy?

还没出生的孩子？那不意味着你……疯子

Susan Bunch: What, you don't think they can hear sounds in there?

什么，你觉得他们听不到里面的声音吗？

Ross Geller: You're not serious, I mean, you really... you really talk to it?

你不是认真的，我是说，你真的……你真的跟它说话？

Susan Bunch: Yeah, all the time. I want the baby to know my voice.

是啊，一直都是。我想让孩子知道我的声音。

Ross Geller: Do you uh, do you talk about me?

你，呃，你在谈论我吗？

Susan Bunch: Yeah, yeah, all the time.

是的，是的，一直都是。

Ross Geller: Really?

真的吗？

Susan Bunch: But um, we just refer to you as Bobo the Sperm Guy.

但是，嗯，我们只是称你为博博精子男。

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, everyone is there but Rachel.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，除了瑞秋，其他人都在那里。

Ross Geller: Look, if she's talking to it, I just think that I should get some belly time too. Not that I believe any of this.

听着，如果她在跟它说话，我只是觉得我也应该有点肚子痛的时间。我一点也不相信。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, I believe it. I think the baby can totally hear everything. I can show you. Look, this will seem a little weird, but you put your head inside this turkey, and then we'll all talk, and you'll hear everything we say.

哦，我相信。我认为婴儿完全能听到一切。我可以给你看。听着，这看起来有点奇怪，但是你把你的头放在火鸡里，然后我们都会说话，你会听到我们说的一切。

Chandler Bing: I'd just like to say that I'm totally behind this experiment. In fact, I'd very much like to butter your head.

我只是想说我完全支持这个实验。事实上，我很想拍你的头。

(Rachel enters.)

（瑞秋进来了。）

Monica Geller: Hey, Rach, did you make your money?

嘿，瑞秋，你赚钱了吗？

Rachel Green: No, not even close. Forget Vail, forget seeing my family, forget shoop, shoop, shoop.

不，一点也不接近。忘了维尔，忘了见我的家人，忘了嘘，嘘，嘘。

Monica Geller: Rach, here's your mail.

瑞秋，这是你的邮件。

Rachel Green: Thanks, you can just put it on the table.

谢谢，你可以把它放在桌子上。

Monica Geller: (insistently) No, here's your mail.

（坚持）不，这是你的邮件。

Rachel Green: Thanks, you can just put it on the table.

谢谢，你可以把它放在桌子上。

Monica Geller: (gives her an envelope) Would you just open it?

（给她一个信封）你能打开它吗？

(Rachel opens it. Inside is the money she needed.)

（瑞秋打开，里面是她需要的钱。）

Rachel Green: Oh my god, oh, you guys are great.

我的天啊，你们太棒了。

Monica Geller: We all chipped in.

我们都参与了。

Joey Tribbiani: (to Monica) We did?

（对莫妮卡）是吗？

Monica Geller: (to Joey) You owe me 20 bucks.

（对乔伊）你欠我20美元。

Rachel Green: Thank you. Thank you so much!

谢谢你。非常感谢你！

Monica Geller: (hands Chandler a bag) Chandler, here you go, got your traditional Thanksgiving feast, you got your tomato soup, your grilled cheese fixin's, and your family size bag of Funyuns.

（递给钱德勒一个袋子）钱德勒，给你，你有你的传统感恩节大餐，你有你的番茄汤，你的烤奶酪套餐，还有你的家庭装的Funyuns。

Rachel Green: Wait, wait, Chandler, this is what you're havin' for Thanksgiving dinner? What, what, what is it with you and this holiday?

等等，等等，钱德勒，这就是你感恩节晚餐吃的东西？你和这个假期有什么关系？

Chandler Bing: All right, I'm nine years old.

好吧，我九岁了。

Ross Geller: Oh, I hate this story.

哦，我讨厌这个故事。

Chandler Bing: We just finished this magnificent Thanksgiving dinner. I have--and I remember this part vividly--a mouthful of pumpkin pie, and this is the moment my parents choose to tell me they're getting divorced.

我们刚吃完这顿丰盛的感恩节晚餐。我吃了一口南瓜馅饼，这一刻我的父母选择告诉我他们要离婚了。

Rachel Green: Oh my god.

我的天啊。

Chandler Bing: Yes. It's very difficult to appreciate a Thanksgiving dinner once you've seen it in reverse.

是的。一旦你看到感恩节晚餐的反面，你就很难欣赏它。

🎬 The subway, Joey spots a gorgeous woman waiting. He goes up to her.

🎬 在地铁里，乔伊发现一个漂亮的女人在等着。他走向她。

Joey Tribbiani: Uh, hi. We uh, we used to work together.

嗯，嗨。我们，呃，我们曾经一起工作过。

Girl: We did?

是吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, at Macy's. You were the Obsession girl, right? I was the Aramis guy. (pretends to spray cologne) Aramis? Aramis?

是的，在梅西百货。你是那个痴迷的女孩，对吧？我是阿拉米人。（假装喷古龙水）阿拉米斯？阿拉米斯？

Girl: Yeah, right.

是的，对。

Joey Tribbiani: I gotta tell you. You're the best in the business.

我得告诉你。你是这行最好的。

Girl: Get out.

出去。

Joey Tribbiani: I'm serious. You're amazing. You know when to spritz, when to lay back.

我是认真的。你太棒了。你知道什么时候该喷水，什么时候该休息。

Girl: Really? You don't know what that means to me.

真的吗？你不知道那对我意味着什么。

Joey Tribbiani: Ooh, you smell great tonight. What're you wearing?

哦，你今晚闻起来好香。你穿什么？

Girl: (provocatively) Nothing.

（挑衅地）没什么。

Joey Tribbiani: Listen, uh, you wanna go get a drink or something?

听着，呃，你想去喝点什么吗？

Girl: Yeah. (she gets up, notices something behind Joey) Oh.

是的。（她站起来，注意到乔伊身后有东西）哦。

Joey Tribbiani: What's wrong?

怎么了？

Girl: I just remembered, I have to do something.

我刚想起来，我得做点什么。

Joey Tribbiani: Oh. What?

哦。什么？

Girl: Um, leave.

嗯，走吧。

Joey Tribbiani: Wait, wait, wait!

等等，等等，等等！

(Joey turns around and sees his face on a poster in the subway. The poster says: What Mario isn't telling you...V.D., you never know who might have it. A variety of scenes are shown with the poster displayed all over New York City.)

（乔伊转过身，在地铁里的一张海报上看到了他的脸。海报上写着：马里奥没有告诉你的东西……V.D.，你永远不知道谁会得到它。海报在纽约市各处展示了各种各样的场景。）

🎬 Central Perk, Joey enters, amongst snickers from the gang.

🎬 中央公园，乔伊进来，在帮派的窃笑声中。

Joey Tribbiani: So I guess you all saw it.

所以我想你们都看到了。

Rachel Green: Saw what?

看到什么了？

Phoebe Buffay: No, we were just laughing. You know, how laughter can be infectious.

没有，我们只是在笑。你知道，笑是多么有感染力。

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Joey enters, upset.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，乔伊进来，心烦意乱。

Joey Tribbiani: Set another place for Thanksgiving. My entire family thinks I have VD.

换个地方过感恩节。我全家都认为我得了性病。

Chandler Bing: Tonight, on a very special Blossom.

今晚，在一朵非常特别的花上。

Commercial Break

商业休息

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，莫妮卡正在做感恩节晚餐。钱德勒站在门口，不想参加庆祝活动。

Monica Geller: Mmm, looking good. Ok, cider's mulling, turkey's turking, yams are yamming. (notices Ross is depressed) What?

嗯，看起来不错。好的，苹果酒的调味品，火鸡的火鸡，山药的调味品。（注意到罗斯情绪低落）什么？

Ross Geller: I don't know. It's just not the same without Mom in the kitchen.

我不知道。妈妈不在厨房就不一样了。

Monica Geller: All right, that's it. You know what? Just get out of my way and stop moping.

好了，就这样。你知道吗？别挡我的路，别闷闷不乐了。

Ross Geller: That's closer.

更近了。

(Rachel enters, excited.)

（瑞秋激动地走了进来。）

Rachel Green: I got the tickets! I got the tickets! Five hours from now, shoop, shoop, shoop.

我拿到票了！我拿到票了！五小时后，嘘，嘘，嘘。

Chandler Bing: Oh, you must stop shooping.

哦，你必须停止购物。

Rachel Green: Ok, I'm gonna get my stuff.

好的，我去拿我的东西。

Joey Tribbiani: Chandler, will you just come in already?

钱德勒，你能进来吗？

Chandler Bing: No, I prefer to keep a safe distance from all this merriment.

不，我宁愿和这些欢乐保持安全距离。

(Phoebe takes a slice of pumpkin pie and waves it in front of Chandler's face.)

（菲比拿了一片南瓜派，在钱德勒面前挥了挥手。）

Phoebe Buffay: Look out, incoming pumpkin pie!

小心，南瓜派来了！

Chandler Bing: Ok, we all laughed when you did it with the stuffing, but that's not funny anymore.

好吧，当你用填料做的时候，我们都笑了，但这不再有趣了。

(Chandler leaves.)

（钱德勒离开了。）

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, Monica, I got a question. I don't see any tater tots.

嘿，莫妮卡，我有个问题。我没看到任何土豆饼。

Monica Geller: That's not a question.

这不是问题。

Joey Tribbiani: But my mom always makes them. It's like a tradition. You get a little piece of turkey on your fork, a little cranberry sauce, and a tot! It's bad enough I can't be with my family because of my disease.

但我妈妈总是做。这就像是一个传统。你的叉子上有一小块火鸡，一点蔓越莓酱，还有一个小孩！因为我的病我不能和家人在一起已经够糟糕的了。

Monica Geller: All right, fine. Tonight's potatoes will be both mashed with lumps, and in the form of tots.

好的，很好。今晚的土豆既要捣碎，又要做成土豆泥。

Ross Geller: Ok, I'm off to talk to my unborn child.

好了，我要去和我未出生的孩子谈谈。

(Ross grabs for some food, Monica slaps his hand away.)

（罗斯抓起一些食物，莫妮卡拍掉了他的手。）

Monica Geller: Ah!

啊！

Ross Geller: Ok, Mom never hit.

好吧，妈妈从来没打过。

(Ross exits.)

（罗斯退出。）

Phoebe Buffay: (stirring pot) Ok, all done.

（搅拌锅）好的，都做好了。

Monica Geller: What, Phoebe, did you whip the potatoes? Ross needs lumps!

什么，菲比，你搅土豆了吗？罗斯需要肿块！

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, I'm sorry, oh, I just, I thought we could have them whipped and then add some peas and onions.

哦，对不起，哦，我只是，我想我们可以把它们搅打一下，然后加些豌豆和洋葱。

Monica Geller: Why would we do that?

我们为什么要这么做？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, 'cause then they'd be like my mom used to make them, you know, before she died.

嗯，因为那样的话，它们就会像我妈妈死前做的一样。

Monica Geller: Ok, three kinds of potatoes coming up.

好的，三种土豆就要来了。

Rachel Green: Ok, good-bye you guys. Thanks for everything. (she starts to leave, and hits everyone with her skis) Oh, sorry! Oh, sorry!
