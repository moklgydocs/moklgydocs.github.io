The One Where Rachel Quits

瑞秋辞职的那个

Written by: Michael Curtis and Gregory S. Malins

作者：迈克尔·柯蒂斯和格雷戈里·马林斯

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Central Perk, Chandler and Rachel are sitting on the couch.

🎬 中央公园，钱德勒和瑞秋坐在沙发上。

Chandler Bing: (reading the comics) Eh..., I don’t, I don’t know.

（看漫画）呃…，我不，我不知道。

Rachel Green: What?

什么？

Chandler Bing: Well, as old as he is in dog years, do you think Snoopy should still be allowed to fly this thing?

好吧，尽管史努比已经三岁了，你认为史努比还应该被允许驾驶这架飞机吗？

Gunther: Rachel?

瑞秋？

Rachel Green: Yeah.

是的。

Gunther: Do you remember when you first came here, how you spent two weeks getting trained by another waitress?

你还记得你第一次来这里的时候，你是如何花了两周时间接受另一位女服务员的培训的吗？

Rachel Green: Oh, sure! Do you need me to train somebody new?

哦，当然！你需要我训练新人吗？

Gunther: (laughs) Good one. Actually, ah, Terry wants you to take the training again, whenever.

（笑）不错。事实上，啊，特里想让你再参加一次训练，无论何时。

Rachel Green: (to Chandler) Eh, do you believe that?

（对钱德勒）呃，你相信吗？

Chandler Bing: (thinks about it) Yeah?

（想了想）是吗？

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 The hallway of Ross’s building, there is a Brown Bird girl selling cookies, as Ross and Chandler come up the stairs.

🎬 罗斯大楼的走廊上，有一个棕色小鸟女孩在卖饼干，罗斯和钱德勒走上楼梯。

Sarah: So that’s two boxes of the Holiday Macaroons. On behalf of the Brown Birds of America, I salute you. (Does the Brown Bird salute, she blows on a bird call, then holds her hand, palm facing out, next to her face, and then waves it like a bird flapping it’s wings.)

这是两盒假日杏仁饼。我代表美国褐鸟向你们致敬。（棕色的鸟会行礼吗？她吹起鸟叫声，然后手掌朝外，紧靠着脸，然后像鸟儿拍打翅膀一样挥舞着手。）

Ross Geller: Just admit it Chandler, you have no backhand.

承认吧，钱德勒，你没有反手。

Chandler Bing: Excuse me little one, I have a very solid backhand.

对不起，小家伙，我的反手很结实。

Ross Geller: Shielding your face and shrieking like a girl... is not a backhand.

遮住你的脸，像个女孩一样尖叫。。。不是反手。

Chandler Bing: I was shrieking... like a Marine.

我在尖叫。。。就像一个海军陆战队员。

(they both start up the stairs.)

（他们俩都上了楼梯。）

Ross Geller: All right here. Watch me execute the three ‘P’s of championship play. Power. (swings the racquet) Precision. (swings the racquet.) and penache. (does a backswing and hits Sarah who’s started up the stairs, knocking her down, they both watch in horror.)

好的。看着我执行冠军赛的三个P。权力（挥拍）精准。（挥动球拍）和佩纳什。（做了一次后摆，击中了爬上楼梯的莎拉，把她撞倒，他们都惊恐地看着。）

🎬 Central Perk, the gang’s all there discussing the incident.

🎬 中央公园，帮派都在那里讨论这件事。

Monica Geller: You broke a little girl’s leg?!!

你弄断了一个小女孩的腿？！！

Ross Geller: I know. I feel horrible. Okay.

我知道。我感觉糟透了。可以

Chandler Bing: (reading the paper) Says here that a muppet got whacked on Seasame Street last night. (to Ross) Where exactly were around ten-ish?

（读报纸）这里说一个布偶昨晚在同一条街上被打了。（对罗斯）十点左右的人到底在哪里？

Ross Geller: Well, I’m gonna go see her. I want to bring her something, what do you think she’ll like?

好吧，我要去看她。我想给她带点东西，你觉得她会喜欢什么？

Monica Geller: Maybe a Hello Kitty doll, the ability to walk...

也许是凯蒂猫娃娃，走路的能力。。。

(Rachel starts to laugh, and Ross notices her.)

（瑞秋笑了起来，罗斯注意到了她。）

Rachel Green: I’m gonna get back to retraining. (gets up)

我要回去再培训了。（站起来）

Ross Geller: All right, see you guys. (starts to leave)

好吧，再见。（开始离开）

Chandler Bing: Look out kids, he’s coming! (Ross continues to leave with his head down in shame.)

小心孩子们，他来了！（罗斯羞愧地低着头继续离开。）

Joey Tribbiani: And I gotta go sell some Christmas trees.

我还要去卖一些圣诞树。

Phoebe Buffay: Have fun. Oh wait, no, don’t! I forgot I am totally against that now.

祝你玩得开心。哦，等等，不，不要！我忘了我现在完全反对。

Joey Tribbiani: What? Me having a job?

什么？我有工作吗？

Phoebe Buffay: No, no, I am against innocent trees being cut down in their prime, and their, their corpses grotesquely dressed in like tinsel and twinkly lights. (to Joey) Hey, how do you sleep at night?

不，不，我反对无辜的树木在盛年被砍伐，他们的，他们的尸体穿着像金属丝和闪烁的灯光。（对乔伊）嘿，你晚上睡得怎么样？

Joey Tribbiani: Well, I’m pretty tired from lugging the trees around all day. Hey, Phoebe listen, you got this all wrong. Those trees were born to be Christmas trees, their fulfilling their life purpose, by, by making people happy.

嗯，我整天拖着树到处走，累极了。嘿，菲比，听着，你搞错了。这些树生来就是圣诞树，它们通过让人们快乐来实现自己的人生目标。

Phoebe Buffay: Really?

真的吗？

(Phoebe turns and looks at Monica, while Joey frantically motions to Chandler to help him out.)

（菲比转过身来看着莫妮卡，而乔伊疯狂地向钱德勒示意要帮他。）

Chandler Bing: Yes. Yes, and ah, ah, the trees are happy too, because for most of them, it’s the only chance to see New York.

是的。是的，而且啊，啊，这些树也很快乐，因为对他们大多数人来说，这是参观纽约的唯一机会。

[cut to Gunther retraining Rachel.]

[转到Gunther再培训课程Rachel。]

Gunther: ...and after you’ve delivered the drinks, you take the empty tray....

……送完饮料后，你就拿空托盘。。。。

Rachel Green: Gunther, Gunther, please, I’ve worked here for two and a half years, I know the empty trays go over there. (points to the counter.)

冈瑟，冈瑟，求你了，我在这里工作了两年半，我知道空盘子在那边。（指着柜台。）

Gunther: What if you put them here. (sets the empty tray on another stack of empty trays on the back counter.)

如果你把它们放在这里呢。（将空托盘设置在后计数器上的另一堆空托盘上。）

Rachel Green: Huh. Well, y'know that’s actually a really good idea, because that way they’ll be closer to the mugs. Y'know what, you should have the other waitresses do that too.

嗯。嗯，你知道这其实是个好主意，因为那样他们会离杯子更近。你知道吗，你应该让其他服务员也这么做。

Gunther: They already do. That’s why they call it the ‘tray spot.’

他们已经有了。这就是为什么他们称之为“托盘点”

Rachel Green: Gee, I always heard them talk about that, I just always thought that it was a club they went to. Oh God, I’m, I’m sorry. (walks away)

哎呀，我总是听到他们谈论这个，我只是一直认为他们去的是一个俱乐部。哦，天哪，我，我很抱歉。（走开）

Gunther: It’s all right. Sweetheart.

没关系。亲爱的。

🎬 Sarah’s bedroom, her room is decorated with a space motif.

🎬 莎拉的卧室，她的房间装饰着一个空间主题。

Ross Geller: So, this must be kinda neat for ya, huh? I mean, your Dad tells me that you get a couple of days off school, and you, you ah, don’t have to sell those cookies anymore.

所以，这对你来说一定很整洁吧？我的意思是，你爸爸告诉我你可以休息几天，你，你啊，不必再卖那些饼干了。

Sarah: Well, I kinda wanted to sell the cookies. The girl who sells the most wins a trip to Spacecamp, and gets to sit in a real space shuttle.

嗯，我有点想卖饼干。卖得最多的女孩赢得了太空营之旅，并坐上了真正的航天飞机。

Ross Geller: Wow, you ah, you really like all this space stuff, huh?

哇，你啊，你真的喜欢这些太空玩意儿，是吗？

Sarah: Yeah. My Dad says if I spend as much time helping him clean apartments, as I do daydreaming about outer space, he’d be able to afford a trip to the Taj Mahal.

是的。我爸爸说，如果我花同样多的时间帮他打扫公寓，就像我做关于外层空间的白日梦一样，他就能负担得起去泰姬陵的旅行。

Ross Geller: I think you would have to clean a whole lot of apartments to go all the way to India.

我想你得打扫一大堆公寓，才能一路去印度。

Sarah: No. The one in Atlantic City, Dad loves the slots. He says he’s gonna double the college money my Grandma left me.

不是。大西洋城的那个，爸爸喜欢吃角子老虎机。他说他要把我奶奶留给我的大学学费翻倍。

Ross Geller: Huh. Well, good luck to Dad. Say, how many more boxes would you have to sell in order to win?

嗯。祝爸爸好运。比如说，你还要卖多少盒才能赢？

Sarah: The girl who won last year sold four hundred and seventy-five.

去年获胜的那个女孩卖了475英镑。

Ross Geller: Yeah.

是的。

Sarah: So far, I’ve sold seventy-five.

到目前为止，我已经卖了75辆了。

Ross Geller: Four hundred, huh? Well, that sounds do-able. (starts to get out his wallet) How much are the boxes?

四百，嗯？好吧，听起来不错。（开始拿出钱包）箱子多少钱？

Sarah: Five dollars a box.

五美元一盒。

Ross Geller: (puts away his wallet) And what is second prize?

（收起钱包）二等奖是什么？

Sarah: A ten speed bike. But, I’d rather have something my Dad couldn’t sell.

一辆十速自行车。但是，我宁愿要我爸爸卖不出去的东西。

Ross Geller: Well, that makes sense.

嗯，这是有道理的。

Sarah: Could you do me one favor, if it’s not too much trouble?

如果不太麻烦的话，你能帮我一个忙吗？

Ross Geller: Yeah, Sarah, anything.

是的，莎拉，什么都行。

Sarah: Could you pull open the curtains for me? The astronauts from the space shuttle are gonna be on the news, and since we don’t have a TV, the lady across the alley said she’d push hers up to a window, so I could watch it.

你能帮我拉开窗帘吗？航天飞机上的宇航员将出现在新闻中，因为我们没有电视，小巷对面的那位女士说她会把她的推到一扇窗户上，这样我就可以看了。

🎬 A hallway, Ross is selling Brown Bird cookies for Sarah, he stops and knocks on a door.

🎬 走廊上，罗斯正在为莎拉卖棕色小鸟饼干，他停下来敲门。

Woman: (looking through her peephole, we see Ross standing in the hallway.) Yesss?

（透过她的窥视孔，我们看到罗斯站在走廊里。）是吗？

Ross Geller: Hi, I’m selling Brown Bird cookies.

嗨，我在卖棕鸟饼干。

Woman: You’re no Brown Bird, I can see you through my peephole.

你不是棕色的鸟，我可以透过我的窥视孔看到你。

Ross Geller: No, hi, I’m, I’m an honorary Brown Bird (does the Brown Bird salute.)

不，嗨，我是，我是一只荣誉棕色鸟（棕色鸟敬礼。）

Woman: What does that mean?

那是什么意思？

Ross Geller: Ah, well, it means that I can sell cookies, but I’m not invited to sleep-overs.

啊，嗯，这意味着我可以卖饼干，但我不被邀请睡过头。

Woman: I can dial 9-1-1 at the touch of a button, y'know. Now, go away!

你知道，我一按按钮就可以拨911。现在，走开！

Ross Geller: No, please, please, um, it’s for a poor little girl who wants to go to Spacecamp more than anything in the world.

不，求你了，求你了，嗯，这是给一个可怜的小女孩的，她最想去太空营。

Woman: I’m pressing, a policeman is on his way.

我在催促，一名警察正在赶来。

Ross Geller: Okay, okay! I’m going. I’m going. (goes across the hall to knock on another door.)

好的，好的！我要走了。我要走了。（穿过大厅去敲另一扇门。）

Woman: I can still see you!

我还能看见你！

Ross Geller: All right!!

好吧！！

🎬 Joey’s work, selling Christmas trees.

🎬 乔伊的工作，卖圣诞树。

Phoebe Buffay: (walking up to Joey) Hey.

（走向乔伊）嘿。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey. What, what are you doing here?

嘿。什么，你在这里干什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, I-I thought a lot about what you said, and um, I realilized duh, all right maybe I was a little judgmental. Yeah, (looks at the tree) oh, but oh...

嗯，我对你说的话想了很多，嗯，我意识到，嗯，好吧，也许我有点主观判断。是的，（看着树）哦，但是哦。。。

Joey Tribbiani: Look now, Phoebe remember, hey, their just fulfilling their Christmas....

听着，菲比，记住，嘿，他们刚刚过完圣诞节。。。。

Phoebe Buffay: Destiny.

命运。

Joey Tribbiani: Sure.

当然。

Phoebe Buffay: Yes.

是的。

Joey Tribbiani: All right.

好的。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay. (One of Joey’s co-workers, walks by with a dead tree.) Yikes! That one doesn’t look very fulfilled.

好的。（乔伊的一个同事拿着一棵枯树走过。）哎呀！那个看起来不太满足。

Joey Tribbiani: Oh, that’s, that’s ah, one of the old ones, he’s just taking it to the back.

哦，那是，那是啊，一个旧的，他只是把它放在后面。

Phoebe Buffay: You keep the old ones in the back, that is so ageist.

你把旧的放在后面，这太老套了。

Joey Tribbiani: Well we have to make room for the fresh ones.

嗯，我们得给新的腾出地方。

Phoebe Buffay: So, what happens to the old guys?

那么，那些老家伙怎么了？

Joey Tribbiani: Well, they go into the chipper.

嗯，他们进了削片机。

Phoebe Buffay: Why, do I have a feeling that’s not as happy as it sounds? (Joey points out one going into the chipper to her, as this haunty, demonic music starts to play in the background) No! Nooooo!!! (she winces in horror and hides her face against Joey’s shoulder, as she sees the tree spit out from the chipper.)

为什么，我有一种感觉不像听起来那么快乐吗？（乔伊指给她看了一个正在跳进削片机的人，背景中开始播放这种闹鬼的恶魔音乐）不！不！！！（当她看到树从削片机里吐出来时，她惊恐地缩了缩，把脸靠在乔伊的肩膀上。）

Joey Tribbiani: (to the guy operating the chipper) Hey! Hey!! (makes the ‘cut it’ motion with his hands)

（对操作削片机的人）嘿！嘿（用手做“剪切”动作）

🎬 Central Perk, all except Phoebe are there, Ross is telling the gang, minus Rachel who’s still being retrained, about the different cookie options.

🎬 中央公园，除了菲比，所有人都在那里，罗斯正在告诉那帮人，除了瑞秋，她还在接受再培训，关于不同的饼干选择。

Ross Geller: ...and these come in the shapes of your favourite Christmas characters, Santa, Rudolph, and Baby Jesus.

……这些都是你最喜欢的圣诞人物的形状，圣诞老人、鲁道夫和小耶稣。

Joey Tribbiani: All right, I’ll take a box of the cream filled Jesus’s.

好吧，我要一盒耶稣的奶油。

Ross Geller: Wait a minute, one box! Come on, I’m trying to send a little girl to Spacecamp, I’m putting you down for five boxes. Chandler, what about you?

等一下，一个盒子！来吧，我要送一个小女孩去太空营，我给你五个盒子。钱德勒，你呢？

Chandler Bing: Ahh, do you have any coconut flavoured deities?

啊，你们有椰子味的神灵吗？

Ross Geller: No, but ah, there’s coconut in the Hanukkah Menoreoes. I tell you what, I’ll put you down for eight boxes, one for each night.

没有，但是啊，光明节菜单上有椰子。我告诉你，我给你记下八个盒子，每晚一个。

(Chandler mouths ‘Okay.’)

（钱德勒说“好的”。）

Ross Geller: Mon?

星期一？

Monica Geller: All right, I’ll take one box of the mint treasures, just one, and that’s it. I-I started gaining weight after I joined the Brown Birds. (to Ross) Remember, how Dad bought all my boxes and I ate them all?

好吧，我要一盒薄荷珍品，就一盒，就这样。我加入棕鸟队后体重开始增加。（对罗斯）还记得吗，爸爸是怎么买了我所有的盒子，我却把它们都吃了？

Ross Geller: Ah, no Mon, Dad had to buy everyone of your boxes because you ate them all. But ah, y'know I’m sure that’s not gonna happen this time, why don’t I put you down for three of the mint treasures and just a couple of the Rudolph’s.

啊，不，妈妈，爸爸不得不给你买所有的盒子，因为你把它们都吃了。但啊，你们知道我肯定这次不会了，为什么我不让你们买三个薄荷珍品和几个鲁道夫的呢。

Monica Geller: No.

没有。

Ross Geller: Oh, come on, now you know you want ‘em.

哦，得了吧，现在你知道你想要他们了。

Monica Geller: Don’t, don’t, don’t, don’t, don’t do this.

别，别，别，别，别这样。

Ross Geller: I’ll tell you what Mon, I’ll give you the first box for free.

我告诉你吧，妈妈，我会免费给你第一盒的。

Monica Geller: (she reaches out for it and stops) Oh God! I gotta go! (runs out)

（她伸手去拿，停了下来）哦，天哪！我得走了！（用完了）

Ross Geller: Come on! All the cool kids are eating ‘em! (chases after her.)

来吧！所有的酷孩子都在吃他们！（追赶着她。）

[cut to Gunther retraining Rachel.]

[转到Gunther再培训课程Rachel。]

Gunther: And when you have a second later, I wanna show you why we don’t just trap spiders under coffee mugs and leave them there.

等你有空的时候，我想告诉你为什么我们不把蜘蛛困在咖啡杯下面，然后放在那里。

Rachel Green: (sitting down next to Chandler) I’m training to be better at a job that I hate, my life officially sucks.

（坐在钱德勒旁边）我正在训练如何更好地做一份我讨厌的工作，我的生活真是糟透了。

Joey Tribbiani: Look Rach, wasn’t this supposed to a temporary thing? I thought you wanted to do fashion stuff?

听着，瑞秋，这不是临时的吗？我以为你想做时尚的事？

Rachel Green: Well, yeah! I’m still pursuing that.

嗯，是的！我仍在努力。

Chandler Bing: How... exactly are you pursuing that? Y'know other than sending out resumes like what, two years ago?

怎么。。。你到底在追求什么？你知道两年前除了发简历什么的吗？

Rachel Green: Well, I’m also sending out.... good thoughts.

嗯，我也在发。。。。好主意。

Joey Tribbiani: If you ask me, as long as you got this job, you’ve got nothing pushing you to get another one. You need the fear.

如果你问我，只要你得到这份工作，你就没有任何理由再去争取另一份工作。你需要恐惧。

Rachel Green: The fear?

恐惧？

Chandler Bing: He’s right, if you quit this job, you then have motivation to go after a job you really want.

他是对的，如果你辞去这份工作，你就会有动力去追求一份你真正想要的工作。

Rachel Green: Well then how come you’re still at a job that you hate, I mean why don’t you quit and get ‘the fear’?

那你为什么还在做一份你讨厌的工作呢？我是说，你为什么不辞职，得到“恐惧”？

(Chandler and Joey both laugh)

（钱德勒和乔伊都笑了）

Chandler Bing: Because, I’m too afraid.
