S03E14 The One with Phoebe's Ex-Partner

The One with Phoebe's Ex-Partner

和菲比的前搭档

The One With Phoebe’s Ex-Partner

和菲比的前搭档在一起的那个

Written by: Wil Calhoun

作者：威尔·卡尔霍恩

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Central Perk, the gang, except Rachel, is watching a new singer.

🎬 中央公园，除了瑞秋，这帮人正在看一位新歌手。

Singer: (singing) ‘Cause every time I see your face, I can’t help but fall from grace. I know.....

（唱）“因为每次我看到你的脸，我都会情不自禁地失宠。我知道。。。。。

Joey Tribbiani: Wow! This girl is good.

哇！这个女孩很好。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh-ho yeah! A song with rhyming words. Oo, I never thought of that before.

哦，好的！歌词押韵的歌曲。哦，我以前从没想过。

Chandler Bing: I like her.

我喜欢她。

Phoebe Buffay: (to Chandler) Why? Because she can sing and play guitar and do both at the same time?

（对钱德勒）为什么？因为她会唱歌和弹吉他，而且两者都能同时做？

Chandler Bing: Well, that’s pretty much all I’m looking for from these people.

嗯，这就是我想从这些人那里得到的。

Monica Geller: (to Phoebe) Look at you. All jealous.

（对菲比）看看你。都嫉妒了。

Ross Geller: Yeah Pheebs, come on, you two have completely different styles. Y'know, she’s more..(shakes his shoulders, like he’s dancing) y'know, and you’re more (sees the look from Phoebe and stops)

是啊，菲比，拜托，你们俩的风格完全不同。你知道，她更…（摇着他的肩膀，像他在跳舞）你知道，你更（看到菲比的表情，停下来）

Singer: (singing) beside meeeee-eeee-ee. (everyone applauds her).

（唱）在meeeee旁边。（大家都为她鼓掌）。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, see, see, everyone else is happy she’s done.

好吧，看，看，其他人都很高兴她完成了。

Singer: Okay, my next song’s called: Phoebe Buffay, What Can I Say. I Really Loved When We Were Singing Partners, And I Shouldn’t Have Left You That Way.

好的，我的下一首歌叫：菲比·布菲，我能说什么。我真的很喜欢我们一起唱歌的时候，我不该就这样离开你。

(The gang all looks at Phoebe.)

（这伙人都看着菲比。）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh no, one of those ‘look for the hidden meaning’ songs.

哦，不，是那种“寻找隐藏的意义”的歌。

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Central Perk, later.

🎬 中央公园，稍后。

Singer: Hey Phoebe.

嘿，菲比。

Phoebe Buffay: Hey Leslie, how’d you know I’d be here?

嘿，莱斯利，你怎么知道我会在这里？

Leslie: I ran into Vlad at the place where they sell the big fish, and he said you played here a lot, so umm....

我在卖大鱼的地方遇到了弗拉德，他说你经常在这里玩，所以。。。。

Chandler Bing: (to Ross) All right listen, I have to go to the bathroom, but if the place with the big fish comes up again. I’d like know whether that’s several big fish or just one big fish.

（对罗斯）好吧，听着，我得去洗手间，但是如果有大鱼的地方再次出现的话。我想知道那是几条大鱼还是一条大鱼。

Joey Tribbiani: (to Leslie) So ah, Phoebe tells us you write jingles.

（对莱斯利）啊，菲比告诉我们你写歌曲。

Phoebe Buffay: Actually I said she abandoned me to write jingles.

事实上，我说她放弃我写歌曲了。

Joey Tribbiani: (to Leslie) Ah, anything we might of heard of?

（对莱斯利）啊，有什么我们可能听说的吗？

Leslie: Ah, yeah, umm. (singing) Home is never far away..

啊，是的，嗯。（唱）家永远不会远离。。

Monica, Ross, and **Joey Tribbiani: **(joining in) Home is Home Star stew.

莫妮卡、罗斯和乔伊·崔比安尼：（加入）家就是家里的明星炖肉。

Leslie: Yeah, but, I don’t do that anymore. I got kinda sick of it, and then I couldn’t come up with anything good, so they fired me.

是的，但是，我不再那样做了。我有点厌倦了，然后我想不出什么好主意，所以他们解雇了我。

Phoebe Buffay: Hmm, bummer.

嗯，糟糕透了。

Leslie: Well, I y'know, I was just, umm, I was just thinking and hoping, that umm, maybe you’d want to get back together?

嗯，我知道，我只是，嗯，我只是在想，希望，嗯，也许你们想重归于好？

Phoebe Buffay: No. But thanks.

不，但是谢谢。

Leslie: Aw come on Phoebe would you just think about it?

噢，拜托，菲比，你能想想吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Okay. No. But thanks.

好的。不，但是谢谢。

Leslie: Okay, ah, see ya Pheebs. (leaves)

好吧，啊，再见，菲比。（离开）

Joey Tribbiani: (to Phoebe) Wow, that was kinda brutal.

（对菲比）哇，那有点残忍。

Phoebe Buffay: Well okay, let this be a lesson to all of you, all right. Once you, once you betray me, I become like the ice woman, y’know. Very cold, hard, unyielding, y'know nothing, nothing can penetrate this icy exterior. (to Monica) Can I have a tissue, please?

好吧，让这成为你们所有人的一个教训，好吧。一旦你，一旦你背叛了我，我就会变得像个冰女人，你知道。非常寒冷，坚硬，不屈，你什么都不知道，没有什么能穿透这冰冷的外表。（对莫妮卡）能给我一张纸巾吗？

Monica Geller: Yeah, sure.

是的，当然。

🎬 Outside the bathroom, Chandler is pacing back and fourth, waiting is use it.

🎬 在浴室外，钱德勒正在踱步，第四，等待是使用它。

Gunther: (to Chandler) Someone in there?

（对钱德勒）里面有人吗？

Chandler Bing: No. This is just part of a dare devil game that I play called ‘wait until the last moment before I burst and die.’

不。这只是我玩的一个胆大包天游戏的一部分，叫做“等到最后一刻，我才会崩溃而死。”

(The door opens.)

（门开了。）

Chandler Bing: Jeez, man did you fall..(sees it’s a beautiful woman coming out of the men’s room) Hi! So ah, did ya, did-did-did ya fall high?

天哪，你摔倒了吗？（看到一个漂亮的女人从男厕所出来）嗨！那么啊,你,你跌高了吗?？

Woman: Someone was in the lady's room, I couldn’t wait. I left the lid up for ya though.

有人在女士的房间里，我等不及了。不过我把盖子留给你了。

(Gunther walks up)

（冈瑟走了过来）

Chandler Bing: (to Gunther) Y'know what Gunther, go ah, go ahead, I’m-I’m talking to ah, (tries to get her to say her name). (to her) This is the part where you say your name.

（对冈瑟）你知道冈瑟，去吧，去吧，我在和她说话，（试着让她说出她的名字）。（对她）这是你说出你名字的部分。

Woman: Ginger.

金吉尔。

Chandler Bing: Ginger. I’m talking to Ginger, so....

金吉尔。我在和金吉尔说话，所以。。。。

Ginger: Don’t you have to use the bathroom?

你不用上厕所吗？

Chandler Bing: Nope, nope, I’d just ah, I’d rather talk to you. (pause) Yes, I do. Yes, I do have to go to the bathroom. (knocks on the door)

不，不，我只是想啊，我想和你谈谈。（停顿）是的，我知道。是的，我必须去洗手间。（敲门声）

Gunther: Someone in here.

这里有人。

🎬 Central Perk, the gang’s putting their coats on to leave.

🎬 中央公园，这帮人穿上外套准备离开。

Phoebe Buffay: Where’s Chandler?

钱德勒在哪里？

Joey Tribbiani: Ah, he can’t make it, he said he had to his... (sees Ginger) Whoa-oh! (hides behind the coat rack.)

啊，他来不了了，他说他得。。。（看到金吉尔）哇哦！（藏在衣架后面。）

Ginger: Joey? Joey Tribbiani?

乔伊？乔伊·特里比亚尼？

(She walks over behind the coat rack, but Joey picks it up and moves it so that he’s still behind it, and she can’t see him.)

（她走到衣架后面，但乔伊捡起衣架并移动它，使他仍在衣架后面，她看不见他。）

Ginger: Joey I can see you okay? You’re hiding behind the coats.

乔伊，我能见你，好吗？你躲在大衣后面。

(Joey puts his finger over his mouth to tell Ross to keep quiet. Ginger looks at Monica who looks away and leave.)

（乔伊用手指捂住嘴，告诉罗斯保持安静。金吉尔看着莫妮卡，莫妮卡转眼就走了。）

Joey Tribbiani: Phew, close one.

唷，关上一个。

🎬 Rachel’s office, Mark is packing his stuff into a box.

🎬 瑞秋的办公室，马克正在把他的东西装进一个盒子。

Ross Geller: Hi.

嗨。

Rachel Green: Hi, sweetie!

嗨，亲爱的！

Ross Geller: Hello.

你好。

Mark: Hi, Ross.

嗨，罗斯。

Ross Geller: Yeah, huh.

是的，嗯。

Rachel Green: I’ve got some bad news.

我有个坏消息。

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Rachel Green: I can get a quick bite to eat, but then I have to come back up here.

我可以很快地吃一口，但我必须回到这里来。

Ross Geller: Come on sweetie! You’ve had to work late every night for the past two weeks, what is it this time?

来吧，亲爱的！在过去的两周里，你每天晚上都得工作到很晚，这次是什么时候？

Mark: Actually, it’s kinda my fault. I-I quit today.

事实上，这是我的错。我今天辞职了。

Ross Geller: (to Rachel) But work comes first! (to Mark) Oh hey, but that’s sad about you though, what happened? Burn out? Burn all out, did ya?

（对瑞秋）但工作是第一位的！（对马克）哦，嘿，但是你真的很伤心，发生了什么事？烧坏了？烧尽了，是吗？

Rachel Green: Nooo, he’s leaving for a better job.

不，他要去找更好的工作了。

Ross Geller: Oh well that’s great, so I guess this is ah, this is good bye then. Huh? (picks a pad up off Rachel’s desk and tosses it into his box) Good bye.

哦，那太好了，所以我想这是啊，那就再见了。嗯？（从瑞秋的桌子上捡起一本便笺簿扔进他的盒子）再见。

Mark: Okay, then.

那好吧。

Ross Geller: Okay.

好的。

Rachel Green: Well we’re gonna miss you around here.

我们会想念你的。

Mark: Yeah, me too.

是的，我也是。

(Rachel goes to hug him but Ross is holding one of her hands and doesn’t let go, so she can only put one arm around him.)

（瑞秋去拥抱他，但罗斯握着她的一只手不放手，所以她只能用一只胳膊搂住他。）

Mark: So, see ya on Saturday.

那么，星期六见。

Rachel Green: Yeah, you bet.

当然可以。

(Ross is shocked, but Rachel drags him out of the office.)

（罗斯很震惊，但瑞秋把他拖出了办公室。）

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Phoebe and Monica are playing with a Ouija board, Phoebe’s beeper goes off and Monica screams.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，菲比和莫妮卡在玩占卜板，菲比的传呼机响了，莫妮卡尖叫起来。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh. (takes her beeper puts in a pot, covers it, and puts the pot in the oven)

哦。（拿起她的呼机，把它放进锅里，盖上盖子，然后把锅放进烤箱）

Monica Geller: Y'know those are a delicacy in India.

你知道这些在印度是美味佳肴。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, that was Leslie calling again to see if we can get back together. That’s the twentieth time today! And good luck Leslie!

是的，莱斯利又打电话来问我们是否能重聚。今天是第二十次了！祝你好运，莱斯利！

Monica Geller: Wow, she must have hurt you pretty bad, huh?

哇，她一定伤得很厉害吧？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, yeah. Y'know, we were best friends, ever since we were little, our Moms worked on the barge together.

嗯，是的。你知道，我们是最好的朋友，从我们很小的时候起，我们的妈妈就一起在驳船上工作。

Monica Geller: Oh, you two must have been so cute running around on a barge.

哦，你们俩在驳船上跑来跑去一定很可爱。

Phoebe Buffay: You never run on a barge!

你从来没有在驳船上跑过！

Joey Tribbiani: (entering) Hey.

（进来）嘿。

Phoebe and **Monica Geller: **Hey.

菲比和莫妮卡·盖勒：嘿。

Joey Tribbiani: Is ah, is Chandler around?

钱德勒在吗？

Monica Geller: No, umm, he met some girl at the coffee house.

不，嗯，他在咖啡馆遇到了一个女孩。

Joey Tribbiani: Oooh.

哦。

Monica Geller: Yeah, Ginger something.

是的，金吉尔什么的。

Joey Tribbiani: Nooo. No, no, ah, are you sure it wasn’t something that sounded like Ginger, like ah, Gingeer?

不。不，不，啊，你确定那不是听起来像姜的东西，像啊，姜吗？

Monica Geller: No, it was Ginger. I remember, because when he told me, I said, (singing) ‘the movie star.’

不，是金吉尔。我记得，因为当他告诉我的时候，我说，（唱）“那个电影明星。”

Joey Tribbiani: Aww, man. That’s the girl I was hiding from. When she finds out he’s my roommate, she’s gonna tell him what I did.

哦，伙计。那就是我躲着的那个女孩。当她发现他是我的室友时，她会告诉他我做了什么。

Monica Geller: Well, what did you do?

嗯，你做了什么？

Joey Tribbiani: What, oh, oh, oh, no, no, I can’t, I can’t tell you that, it’s like the most awful, horrible thing I’ve ever done my whole life.

什么，哦，哦，哦，哦，不，不，我不能，我不能告诉你，这是我一生中做过的最可怕的事情。

Monica Geller: Y'know what, don’t tell us. We’ll just wait until Chandler gets home, (to Phoebe) because it’ll be more fun that way.

你知道吗，别告诉我们。我们会一直等到钱德勒回家，（对菲比）因为那样会更有趣。

Joey Tribbiani: All right!! Okay, it was like four years ago. Okay, Ginger and I had gone out a few times, and then this one weekend, we went up to her Dad’s cabin. Just me, her, and her annoying little dog Pepper. Well that night, I cooked this really romantic dinner....

好吧！！好吧，就像四年前一样。好吧，金吉尔和我出去过几次，然后这个周末，我们去了她爸爸的小屋。只有我，她和她那讨厌的小狗佩珀。那天晚上，我做了一顿非常浪漫的晚餐。。。。

Monica Geller: You gave her food poisoning!?

你让她食物中毒了！？

Joey Tribbiani: I wish. No. After dinner, me, her, and Pepper all fell asleep in front of the fire. Well I woke up in the middle of the night and I saw that the fire was dying out. So, I picked up a log and threw it on. Or, at least what I thought was a log.

我希望如此。没有。晚饭后，我、她和佩珀都在炉火前睡着了。我在半夜醒来，看到火熄灭了。于是，我捡起一根圆木，把它扔了上去。或者，至少我认为是一个日志。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh my God!! You threw Pepper on the fire!

哦，我的天哪！！你把胡椒扔到火上了！

Joey Tribbiani: I wish. See, I guess another thing I probably should’ve told you about Ginger is that she kinda has a ah, artificial leg.

我希望如此。看，我想我应该告诉你关于金吉尔的另一件事是，她有一只啊，假腿。

(Phoebe and Monica both stand up and gasp.)

（菲比和莫妮卡都站起来喘气。）

Monica Geller: Oh my God! Joey, what did you do after you threw her leg on the fire?

哦，我的天啊！乔伊，你把她的腿扔到火上后做了什么？

Joey Tribbiani: I ran!!

我跑了！！

🎬 A street, Chandler is kissing Ginger.

🎬 一条街上，钱德勒正在亲吻金吉尔。

Chandler Bing: Well, that’s the best kiss I’ve had with anyone I’ve ever met in a men’s room.

嗯，这是我和我在男厕所里遇到的任何人所得到的最好的吻。

Ginger: Actually, me too.

事实上，我也是。

Chandler Bing: (sees her foot is in a slush puddle) Op, foot in a puddle, foot all in a puddle.

（看到她的脚在泥坑里）哎呀，脚在泥坑里，脚都在泥坑里。

Ginger: Oh damn, I hate that.

噢，该死，我讨厌那个。

Chandler Bing: Yeah, we’re gonna have to get you out of those shoes.

是的，我们得帮你脱下那双鞋。

Ginger: Oh, don’t worry about it.

哦，别担心。

Chandler Bing: No, really you’re gonna freeze.

不，真的，你会冻僵的。

Ginger: No, I’m not.

不，我不是。

Chandler Bing: You’re not, what do you, what do you got a bionic foot?

你不是，你有什么，你有什么仿生脚？

Ginger: Some day, maybe.

也许有一天吧。

🎬 Ross’s bedroom, Rachel getting into bed while Ross is reading and laughs.

🎬 罗斯的卧室，瑞秋上床睡觉，罗斯在看书，她笑了起来。

Rachel Green: Funny book?

有趣的书？

Ross Geller: Hmmm. Oh, no, no, I just thinking about something funny I heard today. Umm, Mark, Mark saying ‘I’ll see you Saturday.’

嗯。哦，不，不，我只是在想我今天听到的一些有趣的事情。马克，马克说“星期六见。”

Rachel Green: Yeah, at the lecture, I told you that last week, you said you didn’t mind.

是的，在讲座上，我上周告诉过你，你说你不介意。

Ross Geller: Oh, no, no, no, it’s-it’s not the lecture ah, I mind, umm....

哦，不，不，不，这不是讲座啊，我介意，嗯。。。。

Rachel Green: Oh, please tell me it’s not because I’m going with Mark.

哦，请告诉我这不是因为我要和马克一起去。

Ross Geller: Oh, well...

哦，嗯。。。

Rachel Green: Oh my God!!! Ross!!

我的天啊！！！罗斯！！

Ross Geller: Well, I’m sorry, but ah, look if you’re not working with him anymore, why do you have to still do stuff with him?

嗯，我很抱歉，但是啊，如果你不再和他一起工作了，你为什么还要和他一起做事？

Rachel Green: Because, he’s my friend.

因为，他是我的朋友。

Ross Geller: Okay, but do you really need another friend? I mean...

好吧，但是你真的需要另一个朋友吗？我是说。。。

Rachel Green: Okay, well if I stop playing with Joey and Chandler, can I play with Mark?

好吧，如果我不再和乔伊和钱德勒玩，我可以和马克玩吗？

Ross Geller: Is that funny? Am I supposed to be laughing?

有趣吗？我应该笑吗？

Rachel Green: I don’t know, you thought ‘See you Saturday’ was funny. Look honey, Mark is in fashion okay, I like having a friend that I can share this stuff with. You guys would never want to go to a lecture with me.

我不知道，你觉得“星期六见”很有趣。听着，亲爱的，马克很时尚好吧，我喜欢有个朋友可以和我分享这些东西。你们绝对不想和我一起去听讲座。

Ross Geller: Pa-haa!! I would love to go with you.

哈哈！！我很想和你一起去。

Rachel Green: Really!?

真的吗！？

Ross Geller: Yeah, hey I-I have clothes, I even pick them out. I mean for, for all you know I could be a fashion..... monger.

是啊，嘿，我有衣服，我甚至把它们挑出来。我是说，就你所知，我可以成为一种时尚。。。。。贩子。

Rachel Green: Okay. Honey, I would love for you to go with me. (Ross has a worried look on his face) What?

好的。亲爱的，我希望你和我一起去。（罗斯脸上带着担忧的表情）什么？

Ross Geller: What should I wear, now I’m all nervous.

我该穿什么，现在我都紧张了。

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Phoebe is looking at her beeper still in the pot. She takes it out, shakes it, and puts it back in.
