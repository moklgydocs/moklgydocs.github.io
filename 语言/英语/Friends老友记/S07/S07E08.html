S07E08 The One Where Chandler Doesn't Like Dogs

The One Where Chandler Doesn't Like Dogs

钱德勒不喜欢狗的那一集

The One Where Chandler Doesn’t Like Dogs

钱德勒不喜欢狗的那个

Written by: Patty Lin

作者：林佩蒂

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

With Help from: Didi Chow

滴滴周

🎬 Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's, Monica is in the kitchen, Joey, Rachel, and Phoebe are sitting around the table writing one notepads while Chandler is looking over their shoulders.

🎬 莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比的，莫妮卡在厨房，乔伊、瑞秋和菲比围坐在桌子旁写一个记事本，而钱德勒从他们的肩膀上看过去。

Ross Geller: (entering) Hey everybody! Happy Thanksgiving!

（进来）大家好！感恩节快乐！

Chandler Bing: No, no, no. No-no-no.

不，不，不，不，不，不。

Joey, Rachel, and **Phoebe Buffay: **Shhhh!

乔伊、瑞秋和菲比·布菲：嘘！

Ross Geller: What, are we keeping Thanksgiving a secret this year?

什么，今年的感恩节我们要保密吗？

Chandler Bing: No, we're playing this game I learned at work. You have to name all the states in six minutes.

不，我们在玩我在工作中学到的游戏。你必须在六分钟内说出所有州的名字。

Ross Geller: What? That's like insanely easy!

什么？那简直太容易了！

Chandler Bing: Now, that's a lot harder than it sounds. You always forget at least one, or in some cases... fourteen (looks over to Monica).

现在，这比听起来难多了。你总是忘记至少一个，或者在某些情况下。。。十四岁（看着莫妮卡）。

Monica Geller: It's a stupid game and I wasn't playing against other people, so technically I didn't lose.

这是一场愚蠢的比赛，我没有和别人比赛，所以技术上我没有输。

Ross Geller: What? You forgot fourteen states?

什么？你忘了十四个州？

Monica Geller: Nobody cares about the Dakotas. (That’s true in so many ways, trust me, I’ve lived in one and been to the other.)

没人关心达科塔人。（这在很多方面都是正确的，相信我，我住在其中一个地方，也去过另一个地方。）

[Chandler's watch beeps]

[钱德勒的手表发出嘟嘟声]

Chandler Bing: Oh, okay, time's up!

哦，好吧，时间到了！

Rachel Green: All right, I got 48.

好吧，我得了48分。

Chandler Bing: Oh that's not bad, Pheebs?

哦，还不错，菲比？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, I got tired of naming states. So I decided to list the types of celery, and I have one: regular celery.

哦，我厌倦了给州命名。所以我决定列出芹菜的种类，我有一种：普通芹菜。

Chandler Bing: Okay, so Rachel's got 48 and Phoebe has the lead in…vegetables, Joey?

好吧，那么瑞秋得了48分，菲比在…蔬菜方面领先，乔伊？

Joey Tribbiani: Say hello to the new champ of Chandler's dumb states game.

向钱德勒的哑巴国家游戏的新冠军问好。

Ross Geller: Wow, how many have you got?

哇，你有多少？

Joey Tribbiani: Fifty-six!

五十六！

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's. Ross is sitting in the couch doing Chandler's game. Chandler is sitting in his barca-lounger. The girls are in the kitchen.

🎬 莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比的。罗斯坐在沙发上玩钱德勒的游戏。钱德勒坐在他的巴萨躺椅上。女孩们在厨房。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey! How is New England not a state? Huh? They have a sports-team!

嘿！新英格兰怎么不是一个州？嗯？他们有一个运动队！

Chandler Bing: Does South Oregon have a sports-team? (Joey strikes one from his list) There you go.

南俄勒冈州有运动队吗？（乔伊从他的名单中打了一个）好了。

[Cut to the girls in the kitchen.]

[切到厨房里的女孩。]

Rachel Green: (counting the place settings) How come we have one extra place setting?

（数场地设置）我们怎么会有一个额外的场地设置？

Monica Geller: 'Cause you invited your assistant.

因为你邀请了你的助手。

Rachel Green: Oh, right. Sorry. But Tag's not coming; his girlfriend came into town, so he's spending Thanksgiving with her.

哦，对了。很抱歉但是Tag不来了；他的女朋友来了，所以他要和她一起过感恩节。

Monica Geller: Oh! Why didn't you tell me? I made him his own individual sweet potato stuffed pumpkin.

哦！你为什么不告诉我？我给他做了自己的红薯南瓜馅。

Rachel Green: Well, I was going to, but then I figured, you know... you're food is so delicious and perfect, you can never have too many of those pumpkin things.

嗯，我本来想去的，但后来我想，你知道。。。你的食物是如此美味和完美，你永远不会有太多的南瓜的东西。

Monica Geller: Now you think I wouldn't enjoy that, because it is so fake, (Laughs) but I still do.

现在你认为我不会喜欢它，因为它太假了，（笑）但我还是喜欢。

Phoebe Buffay: Regular Celery! (Starts to write that on her list) Oh, I already have that. (She gets up and heads for her room)

普通芹菜！（开始写在她的名单上）哦，我已经有了。（她起身向自己的房间走去）

Ross Geller: Done! With time a-to-spare.

好了！还有时间。

Chandler Bing: Oooh that may be a New World's record (Looks at his watch and picks up Ross' pad)

哦，那可能是一项新的世界纪录（看他的手表，拿起罗斯的笔记本）

Ross Geller: You know, I hate to lecture you guys, but it's kinda disgraceful, that a group of well-educated adults and Joey can't name all the states. Did you ever see a map, or one of those round, colorful things called "a globe?" Hmm?

你们知道，我不想教训你们，但一群受过良好教育的成年人和乔伊不能说出所有的州，这有点丢脸。你有没有看过地图，或者那种叫做“地球仪”的圆形、彩色的东西？

Chandler Bing: Uh, Magellan? You got 46 states. (Smiles and hands Ross back his pad)

嗯，麦哲伦？你有46个州。（微笑着将罗斯的手放回笔记本）

Ross Geller: What? That's impossible.

什么？那是不可能的。

Joey Tribbiani: 46. Wow! Who's well educated now, Mr. I-forgot-ten-states?

46。哇！现在谁受过良好教育，我忘了十个州？

Monica Geller: All right, I'm out of oven space. I'm gonna turn on Joey's. Please, watch him! Do not let Joey eat any of the food!

好吧，我没有烤箱了。我要打开乔伊的。请看着他！不要让乔伊吃任何食物！

Chandler Bing: I am only one man! (Monica heads out) Okay Ross, time is up!

我只是一个人！（莫妮卡抬头）好了，罗斯，时间到了！

Ross Geller: No, just give me another minute.

不，再给我一分钟。

Chandler Bing: Look Ross, if you don't know them by now, you will never know them, okay? That is the beauty of this game. It makes you want to kill yourself.

听着，罗斯，如果你现在还不认识他们，你永远也不会认识他们，好吗？这就是这个游戏的魅力所在。它让你想自杀。

Ross Geller: This-this is crazy! I can do this! All right, uhh, I bet I can get all 50 before dinner.

这太疯狂了！我能做到！好吧，嗯，我打赌我可以在晚饭前拿到全部50英镑。

Chandler Bing: Okay, but if you can't…no dinner!

好吧，但是如果你不能…不要吃晚饭！

Ross Geller: You're on!

你上了！

Joey Tribbiani: (gets up) All right. Don't look at my list, Ross, 'cause there's a lot on there that you don't have.

（站起来）好的。别看我的名单，罗斯，因为上面有很多你没有的。

Monica Geller: (entering, to Joey and Rachel) Hey, did you guys know, that your oven doesn't work?

（进来，对乔伊和瑞秋）嘿，你们知道吗，你们的烤箱坏了？

Joey Tribbiani: But the drawer full of take-out menus is okay, right?

但是满抽屉的外卖菜单没问题，对吧？

Monica Geller: Ross, I'm gonna use yours, okay?

罗斯，我要用你的，好吗？

Ross Geller: Pshhshhh!

嘘嘘！

Monica Geller: Chandler? Can you give me a hand? (Grabs her jacket)

钱德勒？你能帮我一下吗？（抓住她的夹克）

Chandler Bing: Sure, and Joey; do not let Ross look at any of the maps or the globe in your apartment.

当然，还有乔伊；不要让罗斯看你公寓里的地图或地球仪。

Joey Tribbiani: Don't worry, Chandler, it's not a globe of the United States.

别担心，钱德勒，这不是美国的地球仪。

[Chandler and Monica head out with some stuff. Phoebe comes out of her room with a bag.]

[钱德勒和莫妮卡带着一些东西出去了。菲比带着一个包走出了她的房间。]

Phoebe Buffay: Hey you guys I'm gonna go out and take a walk.

嘿，伙计们，我要出去散散步。

Ross Geller: Phoebe, why is your bag moving?

菲比，你的包怎么动了？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, it's not!

哦，不是！

Rachel Green: Seriously, it's moving!

说真的，它在动！

Joey Tribbiani: What the hell is in there?

里面到底是什么？

Phoebe Buffay: It's just my knitting that's all! (A dog sticks its head out of Phoebe's bag. Everyone looks puzzled.) Yes! I knit this. I'm very good.

这只是我的编织而已！（一条狗从菲比的包里探出头来。每个人看起来都很困惑。）是的！我编织这个。我很好。

🎬 Ross' apartment, Monica and Chandler enter.

🎬 罗斯的公寓，莫妮卡和钱德勒进入。

Monica Geller: Ross’s apartment is nice! How come we don't hang out here more often?

罗斯的公寓很好！为什么我们不经常在这里闲逛？

Chandler Bing: I don't know. Maybe it's because it smells a little weird. It's like old pumpkins or something.

我不知道。也许是因为它闻起来有点怪。就像老南瓜什么的。

Monica Geller: That's my pie!

那是我的馅饼！

Chandler Bing: Which smells delicious!

闻起来真香！

Monica Geller: Uh-oh! Uh-oh!

哦！哦！

Chandler Bing: What?

什么？

Monica Geller: We left Joey alone with the food! (Walks towards the window and looks out) Yep! Yep, I knew it! There he is... feeding stuffing to a dog!

我们把食物留给乔伊了！（走向窗户向外看）是的！是的，我就知道！他在那里。。。给狗喂填料！

🎬 Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's, Ross is still doing his list. Rachel and Joey are feeding the dog as the phone rings.

🎬 莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比的，罗斯还在做他的清单。电话铃响时，瑞秋和乔伊正在喂狗。

Phoebe Buffay: (answering the phone) Hi Geller-Bing residence. How can I help?

（接电话）嗨，盖勒宾公寓。我能帮忙吗？

Monica Geller: Phoebe, why is there a dog in our apartment?

菲比，为什么我们的公寓里有条狗？

Phoebe Buffay: I'm sorry, who's this?

对不起，你是谁？

Monica Geller: Phoebe, there's a dog sitting on my couch!

菲比，有只狗坐在我的沙发上！

Chandler Bing: Tell her, I'm allergic, and I will sue!

告诉她，我过敏，我会起诉的！

Phoebe Buffay: No, there's no dog here?

不，这里没有狗？

Monica Geller: Yes there is! He's black and white and shaggy and [Cut to Monica’s apartment] he's sitting next to Rachel and licking Rachel's hand.

有！他又黑又白，头发蓬乱，[切到莫妮卡的公寓]他坐在瑞秋旁边，舔着瑞秋的手。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh my god! Where are you? (Looks around.)

哦，我的天啊！你在哪？（环顾四周。）

Monica Geller: I'll be right there!

我马上就到！

Phoebe Buffay: (Phoebe hangs up and someone knocks on the door. She gasps.) They're here already? How are they doing this?

（菲比挂断电话，有人敲门。她喘着粗气。）他们已经来了？他们是怎么做到的？

Rachel Green: (gets up and opens the door) Hi Tag! What are you doing here?

（站起来开门）嗨，泰格！你在这里干什么？

Tag: I, uh, wanted to see if your offer to spend Thanksgiving with you is still good.

我，呃，想看看你提出的和你一起过感恩节的提议是否仍然有效。

Rachel Green: Well, sure! Come in! (He enters) Well, what-what happened to your girlfriend?

当然可以！进来吧（他进来）你女朋友怎么了？

Tag: We kinda broke up this morning.

我们今天早上有点分手了。

Rachel Green: Oh, I'm sorry.

哦，对不起。

Tag: Yeah, so she went back to Ohio.

是的，所以她回到了俄亥俄州。

Ross Geller: Ohio!! Thank you!

俄亥俄州！！非常感谢。

⏱ Time lapse, Chandler and Monica enter.

[时间流逝，钱德勒和莫妮卡进入。]

Chandler Bing: Huh! Where is the dog?!

啊！狗在哪里？！

Ross Geller: What dog? There-there's no dog here.

什么狗？这里没有狗。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah that dog left!

是啊，那条狗走了！

Monica Geller: (walks to Phoebe's door) Phoebe! Phoebe, open up!

（走向菲比的门）菲比！菲比，开门！

Phoebe Buffay: There's no dog in here.

这里没有狗。

[The dog barks.]

[狗吠。]

Chandler Bing: Phoebe, we can hear the dog barking!

菲比，我们能听到狗在叫！

Phoebe Buffay: No that's just me coughing! (Doing some weird coughing noises and the dog barks again. Phoebe comes out of the room.) Oh, good, there you are! Listen, um, I have a dog in my room.

不，那只是我咳嗽！（一阵奇怪的咳嗽声，狗又叫了起来。菲比走出房间。）哦，太好了，你来了！听着，嗯，我房间里有只狗。

Chandler Bing: What is it doing here?

它在这里干什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, I'm watching it for some friends who went out of town. Wait. (She bends down, picks up the dog, and waves with one of its paws) Hello, my name is Clunkers. May I please stay with you nice people?

嗯，我在为一些出城的朋友看。等待（她弯下腰，抱起狗，用它的一只爪子挥挥手）你好，我的名字叫克朗克斯。我可以和你们这些好人呆在一起吗？

Monica Geller: Oooh, I wish she could stay here, but Chandler is allergic!

哦，我希望她能呆在这里，但钱德勒过敏！

Chandler Bing: Extremely allergic, okay? If I'm anywhere near a dog for more than 5 minutes, my throat will just close up!

非常过敏，好吗？如果我在任何地方靠近一只狗超过5分钟，我的喉咙就会闭上！

Phoebe Buffay: That's odd, 'cause this dog's been living here for the past 3 days

这很奇怪，因为这条狗已经在这里住了三天了

Chandler Bing: (gasping) Really?

（喘息）真的吗？

Monica Geller: Chandler, if that dog's been here that long, and you haven't had a reaction, maybe you're not allergic to this dog?

钱德勒，如果那条狗在这里那么久了，而你没有反应，也许你对这条狗不过敏？

Chandler Bing: Well, it still has to go, right?

嗯，它还得走，对吗？

Monica and **Phoebe Buffay: **Why?

莫妮卡和菲比·布菲：为什么？

Chandler Bing: Okay, it's um…

好吧，嗯…

Joey Tribbiani: (interrupting him) Don't do it!

（打断他）别这样！

Monica Geller: Don't do what?

别做什么？

Chandler Bing: (to Joey) I have to! Okay? It's time! (Joey shrugs as if to say, "Do what you have to do") Okay, I hate dogs.

（对乔伊）我必须！可以是时候！（乔伊耸耸肩，好像在说，“做你必须做的事”）好吧，我讨厌狗。

All: What?

什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Are you crazy?

你疯了吗？

Ross Geller: Are you out of your mind?

你疯了吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Why?

为什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Told ya. (Waves bye-bye.)

告诉你了。（挥手告别。）

Chandler Bing: They are needy, they are jumpy, and you can't tell what they are thinking, and that scares me a little bit.

他们需要帮助，他们神经质，你不知道他们在想什么，这让我有点害怕。

Ross Geller: Right, they are scary. (He jumps up, screaming) Ahh, she just ate a treat out of my hand!!!

没错，他们很吓人。（他跳起来，尖叫）啊，她刚从我手里吃了一顿！！！

Rachel Green: Wait a minute. Do you not like all dogs? I mean, not even puppies?

等一下。你不喜欢所有的狗吗？我是说，连小狗都没有？

Chandler Bing: (scared) Is there a puppy here?

（害怕）这里有小狗吗？

Tag: You don't like puppies?

你不喜欢小狗？

Chandler Bing: (to Tag) Okay, you are new!

好吧，你是新来的！

Joey Tribbiani: Look, Chandler, I told you, never tell anyone about this dog thing. It's like Ross not likin’ ice cream.

听着，钱德勒，我告诉过你，不要告诉任何人关于狗的事。就像罗斯不喜欢冰淇淋一样。

Phoebe Buffay: You don't like ice cream?

你不喜欢冰淇淋？

Ross Geller: It's too cold.

太冷了。

Chandler Bing: Okay, it's just that dogs make me a little uncomfortable.

好吧，只是狗让我有点不舒服。

Ross Geller: (to Phoebe) It hurts my teeth.

（对菲比）我的牙齿疼。

Chandler Bing: And I don't wanna say this, I don't you guys to hate me, but uh, I don't think, I can be around that dog anymore. Okay, so either the dog goes, or I go. (An awkward silence ensues.) Oh my god!!

我不想说这些，我不想你们讨厌我，但是，我不认为，我可以再和那条狗在一起了。好吧，要么狗走，要么我走。（接着是尴尬的沉默。）哦，我的天哪！！

⏱ Time lapse, Ross is still doing Chandler's game. Tag is heading for the balcony.

[时间流逝，罗斯仍在玩钱德勒的游戏。泰格正朝阳台走去。]

Ross Geller: How can I not get this? I'm a college professor; I got 1450 on my S.A.T.s.

我怎么能不得到这个？我是一名大学教授；我的S.A.T.S.成绩是1450。

Monica Geller: 1250.

1250。

Ross Geller: Damn, I forgot you were here.

该死，我忘了你在这里。

Phoebe Buffay: All right. We're gonna take Clunkers to Ross’s. We'll be back in a minute. (Gets up with Monica to do so.)

好的。我们要把旧车送到罗斯家。我们马上回来。（和莫妮卡站起来这样做。）

Rachel Green: Oh, wait before you guys go, can I just ask you a question?

哦，等你们走之前，我能问你们一个问题吗？

Monica Geller: Yeah.

是的。

Rachel Green: When a guy breaks up with his girlfriend, what is an appropriate amount of time to wait before you make a move?

当一个男人和他的女朋友分手时，在你采取行动之前，什么是合适的等待时间？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, I'd say about a month.

哦，我想大概一个月吧。

Monica Geller: Really? I'd say 3 to 4.

真的吗？我想是3比4。

Joey Tribbiani: Half hour. (Rachel turns to look at him and he nods yes.)

半小时。（瑞秋转身看着他，他点头同意。）

Rachel Green: Interesting.

有趣。

Monica Geller: When it's your assistant, I would say never.

如果是你的助手，我会说永远不会。

Joey Tribbiani: All right, Rach, the big question is, does he like you? All right? Because if he doesn't like you, this is all a moo-point.

好吧，瑞秋，最大的问题是，他喜欢你吗？好吧因为如果他不喜欢你，这都是一个moo点。

Rachel Green: Huh. A moo-point?

嗯。一个moo点？

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, it's like a cow's opinion. It just doesn't matter. It's moo.

是啊，就像牛的意见一样。没关系。是穆奥。

Rachel Green: (to Monica and Phoebe) Have I been living with him for too long, or did that all just make sense?
