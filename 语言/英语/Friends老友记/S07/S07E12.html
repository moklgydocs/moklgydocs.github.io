The One Where They’re Up All Night

他们整晚都没睡的那个

Written by: Zack Rosenblatt

作者：扎克·罗森布拉特

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 The Building’s Roof, the entire gang plus Tag are there to look for a comet. They’re looking for a comet on a roof of a New York apartment building. Yeah, that’s realistic. You might as well look for the moon on a bright sunny day.

🎬 大楼的屋顶，整个帮派都在那里寻找彗星。他们在纽约公寓楼的屋顶上寻找彗星。是的，这很现实。你不妨在阳光明媚的日子寻找月亮。

Monica Geller: (looking up) Ross, when’s this comet thing start?

（抬头）罗斯，彗星什么时候开始？

Ross Geller: Well, technically it seven billion years ago… (Well, technically you’d be able to see it for days, well nights; that is if you could see it with all of the bright lights of New York.)

好吧，从技术上讲，这是70亿年前的事了……（好吧，从技术上讲，你可以在白天、晚上看到它；如果你能在纽约所有明亮的灯光下看到它的话。）

All: (groaning) Oh no! Oh no! (They all start to get up a leave.)

（呻吟）哦，不！哦，不！（他们都开始休假了。）

Ross Geller: Okay! Okay! Fine, I’ll stop! No teaching, okay? We’ll just watch the pretty light streaking across the sky. (Comets don’t streak across the sky, meteors do.) Okay? Who’s official name is Bapstein-King.

好的！可以好吧，我停下来！不要教书，好吗？我们只会看着美丽的光线划过天空。（彗星不会划过天空，流星会。）好吗？他的正式名字是巴普斯坦·金。

All: Okay! Okay! (They start to leave again.)

好的！可以（他们又开始离开了。）

Phoebe Buffay: (looking up) There it is! Oh, look at that! Isn’t Mother Nature amazing?

（抬头看）在那儿！哦，看那个！大自然母亲不是很神奇吗？

Chandler Bing: (looking up with her) That’s a plane!

（抬头看着她）那是一架飞机！

Phoebe Buffay: Well, all right. 1700 bags of peanuts flying that high, that’s pretty amazing too.

嗯，好吧。1700袋花生飞那么高，也太神奇了。

Tag: Hey, I wonder if you can see my apartment from up here.

嘿，不知道你能不能从这里看到我的公寓。

Rachel Green: No. No, you can’t.

不，不，你不能。

Tag: What?

什么？

Rachel Green: Oh I don’t-I don’t know.

哦，我不知道。

Ross Geller: Man, look at all those stars! (Yeah, you can see what? Five of them from the city?) Infinite space. It really, really makes you wonder, doesn’t it?

老兄，看看那些星星！（是的，你能看到什么？五个来自城市？）无限空间。这真的让你很好奇，不是吗？

Joey Tribbiani: (looking through his binoculars at a nearby building) Y’know what else makes you wonder?

（用他的双筒望远镜望着附近的一栋楼）你知道还有什么让你好奇的吗？

Ross Geller: Huh?

嗯？

Joey Tribbiani: Check out the rack on this chick! (Turns around to point it out to Ross and finds that Ross is glaring at him. So he quickly puts his binoculars to his eyes and starts looking for the comet.)

看看这个小妞身上的架子！（转过身向罗斯指了指，发现罗斯正瞪着他。于是他迅速将双筒望远镜对准眼睛，开始寻找彗星。）

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 The Roof, continued from earlier.

🎬 屋顶，从前面继续。

Monica Geller: Okay, we’ve been out here for two hours and we haven’t seen any stupid comets. Can we go now? I mean, Chandler’s getting chilly. (She walks over to where Chandler is bundled up in a big coat and shivering.)

好吧，我们已经在这里呆了两个小时了，还没有看到任何愚蠢的彗星。我们现在可以走了吗？我是说，钱德勒越来越冷了。（她走到钱德勒裹着一件大外套的地方，浑身发抖。）

Chandler Bing: (with a quivering voice) No, I’m not!

（声音颤抖）不，我不是！

Joey Tribbiani: Then why are you wearing Monica’s jacket?

那你为什么穿莫妮卡的夹克呢？

Chandler Bing: Because it’s flattering! (Shivers harder) Come on Monica! Come on Monica! (He goes inside.)

因为这是奉承！（颤抖得更厉害）来吧，莫妮卡！快点，莫妮卡！（他走了进去。）

Rachel Green: Yeah actually, I think we’re gonna take off too. We rented a movie.

是的，事实上，我想我们也要起飞了。我们租了一部电影。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh! I won’t say, ‘no’ to a movie!

哦！我不会对电影说‘不’！

Rachel Green: Uh Pheebs, we just actually kinda wanted to be alone.

嗯，菲比，我们只是想一个人呆着。

Phoebe Buffay: Shh! Get me out of here.

嘘！把我弄出去。

Rachel Green: Oh. (They leave, leaving just Joey and Ross.)

哦。（他们离开了，只留下乔伊和罗斯。）

Joey Tribbiani: (whispering) Ross!

（低声）罗斯！

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Come here, check this out!

过来，看看这个！

Ross Geller: What? Is it the comet? (Runs over to where Joey’s standing.)

什么？是彗星吗？（跑到乔伊站的地方。）

Joey Tribbiani: No! No-no. Look, there’s a bug stuck in tar right here. (Bends down to get a closer look.)

不！不，不，看，有只虫子卡在焦油里了。（弯下腰仔细看一看。）

Ross Geller: Joey come—I can’t believe—I bring you here to see the Bapstein-King comet, one of nature’s most spectacular phenomenon, and all you care about are bugs stuck in tar and-and some woman!

乔伊，来吧，我真不敢相信，我带你来这里是为了看巴普斯坦·金彗星，这是自然界最壮观的现象之一，你所关心的只是困在焦油里的虫子和一些女人！

Joey Tribbiani: (standing up) Y’know, there’s two women dude.

（站起来）你知道，有两个女人。

Ross Geller: Show me where?

告诉我在哪里？

Joey Tribbiani: Right-right up here. (Starts looking at them through a piece of pipe.)

就在这里。（开始通过一根管子看着他们。）

Ross Geller: (noticing the pipe and looking at the door) Joey where’s the pipe that was holding the door open?

（注意到烟斗，看着门）乔伊，把门打开的烟斗在哪里？

Joey Tribbiani: (annoyed) I don’t know! (Goes back to looking through the pipe.) (Pause) Yeah, I do.

（生气）我不知道！（回头看看管道。）（停顿）是的，我知道。

Ross Geller: Joey!

乔伊！

Joey Tribbiani: What?! All right—Hey! Don’t look at me! You’re the one who wanted to come up and look for some stupid Burger King comet!

什么？！好吧，嘿！别看我！你就是那个想来找愚蠢的汉堡王彗星的人！

Ross Geller: It’s called the Bapstein-King comet, okay? (Joey starts to groan.) Hey! Hey! Bapstein was a very well respected astronomer!

它叫巴普斯坦·金彗星，好吗？（乔伊开始呻吟）嘿！嘿巴普斯坦是一位非常受人尊敬的天文学家！

Joey Tribbiani: (covering his ears and yelling) Oh no! No! No! (He starts banging on the door.)

（捂住耳朵大喊大叫）哦，不！不不（他开始敲门。）

🎬 Monica and Chandler’s bedroom, Monica is sleeping and Chandler’s sitting in bed, wide awake.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒的卧室，莫妮卡正在睡觉，钱德勒坐在床上，完全清醒。

Chandler Bing: (whispering) Monica!

（耳语）莫妮卡！

Monica Geller: She’s sleeping.

她在睡觉。

Chandler Bing: I know, just quick-quick question, quick question. Which one was Deep Impact and which one was Armageddon?

我知道，快问，快问。哪一个是深度冲击，哪一个是末日？

Monica Geller: Deep Impact was the one with Robert Duval, Armageddon is what’s going to happen to you if you wake me up.

罗伯特·杜瓦尔的《深刻的影响》，如果你叫醒我，世界末日就要降临到你身上了。

Chandler Bing: Sorry, I just…can’t sleep. Ooh! (Turns on the light and Monica groans.) Where is that book that you are reading with the two women who were ice-skating and wearing, wearing those hats with the flowers on it? Because every time I look at that cover I’m like…(Fake snores.)

对不起，我只是…睡不着。哦！（打开灯，莫妮卡呻吟着）你正在和两个滑冰的女人一起读的那本书在哪里？她们戴着那顶上面有花的帽子？因为每次我看那个封面我都像…（假鼾声）

Monica Geller: It is in the living room where there is also a light! And no one will kick you in the shin.

在客厅里也有灯！没有人会踢你的小腿。

Chandler Bing: What?! (Monica kicks him in the shin.) Ow! (He gets out of bed and heads into the living room.)

什么？！（莫妮卡踢了他的小腿）噢！（他下床走进客厅。）

🎬 Phoebe's apartment, there is a beeping noise coming from the living room and Phoebe sleepily goes to investigate.

🎬 菲比的公寓，客厅里传来嘟嘟声，菲比昏昏欲睡地去调查。

Phoebe Buffay: (crossing her fingers and closing her eyes) Please don’t be a space ship. Please don’t be a space ship. (She turns on the light and looks around and finds that it’s the smoke detector that’s beeping.) Oh thank God! (She moves a chair over and starts to investigate how to make the beeping turn off, in frustration she yanks the thing off of the wall. She sets it down and heads for bed, just as she gets there it beeps again. She opens the cover and removes the battery, but it still beeps.) How could you be beeping?! I just disconnected you! I took out your battery! How can…

（交叉手指，闭上眼睛）请不要做太空船。请不要做太空船。（她打开灯，环顾四周，发现是烟雾探测器在响。）哦，感谢上帝！（她把一把椅子移过来，开始研究如何关掉嘟嘟声，沮丧中她把东西从墙上拽了下来。她放下它，朝床走去，就在她到达那里时，它又嘟嘟声了。她打开盖子，取出电池，但它仍然嘟嘟声。）你怎么会嘟嘟声？！我刚刚断开了你的连接！我把你的电池拿出来了！怎么能…

Smoke Detector: Beep!

哔！

Phoebe Buffay: Don’t interrupt me!!

别打断我！！

🎬 The Roof, Ross and Joey are banging on the door.

🎬 屋顶，罗斯和乔伊在敲门。

Ross Geller: Rachel!! Monica!!

瑞秋！！莫妮卡！！

Joey Tribbiani: Come on!

来吧！

(Ross gets fed up with Joey’s banging and stops him by pulling him away from the door.)

（罗斯受够了乔伊的砰砰声，把他从门口拉开，阻止了他。）

Ross Geller: I can’t believe this!!

我真不敢相信！！

Joey Tribbiani: All right well, y’know…I guess we know what we have to do to get down.

好吧，你知道……我想我们知道我们要做什么才能下来。

Ross Geller: (standing at the edge of the roof) Yeah, I guess we don’t have a choice. (Screaming to the street) Help us! Please help us! We’re stuck up on the roof and we can’t get down!!!

（站在屋顶边缘）是的，我想我们别无选择。（向街上尖叫）救救我们！请帮助我们！我们被困在屋顶上，不能下来！！！

Joey Tribbiani: Ross. I was thinking we could just go down the fire escape. (Points it out.)

罗斯。我在想我们可以从消防通道下去。（他指出。）

Ross Geller: (To Joey) I know, I wasn’t finished. (Joey motions him to finish.) (Yelling at the street) But don’t worry! We’re gonna go down the fire escape!!

（对乔伊）我知道，我还没做完。（乔伊示意他说完。）（在街上大叫）但别担心！我们要从消防通道下去！！

🎬 Monica and Chandler's kitchen, Chandler has a jug of milk in his hands and decides to make some warm milk. He opens up the cabinet to get a pot and manages to knock several other pots onto the floor making a lot of noise.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒的厨房，钱德勒手里拿着一罐牛奶，决定做些热牛奶。他打开橱柜拿了一个罐子，设法把另外几个罐子撞到地板上，发出很大的噪音。

Chandler Bing: Shhhhhhhhh!!! (Monica enters) I’m sorry, I thought maybe I’d make some warm milk and it would help me sleep.

嘘！！！（莫妮卡进来）对不起，我想也许我可以做些热牛奶，这样可以帮助我入睡。

Monica Geller: With a wok? (Chandler’s holding a wok.) I thought you were going to read my boring book to put you asleep.

用炒锅？（钱德勒拿着炒锅）我以为你会读我那本无聊的书让你睡着。

Chandler Bing: It got interesting! Damn you Oprah!

真有趣！该死的奥普拉！

Monica Geller: Here, let me make the milk, I’m up anyway.

来，让我来煮牛奶，反正我已经起床了。

Chandler Bing: Hey, y’know what we can do? Y’know, now that we are up? We can just like talk to each other all night long, y’know like we did when we were first going out. It’d be fun!

嘿，你知道我们能做什么吗？你知道，现在我们起床了？我们可以通宵交谈，就像我们第一次出去时那样。那会很有趣的！

Monica Geller: Okay that does sound like fun.

好吧，听起来确实很有趣。

Chandler Bing: Okay, so how bummed were you when the second sister died huh?

好吧，那你在二姐死的时候有多沮丧？

Monica Geller: The second sister dies?!

第二个妹妹死了？！

Chandler Bing: (Pause) No. No, I-I was, I was talking about the book I was reading.

（停顿）不，不，我是，我是在说我正在读的那本书。

Monica Geller: The second sister dies in Archie and Jughead Double Digest?

第二个妹妹死在《阿奇和猪头双重消化》里？

Chandler Bing: That’s correct.

没错。

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Rachel and Tag are making out on the couch.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋的，瑞秋和泰格在沙发上亲热。

Rachel Green: You wanna go in the bedroom? It’s a little more comfortable.

你想去卧室吗？稍微舒服一点。

Tag: Sure.

当然。

Rachel Green: Okay. (They start to head for the bedroom) Oh wait! Umm, did you send those contracts to Milan?

好的。（他们开始向卧室走去）哦，等等！嗯，你把那些合同寄到米兰了吗？

Tag: If this is your idea of sexy talk? (Shakes his head that it’s not working.)

如果这是你的性感话题？（摇摇头，说它不工作了。）

Rachel Green: No seriously, y’know the contracts I gave you, did you overnight them?

不，说真的，你知道我给你的合同，你有没有签过？

Tag: What contracts?

什么合同？

Rachel Green: Okay please tell me that this is just one of your jokes that you do that I don’t get.

好吧，请告诉我，这只是你的一个笑话，你这样做，我不明白。

Tag: Like what?

像什么？

Rachel Green: Y’know, like the thing when you put the phone in your pants? (He starts laughing.) Tag! I’m serious! This isn’t funny! Those contracts absolutely had to go out today!

你知道吗，就像你把电话放在裤子里一样？（他笑了起来。）泰格！我是认真的！这一点都不好笑！那些合同今天一定要到期！

Tag: Rach, I’m sorry, but you didn’t give me any contracts!

瑞秋，对不起，你没有给我任何合同！

Rachel Green: Yes I did! And I put a little Post-It on it that said, "Must go out today," and underlined today three times and, and then I put a little heart in the corner because I didn’t want to seem to bossy.

是的！我在上面贴了一个小帖子，上面写着“今天必须出去”，并在今天画了三次线，然后我在角落里放了一颗心，因为我不想显得专横。

Tag: I’m telling you, you never gave them to me.

我告诉你，你从来没有给过我。

Rachel Green: Y’know what Tag, if we went down to the office you would see those contracts sitting on your desk.

你知道吗，如果我们去办公室，你会看到那些合同放在你的桌子上。

Tag: No, I would see you looking embarrassed because they are not on my desk!

不，我会看到你看起来很尴尬，因为它们不在我的桌子上！

Rachel Green: Or maybe you would see me looking embarrassed because you are talking on the phone with your crotch!

或者你会看到我看起来很尴尬，因为你在用你的裤裆打电话！

Tag: You wanna go down to the office right now?

你现在想去办公室吗？

Rachel Green: No! Come on its late, we’re not gonna go down to the office.

不！快点，很晚了，我们不去办公室了。

Tag: Okay I understand. (Sits down.) I wouldn’t want to be proved wrong either.

好的，我明白了。（坐下）我也不想被证明是错的。

Rachel Green: Okay get your coat! (They get their coats and start to leave. Rachel suddenly stops and sticks the hand up the back of her shirt.) Oh! When did you unhook this? (Her bra.) Nice work!

好的，拿上你的外套！（他们拿了外套，开始离开。瑞秋突然停下来，把手伸到衬衫后面。）哦！你什么时候解开的？（她的胸罩）干得好！

🎬 The Fire Escape, Joey and Ross have reached the last landing. Joey is tugging on the ladder that extends to the ground, but it won’t budge.

🎬 消防梯，乔伊和罗斯已经到达了最后一层楼。乔伊正在拉着一直延伸到地面的梯子，但它一动不动。

Joey Tribbiani: All right, it won’t go down any further. It’s stuck.

好吧，它不会再往下沉了。它卡住了。

Ross Geller: Ugh. Well, we’re just gonna have to jump. (Joey looks at him.) Yeah. Now, we’re gonna have to make sure to land to the right of that patch of ice, okay? Not hit the dumpster on the other side and uh, and try to avoid that-that weird brownish red stuff in the middle. So, when you get down there…you go up to the roof and you let me in.

啊。我们得跳了。（乔伊看着他）是的。现在，我们必须确保降落在那块冰的右边，好吗？不要撞到垃圾桶的另一边，呃，尽量避免中间的那种奇怪的棕红色的东西。所以，当你下来的时候…你上屋顶让我进去。

Joey Tribbiani: Oh whoa-whoa wait a minute! I have to do it?!

哦，等等！我必须这么做？！

Ross Geller: Yeah! Oh yeah, you’ll be fine! It-it’ll be uh, just like bungy jumping. Y’know? But instead of bouncing back up you-you won’t.

是啊！哦，是的，你会没事的！就像蹦极一样。你知道吗？但你不会反弹回来。

Joey Tribbiani: What if I smack my head on the concrete?

如果我的头撞在混凝土上怎么办？

Ross Geller: Well, I’m gonna lie to you Joey, it’s a possibility.

我要对你撒谎，乔伊，这是可能的。

Joey Tribbiani: (looks at the ground and at Ross) I don’t know Ross! I-I tell you what, let’s flip to see who does it, okay? You-you call it in the air, all right?

（看着地面，看着罗斯）我不认识罗斯！我告诉你，我们来看看是谁干的，好吗？你说这是空话，好吗？

Ross Geller: Oh, all right. (Joey flips the coin.) Tails! (The coin bounces off of the landing above them and falls to the ground.) Can you-can you see what it is?

哦，好吧。（乔伊掷硬币）尾巴！（硬币从他们上面的平台上弹下来，落在地上。）你能看到它是什么吗？

Joey Tribbiani: No.

没有。

Ross Geller: Okay. Well, you be careful.

好的。嗯，你要小心。

Joey Tribbiani: What? No! No Ross! No-no! Stop! I’m not jumping! Okay, look I have an audition tomorrow and I can’t go if I break my leg.

什么？不不，罗斯！不，不！停止我不跳！好吧，我明天有个试镜，如果我摔断了腿我就不能去了。

Ross Geller: Well I’m jumping! I have a son! Okay? He won’t have a father if-if I die!

我要跳了！我有一个儿子！可以如果我死了，他就没有父亲了！

Joey Tribbiani: Well all right so, it looks like we’re even!

好吧，看来我们扯平了！

🎬 Phoebe's apartment, Phoebe is still investigating the smoke detector trying to figure out how to stop the beeping.

🎬 菲比的公寓，菲比仍在调查烟雾探测器，试图找出如何阻止嘟嘟声。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay. So, this wire is connected to this wire which plugs into here. (She points at each as she says it.)
