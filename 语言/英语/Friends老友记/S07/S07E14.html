The One Where They All Turn Thirty

Teleplay by: Sherry Bilsing & Ellen Plummer

雪莉·比尔辛和艾伦·普卢默

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Joey is knocking on Rachel’s door, whose door frame is decorated with balloons. The rest of the gang is there as well. Rachel opens the door and the gang blow on noisemakers.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，乔伊正在敲瑞秋家的门，瑞秋家的门框上装饰着气球。帮派的其他成员也在那里。瑞秋打开门，帮派向制造噪音的人吹气。

Ross Geller: Happy birthday!!!

生日快乐！！！

Monica Geller: Happy birthday!!!

生日快乐！！！

(Rachel glares at them and goes back into her room, closing her door.)

（瑞秋瞪了他们一眼，然后回到她的房间，关上门。）

All: Rach! Come on! Rach!

瑞秋！来吧瑞秋！

Monica Geller: It’s your birthday!

今天是你的生日！

Tag: (entering from her room) Hey.

（从她的房间进来）嘿。

Chandler Bing: (To Monica) She’s not as pretty as she was when she was 29.

（对莫妮卡）她不像29岁时那么漂亮了。

Tag: Ms. Green would like to establish some ground rules before she comes out. She would appreciate it if you don’t use the words old or downhill or (To Joey) they still look pretty damn good. (Joey smiles and everyone glares at him.)

格林女士想在她出来之前制定一些基本规则。如果你不用old或downhill（对Joey来说）这些词，她会很感激你的。（乔伊笑了，每个人都瞪着他。）

Joey Tribbiani: They do!

是的！

Phoebe Buffay: Rachel! Come on out! Monica made breakfast!

瑞秋！出来吧！莫妮卡做了早餐！

Monica Geller: Chocolate-chip pancakes!

巧克力薄片煎饼！

(There is no response from Rachel.)

（瑞秋没有回应。）

Ross Geller: We’ve got presents!

我们有礼物！

(She opens the door.)

（她打开门。）

Rachel Green: Good ones?

好的？

Monica Geller: They all came from the list you handed out to us two weeks ago.

它们都是两周前你发给我们的名单上的。

Rachel Green: Well, can I keep the presents and still be 29?

好吧，我能保留这些礼物，并且仍然是29岁吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Come on Rach! Look, turning thirty is not that big a deal.

来吧，瑞秋！看，三十岁没什么大不了的。

Ross Geller: Oh really. Is that how you felt when you turned thirty?

哦，真的。这就是你三十岁时的感受吗？

[Flashback to Joey’s thirtieth birthday party. It is being held in Monica and Chandler’s apartment.]

[倒叙乔伊的30岁生日派对。派对在莫妮卡和钱德勒的公寓举行。]

Joey Tribbiani: (screaming) Why God?!! Why?!! We had a deal!! Let the others grow old! Not me!! (He buries his head in Phoebe’s lap for comfort.)

（尖叫）为什么上帝？！！为什么？我们说好了！！让其他人变老吧！不是我！！（他把头埋在菲比的大腿上以示安慰。）

[Cut back to Rachel’s party, everyone is now eating breakfast, except Rachel.]

[回到瑞秋的派对，除了瑞秋，所有人都在吃早餐。]

Rachel Green: Y’know, I’m still 29 in Guam.

你知道，我在关岛还是29岁。

Ross Geller: Hey, 30 is not that old! Do you know how old the Earth is?

嘿，30岁没那么老！你知道地球有多老吗？

Rachel Green: Late thirties? Oh come on you guys! Is it just me? Am I overreacting to this?

三十多岁？哦，来吧，伙计们！只是我吗？我是不是反应过度了？

Chandler Bing: No Rach, it’s not just you. My thirtieth birthday certainly wasn’t that much fun.

不，瑞秋，不只是你。我三十岁的生日当然没那么有趣。

[Flashback to Chandler’s thirtieth birthday party. It is also being held in Monica and his apartment. He is about to blow out the candles on his birthday cake.]

[回忆钱德勒的30岁生日派对。派对也在莫妮卡和他的公寓举行。他正要吹灭生日蛋糕上的蜡烛。]

Joey Tribbiani: (screaming) And now Chandler! We’re all gettin’ so old! (Looking up) Why are you doing this to us?! (Turns away crying.)

（尖叫）现在是钱德勒！我们都老了！（抬头）你为什么这样对我们？！（哭着转过身去。）

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, breakfast is finished but Rachel’s still down.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，早餐吃完了，但瑞秋还没起床。

Monica Geller: Rach, you’re in a great place in your life. Come on, you’ve got a great job! Good friends…

瑞秋，你在你的生活中处于一个很好的位置。来吧，你有一份很棒的工作！好朋友…

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, you’re roommate is a soap opera star.

是啊，你的室友是个肥皂剧明星。

Rachel Green: Look, y’know I know my life’s going pretty well, but I look around and I just see so many people who’ve accomplished so many other goals by the time they’re thirty.

听着，你知道我的生活很顺利，但是我环顾四周，我看到很多人在三十岁的时候完成了很多其他的目标。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, but you shouldn’t compare yourself to me.

是啊，但你不应该拿自己和我比较。

[Flashback to: The Street in front of Central Perk, Ross and Joey are holding a yellow tape across the road and everyone is cheering Phoebe as she bounces around the corner on a hippity-hop.]

[倒叙：中央公园前的街道上，罗斯和乔伊拿着黄色胶带穿过马路，当菲比在街角蹦蹦跳跳时，每个人都在为她欢呼。]

All: Come on Phoebe! You can do it Phoebe! Come on!

来吧，菲比！菲比，你能行！来吧

Rachel Green: There you go!

好了！

(She crosses the line and they all cheer again.)

（她越线了，他们又都欢呼起来。）

Phoebe Buffay: I did it! One mile on a hippity-hop! That’s it!! That’s everything I wanted to do before I was thirty. Oh, except I wanted to patch things up with my sister. But oh well. Yay!! (They all cheer again.) And-and girls this thing is a Godsend if you know what I mean. (Rachel and Monica look intrigued and as they all head into Central Perk, Rachel picks up the hippity-hop that Phoebe left behind.)

我做到了！嬉皮舞步一英里！就这样！！这就是我三十岁以前想做的一切。哦，除了我想和我妹妹和好。但是哦，好吧。耶！！（他们再次欢呼）女孩们，如果你们明白我的意思，这件事是天赐的。（瑞秋和莫妮卡看起来很好奇，当他们都走进中央咖啡馆时，瑞秋拿起了菲比留下的嬉皮舞步。）

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, continued from earlier.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋的，从早些时候开始继续

Rachel Green: Thirty. Ugh, I mean thirty! Monica, do you remember mean, old Mrs. Kreeger in the fifth grade? She was thirty!

三十。啊，我是说三十！莫妮卡，你还记得五年级的克雷格老太太吗？她三十岁！

Tag: Come on, let’s have some fun. Huh? (To Rachel) What do you want to do today?

来吧，让我们玩得开心点。嗯？（对瑞秋）你今天想做什么？

Rachel Green: Nothing. I don’t want to do anything.

没什么。我什么都不想做。

Monica Geller: Well, doing nothing on your thirtieth is better than doing something stupid, like Ross.

嗯，三十多岁的时候什么都不做总比做罗斯那样的蠢事好。

Ross Geller: Hey! That was a practical purchase! I needed that car for transportation! Okay? I-I have a child!

嘿！这是一个实际的购买！我需要那辆车来运输！可以我有个孩子！

[Flashback to: A street, Ross is sitting in his newly purchased MGB. Which is one of the better British sports cars ever made. Of course, ‘better’ is a relative term. Which reminds me of a joke. Why don’t the British make computers? Because they couldn’t figure out how to make them leak oil. Anyway, the gang is all staring at his new purchase.]

[回忆：一条街上，罗斯坐在他新买的MGB里。这是英国有史以来最好的跑车之一。当然，“更好”是一个相对的词。这让我想起了一个笑话。为什么英国人不生产电脑？因为他们不知道如何让电脑漏油。不管怎样，这帮人都在盯着他的新购买。]

Ross Geller: How hot do I look in this, huh?!

我穿这件衣服看起来有多性感啊？！

Chandler Bing: Ross, a sports car? Wouldn’t it have been cheaper to just stuff a sock down there?

罗斯，一辆跑车？在那里塞一只袜子不是更便宜吗？

Ross Geller: That’s not what this is about okay? I-I am a sports car enthusiast. I have always been into cars.

事情不是这样的好吗？我是一个跑车爱好者。我一直喜欢汽车。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, what’s the horsepower on this thing?

嘿，这东西的马力是多少？

Ross Geller: (giddy) I don’t know, but-but look how shiny!

（头晕）我不知道，但看看有多亮！

Monica Geller: I can’t believe you bought this.

真不敢相信你买了这个。

Rachel Green: Really! God Ross, what were you thinking? (To Phoebe, quietly) I know it’s really shallow, but a part of me wants him again.

真的！天哪，罗斯，你在想什么？（悄悄地对菲比说）我知道这很肤浅，但我的一部分又想要他。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, well get in line missy. (To Ross) So, can I have a ride stud?

哦，好吧，小姐。（对罗斯）那么，我能要一个骑行钉吗？

Ross Geller: Hop in. (Phoebe hops in.) Get ready for the smoothest ride of your life.

上车。（菲比跳了进来。）准备好迎接人生中最顺利的旅程。

(He starts the car and surprisingly in fires right up and comes to a nice idle. (Both can be rarities with British sports cars with their lovely Lucas ignition systems, which tend not to work especially in the rain.) Anyway, this being New York he is parallel parked on a street with the car in front of him only inches ahead of his bumper, likewise with the car behind him. He’s completely boxed in and can’t move more than two inches. He tries to get out several times by bumping the bumpers of both cars to no avail.)

（他启动了汽车，令人惊讶的是，他马上就着火了，然后很好地停了下来。（这两款车都是英国跑车的稀罕之物，因为英国跑车配备了可爱的卢卡斯点火系统，特别是在雨中，点火系统往往不起作用。）不管怎么说，在纽约，他平行停放在街道上，前面的车离保险杠只有几英寸远，后面的车也一样。他完全被困在车内，不能移动超过两英寸。他试图通过撞两辆车的保险杠几次逃出，但都没有用。）

Ross Geller: Damnit! (Shuts the car off.)

该死！（关闭汽车。）

Phoebe Buffay: (getting out) Okay, who’s next?

（出来）好的，下一个是谁？

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Phoebe is pouring Rachel some coffee.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，菲比正在给瑞秋倒咖啡。

Rachel Green: Y’know what? I am going to do something today. I’m not just gonna sit around like some old lady. I’m gonna get something pierced. Like my uh, like my nose or my tongue or something.

你知道吗？我今天要做点什么。我不会像个老太太那样坐着。我要穿点东西。比如我的鼻子舌头什么的。

Phoebe Buffay: Really?! ‘Cause y’know that hurts.

真的吗因为你知道那很痛。

Rachel Green: So what?! Y’know what? The way I see it—(Phoebe pulls out a hair from the back of her head)—Ow! Son of a bitch!!

那又怎样？！你知道吗？在我看来-（菲比从她的后脑勺拔出一根头发）-噢！狗娘养的！！

Tag: Look Rachel, I know what you’re going through. I’m totally freaked about turning 25.

听着，瑞秋，我知道你在经历什么。我快25岁了，真是吓坏了。

Rachel Green: (glares at him) Get out, get out of my apartment.

（怒视着他）滚出，滚出我的公寓。

Monica Geller: All right Rach, for what it’s worth, I think that you’re doing great. I mean y’know let’s face it, no one handles this well.

好吧，瑞秋，无论如何，我认为你做得很好。我是说，你知道，让我们面对现实吧，没人能处理好这件事。

Phoebe Buffay: Least of all you.

至少是你。

Tag: Why? What you’d do?

为什么？你会怎么做？

Monica Geller: Weren’t you asked to leave sonny?

你不是被要求离开桑尼吗？

[Flashback to Monica and Chandler’s apartment. Chandler has a bunch of people over in formal wear to give Monica a surprise birthday party. Joey is coming out of the bathroom and removing his tie.]

[倒叙莫妮卡和钱德勒的公寓。钱德勒有一群穿着正装的人过来给莫妮卡一个惊喜的生日派对。乔伊从浴室出来，脱下领带。]

Chandler Bing: (To Joey) Would you put that back on?! Monica’s gonna be here any minute!

（对乔伊）你能把它穿回去吗？！莫妮卡随时都会来！

Joey Tribbiani: But it hurt’s my Joey’s Apple.

但那是我乔伊的苹果。

Chandler Bing: (frustrated) Okay, for the last time. It’s not named for each individual man.

（沮丧地）好吧，最后一次。它不是为每个人命名的。

(Joey walks away and Mr. and Mrs. Geller walk up. Mr. Geller is wearing this ancient velvet tuxedo.)

（乔伊走开了，盖勒夫妇走了过来。盖勒先生穿着这件古老的天鹅绒晚礼服。）

Mrs. Geller: (To Chandler) You’ve done a wonderful job with this party Chandler. Everything looks so lovely.

（对钱德勒）你在这个聚会上做得很好，钱德勒。一切看起来都很可爱。

Chandler Bing: Oh well, not as lovely as you. I mean, I can’t believe that you would have a thirty-year-old daughter! (To Mr. Geller) And you! I can’t believe that you would have a tux that’s thirty years old! (Puts his hand on Mr. Geller’s shoulder.)

哦，没有你那么可爱。我是说，我真不敢相信你会有一个三十岁的女儿！（对盖勒先生）还有你！我真不敢相信你会穿三十岁的晚礼服！（把手放在盖勒先生的肩膀上。）

Mr. Geller: It’s older than that. Ross was actually conceived right near this tuxedo.

它比那还要古老。罗斯实际上就是在这件燕尾服附近怀上的。

Chandler Bing: Ohh! (He quickly removes his hand and looks at it.)

哦！（他迅速把手移开，看了看。）

Rachel Green: (entering) Hey! Everybody hide! Hide! I saw her! She’s coming!

（进来）嘿！大家都躲起来！隐藏我看见她了！她来了！

Chandler Bing: Okay! Okay! Everybody down! Everybody down! (Rachel turns off the lights and everyone crouches. As everyone crouches, a ripping noise erupts from the assemblage.)

好的！可以大家都趴下！大家都趴下！（瑞秋关了灯，大家都蹲下。当大家蹲下时，一种撕裂的声音从集合中爆发。）

Mr. Geller: Crap.

废话。

(We hear some fumbling at the door, then silence.)

（我们听到有人在门口摸索，然后是沉默。）

Chandler Bing: (getting up to investigate) Okay, everybody stay here. I will find out what’s going on.

（站起来调查）好的，大家呆在这里。我会查清楚到底发生了什么。

(He goes out into the hall and finds a very drunk Monica lying up against Joey and Rachel’s door.)

（他走出大厅，发现一个喝醉了的莫妮卡靠在乔伊和瑞秋的门上。）

Monica Geller: Heyyy!! You got the door open!! (Giggles.)

嘿！！你把门打开了！！（咯咯地笑。）

Chandler Bing: Hey-hey are you drunk?

嘿，嘿，你喝醉了吗？

Monica Geller: Nooo! (Giggles) Okay. (She tries to pull herself up by Rachel and Joey’s doorknob, but the door opens and she almost falls into the their apartment. She manages to catch herself.) Whoa! (Stands up, unsteadily) Okay. See I was, I was a little nervous about turning (whispering) thirty. (Giggles.) So the bus boys took me out for some drinks. (Pause) I wanna puke on you later!

不！（咯咯笑）好的。（她试图靠着瑞秋和乔伊的门把手把自己拉起来，但门开了，她差点掉进他们的公寓。她设法抓住了自己。）哇！（站起来，摇摇晃晃）好的。看到了吧，我有点紧张，快三十岁了。（咯咯笑）于是巴士司机带我出去喝了几杯。（停顿）我想晚一点吐在你身上！

Chandler Bing: Okay, here is the thing. We have thrown a very formal surprise party for you in there! All of your friends are in there and your parents!

好吧，事情是这样的。我们在那里为你举办了一个非常正式的惊喜派对！你所有的朋友和你的父母都在里面！

Monica Geller: Noo!!!

不！！！

Chandler Bing: Yes!

是的！

Monica Geller: Noo!!

不！！

Chandler Bing: Yes!!

是的！！

Monica Geller: Oh no! My parents have never seen me drunk! (Pause) That they know of.

哦，不！我父母从没见过我喝醉！（停顿）他们知道的。

Chandler Bing: Okay, here’s the thing. We’re gonna get you some coffee and they will never know that you’re drunk.

好吧，事情是这样的。我们给你弄点咖啡，他们永远不会知道你喝醉了。

Monica Geller: Really?! You promise?

真的吗？！你保证？

Chandler Bing: Yeah, I’ll take care of it.

是的，我会处理的。

Monica Geller: Okay. I love you so much. (Kisses him.)

好的。我非常爱你。（吻他。）

Chandler Bing: (laughing) Okay we have to do something about your breath.

（笑）好吧，我们得对你的呼吸做点什么。

Monica Geller: What about your breath?! (Breathes on him.)

你的呼吸呢？！（对着他呼吸。）

Chandler Bing: That’s still yours. Okay, now remember it’s a surprise party. So, when you go in, act surprised.

那还是你的。好的，现在记住这是一个惊喜派对。所以，当你进去的时候，表现得很惊讶。

Monica Geller: Okay. I can do that.

好的。我能做到。

Chandler Bing: Okay.

好的。

(Chandler opens the door and Monica sneaks up on it. They go inside.)

（钱德勒打开门，莫妮卡偷偷走了上去。他们走了进去。）

All: Surprise!!!

惊喜！！！

(Monica screams and they all stare at her.)

（莫妮卡尖叫着，他们都盯着她看。）

Commercial Break

商业休息

🎬 Ross’s birthday, Joey is now trying to get his car out while Ross is directing him.

🎬 罗斯的生日，乔伊正试图把车开出来，而罗斯正在指挥他。

Ross Geller: Okay, forward. Forward—Stop! (The car moves an inch and Ross runs to the back of the car.) Okay, back—Stop! (The car barely moves and Ross runs back to the front.) Okay, forward—Stop! Stop! Stop!

好的，向前。前进停止！（汽车移动了一英寸，罗斯跑到汽车后部。）好的，后退！（汽车几乎一动不动，罗斯跑回车头。）好的，向前停下！停止停止

Monica Geller: Ross, just forget about it. This guy’s got you totally wedged in.

罗斯，算了吧。这家伙让你完全陷入困境。

(A beautiful woman approaches.)

（一位美女走近。）

Woman: (To Joey) Is this yours?

（对乔伊）这是你的吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Well actually…

事实上…

Ross Geller: No-no-no! It’s mine! It’s-it’s mine. (The woman walks away.)

不，不！是我的！是我的。（女人走开了。）

Joey Tribbiani: Dude, you soooo need this car.

老兄，你太需要这辆车了。

Phoebe Buffay: (running up) Okay. Okay, here’s what we’re gonna do. Okay, I’m gonna break into this mini-van and put it in neutral. You guys push it forward so Ross can drive out of his spot. Okay? All right, here we go. (She opens her coat and reveals that thing car thieves use to break into cars as Ross jumps in behind the wheel.
