The One With The Engagement Picture

Teleplay by: Patty Lin

林佩蒂

Story by: Earl Davis

厄尔·戴维斯

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

🎬 Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's, Monica and Phoebe are going through a bunch of pictures as Chandler enters.

🎬 莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比的，莫妮卡和菲比正在看一堆照片，钱德勒进来了。

Chandler Bing: Hey.

嘿。

Monica Geller: What’s the matter?

怎么了？

Chandler Bing: Someone on the subway licked my neck! Licked my neck!!

地铁上有人舔了我的脖子！舔我的脖子！！

Phoebe Buffay: Oh Willie’s still alive!

哦，威利还活着！

Chandler Bing: What are you guys doing?

你们在干什么？

Monica Geller: Oh, my mom called, they’re gonna run our engagement announcement in the local paper, so we’re looking for a good picture of us.

哦，我妈妈打电话来了，他们要在当地报纸上刊登我们的订婚公告，所以我们要找一张我们的好照片。

Chandler Bing: Oooh, I’m afraid that does not exist.

哦，恐怕那不存在。

Monica Geller: That’s not true, there are great pictures of us!

那不是真的，有我们的好照片！

Chandler Bing: No, there are great pictures of you standing next to a guy who’s going like this… (Makes what can only be described as a toothy frown. Henceforth, this shall be known as The Face.)

不，有很多很好的照片，你站在一个像这样的家伙旁边……（这张脸只能用牙齿皱眉来形容。从今以后，这张脸将被称为脸。）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh my God! That’s the creep that you’re with at the Statue of Liberty.

哦，我的天啊！那是你在自由女神像的怪物。

Chandler Bing: I don’t know what it is, I just can’t take a good picture.

我不知道那是什么，我就是拍不好照片。

Monica Geller: (looking at one) Oh, here’s a great one.

（看着一个）哦，这是一个很棒的。

Chandler Bing: Yeah, I’m not in that.

是的，我不在里面。

Monica Geller: I know, but look at me all tan.

我知道，但是看着我晒成褐色。

Phoebe Buffay: Hey, why don’t you guys go, get portraits done by a professional photographer.

嘿，你们为什么不去，让专业摄影师拍肖像呢。

Monica Geller: That’s a good idea! I bet they have one of those wind machines! Y’know… (Does the whole hair blowing in the wind model type poses.)

好主意！我打赌他们有一台风力发电机！你知道…（风中吹动的整根头发都是模特式的姿势。）

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah that’s great! Next to that, Chandler won’t look so stupid.

是啊，太棒了！除此之外，钱德勒看起来不会那么愚蠢。

Monica Geller: Chandler what do you say?

钱德勒，你说呢？

Chandler Bing: All right, but I should warn you, I’m not going. I’m going. (Does The Face while saying that last part.)

好吧，但我要警告你，我不去。我要走了。（说出最后一部分时，脸上没有表情。）

(They both get dejected and go sit down.)

嘿，离婚的男人不是坏人！

Chandler Bing: They have that on the napkins at the club.

俱乐部的餐巾纸上有。

Rachel Green: Oh, I gotta get back to work.

哦，我得回去工作了。

Phoebe Buffay: You don’t have to be back for a half-hour!

半小时之内你不必回来！

Rachel Green: Yeah but, my assistant Tag does sit-ups in the office during lunch. Ohh! I could just spread him on a cracker.

是的，但是，我的助手泰格午餐时在办公室做仰卧起坐。哦！我可以把他放在饼干上。

Chandler Bing: Rach, if you have a crush on this guy, why would you hire him? I mean y’know you can’t date him right?

瑞秋，如果你喜欢这个家伙，你为什么要雇用他？我是说你知道你不能和他约会对吧？

Rachel Green: Oh no, I know that. I know that. Although, we made a joke that we spend so much time together he should call me his work wife.

哦，不，我知道。我知道。尽管如此，我们开了个玩笑，说我们在一起的时间太长了，他应该称我为他的工作妻子。

Ross Geller: Soon he’ll be able to call you, that lady he knew who got fired.

很快他就能给你打电话了，他认识的那个被解雇的女士。

Rachel Green: I am not gonna get fired, because I’m not gonna act on it.

我不会被炒鱿鱼，因为我不会采取行动。

Phoebe Buffay: So you wouldn’t mind if he was dating someone else?

那你不介意他和别人约会吗？

Rachel Green: Why? Is he? He is! Isn’t he? He’s dating that slut in marketing!

为什么？是吗？他是！他不是吗？他在和市场部的那个荡妇约会！

Ross Geller: Maybe I should open a divorced men’s club.

也许我应该开个离婚男人俱乐部。

Chandler Bing: Dude that is so sad.

老兄，这太可悲了。

Ross Geller: I could put uh-uh a basketball court in the back.

我可以在后面放个篮球场。

Chandler Bing: Could I play?

我能玩吗？

🎬 Rachel’s Outer Office, she’s returning from lunch to see Tag not doing his sit-ups.

🎬 瑞秋的外办公室，她午饭后回来看到泰格没有做仰卧起坐。

Rachel Green: Oh, no sit-ups today Tag?

哦，今天没有仰卧起坐，标签？

Tag: I just did them.

我刚做了。

Rachel Green: Oh, well drop and give me ten more!

哦，好吧，停下来再给我十个！

Tag: What?

什么？

Rachel Green: Uh, I-I had a drink with lunch. Did those cost reports come in?

呃，我午饭的时候喝了一杯。那些成本报告收到了吗？

Tag: Yeah, I filled them out last night?

是的，我昨晚填的？

Rachel Green: Oh, great could you make me four copies of those?

哦，太好了。你能给我复印四份吗？

(He gets up to make the copies leaving Rachel alone with his stuff. She notices his sweater in his backpack and holds it up to her nose as Melissa, a coworker, walks up.)

（他站起来复印，留下瑞秋一个人带着他的东西。她注意到他的背包里有他的毛衣，把它举到鼻子前，同事梅丽莎走了过来。）

Melissa: Hey Rachel!

嘿，瑞秋！

Rachel Green: (startled) Ahh, hi! Hi! Melissa, what’s up? I’m just uh, about to umm, go out to the store to get some stuff to put in my backpack. Y’know, like dried fruit and granola and stuff. What’s up? (She has put on the backpack.)

（吃惊地）啊，嗨！你好梅丽莎，怎么了？我正要去商店买些东西放在我的背包里。你知道，像干果和格兰诺拉麦片之类的。怎么了？（她已经背上了背包。）

Melissa: Umm, is Tag here?

嗯，标签在这儿吗？

Rachel Green: No. Why?

没有，为什么？

Melissa: Oh, I was gonna talk to him about doing something tonight.

哦，我本来想和他谈谈今晚做点什么的。

Rachel Green: Really?! Got a little crush on Tag there do ya?

真的吗？！对Tag有点迷恋是吗？

Melissa: Well, we’ve been flirting back and forth, but I was hoping that tonight it would turn into something a little more than that.

好吧，我们一直在调情，但我希望今晚的调情会比这更进一步。

Rachel Green: Okay, whoa-whoa easy there Melissa! This ain’t a locker room, okay? But, y’know I remember him saying that-that he had plans tonight.

好吧，哇，哇，别紧张，梅丽莎！这不是更衣室，好吗？但是，你知道我记得他说过他今晚有计划。

Melissa: Oh no!

哦，不！

Rachel Green: Oh yeah. All right, back to work.

哦，是的。好了，回去工作。

Melissa: Hey! Isn’t that Tag’s backpack.

嘿！那不是Tag的背包吗。

Rachel Green: Yeah Melissa, I don’t want to be known as the uh, office bitch, but I will call your supervisor.

是的，梅丽莎，我不想被称为办公室婊子，但我会打电话给你的主管。

(Melissa beats a hasty retreat.)

（梅丽莎急忙退却了。）

🎬 A Portrait Studio, Chandler and Monica are trying to take their engagement picture. Monica has a beautiful smile, while Chandler isn’t.

🎬 在一家肖像工作室，钱德勒和莫妮卡正试图拍订婚照。莫妮卡有一个美丽的微笑，而钱德勒没有。

The Photographer: (taking pictures) Great! That’s great Monica! Great! Now, Chandler, you want to give us a smile?

（拍照）太棒了！太好了，莫妮卡！伟大的钱德勒，你想给我们一个微笑吗？

Chandler Bing: Okay. (Does The Face.)

好的。（脸上没有表情。）

钱德勒，听我说，亲爱的，我知道你能行。可以你有一个美丽的微笑。

是吗？（他笑得很美。）

是啊！（他们转向摄像机，钱德勒又做了个鬼脸。）好吧，也许你不必笑。我们试试别的吧。让我们试试，嗯，试着看起来性感。

Chandler Bing: Okay. (You’ll have to see it, I can’t describe the face he makes, but it isn’t good.)

好的。（你得看看，我无法描述他脸上的表情，但这并不好。）

Rachel Green: Hi Joey! What are you doing here?

嗨，乔伊！你在这里干什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Uhh, well I’ve got an audition down the street and I spilled sauce all over the front of my shirt. (Removes his hand to reveal a huge sauce stain.) You got an extra one?

嗯，我在街上有个试镜，我把酱汁洒了我衬衫的前面。（移开他的手，露出一个巨大的酱汁污渍）你还有一个吗？

是的，当然。嗯……这里。（递给他一个。）

好吧，别紧张。我去。

好吧，我知道。等等（她走向沙发）嘿，罗斯？

是吗？

什么？！不

好吧！我要回我的钥匙！

我没有！

就在那儿！（指着她的手。）

哦，好吧，夏洛克！（把钥匙递过来。）
