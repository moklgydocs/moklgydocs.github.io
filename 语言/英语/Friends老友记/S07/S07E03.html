The One With Phoebe’s Cookies

拿菲比饼干的那个

Written by: Sherry Bilsing & Ellen Plummer

作者：雪莉·比尔辛和艾伦·普卢默

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Central Perk, Rachel, Phoebe, Joey, Ross, and Monica are all there as Chandler enters wearing glasses.

🎬 中央公园、瑞秋、菲比、乔伊、罗斯和莫妮卡都在，钱德勒戴着眼镜进来。

Chandler Bing: Hey, you guys!

嘿，你们这些家伙！

Ross and **Rachel Green: **Hey!

罗斯和瑞秋·格林：嘿！

Chandler Bing: So, what do you think?

那么，你觉得呢？

Ross Geller: About what?

关于什么？

Rachel Green: Yeah, what?

是啊，什么？

Joey Tribbiani: What?

什么？

Chandler Bing: Are you kidding? Okay, I’ll give you a hint; I’ll give you a hint. (Points to his glasses.)

你在开玩笑吗？好的，我给你一个提示；我给你一个提示。（指着他的眼镜。）

Joey Tribbiani: Eyes! No, no. Your eyes! No. Chandler’s eyes!

眼睛！不，不，你的眼睛！不，钱德勒的眼睛！

Chandler Bing: I got glasses!

我有眼镜！

Ross Geller: Well, you-you’ve always had glasses.

嗯，你一直戴着眼镜。

Chandler Bing: No I didn’t!

不，我没有！

Ross Geller: Are you sure?

你确定吗？

Rachel Green: Yeah-yeah, did-didn’t you use to have a pair? They were really round, burgundy, and they made you look kind of umm…

是的，你以前不是有一双吗？他们真的很圆，勃艮第，他们让你看起来有点嗯…

Joey Tribbiani: Feminine.

女性化。

Rachel Green: Yes!

是的！

Chandler Bing: No!

不！

Monica Geller: Sweetie, I think the glasses look great. They make you look really sexy.

亲爱的，我觉得这副眼镜很好看。它们让你看起来很性感。

Chandler Bing: Really?

真的吗？

Monica Geller: Yeah!

是啊！

Chandler Bing: (sitting down on the arm of her chair) You didn’t think I used to wear glasses, right?

（坐在她的椅子扶手上）你以为我以前不戴眼镜，对吗？

Monica Geller: Of course! (She mouths, "I have no idea," to the rest of the gang.)

当然！（她对其他人说：“我不知道。”

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's, Rachel, Phoebe, Chandler, and Monica are making some sandwiches.

🎬 莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比、瑞秋、菲比、钱德勒和莫妮卡正在做三明治。

Phoebe Buffay: So what do you guys want for an engagement present?

你们想要什么订婚礼物？

Chandler Bing: That’s okay Pheebs, we’re not having a party or anything, so you don’t have to get us…

没关系，菲比，我们没有聚会什么的，所以你不必让我们…

Monica Geller: (interrupts him) If someone wants to give us a present, we don’t want to deprive them of that joy.

（打断他）如果有人想送我们礼物，我们不想剥夺他们的快乐。

哦，你知道你应该买什么吗？一个小型的便携式CD播放器。

Monica Geller: Oh, I already have one.

哦，我已经有一个了。

Phoebe Buffay: Not unless someone borrowed it and left it at the gynecologist.

除非有人借了它并把它留在妇科医生那里。

Rachel Green: Yeah, and-and-and by someone, she means Joey.

是的，她指的是乔伊。

Monica Geller: Hey, I know I what I want!

嘿，我知道我想要什么！

Chandler Bing: What we want honey.

我们想要什么，亲爱的。

Monica Geller: No, you don’t want this. I want to have your grandmother’s cookie recipe.

不，你不要这个。我想要你祖母的饼干食谱。

Phoebe Buffay: You mean the chocolate chip cookie recipe?

你是说巧克力饼干的配方？

Monica Geller: Uh-huh, yeah.

嗯，是的。

Phoebe Buffay: You mean the one that my grandmother made me swear on her deathbed that I would never let out of our family?

你是说我祖母让我在临终前发誓我永远不会让我们家的人出去的那个？

Chandler Bing: Dying people say the craziest things.

垂死的人说的是最疯狂的话。

Monica Geller: I wanted it for years! I was gonna make cookies for my children.

我想要它好几年了！我要给我的孩子们做饼干。

Phoebe Buffay: Break my heart—Oh, all right.

让我心碎哦，好吧。

Monica Geller: Okay. I’m gonna be the mom that makes the world’s best chocolate chip cookies.

好的。我要做世界上最好的巧克力饼干的妈妈。

Chandler Bing: Our kids are gonna be fat aren’t they.

我们的孩子会胖的，不是吗。

Joey Tribbiani: (entering) Ahoy!

（进来）喂！

Chandler Bing: Hey! How’s the boat?!

嘿！船怎么样了？！

Joey Tribbiani: Great! I’m finally getting into this sailing stuff.

太好了！我终于开始从事帆船运动了。

Monica Geller: Oh, so you finally took it out of the marina huh?

哦，你终于把它从码头拿出来了，是吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Why would I do that? It took three guys to get the thing in there!

我为什么要这么做？三个人才把东西弄进去！

Phoebe Buffay: If you don’t sail your boat, what do you do on it?

如果你不驾驶你的船，你会在船上做什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Oh, it’s great! It’s a great place to just kinda, sit, hang around, drink a few beers, eat some chips. (He says that as he sits, hangs around, drinks a beer, and eats a chip.)

哦，太棒了！这是一个坐下来，闲逛，喝几杯啤酒，吃些薯条的好地方。（他一边坐着，一边闲逛，一边喝啤酒，一边吃薯片。）

Chandler Bing: Well, it’s good that you finally have a place to do that.

很好，你终于有地方这么做了。

Rachel Green: Y’know Joey, I could teach you to sail if you want.

你知道乔伊，如果你愿意，我可以教你航海。

Joey Tribbiani: You could?

你可以吗？

Rachel Green: Yeah! I’ve been sailing my whole life. When I was fifteen my dad bought me my own boat.

是啊！我一生都在航海。我十五岁时，爸爸给我买了自己的船。

Phoebe Buffay: Your own boat?

你自己的船？

Rachel Green: What?! What?! He was trying to cheer me up! My pony was sick.

什么？！什么？！他想让我高兴起来！我的小马病了。

🎬 Central Perk, Chandler and Monica are there. He takes off his glasses and starts chewing on the ear piece.

🎬 中央公园、钱德勒和莫妮卡在那里。他摘下眼镜，开始嚼耳塞。

Chandler Bing: Do you know what I was thinkin’?

你知道我在想什么吗？

Monica Geller: What?

什么？

Chandler Bing: Nothing, I just like to go like this. (Does it again.)

没什么，我只是喜欢这样。（再来一次。）

Ross Geller: (entering from the bathrooms) Hey Chandler, what are you doing tonight?

（从浴室进来）嘿，钱德勒，你今晚干什么？

Chandler Bing: Uh why, do you have a lecture?

嗯，为什么，你有讲座吗？

Ross Geller: No, why?

没有，为什么？

Chandler Bing: Then free as a bird. What’s up?

然后像鸟一样自由。怎么了？

Ross Geller: My dad wanted to know if you wanted to play racquetball with us.

我爸爸想知道你是否想和我们一起打壁球。

Monica Geller: Wow! That’s great! Dad must really like you, he doesn’t ask just anyone to play.

哇！太好了！爸爸一定很喜欢你，他不要求任何人玩。

Ross Geller: Yeah and he didn’t really ask for you, he asked for Chancy, I assumed he meant you.

是的，他不是真的要找你，他要的是钱西，我想他是指你。

Chandler Bing: Well, did-did you correct him?

嗯，你纠正他了吗？

Ross Geller: No, I-I thought it would be more fun this way.

不，我以为这样会更有趣。

Monica Geller: This is so cool, maybe this is something you can do every week.

这太酷了，也许这是你每周都可以做的事情。

Ross Geller: Or you can sit with him on the front porch and make sure no one steals the trash cans. He does that every week too.

或者你可以和他坐在前廊，确保没有人偷垃圾桶。他每周也这样做。

Monica Geller: Oh, just so you know, you-you have to let him win.

哦，你要知道，你必须让他赢。

Ross Geller: Yeah.

是的。

Monica Geller: He hates to lose.

他讨厌输。

Chandler Bing: Oh no problem, maybe I’ll play with my left hand.

哦，没问题，也许我会用左手玩。

Ross Geller: You’re not a lefty?

你不是左撇子？

Chandler Bing: Does anybody know me?!

有人认识我吗？！

(Phoebe enters, walks up to Monica, and exhales exasperatedly.)

（菲比进来，走向莫妮卡，恼怒地呼气。）

Monica Geller: What’s wrong Phoebe?

菲比怎么了？

Phoebe Buffay: I just went to my old apartment to get you the-the cookie recipe and the stupid fire burned it up!

我刚去我的旧公寓给你拿饼干食谱，那愚蠢的火把它烧了！

Monica Geller: No!! Why didn’t you make a copy and-and keep it in a fireproof box and keep it at least a hundred yards from the original?!!

不！！你为什么不复印一份，放在防火的盒子里，离原件至少一百码远？！！

Phoebe Buffay: (pauses as he figures out how to answer that) Because I’m normal! That was the one legacy my grandmother left me, and I know you wanted it as an engagement present.

（他停下来想怎么回答）因为我很正常！那是我祖母留给我的一笔遗产，我知道你想把它作为订婚礼物。

Ross Geller: Oh, we have to get you an engagement present?

哦，我们得给你一份订婚礼物？

Chandler Bing: Don’t worry about it Pheebs.

别担心，菲比。

Ross Geller: No one got me an engagement present.

没有人给我订婚礼物。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, here I wish you health and happiness. (She hands Monica a cookie in a plastic baggie.)

好的，在这里我祝你健康快乐。（她递给莫妮卡一个塑料袋里的饼干。）

Chandler Bing: An old cookie?

一块旧饼干？

Monica Geller: (To Chandler) This is what happens when you don’t register for gifts!

（对钱德勒）这就是当你不注册礼物时会发生的事情！

Phoebe Buffay: See no-no, I made a batch and I froze it, and this is the only one left.

看，不，不，我做了一批，我把它冷冻了，这是唯一剩下的一批。

Chandler Bing: We can’t accept this.

我们不能接受这个。

Phoebe Buffay: Why not?

为什么不呢？

Chandler Bing: ‘Cause it’s gross.

因为太恶心了。

Monica Geller: No! Wait! I think I can figure out the recipe from this cookie! I do stuff like this at work all the time.

不！等待我想我能从这个曲奇里猜出食谱！我总是在工作中做这样的事情。

Phoebe Buffay: Really?!

真的吗？！

Monica Geller: Yeah! I bet I can do it.

是啊！我打赌我能做到。

(Chandler looks over and sees Ross glaring at them.)

（钱德勒看了看，看到罗斯怒视着他们。）

Chandler Bing: Okay, we owe you a present.

好吧，我们欠你一份礼物。

Ross Geller: Two! I’ve been engaged twice!

两个！我已经订婚两次了！

🎬 The Mr. Bowmont (Joey’s boat), she’s shown to be in one of the waterways around New York, but in reality she’s in a sound stage on the Warner Bros. lot and we see New York from the water in some rather poor green screen shots. Rachel and Joey are on board.

🎬 鲍蒙特先生（乔伊的船），她在纽约附近的一条水道上，但事实上她在华纳兄弟公司的一个舞台上。我们在一些相当糟糕的绿色屏幕截图中从水中看到纽约。瑞秋和乔伊在船上。

Joey Tribbiani: (drinking a beer) Look at this clown! Just because he’s got a bigger boat he thinks he can take up the whole river. (Yelling) Get out of the way jackass! (To Rachel) Who names his boat Coast Guard anyway?

（喝啤酒）看这个小丑！就因为他有一条更大的船，他认为他可以把整条河都吃光。（大叫）滚开，蠢货！（对瑞秋）谁给他的船起名叫海岸警卫队？

那是海岸警卫队。

他们在外面干什么？海岸就在那边。（指向海岸，同时他身后有海岸。）

好的。

好吧，我再复习一遍基本要点，你准备好了吗？

哦，好的。这就是你想做的吗？你想过去对那些又老又辣的小妞喊一声吗？好吧，让我们来做水手乔。快速提问，（抓住一些索具）这叫什么？

嗯，船绳。

错了！你怎么把主帆拉上去？

嗯，擦一下？

Rachel Green: No. What do you do if I say we are coming about?

不。如果我说我们要来，你怎么办？

Joey Tribbiani: I’d say, come again. No-no, wait I-I-I know this one, I know this one, uh…

我想说，再来一次。不不，等等，我知道这个，我知道这个，呃…

(Rachel blasts an air horn in his ear.)

（瑞秋对着他的耳朵吹了一个空气喇叭。）

时间到了，现在你死了。

又聋！

好吧，你继续开你的玩笑吧，但是如果你不知道你在海上干什么，你会死在海上的。我能和你接通吗，水手？！（她用耳光拍他的额头来给每个单词加标点符号。）

是的。

别光说是！这不是游戏，乔伊，你真的会受伤的。好吧，那你是想集中注意力还是想死？！

Joey Tribbiani: I want to make a ship to shore call to Chandler.

我想打个电话给钱德勒。

我不想谈这个。你知道，你至少可以帮我省下一整块饼干。（他抓起饼干剩下的东西，砰的一声塞进嘴里。）

我真不敢相信！现在我祖母留给我的唯一遗产就是这个面包屑。（她用手指把它捡起来，递给莫妮卡。）祝你婚姻长久幸福。

（和钱德勒一起进来）嘿。

Monica Geller: Hey! How was it?

嘿！怎么样？

Ross Geller: Well I had a great time! Umm, Chancy on the other hand…

我玩得很开心！嗯，另一方面，Chancy…

Chandler Bing: I will tell the story! It was going great. I let him win. We were bonding. He even said I could call him dad.

我会讲故事的！一切都很顺利。我让他赢了。我们在一起。他甚至说我可以叫他爸爸。

Ross Geller: And what did he ask you not to call him?

他叫你不要给他打电话是什么意思？

Chandler Bing: Daddy. All right look, here’s the story. (Flashback to Chandler about to enter the steam room as he does the voice-over.) Well, we had just finished playing racquetball and we were gonna take a steam. I walk into the steam room and it was really steamy. (The flashback shows his glasses fog up and him trying to find his way around the steam filled room. He takes off his glasses.) So I take off my glasses and that’s when in happened.

爸爸。好吧，听着，故事是这样的。（倒叙到钱德勒即将进入蒸汽室，他正在做配音。）嗯，我们刚刚打完壁球，我们要去蒸汽室。我走进蒸汽室，里面真的有蒸汽。（倒叙显示他的眼镜模糊了，他试图在充满蒸汽的房间里找到自己的路。他摘下了眼镜。）所以我摘下了眼镜，这就是发生的事情。

[Cut to the flashback, Chandler’s no longer doing the voice-over.]

[转到倒叙，钱德勒不再做画外音了。]

Chandler Bing: Guys?

伙计们？

Ross Geller: Over here. (You can see Ross sitting at the far wall.)

在这里。（你可以看到罗斯坐在远处的墙上。）

Mr. Geller: Have a seat son. (You can see Mr. Geller sitting closer to the door as Chandler walks over and sits in his lap.) Hey!! (Chandler quickly jumps off and sits next to him with a shocked expression on his face.)

请坐，儿子。（你可以看到盖勒先生坐得离门更近，钱德勒走过来坐在他的膝盖上。）嘿！！（钱德勒迅速跳了下来，坐在他旁边，脸上带着震惊的表情。）
