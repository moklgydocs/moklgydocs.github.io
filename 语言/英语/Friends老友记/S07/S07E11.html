The One With All The Cheesecakes

那个有所有芝士蛋糕的

Written by: Shana Goldberg-Meehan

作者：沙娜·戈德伯格·米汉

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

With Help from: Aaron Howard-Miller

亚伦·霍华德·米勒

🎬 Chandler and Monica’s, Chandler is sitting at the kitchen table eating cheesecake. The box it came in is also on the table.

🎬 钱德勒和莫妮卡家，钱德勒正坐在厨房桌子旁吃奶酪蛋糕。它进来的盒子也在桌子上。

Chandler Bing: Ohh. Mmm.

哦。嗯。

Rachel Green: (Comes in the front door and walks towards the kitchen.) Hi.

（从前门进来，朝厨房走去。）嗨。

Chandler Bing: Hey, you have got to try this cheesecake.

嘿，你得尝尝这个芝士蛋糕。

Rachel Green: Oh, y’know I'm not that much of a sweet tooth. I—(Chandler puts a forkful of the cheesecake in her mouth.)—Wow. My God, so creamy. Oh my God, this is the best cheesecake I have ever had. Where did you get this? (She reaches over to look at the label on the box.)

哦，你知道我不太喜欢吃甜食。我-（钱德勒把一叉奶酪蛋糕放进她的嘴里。）-哇。我的天啊，好滑啊。天哪，这是我吃过的最好的奶酪蛋糕。你从哪儿弄来的？（她伸手看了看盒子上的标签。）

Chandler Bing: (nervously) It was at the front door. When I got home. Somebody sent it to us.

（紧张地）在前门。当我到家的时候。有人寄给我们的。

Rachel Green: Chandler, this is not addressed to you. This is addressed to Mrs. Braverman downstairs. (Gasping) Thief.

钱德勒，这不是写给你的。这是写给楼下的Braverman太太的。（喘息）小偷。

Chandler Bing: I—no! I didn't read the box before I opened it. And you can't return a box after you've opened the box.

不！我打开盒子之前没有读过。而且你不能在打开箱子后还箱子。

Rachel Green: Why, why not?

为什么，为什么不呢？

Chandler Bing: Because it's too delicious.

因为太好吃了。

Rachel Green: Chandler, you stole this cheesecake. That is wrong.

钱德勒，你偷了这个芝士蛋糕。这是错误的。

Chandler Bing: No-no-no! It is going to be okay, because Mrs. Braverman is gonna send away for a free one and that way we all win! The only losers are the big cheesecake conglomerate, (Reading the label) Momma’s Little Bakery. (Pause) I feel terrible, I’m a horrible, horrible, horrible person.

不，不！一切都会好起来的，因为Braverman太太要送走一个免费的，这样我们都赢了！唯一的输家是大芝士蛋糕集团（阅读标签）妈妈的小面包店。（停顿）我感觉很糟糕，我是一个可怕、可怕、可怕的人。

Rachel Green: (taking a bite) Oh, I’m sorry what?

（咬了一口）哦，对不起什么？

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Central Perk, everyone except Rachel is there as Joey gets up and starts putting on his coat.

🎬 中央公园，乔伊起床开始穿外套时，除了瑞秋，其他人都在那里。

Joey Tribbiani: All right, I should get going, big day a work. Y’know I’m in a coma? Today, they do this test on me and it turns out I’m not brain dead.

好吧，我该走了，今天是重要的一天。你知道我昏迷了吗？今天，他们在我身上做了这个测试，结果证明我并没有脑死亡。

Chandler Bing: So…

那么…

Joey Tribbiani: Ah-ah-ah Mr. Smartie Pants, it’s just not my character that’s not brain dead. Hey, so Pheebs, we still on for tonight?

啊，Smartie先生，不是我的角色没有脑死亡。嘿，菲比，我们今晚还有约会吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Absolutely!

当然！

Joey Tribbiani: I’ll see you at 8:00.

8点见。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay. (Joey exits.)

好的。（乔伊走了。）

Chandler Bing: Oh, what’s at 8:00?

哦，8点有什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, I have dinner plans with Joey. We get together about once a month to discuss the rest of you guys.

哦，我和乔伊有晚餐计划。我们大约每个月聚一次，讨论你们其他人。

Ross Geller: Wow, did not know that! May I say how lovely you look today?

哇，我不知道！我可以说你今天看起来多可爱吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Duly noted.

注意到了。

Ross Geller: Thanks. (Phoebe gets up to get a refill.) (To Monica) Oh! So for tomorrow, do you want to rent a car and drive down together or what?

谢谢。（菲比站起来要续杯。）（对莫妮卡）哦！那么明天，你想租辆车一起开车去吗？

Monica Geller: What are you talking about?

你在说什么？

Ross Geller: Cousin Frannie’s wedding, its tomorrow night.

弗兰尼表哥的婚礼，明晚举行。

Monica Geller: You were invited?!

你被邀请了？！

Ross Geller: No.

没有。

Monica Geller: My God, I can’t believe this! I mean I knew that mom and dad were invited, but I thought that was it! I mean from the ages 7 to 9 Frannie and I were inseparable!

天哪，我真不敢相信！我的意思是我知道爸爸妈妈被邀请了，但我想就是这样！我是说，从7岁到9岁，弗兰妮和我形影不离！

Chandler Bing: Well, maybe since the age of 9, Frannie’s made some new friends.

嗯，也许从9岁起，弗兰妮交了一些新朋友。

Ross Geller: Well l-look okay, it’s probably just a mistake. Let me call Aunt Sheryl okay? Maybe you are invited and the invitation just got lost in the mail.

好吧，听着，这可能只是个错误。让我给谢丽尔阿姨打电话好吗？也许你被邀请了，但邀请信在邮件中丢失了。

Monica Geller: Yeah, well you call her and tell her that y’know when we were kids her precious little Frannie tried to undress me several times, okay? And if I hadn’t have stopped her, there probably wouldn’t even be a wedding to go too.

是啊，你打电话给她，告诉她你知道，当我们还是孩子的时候，她可爱的小弗朗尼好几次想帮我脱衣服，好吗？如果我没有阻止她，可能连婚礼都不会举行了。

Ross Geller: Y’know, she tried to undress me too.

你知道，她也试着给我脱衣服。

Chandler Bing: I used to undress my cousin Glenn. (Monica looks at him then sushes him.)

我过去常给我表妹格伦脱衣服。（莫妮卡看了他一眼，然后拍了拍他。）

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Monica, Chandler, Rachel, and Ross are playing Monopoly as Phoebe enters angrily.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋、莫妮卡、钱德勒、瑞秋和罗斯在扮演大富翁，菲比愤怒地走进来。

Phoebe Buffay: Joseph Francis Tribbiani are you home yet?!!

约瑟夫·弗朗西斯·特里比亚尼你回家了吗？！！

Rachel Green: Umm, I think he’s still out. What’s wrong?

嗯，我想他还没回来。发生了什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, I’ll tell you Rachel Karen Green, I had plans with Joey tonight and he left me this note. (Hands it to Rachel.)

好吧，我告诉你瑞秋·凯伦·格林，我今晚和乔伊有安排，他给我留了这张纸条。（把它交给瑞秋。）

Rachel Green: (reading the note) "Pheebs, can’t make it, got a date. Talk to you later. Big Daddy." (Laughs) Big Daddy?

（读纸条）“菲比，不行了，有个约会。待会儿再跟你谈。大爸爸。”（笑）大爸爸？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh that’s a nickname we were trying out.

哦，那是我们试着取的绰号。

Ross Geller: Hey, y’know what nickname never caught on? The Ross-A-Tron! (Monica shakes her head in disgust.)

嘿，你知道什么绰号从来没流行过吗？罗斯-阿-特隆！（莫妮卡厌恶地摇摇头。）

Joey Tribbiani: (entering) Hey!

（进来）嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Oh! Here’s Joseph Francis!

哦！这是约瑟夫·弗朗西斯！

Joey Tribbiani: Oh-Wha-Ho! What are you middle naming me for?! I left you a note!

哦，哇哦！你叫我什么名字？！我给你留了张便条！

Phoebe Buffay: So what?! That doesn’t give you the right to ditch me!

那又怎样？！你没有权利抛弃我！

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, you can cancel plans with friends if there is the possibility for sex!

嘿，如果有可能发生性关系的话，你可以取消和朋友的计划！

Ross Geller: Phoebe he’s right, that is the rule.

菲比，他是对的，这是规则。

Phoebe Buffay: I don’t accept this rule. When me make plans, I expect you to show up. Okay, I can’t just be a way to kill time ‘til you meet someone better! Y’know boyfriends and girlfriends come and go, but this (Motions that their friendship) is for life!

我不接受这个规则。当我制定计划时，我希望你能出现。好吧，在你遇到更好的人之前，我不能只是一种消磨时间的方式！你知道男朋友和女朋友来来往往，但这（表明他们的友谊）是终身的！

Joey Tribbiani: Wow! I’m so sorry; I had no idea it would bother you this much.

哇！我很抱歉；我不知道这会让你这么烦恼。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, it does.

是的。

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, can I-can I make it up to you? Huh? I’m sorry. (They hug.) How about uh, dinner tomorrow night?! I’ll pay for myself!

好吧，我能补偿你吗？嗯？我很抱歉。（他们拥抱）明天晚上吃晚饭怎么样？！我会自己付钱的！

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, you wore me down.

好吧，你把我累坏了。

Ross Geller: Hey Joe, while you’re over there how about another beer for the Ross-A-Tron?

嘿，乔，当你在那里的时候，再来一杯罗斯-阿-特隆啤酒怎么样？

Joey Tribbiani: The Ross—Is that back?!! (Chandler motions no.)

那个罗斯回来了？！！（钱德勒动议编号）

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Rachel enters to find Chandler staring at another cheesecake box.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，瑞秋进来发现钱德勒盯着另一个芝士蛋糕盒。

Rachel Green: Hi!

嗨！

Chandler Bing: Another cheesecake came! They delivered it to the wrong address again!

又来了一块芝士蛋糕！他们又把它送到错误的地址了！

Rachel Green: So just bring it back downstairs, what’s the problem?

那就把它带下楼去，有什么问题吗？

Chandler Bing: I can’t seem to say goodbye.

我好像不能说再见。

Rachel Green: Are you serious?! Chandler, we ate an entire cheesecake two days ago and you want more?

你是认真的吗？！钱德勒，两天前我们吃了一整块芝士蛋糕，你还要吗？

Chandler Bing: Well I’ve forgotten what it tastes like okay?!

我忘了它是什么味道了，好吗？！

Rachel Green: It was cheesecake. It was fine. It had a buttery, crumbly, graham cracker crust, with a very rich yet light, cream cheese filling… (Pause) Wow! My whole mouth just filled with saliva!

是芝士蛋糕。很好。它有一个奶油状的，易碎的，格雷厄姆饼干外壳，里面有一个非常丰富但很轻的奶油奶酪填充物…（停顿）哇！我满嘴都是唾液！

Chandler Bing: (closing the box) Y’know what? Forget it! We are just hungry! We have not had lunch! We are just light-headed! So let us go out and have lunch and forget about the cheesecake.

（合上盒子）你知道吗？算了吧！我们只是饿了！我们还没吃午饭！我们只是头晕！所以，让我们出去吃午饭，忘掉芝士蛋糕吧。

Rachel Green: Yeah and we’ll drop it off downstairs so that we’re not tempted.

是的，我们会把它放在楼下，这样我们就不会被诱惑了。

Chandler Bing: Good idea. Where do you want to go to lunch?

好主意。你想去哪里吃午饭？

Rachel Green: Momma’s Little Bakery, Chicago, Illinois. (They exit with the cheesecake.)

妈妈的小面包店，伊利诺斯州芝加哥。（他们带着奶酪蛋糕离开。）

🎬 Central Perk, Monica and Joey are there. Joey is reading a newspaper.

🎬 中央公园，莫妮卡和乔伊在那里。乔伊正在看报纸。

Joey Tribbiani: Awww! Mel Torme died.

啊！梅尔·托姆死了。

Monica Geller: Joey, that paper’s like a year old!

乔伊，那家报纸有一年了！

Joey Tribbiani: Aw! Does that mean the Sam Goody’s sale is over?!

噢！那是不是意味着山姆糖果的销售结束了？！

Ross Geller: (entering) Hey.

（进来）嘿。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey.

嘿。

Monica Geller: Hey.

嘿。

Ross Geller: So I finally heard back from Aunt Sheryl and apparently it wasn’t a mistake. Ahh, there’s-there’s limited seating in the hall.

所以我终于收到了谢丽尔阿姨的回信，显然这不是一个错误。啊，大厅里的座位有限。

Monica Geller: Limited seating?! (Screechingly) I am just one tiny person!

座位有限？！（尖叫）我只是一个小小的人！

Ross Geller: Well yeah, but she doesn’t know that. I mean, the last time she saw you-you would’ve turned one of those little wedding chairs into kindling.

是的，但她不知道。我是说，她最后一次见到你的时候，你会把一把婚礼用的小椅子变成火柴。

Monica Geller: (disgusted) Limited seating! Oh, that is such a lame excuse! That’s not the reason she’s not inviting me!

（恶心）座位有限！哦，这是一个多么站不住脚的借口！这不是她不邀请我的原因！

Ross Geller: Oh what’s the big deal?! I wasn’t even invited to the ceremony, just the reception. And-and y’know what? If it makes you feel any better, Joan and I will just make an appearance and then, and then we’ll-we’ll leave early as a sign of protest.

哦，有什么大不了的？！我甚至没有被邀请参加典礼，只是参加招待会。你知道吗？如果这能让你感觉好一点，琼和我会先露面，然后，我们会——我们会早点离开，以示抗议。

Monica Geller: Joan?

琼？

Ross Geller: Yeah, Joan Tedeski my date. She’s an assistant professor in the Linguistics department. Tall, very beautiful, and despite what some people say, not broad backed!

是的，琼·特德斯基是我的约会对象。她是语言学系的助理教授。高高的，非常漂亮，尽管有些人说，不是宽背！

Monica Geller: Wait a minute, you got Ross Gellar and guest?! I wasn’t invited and you got "and guest?!"

等等，你有罗斯·盖勒和客人吗？！我没有被邀请，你却得到了“还有客人？！”

Joey Tribbiani: Uh-uh, excuse me, I do have to interrupt on Ross’s behalf. I-I think the rule applies here y’know, since she has a chance to get on broad back…

呃，对不起，我得代表罗斯打断一下。我-我认为规则适用于这里，你知道，因为她有机会得到广泛的支持…

Ross Geller: (interrupting) Not broad backed!

（打断）不是宽背！

Monica Geller: Wait a minute, y’know, you’re bringing me!

等一下，你知道，你要带我来！

Ross Geller: What? I can’t cancel on Joan!

什么？我不能取消琼的约会！

Monica Geller: Why not?!

为什么不呢？！

Ross Geller: Bec—Did you not hear me?! She’s an assistant professor in the Linguistics department, okay? They’re wild! Why do you want to come anyway?

你没听见我说话吗？！她是语言学系的助理教授，好吗？他们太野了！你到底为什么想来？

Monica Geller: Because! She’s my cousin. I mean, we grew up together! We’re family y’know? Well that’s important to me.

因为！她是我表妹。我是说，我们是一起长大的！我们是一家人，你知道吗？这对我很重要。

Ross Geller: Okay, all right, I’ll take you. I’ll go call Joan. (Does so.)

好吧，好吧，我带你去。我去给琼打电话。（确实如此。）

Joey Tribbiani: Aww that’s nice. Family should be there, huh? This is her wedding, happiest day of her life.

啊，那太好了。家人应该在那里，是吗？这是她的婚礼，她一生中最快乐的一天。

Monica Geller: (laughs) We’ll see.

（笑）我们拭目以待。

🎬 The Lobby of Chandler and Rachel’s building, Chandler and Rachel are returning from lunch.

🎬 钱德勒和瑞秋的大楼大厅，钱德勒和瑞秋正在吃午饭回来。

Chandler Bing: Well, thank you for lunch.

谢谢你的午餐。

Rachel Green: What? Wait a minute, I didn’t pay, I thought you paid!

什么？等等，我没付钱，我以为你付钱了！

Chandler Bing: So apparently we just don’t pay for food anymore. (Rachel laughs then Chandler notices something.) Do you see what I see?

所以很明显我们不再付钱买食物了。（瑞秋笑了，钱德勒注意到了什么。）你看到我看到的了吗？

Rachel Green: (gasps) Its still there! (The cheesecake they returned to Mrs. Braverman is still lying in front of her door.)

（喘息）它还在那里！（他们还给布拉弗曼夫人的奶酪蛋糕仍然放在她的门前。）

Chandler Bing: Mrs. Braverman must be out. (They move closer to it.)

布雷弗曼夫人一定出去了。（他们向它靠近。）

Rachel Green: She could be out of town. Maybe she’ll be gone for months.

她可能出城了。也许她会离开几个月。

Chandler Bing: By then, the cheesecake may have gone bad. We don’t want her to come back to bad cheesecake.

到那时，奶酪蛋糕可能已经坏了。我们不想让她回到坏奶酪蛋糕上来。

Rachel Green: No that could kill her.

不，那会杀了她。

Chandler Bing: Well, we don’t want that.

嗯，我们不想那样。

Rachel Green: No so we’re protecting her.

不，所以我们在保护她。

Chandler Bing: But we should take it.

但我们应该接受它。

Rachel Green: But we should move quick.

但我们应该快点行动。

Chandler Bing: Why?

为什么？

Rachel Green: Because I think I just heard her moving around in there.

因为我想我刚听到她在里面走动。

Chandler Bing: Go! Go! Go! Go! Go! Go! Go! Go! Go! Go! (Rachel grabs the cheesecake and they take off upstairs.)

走！走！走！走！走！走！走！走！走！走！（瑞秋抓起奶酪蛋糕，他们上楼了。）

🎬 Central Perk, Monica and Phoebe are entering. As Phoebe is sitting down, she recognizes someone sitting at the counter.

🎬 中央公园，莫妮卡和菲比进入。菲比坐下时，她认出有人坐在柜台旁。

Phoebe Buffay: (gasps) (whispering) Oh my God! That’s David!

（喘息）（耳语）哦，我的天啊！那是大卫！

Monica Geller: David who?

大卫，谁？

Phoebe Buffay: David the scientist guy, David that I was in love with, David who went to Russia and broke my heart David!

科学家大卫，我爱的大卫，去俄罗斯伤了我心的大卫，大卫！

David: (noticing Phoebe) Oh my God!

（注意到菲比）哦，天哪！

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, you say someone’s name enough, they turn around.

哦，你说某人的名字够多了，他们就会转过身来。

David: Phoebe?

菲比？

Phoebe Buffay: David! (He kisses her cheek) What-what are you doing here? Aren’t you supposed to be in Russia?

大卫！（他吻了吻她的脸颊）你在这里干什么？你不是应该在俄罗斯吗？

David: Yeah, I’m just, I’m just in town for a conference. Umm, God you look phenomenal!

是的，我只是，我只是来参加一个会议。哦，天哪，你看起来真了不起！

Phoebe Buffay: Well… Yeah. You look great too. Did you get a haircut?

嗯……是的。你看起来也很棒。你理发了吗？

David: Yeah. Well I-I got like thirty of them.

是的。嗯，我——我大概有三十个。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah.

是的。

David: Umm, look I-I-I got a confession to make…

嗯，听着，我-我-我要坦白…

Phoebe Buffay: Uh-huh.
