Written by: Doty Abrams

作者：多蒂·艾布拉姆斯

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

Episodes Originally Transcribed by: Eric Aasen, Mindy Mattingly Phillips, and guineapig.

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Monica is at the kitchen table and Chandler is in the living room.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，莫妮卡在厨房餐桌旁，钱德勒在客厅。

Monica Geller: Do you realize that four weeks from today we’re getting married? Four weeks baby!! Four weeks!!!

我想我应该买那些。帕特，我想买一份誓言。（笑）

你知道我要说什么吗？
