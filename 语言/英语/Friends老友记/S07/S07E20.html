S07E20 The One with Rachel's Big Kiss

The One with Rachel's Big Kiss

瑞秋的大吻

The One With Rachel’s Big Kiss

有瑞秋大大的吻的那个

Written by: Shana Goldberg-Meehan and Scott Silveri

作者：沙娜·戈德伯格·米汉和斯科特·西尔维尼

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Central Perk, Joey, Monica, and Rachel are sitting on the couch and Ross is sitting on the chair, and they’re all talking.

🎬 中央公园、乔伊、莫妮卡和瑞秋坐在沙发上，罗斯坐在椅子上，他们都在说话。

Rachel Green: Hey! Out of all of us, who do you think is gonna get married next?

嘿！在我们当中，你认为下一个谁会结婚？

Joey Tribbiani: Probably Monica and Chandler.

可能是莫妮卡和钱德勒。

(A woman walks up to Gunther in the background.)

（一名妇女走到背景中的冈瑟面前。）

Woman: (to Gunther) Hi. Could I have a pack of Newport Lights, please?

（对冈瑟）嗨。请给我一包纽波特灯好吗？

Gunther: Oh umm, uh we don’t sell cigarettes, but they have them at the newsstand across the street. (Points.)

哦，嗯，我们不卖香烟，但是街对面的报摊上有。（点数）

Woman: That’d be great, thanks. (Gunther goes and gets them.)

那太好了，谢谢。（冈瑟去接他们。）

Rachel Green: (to the gang) Oh my God, Melissa Warburton. I don’t think I have the energy for this.

（对帮派）哦，我的天，梅丽莎·沃伯顿。我想我没有精力做这件事。

Melissa: (Gasps when she notices Rachel) Oh my God! Ray-ray Green?! (Screams)

（当她注意到瑞秋时喘息）哦，我的上帝！雷-雷-格林？！（尖叫声）

Rachel Green: (screaming) Melissa!

（尖叫）梅丽莎！

Melissa: You have been M.I.A for the past seven sorority newsletters, what’s up with you?!

在过去的七期女生联谊会通讯中，你都是麻省理工硕士，你怎么了？！

Rachel Green: Wh—(Turns and looks at the gang who’s staring)—Why don’t I tell you over here? (She walks Melissa away from the gang.)

嗯-（转过身看着盯着你看的那帮人）-为什么我不告诉你呢？（她带着梅丽莎离开帮派。）

Melissa: So last I heard you were gonna get married. (Grabs Rachel’s hand and notices that she doesn’t have a ring on it.) (Sadly) Oh poor Ray-ray.

上次我听说你要结婚了。（抓住瑞秋的手，发现她手上没有戒指。）（悲伤地）哦，可怜的雷。

Rachel Green: Oh no-no, no! It’s good! It’s all good! I-I actually work at Ralph Lauren!

哦，不，不，不！很好！一切都很好！我在拉尔夫·劳伦工作！

Melissa: Shut up!

闭嘴！

Rachel Green: I will not! I’m the divisional head of men’s sportswear!

我不会的！我是男式运动服的部门主管！

Melissa: Oh shut up more! Now, are you friends with Ralph?

哦，闭嘴！现在，你和拉尔夫是朋友吗？

Rachel Green: Oh please…

哦，求你了…

Melissa: (excitedly) Are you?!

（兴奋地）你是吗？！

Rachel Green: No.

没有。

Melissa: Listen, we-we have to have dinner. What-what are you doing tomorrow night?

听着，我们得吃饭了。明天晚上你干什么？

Rachel Green: Oh tomorrow, oh I don’t know. Um…

哦，明天，哦，我不知道。嗯…

Melissa: You do now. You’re having dinner with me.

你现在知道了。你要和我一起吃饭。

Rachel Green: Shut up.

闭嘴。

Melissa: I-I’ve got to go. This has been so great Ray-ray! (Gunther returns with her cigarettes.) Oh, there you are. (Takes them from Gunther.) (To Rachel) Umm, so listen, just call me. Here’s my card. (Hands the card over.)

我，我得走了。这太棒了，雷！（冈瑟拿着香烟回来了。）哦，你来了。（把他们从冈瑟带走。）（对瑞秋）嗯，听着，打电话给我。这是我的名片。（把卡片递过来。）

Rachel Green: Oh, wow thanks! (Reading the card) Oh you’re in real estate!

哦，哇，谢谢！（读卡片）哦，你在做房地产！

Melissa: Oh no, that’s-that’s an old card. Umm, I wanted to get out of that and-and do something where I can really help people and-and make a difference.

哦，不，那是一张旧卡。嗯，我想从中解脱出来，做一些我能真正帮助人们、改变现状的事情。

Rachel Green: Wow! What do you do now?

哇！你现在做什么？

Melissa: (quietly) I’m a party planner. I’ll see you tomorrow. (Exits.)

（平静地）我是个派对策划人。明天见。（退出。）

Rachel Green: Okay! (Joins the rest of the gang.)

好的！（加入其他帮派。）

Joey Tribbiani: Hey guys! Look who’s back! It’s Ray-ray!

嘿，伙计们！看谁回来了！是雷雷！

Rachel Green: Shut up that was my friend Melissa from college.

闭嘴，那是我大学的朋友梅丽莎。

Ross Geller: She seems really, really fun!

她看起来真的，真的很有趣！

Rachel Green: She’s actually very sweet and we used to be very close.

她其实很可爱，我们以前也很亲密。

Monica Geller: Wait a minute, she isn’t… She’s not the one who you…

等一下，她不是…她不是那个你…

Joey Tribbiani: (excitedly) Who you what? Who you what?!

（兴奋地）你是谁？什么？你是谁什么？！

Rachel Green: (To Monica) Yes.

（对莫妮卡）是的。

Monica Geller: Wow!

哇！

Joey Tribbiani: (excitedly) Wow? Wow what?! Wow what?! Who you—what?!!

（兴奋地）哇？哇什么？！哇什么？！你是谁？！！

Rachel Green: It’s not a big deal!

没什么大不了的！

Monica Geller: (To Joey) They were lovers.

（对乔伊）他们是情侣。

Ross Geller: What?!

什么？！

Joey Tribbiani: What?!

什么？！

Rachel Green: No we weren’t! It was nothing! It was one night, senior year we went to a party, had a lot of sangria and y’know, ended up…kissing for a bit.

不，我们没有！没什么！那是大四的一个晚上，我们参加了一个聚会，喝了很多桑格里亚酒，最后…接吻了一会儿。

Ross Geller: So that’s two of my wives.

那是我的两个妻子。

哦，哇。我们何不把我（抓住她的别针）放在手里，拿着一个曼哈顿，和那个可爱的调酒师说话呢。（把她的别针放在吧台上笑了。莫妮卡只是瞪了她一眼。）这些别针不是用来玩的，是吗？

（对钱德勒）好的，红色的是我的客人，蓝色的是你的。

这太可悲了。我的意思是，我只有大约十个大头针。

钱德勒，放松点，这不是比赛。（对瑞秋）当然不是很接近。

（进来）你好！

嘿！

嘿，瑞秋。（盯着她看。）

别胡思乱想了！！

Monica Geller: Okay, I think that’s it. The seating chart is done. This is our wedding. They all look like they’re having fun don’t they?

好吧，我想就是这样。座位表完成了。这是我们的婚礼。他们看起来都很开心，不是吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, so where are my parents gonna be?

嘿，那我父母要去哪里？

Monica Geller: Oh! (Gets up) Let’s see, well…if this is the wedding hall then umm (Walks away) you’re parents will be at home in Queens.

哦！（站起来）让我想想，好吧……如果这是婚礼大厅，那么（走开）你的父母将在皇后区的家里。

Joey Tribbiani: What they’re not invited?! Oh no, that’s terrible! They’re gonna be crushed!

什么他们没被邀请？！哦，不，那太可怕了！他们会被压死的！

Monica Geller: Why would they think they’re invited?

为什么他们会认为他们被邀请了？

Joey Tribbiani: (looking around) You got me. I don’t…

（环顾四周）你抓住我了。我不…

Monica Geller: Joey!

乔伊！

Joey Tribbiani: Well, I’m sorry. I thought parents were coming! Y’know? Your parents are comin’! Chandler’s parents are comin’! Ross’s parents are comin’!

嗯，对不起。我还以为父母会来呢！你知道吗？你父母来了！钱德勒的父母来了！罗斯的父母来了！

Monica Geller: Ross’s parents are my parents!

罗斯的父母是我的父母！

Joey Tribbiani: Well-well—see? Parents are comin’!

好吧，我明白了？父母来了！

Chandler Bing: Y’know I think we should invite them.

我想我们应该邀请他们。

Monica Geller: Oh please, you just want more blue pins.

噢，拜托，你只是想要更多的蓝色别针。

Chandler Bing: Well this is just sad!

这真是令人伤心！

Monica Geller: All right, all right. Maybe I can fit them in if I just do some rearranging. But uh, Rachel may actually have to sit at the bar!

好吧，好吧。如果我能重新安排一下，也许我能把它们放进去。但是瑞秋可能真的得坐在酒吧里！

Rachel Green: That is not a problem.

那不是问题。

Joey Tribbiani: (leaning down to her) Maybe you’ll order a little sangria?

（俯身向她）也许你可以点一点桑格里亚酒？

Rachel Green: Oh, get out of here! (Jumps back and walks away.)

哦，滚出去！（往后一跳，走开了。）

🎬 Ralph Lauren, Rachel is showing Chandler the selection of tuxedos.

🎬 拉尔夫·劳伦，瑞秋正在向钱德勒展示礼服的选择。

Chandler Bing: Well, thanks a lot for hookin’ me up Rach. I want you to know that I want you to attend our wedding as my guest.

嗯，非常感谢你帮我介绍瑞秋。我想让你知道我想让你作为我的客人参加我们的婚礼。

Rachel Green: I’m Monica’s maid of honor. Okay? Don’t try to blue pin me!

有些。

你是说这些晚礼服在红地毯上被人大喊，“你穿了吗？！你看起来棒极了！”

亲爱的，我建议你多看一点ESPN，少看一点E！？

他为洋基队打球。说真的，ESPN！只是偶尔，把它放在背景上。（钱德勒点点头，瑞秋抓起另一件礼服）哦，这件是皮尔斯·布罗斯南！

皮尔斯·布罗斯南？

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Joey and Phoebe are moving chess pieces around on the board and hitting the timer at random.

好吧，它看起来很野，你知道的，香草味的。

我不是香草的！我做了很多疯狂的事情！我是说我喝醉了，在维加斯结婚了！

给罗斯。

好的！好吧是的因为我无法想象。

哦，你应该进入我的脑海。

一个奇怪的瘦约会，头上挂着衣架？
