S07E13 The One Where Rosita Dies

The One Where Rosita Dies

罗西塔死的那个

The One Where Rosita Dies

罗西塔死的那个

Teleplay by: Brian Buckner & Sebastian Jones

布莱恩·巴克纳和塞巴斯蒂安·琼斯

Story by: Sherry Bilsing & Ellen Plummer

雪莉·比尔辛和艾伦·普卢默

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Rachel is trying to move Joey’s chair and not having much luck at it as Joey enters.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，瑞秋正试图移动乔伊的椅子，但乔伊进来时运气不佳。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey.

嘿。

Rachel Green: Hey.

嘿。

Joey Tribbiani: What are you doing?

你在干什么？

Rachel Green: Well, y’know I was thinking of moving the couch over here.

嗯，你知道我想把沙发搬到这里来。

Joey Tribbiani: (laughs) Why would you want to do that?

（笑）你为什么要这么做？

Rachel Green: So that there will be a decent place for me to sit.

这样我就能有个像样的地方坐了。

Joey Tribbiani: Rach, there is a decent place to…

瑞秋，有个像样的地方…

Rachel Green: And your lap does not count! Okay? Come on help me move this.

你的膝盖不算在内！可以来，帮我搬这个。

Joey Tribbiani: No. No. No.

不，不，不。

Rachel Green: No?

没有？

Joey Tribbiani: No. Rosita does not move.

不，罗西塔一动不动。

Rachel Green: I’m sorry, Rosita? As in…

对不起，罗西塔？如…

Joey Tribbiani: As in Rosita does not move.

就像罗西塔不动一样。

Rachel Green: Joey, it’s just a chair! What’s the big deal?

乔伊，只是一把椅子！有什么大不了的？

Joey Tribbiani: The big deal is that it is the exact equal distance from the bathroom to the kitchen and it’s at the perfect angle so you don’t get any glare coming of off Stevie.

重要的是，从浴室到厨房的距离是完全相等的，而且角度非常好，所以你不会从史蒂维身上看到任何刺眼的东西。

Rachel Green: Stevie the TV?

史蒂维在看电视吗？

Joey Tribbiani: (glaring at her) Is there a problem?

（怒视着她）有什么问题吗？

Rachel Green: No! (Joey sets his beer and bag of chips down and heads into his room.) Oh what does he know! Come on Rosita, us chichas got to stick together! (She tries pulling on the back of the chair, until the hinge breaks and the back falls off.) You bitch!

不！（乔伊放下啤酒和一袋薯条，走进房间。）哦，他知道什么！来吧，罗西塔，我们必须团结起来！（她试着拉椅子的靠背，直到铰链断裂，靠背脱落。）你这个婊子！

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Central Perk, Chandler and Ross are on the couch talking. Phoebe is getting coffee.

🎬 中央公园，钱德勒和罗斯在沙发上聊天。菲比正在喝咖啡。

Ross Geller: Hey, y’know what’s weird? After you guys get married, when you introduce me to people you’re gonna have to say, "This is my brother-in-law Ross." Not, "My friend Ross," "brother-in-law Ross." That’s weird isn’t it?

嘿，你知道什么很奇怪吗？你们结婚后，当你们把我介绍给别人时，你们会说，“这是我的姐夫罗斯。”而不是“我的朋友罗斯”，“姐夫罗斯”。这很奇怪，不是吗？

Chandler Bing: Couldn’t I just say, "This is Ross?"

我就不能说“这是罗斯吗？”

Ross Geller: (disappointed) Sure, do whatever you want.

（失望）当然，想干什么就干什么。

(Phoebe sits down between Chandler and Ross.)

（菲比坐在钱德勒和罗斯之间。）

Monica Geller: (entering, carrying a newspaper) Hey Ross! So, I was checking out the uh, real estate section…

（进来，拿着报纸）嗨，罗斯！所以，我在查看房地产部分…

Ross Geller: Yeah?

是吗？

Monica Geller: Look at this. (Hands him the newspaper.)

看这个。（把报纸递给他。）

Ross Geller: Oh, it looks like mom and dad’s house. Oh, it even has a tree with a broken limb out front and the uh, the window in the attic is…Oh my God!!

哦，看起来像是爸爸妈妈的房子。哦，它甚至有一棵树枝断了的树，阁楼的窗户是…哦，我的天哪！！

Phoebe Buffay: What? What happened to the window in the attic?!

什么？阁楼的窗户怎么了？！

Monica Geller: I can’t believe mom and dad are selling the house!

我不敢相信爸爸妈妈要卖房子！

Ross Geller: I can’t believe they-they didn’t even tell us!

我不敢相信他们竟然没告诉我们！

Phoebe Buffay: I can’t believe I still don’t know what happened to the window in the attic!

我不敢相信我还是不知道阁楼的窗户怎么了！

(Ross calls his parents on his cell phone.)

（罗斯用手机给父母打电话。）

Ross Geller: (on phone) Uh, hello dad! Monica and I just saw the house in the paper! (Listens) Yes we’re surprised! (Listens) Who did you leave a message with?

（打电话）嗯，你好，爸爸！莫妮卡和我刚在报纸上看到那所房子！（听）是的，我们很惊讶！（听）你给谁留言了？

Chandler Bing: (knocking on the window while outside) Sorry! (Runs off.)

（在外面敲窗户）对不起！（跑掉了。）

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Joey is examining the injury to Rosita while Rachel is apologizing to him.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，乔伊正在检查罗西塔的伤势，瑞秋正在向他道歉。

Rachel Green: Joey, Joey I am so sorry.

乔伊，乔伊，我很抱歉。

Joey Tribbiani: I told you not to move it! Rach, how would you feel if say, I wanted to move you mom, and you said don’t, and I did it anyway and her head fell off?

我告诉过你不要动它！瑞秋，如果你说，妈妈，我想让你搬家，而你说不要，你会怎么想，反正我这么做了，她的头掉了下来？

Rachel Green: Okay, come on—Joey, I’ll buy you a new one! All right? We’ll go down to the store right now and we’ll-we’ll get you a new chair.

好吧，来吧，乔伊，我给你买一个新的！好吧我们现在就去商店，给你买把新椅子。

Joey Tribbiani: (slowly turning and glaring at her) She’s not even cold yet!

（慢慢地转过身，瞪着她）她还没冷呢！

Rachel Green: But don’t you think Rosita would’ve wanted you to move on? I mean y’know, she did always put…your comfort first.

但你不觉得罗西塔会希望你离开吗？我是说，你知道，她总是把…你的安慰放在第一位。

Joey Tribbiani: That’s true.

没错。

(Rachel turns for the door and makes the "Wow!" face.)

（瑞秋转身向门口走去，做了一个“哇”的表情。）

Rachel Green: (grabbing her coat) Okay? You ready?

（抓起她的外套）好吗？准备好了吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, I… (Shuts off the TV.) I don’t want Stevie to see her like this.

是的，我…（关掉电视。）我不想让史蒂夫看到她这样。

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Ross and Monica are still going on about the house.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，罗斯和莫妮卡还在谈论房子的事。

Ross Geller: I can’t believe we have to say goodbye to the house we grew up in. Man, some-some stranger’s gonna be living in my room.

我真不敢相信我们不得不跟我们长大的房子说再见。伙计，有陌生人会住在我的房间里。

Monica Geller: Well, after 15 years of mom and dad keeping it as a shrine to you, it’s time the velvet ropes came down.

嗯，在爸爸妈妈把它作为圣地供奉给你15年之后，是时候放下丝绒绳索了。

Ross Geller: They kept your room for a while.

他们把你的房间留了一段时间。

Monica Geller: Oh please! Dad turned my room into a gym 20 minutes after I moved out! I gotta say, a tanning bed and a stack of Victoria’s Secret catalogues, not a gym!

噢，求你了！我搬出去20分钟后，爸爸把我的房间变成了健身房！我得说，一张晒黑床和一堆维多利亚的秘密目录，而不是健身房！

Ross Geller: Come on, you know they love you.

拜托，你知道他们爱你。

Monica Geller: As much as they love you?

就像他们爱你一样？

Ross Geller: I was their first born! They thought she was barren! It’s not my fault.

我是他们的长子！他们认为她不生育！这不是我的错。

Phoebe Buffay: (entering) Hey.

（进来）嘿。

Ross Geller: Hey.

嘿。

Monica Geller: Hey!

嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Ugh, I hate this year!

啊，我讨厌今年！

Ross Geller: What’s wrong with this year?

今年怎么了？

Phoebe Buffay: Well okay, it’s already February and I’ve only given two massages and they were both the worst tippers in the world!

好吧，现在已经是二月了，我只做了两次按摩，他们都是世界上给小费最差的人！

Monica Geller: That was me and Ross.

那是我和罗斯。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh that’s right!

哦，对了！

Ross Geller: Hey, y’know if you want to pick up some extra cash? Some friends of mine made good money doing telemarketing.

嘿，你知道你想多拿些现金吗？我的一些朋友做电话营销赚了很多钱。

Monica Geller: Oh that’s a great idea. You’re really good on the phone.

哦，那是个好主意。你的电话打得很好。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah and yeah, and it would probably be better than the last telephone job I had. Y’know, I probably wouldn’t have to say spank as much. (Monica and Ross are shocked.)

是啊，是啊，这可能比我上次的电话工作要好。你知道，我可能不需要说那么多“屁股”。（莫妮卡和罗斯震惊了。）

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh yeah, like you never called!

哦，是的，就像你从来没打过电话一样！

🎬 The telemarketing office, Phoebe is getting shown to her desk by the supervisor.

🎬 电话营销办公室，主管带菲比到她的办公桌前。

Supervisor: So basically this is very easy. You read from the script and try to sell as much toner as you possibly can.

所以基本上这很容易。你阅读了剧本，并尽可能多地销售墨粉。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, I can do that! Oh, by the way, I love my office.

好的，我能做到！哦，顺便说一下，我喜欢我的办公室。

Supervisor: (laughs) Why don’t we do a trial run.

（笑）我们为什么不试一下呢。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh okay. Umm, all right. (Picks up the phone and starts reading from the script.) Hi, this is Phoebe from Empire Office Supplies, can I speak to your supply manager please?

哦，好的。嗯，好吧。（拿起电话开始读剧本。）嗨，我是帝国办公用品公司的菲比，我能和你们的供应经理通话吗？

Supervisor: I’m the supply manager.

我是供应经理。

Phoebe Buffay: Umm, okay I would like to talk to you about your toner needs.

嗯，好的，我想和你谈谈你的碳粉需求。

Supervisor: We don’t need any toner.

我们不需要碳粉。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh okay, well I’m sorry to bother you. Bye-bye. (Hangs up the phone.) Yeah you’re right, this is easy.

哦，好吧，很抱歉打扰你。再见。（挂断电话）是的，你说得对，这很简单。

Supervisor: Okay, what was wrong with that call?

好吧，那个电话怎么了？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh well, all right…um, no offense, but you were kind of rude.

哦，好吧……嗯，无意冒犯，但你有点粗鲁。

Supervisor: They’re always going to tell you they don’t need toner, but that’s okay because whatever they say, you can find the answer to it here in this script.

他们总是告诉你他们不需要碳粉，但这没关系，因为不管他们说什么，你都可以在这个脚本中找到答案。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh.

哦。

Supervisor: So, I think you’re ready to sell toner, do you have any last questions?

那么，我想你已经准备好卖碳粉了，你还有什么问题吗？

Phoebe Buffay: No. (Pause) Oh wait yes! I do, I do have one question. What is toner?

不。（停顿）哦，等等，是的！我有，我有一个问题。什么是碳粉？

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Chandler is entering.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，钱德勒进来了。

Chandler Bing: Joey! Joe! (Sees that he’s not here and starts investigating. He picks up the bag of chips.) Full bag. (He picks up the beer.) Beer’s still cold. Something terrible must’ve happened here! (He decides it’s not that important; sits down on Rosita, and the back falls off causing him to flip over.) Oh no-no-no-no-no-no! (Runs over to Stevie.) Stevie, I was never here! (Runs out.)

乔伊！乔！（看到他不在这里，开始调查。他捡起了那袋薯条。）满满的。（他拿起啤酒）啤酒还是凉的。这里一定发生了什么可怕的事！（他觉得这没那么重要；坐在罗西塔身上，后背掉了下来，让他翻了个身。）哦，不，不，不！（跑向史蒂夫）史蒂夫，我从来没来过这里！（用完了。）

🎬 Ross and Monica’s parent’s garage, Ross and Monica are arriving to go through their things. Mr. Geller is in the garage.

🎬 罗斯和莫妮卡父母的车库，罗斯和莫妮卡要来检查他们的东西。盖勒先生在车库里。

Ross Geller: Dad?

爸爸？

Mr. Geller: I’m here!

我在这里！

Ross Geller: (entering with Monica) Hey!

（和莫妮卡一起进来）嘿！

Mr. Geller: Hi. God, it seems like just yesterday you guys used to come out to watch me work.

嗨。天啊，好像就在昨天你们还经常出来看我工作。

Ross Geller: Dad, we-we can’t believe you’re selling the house.

爸爸，我们真不敢相信你要卖房子。

Mr. Geller: Well, it’s time for a new family to start their memories here and hopefully their check will clear before they find the crack in the foundation and the asbestos in the ceiling.

Geller先生：嗯，是时候让一个新的家庭开始他们的记忆了，希望他们的检查会在他们发现地基裂缝和天花板石棉之前清楚。

Ross Geller: (To Monica) Let’s grab our stuff and get the hell out of here.

（对莫妮卡）我们拿着东西离开这里。

Mr. Geller: I’m sorry we can’t store your childhood things anymore.

很抱歉，我们不能再储存你童年的东西了。

Monica Geller: Oh, that’s okay, I can’t wait to see everything again! All of the memories…

哦，没关系，我迫不及待地想再看到一切！所有的记忆…

Mr. Geller: Well, I don’t know what’s in the boxes down here, but I do know there are six or seven Easy Bake Ovens in the attic.

嗯，我不知道下面的盒子里有什么，但我知道阁楼里有六七个简易烤箱。

Monica Geller: I used to love to play restaurant.

我以前喜欢在餐馆玩。

Ross Geller: Yeah, not as much as you used to love to play uncooked batter eater.

是啊，不像你以前那么喜欢玩生吃面糊的游戏了。

Monica Geller: Hey, it is unreasonable to expect a child to wait for a light bulb to cook brownies! (She goes to the attic.)

嘿，指望孩子等着电灯泡来煮布朗尼是不合理的！（她走向阁楼。）

Mr. Geller: So, I think you’re boxes are over here. (They walk over to them.)

所以，我想你们的箱子在这里。（他们走向他们。）

Ross Geller: Wow! Great! (Finds a pack of cigarettes.) Wait, dad who-who’s cigarettes are these?

哇！伟大的（找到一包香烟）等等，爸爸，这些是谁的香烟？

Mr. Geller: I don’t know. They-they must be your mother’s, but please, please don’t ask her. I’ll throw these away. (He puts them in his pocket as Ross finds something of interest in one of his boxes.)

我不知道。它们一定是你妈妈的，但是请不要问她。我要把这些扔掉。（罗斯在他的一个盒子里发现了一些有趣的东西，他把它们放进了口袋。）

Ross Geller: Cool! Dad! My report cards! Hey, check this out dad, (reading his grades) Math, A. Science, A. History, A. Gym…(He puts it away and finds something else.) Oooh, my rock polisher!

酷！爸爸！我的成绩单！嘿，看看这个，爸爸，（看他的成绩）数学，A.科学，A.历史，A.体操…（他把它收起来，找到了别的东西。）哦，我的岩石抛光机！

Mr. Geller: Oh look, look there’s your old makeup kit!

哦，看，看，这是你的旧化妆包！

Ross Geller: It’s a clown kit! Clown kit!

这是小丑套装！小丑套装！

Mr. Geller: Well, the white seems to be untouched. (He throws it back into the box as Mr. Geller moves a tarp and makes a discovery.) Uh-oh.

嗯，白色的好像没有动过。（盖勒先生移动防水布并发现了一个问题，他把它扔回箱子里。）哦。

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Mr. Geller: Y’know how the garage floods every Spring?

你知道每年春天车库都会泛滥吗？

Ross Geller: How are you ever going to sell this place?

你打算怎么卖掉这个地方？

Mr. Geller: I think I accidentally used Monica’s boxes to keep the water away from the Porsche.

我想我不小心用了莫妮卡的盒子来挡水。

Ross Geller: Oh no. Dad! Dad! What…(He goes to open one of her boxes and it rips apart.) Oh God…everything’s ruined! Dad, she’s gonna be crushed!

哦，不，爸爸！爸爸！什么…（他去打开她的一个盒子，盒子裂开了。）哦，天哪……一切都毁了！爸爸，她会崩溃的！

Mr. Geller: You don’t secretly smoke do you?

你不会偷偷抽烟吧？

Ross Geller: No!

不！

Mr. Geller: So it’s just your mother then.

那就只有你妈妈了。

🎬 The telemarketing office, Phoebe is hard at work.

🎬 电话营销办公室，菲比正在努力工作。

Phoebe Buffay: (on phone) Hi, this Phoebe from Empire Office Supplies, can I speak to your supply manager please? (Listens) Earl, thanks. (Listens) Hi Earl, this is Phoebe from Empire Office Supplies I’d like to talk to you about your toner needs. (She’s reading from the script.)

（在电话中）嗨，我是帝国办公用品公司的菲比，我能和你们的供应经理说话吗？（听）厄尔，谢谢。（听）嗨，厄尔，我是帝国办公用品公司的菲比，我想和你谈谈你的碳粉需求。（她在读剧本。）

[Cut to Earl’s office, who is played by Jason Alexander, George from Seinfeld. They cut back and forth between Phoebe’s and Earl’s offices with each of their lines.]

[转到厄尔的办公室，由宋飞的杰森·亚历山大、乔治扮演。他们用各自的台词在菲比和厄尔的办公室之间来回切换。]

Earl: I don’t need any toner.

我不需要任何墨粉。

Phoebe Buffay: I’m hearing what you’re saying, but at our prices everyone needs toner.

我听到你说的了，但按我们的价格，每个人都需要碳粉。

Earl: Not me.

不是我。

Phoebe Buffay: May I ask why?

我可以问一下为什么吗？

Earl: You wanna know why. You wanna know why?

你想知道为什么。你想知道为什么吗？

Phoebe Buffay: I surely do!

我当然喜欢！

Earl: Okay, I don’t need any toner because I’m going to kill myself.

好吧，我不需要任何爽肤水，因为我要自杀了。

(Phoebe desperately tries to find the scripted response to that line.)

（菲比拼命想找到对那句台词的脚本化回应。）

Phoebe Buffay: (doesn’t have any luck) Umm, is-is that because you’re out of toner?

（一点运气都没有）嗯，是因为你的墨粉用完了吗？

Commercial Break

商业休息

🎬 The telemarketing office, Phoebe is still talking to Earl.

🎬 电话营销办公室，菲比还在和厄尔说话。

Earl: Okay, so…no toner today. Thanks anyway, bye-bye.

好吧，那么…今天没有化妆水了。谢谢你，再见。

Phoebe Buffay: No-no wait-wait! I can’t just let you hang up! Just please talk to me.

Rachel Green: Uh-huh! Nice try, but you don’t get that chair anymore! All right? That is my chair now! You can sit on my lap! (Joey starts to get up.) No I take that back!

嗯！很好的尝试，但是你再也找不到那把椅子了！好吧那是我的椅子！你可以坐在我腿上！（乔伊开始站起来）不，我收回那句话！

Chandler Bing: I think I should get the chair!

我想我应该去拿椅子！

(Rachel and Joey both laugh at that suggestion.)

（瑞秋和乔伊都嘲笑这个建议。）

Joey Tribbiani: How do you figure?

你觉得怎么样？

Chandler Bing: Because you (Points to Joey) broke a chair and you (Points to Rachel) broke a chair! The only one around here that hasn’t broke a chair, is me!

因为你（指着乔伊）弄坏了一把椅子，你（指着瑞秋）弄坏了一把椅子！这里唯一没有弄坏椅子的人是我！

Rachel Green: No-no-no! This chair’s not going anywhere.

不，不！这把椅子哪儿也去不了。

Chandler Bing: Well, where’s the logic in that?!

那么，这其中的逻辑在哪里？！

Rachel Green: The logic is, that there are two of us and we are both strong enough to break a chair in half!

逻辑是，我们有两个人，我们都足够强壮，可以把一把椅子摔成两半！

Chandler Bing: So Joey breaks my chair and I get nothing!

所以乔伊弄坏了我的椅子，我什么也得不到！

(Joey whispers in Rachel’s ear to confirm his response.)

（乔伊在瑞秋耳边小声确认他的回答。）

Joey Tribbiani: That’s right!

没错！

Chandler Bing: What are you guys? Like a gang or something?!

你们是什么人？像个帮派什么的？！

(They confer again.)

（他们再次商议。）

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah! We are!

是啊！我们是！

(Rachel whispers in Joey’s ear.)

（瑞秋对着乔伊耳语。）

Rachel Green: We’re the Cobras!

我们是眼镜蛇！

🎬 Earl’s Office, Earl has his head in his hands as Phoebe enters.

🎬 厄尔的办公室，菲比进来时厄尔双手捧着头。

Phoebe Buffay: (to Marge) Excuse me! Can you tell me where I can find Earl? He’s the supply manager around here.

（对玛姬）对不起！你能告诉我在哪里能找到厄尔吗？他是这附近的供应经理。

Marge: Sorry, I don’t know any Earl.

对不起，我不认识伯爵。

Earl: (screaming) I’m right here!!!!

（尖叫）我就在这里！！！！

Phoebe Buffay: (goes over to his desk) Earl! I’m Phoebe.

（走向他的办公桌）厄尔！我是菲比。

Earl: Phoebe? The lady who sells toner?

菲比？卖碳粉的那位女士？

Phoebe Buffay: Umm, look it, you-you can’t kill yourself.

嗯，看，你不能自杀。

Earl: (exhales) Look, um I really appreciate your coming down…

（呼气）听着，嗯，我真的很感激你能下来…

Phoebe Buffay: No-no I can’t! I can’t let you do it!

不，不，我不能！我不能让你这么做！

Earl: Why?!

为什么？！

Phoebe Buffay: Because it was fate that made me call you today!

因为是命运让我今天给你打电话！

Earl: I thought it was toner.

我还以为是碳粉呢。

Phoebe Buffay: No! Think about it okay? This isn’t even my regular job! Okay? And my first day on the job, you’re my first call! And-and somebody else might’ve hung up on you, but I wouldn’t do that because I know about this stuff. My mom killed herself.

不！好好想想，好吗？这甚至不是我的常规工作！可以我第一天上班，你是我的第一个电话！也许有人挂了你的电话，但我不会这么做，因为我知道这件事。我妈妈自杀了。

Earl: Really?!

真的吗？！

Phoebe Buffay: Yes.

是的。

Earl: How?

怎么办？

Phoebe Buffay: I’m not gonna give you tips! Look don’t you see that this-this…this all came together so that I could stop you from doing this.

我不会给你小费的！听着，你没看到这…这一切都是为了让我阻止你这么做。

Earl: Couldn’t it just be a coincidence?

这难道不是巧合吗？

Phoebe Buffay: No, it’s fate!

不，这是命运！

Earl: It doesn’t really seem like enough to be fate.

这似乎还不足以成为命运。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh. Well umm, okay here’s a weird thing. My mother was also a supply manager.

哦。嗯，好吧，有件奇怪的事。我母亲也是一名供应经理。

Earl: I’m actually the office manager.

实际上我是办公室经理。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh my God! So was she! And! Get this, okay? Your-your name is Earl, right? Her name was Pearl, P-Earl.

哦，我的天啊！她也是！还有！拿着这个，好吗？你叫厄尔，对吗？她的名字叫珀尔，P伯爵。

Earl: Well, was there anything else?!

嗯，还有别的吗？！

Phoebe Buffay: Sure! (Thinks.) Umm, where are you from?

当然！（想）嗯，你是哪里人？

Earl: Philadelphia.

费城。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh my God! So was she! Oh, I’ve got-I’ve got goose bumps. (She holds out her arm.)

哦，我的天啊！她也是！哦，我起鸡皮疙瘩了。（她伸出手臂。）

Earl: (inspecting it) Really?

（检查）真的吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, y’know I’m wearing layers and it’s warm.

嗯，你知道我穿多层衣服，而且很暖和。

Earl: Yeah-yeah.

是的。

Phoebe Buffay: But if—no look, okay. These jerks might not care about you, but the universe does! And that says a lot!

但是如果没有，看，好的。这些混蛋可能不在乎你，但宇宙在乎！这说明了很多！

Earl: (To All) Did you hear that?! I don’t need you guys to care about me! Because the universe cares! The whole universe! (Laughs as everyone ignores him.) (To Phoebe) I really wished they’d care just a little bit though.

（对大家）你听到了吗？！我不需要你们关心我！因为宇宙在乎！整个宇宙！（笑着，大家都不理他。）（对菲比）我真希望他们能关心一点。

Phoebe Buffay: Y’know, I don’t-I don’t think it’s you. This is a freaky place. (To All) Hey! Guys! (Everyone looks up.) (To Earl) Oh no, it’s you.

你知道，我不，我不认为是你。这是个奇怪的地方。（对大家）嘿！伙计们！（每个人都抬起头来。）（对厄尔）哦，不，是你。

Earl: Yeah.

是的。

🎬 The Geller’s Garage, Monica is picking through her ruined childhood heirlooms with Ross.

🎬 盖勒的车库，莫妮卡正在和罗斯一起挑选她那被毁掉的童年传家宝。

Monica Geller: Oh, this terrible! Everything is destroyed! Look at this. (She picks up some kind of furry thing.) It obviously meant enough for me to save it, and I don’t even know what it is! Ohh, it’s still soft. (She rubs it against her cheek.) What do you think this is?

哦，真糟糕！一切都毁了！看看这个。（她捡起一些毛茸茸的东西。）很明显，这对我来说已经足够拯救它了，而我甚至不知道它是什么！哦，还是很软。（她在脸颊上擦了擦）你认为这是什么？

Ross Geller: All right. I think it was a mouse.

好的。我想是一只老鼠。

(Monica screams, throws the mouse down, and rubs her hands on Ross’s sweater to clean them.)

（莫妮卡尖叫着，把老鼠扔了下来，用手在罗斯的毛衣上擦了擦。）

Mr. Geller: (entering) How are you honey?

（进来）你好吗，亲爱的？

Monica Geller: How do you think I am?! You’ve wrecked all my childhood memories. You love Ross more than me. And I just rubbed a dead mouse on my face!

你觉得我怎么样？！你毁了我所有童年的记忆。你爱罗斯胜过爱我。我刚刚在脸上擦了一只死老鼠！

(Ross gets up to let his dad sit next to Monica.)

（罗斯站起来让他爸爸坐在莫妮卡旁边。）

Mr. Geller: Sweetheart, we love you just as much as Ross! Now, I’m sorry about everything that happened and I’d probably never be able to make it up to you, but here’s a start. (He hands her a small box.)

亲爱的，我们和罗斯一样爱你！现在，我为发生的一切感到抱歉，我可能永远也无法补偿你，但这是一个开始。（他递给她一个小盒子。）

Monica Geller: (opening it) What’s this?

（打开）这是什么？

Mr. Geller: It’s the key to my Porsche. Well, the key to your Porsche.

这是我的保时捷的钥匙。好吧，你的保时捷钥匙。

Monica Geller: (shocked) What?!

（震惊）什么？！

Ross Geller: (even more shocked) What?!!!

（更加震惊）什么？！！！

Mr. Geller: I’ve been thinking about getting rid of it. I was driving it the other day and saw my reflection in a store window. Your mother’s right, I do look like an ass.

我一直在考虑摆脱它。前几天我开着它，在商店橱窗里看到了我的倒影。你妈妈说得对，我看起来真像个混蛋。

Monica Geller: Wait, you’re giving me your Porsche, you’re kidding me right?!

等等，你要把你的保时捷给我，开玩笑吧？！

Ross Geller: Well w-w-w-w-wait, w-wait, wait, wait a minute! I mean a couple of stupid boxes get wet and she gets a Porsche?!

等等，等等，等等，等一下！我是说，几个愚蠢的盒子被弄湿了，她就得到了一辆保时捷？！

Mr. Geller: (To Monica) Why don’t we take it for a spin?

（对莫妮卡）我们为什么不去转转呢？

Monica Geller: All right!

好吧！

Ross Geller: Well, what about me?! I’m a medical marvel!!

那我呢？！我是一个医学奇迹！！
