The One on the Last Night

The One The Last Night

昨晚的那个

Written by: Scott Silveri

作者：斯科特·西尔维尼

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

Directed by: David Schwimmer (Yeah, that David Schwimmer.)

导演：大卫·施维默（是的，就是大卫·施维默。）

🎬 Chandler and Joey's, Joey is balancing a mini hockey stick on his hand as Chandler enters from his room carrying a bunch of bills.

🎬 钱德勒和乔伊的，当钱德勒拿着一堆钞票从房间走出来时，乔伊正在平衡手上的一根迷你曲棍球棒。

Chandler Bing: (watches Joey for a moment) Okay! (Joey quickly moves the hockey stick so that he’s scratching his back with it.) Listen, I’m gonna be moving out so you will be in charge of paying the rent.

（看了乔伊一会儿）好的！（乔伊迅速地移动曲棍球棒，这样他就可以用它搔背了。）听着，我要搬出去，这样你就可以负责付房租了。

Joey Tribbiani: Right! And when is that due?

对！什么时候到期？

Chandler Bing: First of the month.

这个月的第一天。

Joey Tribbiani: And that’s every month?

那是每个月吗？

Chandler Bing: No, just the months you actually want to live here.

不，只是你想在这里住几个月。

Joey Tribbiani: Ahhh.

啊。

Chandler Bing: Okay, here is the phone bill. (Hands it to Joey.)

好的，这是电话账单。（递给乔伊。）

Joey Tribbiani: (looking at it) Oh my God!!

（看着它）哦，我的天啊！！

Chandler Bing: That’s our phone number. Now look, I know I kinda sprung this whole me moving out on thing, so why don’t I just—why don’t I just cover you for a while?

那是我们的电话号码。听着，我知道这件事是我干的，所以我为什么不让我掩护你一段时间呢？

Joey Tribbiani: No-no! No way! Joey Tribbiani does not take charity…anymore.

不，不！不可能！乔伊·特里比亚尼不再做慈善了。

Chandler Bing: It’s not charity, Joe…

这不是慈善，乔…

Joey Tribbiani: No! Forget it! Okay—I mean thanks, but I’m done taking money from you. All right, I can take of myself. Now, what’s next? Come on.

不！算了吧！好吧，我是说谢谢，但我已经不想再拿你的钱了。好吧，我可以照顾好自己。现在，下一步是什么？来吧

Chandler Bing: Okay uh, here’s the electric bill. (Hands it to him.)

好的，这是电费单。（递给他。）

Joey Tribbiani: This is how much we pay for electric?!!!

这就是我们买电要付的钱？！！！

Chandler Bing: Uh, yeah.

嗯，是的。

(Joey runs over and shuts off the lights.)

（乔伊跑过去关灯。）

Chandler Bing: So, we’ll do the rest of the bills later then?

那么，我们以后再付账？

Opening Credits

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Chandler is talking to Phoebe about trying to give Joey some money.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，钱德勒正和菲比谈论给乔伊一些钱的事。

Phoebe Buffay: So is Joey going to have to give up the apartment?

那么乔伊要放弃公寓了吗？

Chandler Bing: No, I hope not! I tried to offer him some money, but he wouldn’t take it.

不，我希望不是！我想给他一些钱，但他不肯接受。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, how much do you think he needs?

嗯，你认为他需要多少钱？

Chandler Bing: I figure that $1,500 would cover him for a few months, y’know? But I have to trick him into taking it so I won’t hurt his pride.

我想1500美元可以支付他几个月的费用，你知道吗？但我必须骗他接受，这样我才不会伤害他的自尊心。

Phoebe Buffay: Why don’t you hire him as an actor? You could have him dress up and put on little skits. Whatever you want.

你为什么不雇他当演员？你可以让他穿上衣服，演些小短剧。你要什么都行。

Chandler Bing: Well that would help the pride thing.

那会有助于骄傲。

Monica Geller: (entering from her room) Hey!

（从她的房间进来）嘿！

Chandler Bing: Hey! Wow! You look great! Wanna move in with me tomorrow?

嘿！哇！你看起来很棒！明天想和我一起住吗？

Monica Geller: (thinks) Okay.

（想）好的。

Chandler Bing: Okay! (They kiss) So, what do you girls have planned for tonight?

好的！（他们接吻）那么，你们女孩今晚有什么计划？

Monica Geller: Well, instead of being sad that tonight is my last night together with Rachel we thought we’d go out to dinner and celebrate the fact that Rachel is moving in with Phoebe.

今晚是我和瑞秋的最后一个晚上，我们不是伤心，而是想出去吃晚饭，庆祝瑞秋要和菲比搬进来。

Phoebe Buffay: And also, my birthday.

还有，我的生日。

Monica Geller: It’s not your birthday.

今天不是你的生日。

Phoebe Buffay: What a mean thing to say! I would never tell you it’s not your birthday!

说这话真卑鄙！我永远不会告诉你今天不是你的生日！

(Joey and Ross enter.)

（乔伊和罗斯进来了。）

Joey Tribbiani: Hey!

嘿！

Ross Geller: Hey!

嘿！

Chandler Bing: Hey!

嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Hey! So you guys have anything planned for the big last night?

嘿！你们昨晚有什么大计划吗？

Chandler Bing: Well, instead of just hanging out, we figure we’d do nothing.

好吧，我们不只是闲逛，我们想我们什么都不会做。

Ross Geller: Whoa-whoa-whoa, Knicks season opener tonight. I thought maybe you guys would come over and watch it.

哇，哇，尼克斯队今晚的赛季揭幕战。我想也许你们会过来看。

Joey Tribbiani: I don’t know Ross, not if you’re gonna talk about how you gave up a career in basketball to become a paleontologist.

我不知道罗斯，如果你想谈谈你是如何放弃篮球生涯而成为一名古生物学家的话。

Ross Geller: I did give up a career in basketball to become a paleontologist!

我确实放弃了篮球生涯，成为了一名古生物学家！

Rachel Green: (entering from her room) Pheebs, I was wondering…

（从她的房间进来）菲比，我在想…

Monica Geller: You’re not dressed yet?! We’re supposed to start having fun in 15 minutes!

你还没穿衣服？！我们应该在15分钟后开始玩！

Rachel Green: Well and clearly not a minute sooner.

很明显，一分钟也不早。

Monica Geller: Rachel, you are packed though right, I mean please tell me that you’re packed.

瑞秋，你已经打包了，对吧，请告诉我你打包了。

Rachel Green: Of course I packed! Monica relax! I just wanted to ask Phoebe her opinion on what I should wear tonight.

我当然打包了！莫妮卡，放松！我只是想问问菲比她对我今晚该穿什么的看法。

Phoebe Buffay: My God, I can’t get a minute of peace around this place.

我的天啊，我在这地方一分钟也不能安宁。

[Cut to Rachel’s bedroom, Phoebe and her are entering. And it’s obvious that she’s not packed.]

[切到瑞秋的卧室，菲比和她正进来。很明显，她没有收拾行李。]

Phoebe Buffay: Oh my God! Monica’s just gonna kill you.

哦，我的天啊！莫妮卡会杀了你的。

Rachel Green: Yeah, yeah, I know.

是的，是的，我知道。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, what you’re wearing is fine for that.

嗯，你穿的很好。

Monica Geller: (entering) Rachel, I need to borrow—You’re not packed!!!! You’re not packed even a little bit!

（进来）瑞秋，我要借你的钱，你没打包！！！！你连一点行李都没有打包！

Rachel Green: Surprise!!

惊喜！！

Monica Geller: What?

什么？

Rachel Green: No, no don’t get mad because look—this is what happened. So I-I started packing, then I realized, "What am I doing? I am lousy at packing!" Right? But you love packing! So, as a gift to you, on our last night, ta-da!

不，不，别生气，因为事情就是这样发生的。所以我开始打包，然后我意识到，“我在做什么？我打包很糟糕！”对吧？但是你喜欢包装！所以，作为给你的礼物，在我们的最后一晚，塔达！

Monica Geller: (grabs a bag of those Styrofoam peanuts) I’ll be coordinator! Oh my God! I’m so sorry, I didn’t get you anything! Okay, look everybody has to help! Okay? You can help, can’t you Phoebe?

（抓起一袋泡沫塑料花生）我来做协调员！哦，我的上帝！很抱歉，我没有给你任何东西！好吧，听着，每个人都得帮忙！可以菲比，你能帮忙吗？

Phoebe Buffay: I have plans.

我有计划。

Monica Geller: You’re plans were with us.

你的计划和我们在一起。

Phoebe Buffay: That’s right.

没错。

Monica Geller: All right, Chandler can make boxes, Ross can wrap, and Joey can lift things. Now Phoebe, go tell the guys they have to help out!

好吧，钱德勒会做盒子，罗斯会包装，乔伊会提东西。现在菲比，去告诉那些家伙他们必须帮忙！

Phoebe Buffay: Okay.

好的。

Monica Geller: Okay! (To Rachel) Oh my God, thank you!!

好的！（对瑞秋）哦，天哪，谢谢你！！

[Cut to living room, Phoebe enters and closes Rachel’s door behind her.]

[切到客厅，菲比走进来，关上瑞秋身后的门。]

Phoebe Buffay: Hurry! Monica’s gonna make you pack! She’s got jobs for everyone! Now, it’s too late for me, but save your selves! (The guys scramble for the door.)

快点！莫妮卡会让你打包的！她每个人都有工作！现在，对我来说已经太晚了，但要拯救你自己！（这些人争先恐后地向门口跑去。）

Monica Geller: (entering and interrupting the guy’s escape attempt) Okay! The movers will be here in 11 hours. Rachel has not packed. Now, everybody has to help! Chandler, we’re gonna start with…

（进入并打断那家伙的逃跑企图）好的！搬运工将在11小时后到达这里。瑞秋还没有收拾行李。现在，每个人都要帮忙！钱德勒，我们先从…

Chandler Bing: Oh nope, I-I have plans with Joey.

哦，不，我和乔伊有计划。

Monica Geller: I thought you said you were going to do nothing.

我以为你说你什么都不做。

Chandler Bing: Yes, but for the last time.

是的，但这是最后一次。

Monica Geller: Okay fine, now Ross…

好吧，罗斯…

Ross Geller: Oh, but-but I can’t do it.

哦，但是我做不到。

Monica Geller: Why not?

为什么不呢？

Joey Tribbiani: (quietly) I’ve got Ben.

（轻轻地）我有本。

Ross Geller: Because, because I’ve got Ben.

因为，因为我有本。

Monica Geller: It’s almost 8 o’clock, it’s almost past his bedtime. Where-where is he?

快8点了，快过他的就寝时间了。他在哪里？

Chandler Bing: He’s at a dinner party.

他在一个晚宴上。

Monica Geller: Is he really coming? Because I can see right into your apartment!

他真的要来吗？因为我能看到你的公寓！

Ross Geller: Of course he is! What, do you think I’d just use my son as-as an excuse? What kind of father do you think I am?

他当然是！什么，你觉得我会用我儿子作为借口吗？你认为我是什么样的父亲？

Monica Geller: All right, sorry. (Goes back to Rachel’s room.)

好吧，对不起。（回到瑞秋的房间。）

Ross Geller: (to the guys) I gotta go make a fake Ben.

（对伙计们）我得去做个假本。

🎬 Chandler and Joey's, Joey is entering carrying two pizzas.

🎬 钱德勒和乔伊家，乔伊拿着两个比萨饼进来。

Joey Tribbiani: Here it is! Our last pizzas together as roommates.

给你！我们最后一次一起吃披萨是室友。

Chandler Bing: Oh, I wish I’d know you were going to do that, I ordered Chinese.

哦，我希望我知道你会这么做，我点了中国菜。

Joey Tribbiani: Oh that’s okay. Hey, actually in a way it’s kinda nice. Me, bringing the food of my ancestors, you, the food of yours!

哦，没关系。嘿，事实上，从某种程度上说，这有点不错。我，带来我祖先的食物，你，你的食物！

(Chandler stares at him, dumbfounded, then finally agrees.)

（钱德勒目瞪口呆地望着他，最后同意了。）

Chandler Bing: Say, Joe, I had a strange idea of what we could do for our last night. What do you say we play a little uh, foosball for money?

喂，乔，我有一个奇怪的想法，我们昨晚能做些什么。你觉得我们玩一点呃足球是为了钱吗？

Joey Tribbiani: What, are you crazy? You haven’t beaten me once since my injury plagued ’97 season. It would be easier if you just give me your money.

什么，你疯了吗？自从我97赛季受伤以来，你一次也没赢过我。如果你把钱给我就容易多了。

Chandler Bing: Yes it would. What do you say to $50?

是的。50美元怎么样？

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, you’re on.

好的，你上了。

Chandler Bing: Okay, let’s play! The big game, Italy vs. China, apparently.

好的，我们玩吧！这场重要的比赛，显然是意大利对中国。

(They start playing.)

（他们开始玩了。）

🎬 Rachel’s bedroom, she’s in her closet bringing down her pair of roller blades from a top shelf.

🎬 瑞秋的卧室，她在衣橱里从架子上拿下她的一对滚轴刀片。

Rachel Green: Ohhhhhh, look it’s the roller blades.

哦，看，是滚轴刀片。

Monica Geller: (starting to cry) Oh God!

（开始哭）哦，天哪！

Rachel Green: You remember when we got these?

你还记得我们什么时候买的吗？

Monica Geller: No.

没有。

Rachel Green: I guess you weren’t there. (They hug.)

我猜你当时不在。（他们拥抱。）

Phoebe Buffay: You guys, we said we were gonna have fun! Come on, hey, remember the time… (She starts laughing hysterically.) You don’t remember?

你们这些家伙，我们说过我们会玩得开心的！来吧，嘿，还记得那个时候吗…（她开始歇斯底里地笑起来）你不记得了？

Rachel Green: I’m sorry Pheebs, I guess I’m just really said that I’m leaving.

对不起，菲比，我想我只是真的说我要走了。

Monica Geller: I’m gonna miss you so much.

我会非常想你的。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, this doesn’t have to be so sad though. Y’know? Maybe instead of just thinking about how much you’re gonna miss each other, you should like think of the things you’re not gonna miss.

不过，这也没必要这么难过。你知道吗？也许你不应该只想着你们会多么想念对方，你应该想想你不会错过的事情。

Monica Geller: I don’t think there’s anything.

我觉得什么都没有。

Phoebe Buffay: Come on, there’s gotta be something.

拜托，一定有什么事。

Monica Geller: Nope, she’s perfect.

不，她很完美。

Rachel Green: I have one.

我有一个。

Phoebe Buffay: Good! Great! You can go first.

很好！伟大的你可以先走。

Rachel Green: Uh well, I guess I’m not gonna miss the fact that you’re never allowed to move the phone pen. (Laughs. Monica lags behind the laugh a little bit.)

嗯，我想我不会错过一个事实，那就是你永远不允许移动电话笔。（笑。莫妮卡稍微落后于笑声。）

Phoebe Buffay: Good that’s a good one. Okay, Monica, anything? Y’know? Does Rachel move the phone pen?

很好，很好。好吧，莫妮卡，有什么事吗？你知道吗？瑞秋移动电话笔了吗？

Monica Geller: Aw, sometimes. Always, actually.

哦，有时候。总是，实际上。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, good. There you go. Doesn’t anyone feel better?

好的，很好。好了。难道没有人感觉好些吗？

Monica Geller: Not just the phone pen. I never get my messages.

不仅仅是电话笔。我从来没有收到过我的留言。

Rachel Green: You get your messages!

你收到你的留言了！

Monica Geller: Yeah, well I don’t think it really counts if you have to read them off the back of your hand after you fall asleep on the couch.

是啊，我觉得如果你在沙发上睡着后要从手背上念出来，那就不算了。

Rachel Green: So-so, you missed a message from who? Chandler or your mom? Or Chandler? Or your mom?

那么，你错过了谁的留言？钱德勒还是你妈妈？还是钱德勒？还是你妈妈？

Phoebe Buffay: Great! It worked! No one’s sad.

太好了！成功了！没有人难过。

🎬 Chandler and Joey's, they are just finishing up another game of foosball.

🎬 钱德勒和乔伊家，他们刚刚结束另一场足球比赛。

Joey Tribbiani: (scores) Yes! I win again! Ha-ha! That’s like 500 bucks you owe me! Whoo-ho-hoo! (Goes over to the fridge and starts opening and closing the door rapidly.) $500 that is a loooot of electricity! (By the way, there’s nothing in the fridge.) Whoo-ho-ho! (Notices the sparseness of the fridge.) I gotta buy some food.

（得分）是的！我又赢了！哈哈！你欠我500美元！呜呜呜！（走到冰箱前，开始快速地打开和关闭门。）500美元那是一大笔电！（顺便说一句，冰箱里什么也没有。）哇哦！（注意到冰箱的稀疏）我得买些食物。

Chandler Bing: Okay, give me a chance to win my money back. Okay? Sudden death, one goal, $1,000.

好吧，给我个机会赢回我的钱。可以突然死亡，一个目标，1000美元。

Joey Tribbiani: You serious?

你是认真的吗？

Chandler Bing: Oh yes!

哦，是的！

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, get ready to owe me!

好吧，准备好欠我的！

Chandler Bing: Okay.

好的。

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, here we go. Ready?

好的，我们开始吧。准备好的

[In slow motion, as some haunty demonic music plays in the background, Joey throws the ball in, Chandler quits playing and goes for his Chinese food. Joey smacks the ball really hard, shooting it down the table. Chandler slowly takes a bite, the ball bounces off of the wall, heads back up the table, and scores the goal for Chandler.]

[在慢镜头中，当一些闹鬼的恶魔音乐在背景中播放时，乔伊把球扔了进去，钱德勒放弃了比赛，去吃他的中国菜。乔伊使劲拍球，把球打到桌子上。钱德勒慢慢咬了一口，球从墙上弹开，头朝桌子上，然后为钱德勒进了一球。]

Chandler Bing: No! No! No! No—(Joey looks at him)—one can beat me.

不！不不不-（乔伊看着他）-没人能打败我。

(In frustration, Joey kicks the table, breaking it.)

（乔伊沮丧地踢桌子，打破了桌子。）

Chandler Bing: See? Now, that’s why only the little fake men are supposed to do all the kicking.

看到了吗？现在，这就是为什么只有小假男人才应该做所有的踢腿。

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, the phone rings and Monica answers it as Rachel and Phoebe move a box into the living room.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，电话铃响了，莫妮卡接了电话，瑞秋和菲比把一个盒子搬进客厅。

Monica Geller: Hello? (Listens) Oh, hi Ross! (To Rachel) See? Other people call me!

喂？（听）哦，嗨，罗斯！（对瑞秋）看到了吗？别人叫我！

Rachel Green: Ooh, your brother. Score!

哦，你哥哥。分数

Monica Geller: (on phone) What’s up?

（打电话）怎么了？

Ross Geller: Oh, I’m just over here with Ben. I thought we’d say hi.

哦，我和本就在这里。我想我们应该打个招呼。

Monica Geller: Oh, put him on!

哦，让他穿上！

Ross Geller: Ben, say hi to Aunt Monica. (He holds the phone to the fake Ben he has created out of a pumpkin.) Oh, I guess he doesn’t feel like talking right now. He’s smiling though! Okay, talk to you later.

本，向莫妮卡阿姨问好。（他把电话拿给他用南瓜做的假本。）哦，我想他现在不想说话了。不过他在笑！好的，待会儿再谈。

🎬 Rachel’s bedroom, Rachel is entering carrying two glasses of wine. She gives one to Phoebe, keeps one, and completely ignores Monica.

🎬 瑞秋的卧室，瑞秋端着两杯酒走进来。她给了菲比一杯，留着一杯，完全无视莫妮卡。

Chandler Bing: I invented the game of Cups as a way to give Joey money.

我发明杯子游戏是为了给乔伊钱。

Ross Geller: And now you want that money back.

现在你想拿回那笔钱。

Chandler Bing: Exactly.

没错。

Ross Geller: Chandler, what kind of an idiot do you take me for? (As he picks up the fake Ben.)

钱德勒，你认为我是什么样的白痴？（当他拿起假本时。）

Chandler Bing: It’s not a real game! I made it up!

这不是真正的游戏！是我编的！

Ross Geller: I’m sorry you lost your money, but I won it fair and square.

很抱歉你丢了钱，但我公平地赢了。

Chandler Bing: At a fake game!!

在一场假比赛中！！

Ross Geller: Now, if you wanna try to make some of it back, I’d be glad to play you for it. But I should warn you, I am very good at Cups.

现在，如果你想找回一些，我很乐意和你玩。但我要警告你，我很擅长杯子。

Chandler Bing: Okay! Now I assume the Saucer card came up when you played last.

好的！现在我猜你上次玩的时候碟子牌出现了。

Ross Geller: No.

没有。

Chandler Bing: Hmm, let’s see if it comes up this time. (He looks at his cards and shrugs.)

嗯，让我们看看这一次是否会出现。（他看了看卡片，耸了耸肩。）
