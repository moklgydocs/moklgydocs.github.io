The One Where Chandler Can’t Cry

钱德勒不会哭的那个

Written by: Andrew Reich & Ted Cohen

作者：安德鲁·里奇和特德·科恩

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Rachel is still looking out the window as Joey enters.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，乔伊进来时，瑞秋仍在看着窗外。

Joey Tribbiani: (sees Rachel) Oh, uh, hey Rach. I uh, I was just coming over here to uh… Oh wait, I don’t have to lie to you, you don’t live here anymore. Uh, I’m eating their food. What are you doing?

（见瑞秋）哦，呃，嘿，瑞秋。我呃，我只是来这里，呃……等等，我不必对你撒谎，你不再住在这里了。呃，我在吃他们的食物。你在干什么？

Rachel Green: Ross is on a date with my sister and they shut the drapes two and a-half-hours ago.

罗斯在和我妹妹约会，他们两个半小时前关上了窗帘。

Joey Tribbiani: Whoa, I didn’t know we could date your sister!

哇，我不知道我们可以和你妹妹约会！

(Chandler and Monica enter from their room and Joey quickly hides the bag of potato chips behind his back.)

（钱德勒和莫妮卡从他们的房间走了进来，乔伊很快把一袋薯片藏在背后。）

Monica Geller: Joey we know you steal our food.

我们知道你偷了我们的食物。

(Joey offers them some potato chips.)

（乔伊给了他们一些薯片。）

Chandler Bing: I’m good.

我很好。

Monica Geller: (To Rachel) Oh, are the drapes still closed hon?

（对瑞秋）哦，窗帘还关着吗，亲爱的？

Rachel Green: Yeah. And y’know who should’ve shut their drapes? Is that perverted old couple two doors over.

是啊。你知道谁该关上窗帘吗？那对变态老夫妇就在两扇门那边。

Chandler Bing: (looking) Is that a swing?

（看）那是秋千吗？

Rachel Green: Oh don’t even ask!

哦，别问了！

Chandler Bing: Yuck!

恶心！

Joey Tribbiani: I can’t believe Ross went out with Rachel’s sister! When Chandler made out with my sister I was mad at him for 10 years.

我不敢相信罗斯和瑞秋的妹妹出去了！当钱德勒和我妹妹做爱时，我对他生气了10年。

Chandler Bing: That was like 5 years ago.

那是5年前的事了。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah you got 5 years left!

是的，你还有5年！

Chandler Bing: Joey…

乔伊…

Joey Tribbiani: You wanna make it 6?!

你想成为第六名？！

🎬 中央公园，罗斯坐在沙发上看书，瑞秋进来了。这是第二天。

哦，罗斯，嗨！嗨，你好吗？你在这儿！

嘿！

（对冈瑟）我要喝杯咖啡。（对罗斯）你昨晚的重要约会怎么样？

嗯，还好。是的，很有趣。

是啊，有趣？太棒了！你们俩合得来吗？

我想是的。

那么，呃，发生什么事了吗？因为谣传你们把窗帘关上了！

没有。没有。什么都没发生。我关上窗帘，让她看我最喜欢的化石的幻灯片。

哦，幻灯片。（笑）所以真的什么都没发生。

Ross Geller: Something could’ve happened. All right? She-she really dug my slides. And-and she was definitely giving me the vibe.

可能发生了什么事。好吗？她真的很喜欢我的幻灯片。而且她肯定给了我一种感觉。

Rachel Green: Right. Was it the, "Please don’t show me another picture of a trilobite vibe?"

对。是不是“请不要再给我看一张三叶虫的照片？”

Ross Geller: Anyway, if she, if she wasn’t in to me, why-why would she ask me out again?

不管怎样，如果她，如果她不喜欢我，为什么她还要约我出去？

Rachel Green: She asked-asked you out again?

她又约你出去了？

Ross Geller: Yeah-uh-huh. Tomorrow night, Valentine’s Day, the most romantic day of the year. Who knows what could happen? I might not be shutting my drapes to show her my slides, if you know what I mean.

是啊，嗯。明晚，情人节，一年中最浪漫的一天。谁知道会发生什么？我可能不会关上窗帘让她看我的幻灯片，如果你知道我的意思的话。

Rachel Green: Okay-okay-okay-okay-okay-okay-okay! I got it! I got it! I got it! I can’t! I can’t! I can’t! I cannot go with you and my sister thing. Okay? I just can’t. It’s just too weird, all right? I imagine the two of you together and I freak out. It freaks me out. I can’t do it! I can’t do it.

好吧！我明白了！我明白了！我明白了！我明白了！我不能！我不能！我不能！我不能跟你和我妹妹一起去。好吗？我就是不能。这太奇怪了，好吗？我想象你们两个在一起，我会发疯。我发疯了。我做不到！我做不到。

Ross Geller: (while Rachel is finishing her rant) Okay! Okay! Okay! It’s okay. (Rachel stops.) It’s okay. Hey, it’s too weird for you, I won’t see her again.

（瑞秋说完她的咆哮）好吧！好吧！好吧！没事。（瑞秋停下来）没事的。嘿，这对你来说太奇怪了，我再也见不到她了。

Rachel Green: Thank you. I…yeah.

谢谢。我……是的。

Ross Geller: I mean after tomorrow night.

我是说明晚之后。

Rachel Green: No-no-no! No-no-no! Please Ross, I can’t! I can’t do it! (Starts to freak out.) It’s just gonna freak me out!!!

不，不，不，不，不，拜托，罗斯，我不能！我做不到！（开始发疯）这会让我发疯的！！！

Ross Geller: Okay! Okay! Ooh-hey-hey-hey! Okay! Okay! Okay! I’ll-I’ll tell her tonight I can’t see her anymore.

好吧！好吧！好吧！好吧！好吧！好吧！我今晚会告诉她我再也见不到她了。

Rachel Green: Ross thanks.

罗斯，谢谢。

Ross Geller: You want me to call her right now?

你要我现在就给她打电话吗？

Rachel Green: Oh no! No! No-no-no-no! No, I mean come on that’s-that’s crazy—I mean that’s crazy. So what’s-what’s going on with you? What is going on with you?

哦，不！不！不，不！不，我是说，那太疯狂了，我是说那太疯狂了。你怎么了？你怎么了？

Ross Geller: Well umm, oh! I might be teaching another class this semester!

嗯，哦！这学期我可能要教另一门课了！

Rachel Green: Yeah do it now, call right now.

是的，现在就做，马上打电话。

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Chandler, Monica, Phoebe, and Joey are watching ET.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，钱德勒，莫妮卡，菲比和乔伊在看ET.

Monica Geller: (crying) This is my favorite part.

（哭）这是我最喜欢的部分。

Phoebe Buffay: (crying) Yeah me too.

（哭）是的，我也是。

(We see the TV and it’s the last scene where ET is saying goodbye.)

（我们看到了电视，这是ET说再见的最后一幕。）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh y’know what’s sadder than this? Bambi. I cried for three days with that movie. No wait two! Because on the third day my mother killed herself so I was partly crying for that.

哦，你知道还有什么比这更悲伤吗？斑比。看了那部电影我哭了三天。不，等两天！因为第三天我妈妈自杀了，所以我哭了一部分。

Chandler Bing: (totally not crying) Well see now that I can see crying over, but Bambi is a cartoon!

（完全没有哭）好吧，现在我可以看到哭了，但是斑比是个卡通人物！

Joey Tribbiani: (crying) You didn’t cry when Bambi’s mother died?

（哭）斑比的妈妈死的时候你没哭吗？

Chandler Bing: Yes it was very sad when the guy stopped drawing the deer!

是的，当那家伙停止画鹿的时候，真是太伤心了！

Monica Geller: Chandler there’s nothing wrong with crying! I mean you don’t have to be so macho all the time.

钱德勒哭没什么不对的！我的意思是你不必总是那么有男子气概。

Chandler Bing: I’m not macho.

我不是男子汉。

Monica Geller: Yeah you’re right. I don’t know what I was thinking.

是的，你说得对。我不知道我在想什么。

Chandler Bing: No, I guess I just never really cried. Y’know? I’m not a crying kind of guy.

不，我想我只是从来没有真正哭过。你知道吗？我不是那种会哭的人。

Joey Tribbiani: Come on man there’s gotta be something that gets you choked up! Like uh, uh oh, what if you saw a three-legged puppy?

拜托，老兄，一定有什么东西让你窒息了！比如，呃，哦，如果你看到一只三条腿的小狗呢？

Chandler Bing: I’d be sad sure, but I wouldn’t cry.

我当然会难过，但我不会哭。

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, what if the puppy said, "Help me Chandler. All the other puppies pick on me."

好吧，如果小狗说：“帮帮我，钱德勒。”。所有其他的小狗都挑我的毛病。”

Chandler Bing: Cry?! I just found a talking puppy, I’m rich!

哭？！我刚找到一只会说话的小狗，我很有钱！

Monica Geller: Oh, I’ve got it! I have got it! (Gets up and gets something from the dresser underneath the TV.) Pictures from your childhood. This will get you going good!

哦，我明白了！我明白了！（站起来，从电视下面的梳妆台上拿些东西。）你童年的照片。这会让你走得很好！

(Chandler starts paging through the album.)

（钱德勒开始翻阅相册。）

Phoebe Buffay: All right, what’s going on there? (Points to a picture.)

好吧，那是怎么回事？（指着一张照片。）

Chandler Bing: Oh, that’s Parent’s Day, first grade. That’s me with the janitor Martin.

哦，那是一年级的家长节。我和看门人马丁在一起。

Monica Geller: Where were your parents?

你父母在哪里？

Chandler Bing: Oh they didn’t want to come!

哦，他们不想来！

Phoebe Buffay: Oh Chandler!

哦，钱德勒！

Monica Geller: Poor thing!

可怜的家伙！

Phoebe Buffay: So that story doesn’t make you cry?

所以那个故事不会让你哭？

Chandler Bing: No! Look, I don’t cry! It’s not a big deal! Okay?!

不！听着，我不哭！没什么大不了的！好吗？！

Joey Tribbiani: No! It’s not okay! It’s not okay at all!! You’re dead inside!!

不！不好！根本不好！！你死在里面了！！

🎬 Central Perk, Phoebe is putting away her guitar as a man approaches.

🎬 中央公园，菲比正在收起她的吉他，一个男人走近了。

The Fan: Excuse me.

对不起。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah?

是吗？

The Fan: Are you Phoebe Buffay?

你是菲比·布菲吗？

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah.

是的。

The Fan: Can-can I get your autograph, I’m your biggest fan. (Holds out a napkin and a pen.)

我能得到你的签名吗，我是你最大的粉丝。（拿出一张餐巾纸和一支笔。）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh you’re my biggest fan? I’ve always wanted to meet you! Hi! (Shakes his hand.) Sure! Yeah! (Signs the autograph)

哦，你是我最大的粉丝？我一直想见你！嗨！（和他握手）当然！耶！（签名）

The Fan: Wow! Wow, thanks a lot! I just wanna say, I think you’re really talented.

哇！哇，非常感谢！我只想说，我觉得你真的很有天赋。

Phoebe Buffay: You’re just saying that because you’re my biggest fan. (The fan leaves and Joey approaches.) (To Joey) Joey listen, take good care of that guy, okay? (Points) He’s a fan. (To the fan as she’s leaving) Bye! (Exits)

你这么说是因为你是我最大的粉丝。（粉丝离开，乔伊走近。）（对乔伊）乔伊听着，好好照顾那个家伙，好吗？（指着）他是粉丝。（对粉丝离开时）再见！（退出）

Joey Tribbiani: (to the fan) So, you saw me on Days Of Our Lives huh? Want me to, want me to do a little Dr. Drake Remoray for ya?

（对歌迷）那么，你在我们有生之年见过我，是吗？想让我，想让我为你做点德雷克医生的事吗？

The Fan: I have no idea what you’re talking about. But I, but I just got Phoebe Buffay’s autograph!

我不知道你在说什么。但是我，但是我刚刚得到菲比·布菲的签名！

Joey Tribbiani: Oh, you’re Phoebe’s fan!

哦，你是菲比的粉丝！

The Fan: Oh yeah! I’ve seen all her movies.

哦，是的！我看过她所有的电影。

Joey Tribbiani: Movies?

电影？

The Fan: That was Phoebe Buffay, the porn star.

那是菲比·布菲，色情明星。

Joey Tribbiani: (laughs) I don’t think so.

（笑）我不这么认为。

The Fan: No-no, it was! She was in Sex Toy Story 2, Lawrence of Alabia, and I got her autograph! The guys at the comic book store aren’t gonna believe this! (Exits.)

不，是的！她在《欲望玩具总动员2》，阿拉巴马州的劳伦斯，我得到了她的亲笔签名！漫画书店的伙计们不会相信的！（退出）

嘿，冈瑟，别再让那个家伙进来了！他刚说菲比是个色情明星！

嗯，我不会说她是明星，但她真的很棒。你应该看看她的小玩意儿。

🎬 Central Perk, time lapse, Rachel is at the counter as Jill enters.

嘿！

嘿！

哦，好吧。你知道吗，亲爱的？忘记一个男人最好的办法就是开始和别人约会。哦！有一个很棒的家伙，你会在工作中爱上他，叫鲍勃！他在人力资源方面是个真正的后起之秀。

你知道，谢谢你试着让我高兴起来，但我不会随便跟你工作的人约会。

不是随机的，是鲍勃。

可能是因为不够成熟。或者不够聪明。也许他不喜欢我的穿着方式。不，不可能。这真的是件聪明的事。哦，我太傻了！我就像一个非常漂亮的傻女孩！

你也问过他？！

嗯。

因为你是我妹妹，罗斯和我有着悠久的历史…

我不明白，你想和罗斯出去吗？

没有。

你不想要他，但你不想让我拥有他？

（换个话题）你认识人力资源部的鲍勃…

啊！我真不敢相信你也这么做了！你让我怀疑我有多聪明！（喘息）你让我怀疑我的时尚感！

听着，这没什么大不了的！你只是不跟罗斯约会！外面还有一百万其他男人，你只是…

嘿！你没有权利告诉我该怎么做。

Rachel Green: I’m not telling you what to do! I am telling you what not to do!

我不是在告诉你该做什么！我是在告诉你不该做什么！

Jill: Why are you so jealous of me?

你为什么这么嫉妒我？

Rachel Green: Jill this is not about me being jealous of you! This is about you being a brat! Wanting what you can’t have!

吉尔，这不是因为我嫉妒你！这是因为你是个孩子！想要你不能拥有的东西！

Jill: Can’t have?! Excuse me, the only thing I can’t have is dairy! (Starts to storm out.)

不能吃？！对不起，我唯一不能吃的东西就是奶制品！（开始狂风暴雨。）

Rachel Green: All right, all right, well you just blew your chances at dating Bob!

好吧，好吧，你刚刚错过了和鲍勃约会的机会！

Jill: Who?!

谁？！

Rachel Green: In Human Resources!!!!!!

在人力资源方面！！！！！！
