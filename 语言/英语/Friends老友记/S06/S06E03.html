S06E03 The One with Ross's Denial

The One with Ross's Denial

罗斯否认的那个

The One With Ross’s Denial

罗斯否认的那个

Written by: Seth Kurland

作者：塞思·库尔兰

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Joey, Monica, and Phoebe are there as Chandler enters.

🎬 钱德勒进来时，莫妮卡和瑞秋、乔伊、莫妮卡和菲比在那里。

Chandler Bing: (entering) Hey!

（进来）嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Hey! Check it out! This is unbelievable! Joey has been holding his breath for almost four minutes!

嘿！过来看！真是难以置信！乔伊屏住呼吸已经快四分钟了！

(We see Joey who has puffed up his cheeks and Chandler nonchalantly reaches down and pinches Joey’s nose shut. In a few seconds, Joey has to move because he’s now forced to actually hold his breath.)

（我们看到乔伊鼓起了双颊，钱德勒漫不经心地俯下身，捏住乔伊的鼻子。几秒钟后，乔伊不得不移动，因为他现在不得不屏住呼吸。）

Joey Tribbiani: (To Chandler) Dude! What are yo—you trying to kill me?!

（对钱德勒）伙计！你想干吗杀我？！

Rachel Green: (entering from her room) Pheebs, I wanna ask you something.

（从她的房间进来）菲比，我想问你一件事。

Phoebe Buffay: Uh-huh, what?

嗯，什么？

Rachel Green: Well since I’m movin’ out and-and you’re so beautiful…

既然我要搬出去，而你又那么漂亮…

Phoebe Buffay: Oh!

哦！

Rachel Green: …how about I move in with you?

……我搬来和你住怎么样？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, that would be great but then what do we do about Denise?

好吧，那太好了，但是我们该怎么处理丹尼斯呢？

Monica Geller: Who’s Denise?

丹尼斯是谁？

Phoebe Buffay: My roommate.

我的室友。

Rachel Green: You have a roommate?!

你有室友吗？！

Phoebe Buffay: Yes, Denise. Denise!

是的，丹尼斯。丹尼斯！

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, what is with the secrecy Phoebe? Huh? And what about this Denise, is she cute?

嘿，菲比，保密是怎么回事？嗯？丹妮丝怎么样，她可爱吗？

Chandler Bing: Pheebs, I don’t understand. How can you have a roommate that none of us know anything about?

菲比，我不明白。你怎么会有一个我们都不知道的室友？

Phoebe Buffay: Maybe because you never listen to anything that I say. I talk about her all the time! DENISE!!!!!

也许是因为你从来不听我说的话。我一直在谈论她！丹尼斯！！！！！

Joey Tribbiani: Hey Rach, listen I was thinkin’ uh, I’m gonna have an extra room over at my place…

嘿，瑞秋，听着，我在想，呃，我要在我家多留一个房间…

Rachel Green: Oh, that’s true.

哦，那是真的。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, why don’t you move in with me? It’ll be great! We could stay up late, watch movies, and you know about Naked Thursday’s right?

是啊，你为什么不和我一起住？那太好了！我们可以熬夜看电影，你知道裸体星期四是什么吧？

Rachel Green: Yeah, yeah I think I’m gonna find my own place.

是的，是的，我想我会找到自己的地方。

Chandler Bing: Hey-hey! I thought Naked Thursday’s was just our thing man!

嘿嘿！我以为裸体星期四是我们的事，伙计！

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Central Perk, Ross is reading a magazine, eating a cookie, and drinking some coffee as Phoebe enters.

🎬 中央公园，菲比进来时，罗斯正在看杂志，吃着饼干，喝着咖啡。

Phoebe Buffay: Hey.

嘿。

Ross Geller: Hey!

嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: So, what did Rachel say when you told her you were still married to her?

那么，当你告诉瑞秋你还和她结婚时，她说了什么？

Ross Geller: Oh, that. Umm, she took it really well.

哦，那个。嗯，她接受得很好。

Phoebe Buffay: You didn’t tell her did you?

你没告诉她吧？

Ross Geller: No.

没有。

Phoebe Buffay: Of course not, because you’re in love with her.

当然不是，因为你爱上了她。

Ross Geller: I am not in love with her. She was very upset about having to move out so I eh, didn’t tell her we were still married because she would only get more upset. I-I just comforted her, as a friend.

我不爱她。她对不得不搬出去感到非常不安，所以我没有告诉她我们仍然结婚，因为她只会更加不安。我只是作为朋友安慰了她。

Phoebe Buffay: What do you mean, comforted her?

你什么意思，安慰她？

Ross Geller: It’s nothing, I just gave her a hug.

没什么，我只是拥抱了她一下。

Phoebe Buffay: Ah-ha! A classic sign of love, the hug!

啊哈！一个经典的爱的象征，拥抱！

Ross Geller: It’s also a sign of friendship.

这也是友谊的象征。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, not in your case Lovey Loverson. (Tries to take a bite out of Ross’s cookie.)

是的，对你来说不是，亲爱的洛弗森。（试图咬一口罗斯的饼干。）

Ross Geller: (grabs back his cookie) It was a hug!

（抓回他的饼干）那是一个拥抱！

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, just tell me this, did you or did you not smell her hair?

好吧，告诉我，你有没有闻到她的头发？

Ross Geller: S-s-smell her hair? What if I did?

闻闻她的头发？如果我做了呢？

Phoebe Buffay: Ninety percent of a women’s pheromones come out the top of her head! That’s why, that’s why women are shorter. So that men will fall in love when they hug them! (Ross is staring at her dumbfounded.) Oh come on Ross, you’re a scientist.

女人90%的信息素都是从头顶出来的！这就是为什么，这就是为什么女人比较矮。这样男人在拥抱他们时就会坠入爱河！（罗斯目瞪口呆地看着她。）哦，得了吧，罗斯，你是个科学家。

Ross Geller: I was hugging her as a friend. It’s not my fault her-her hair got in my face, she’s got a lot of it and it smells all-all uh…coconutty. (Phoebe raises her eyebrows.) What?! Oh, that doesn’t mean I have feelings for Rachel! Maybe it means I have feelings for coconuts!

我把她当作朋友拥抱。不是我的错，她的头发沾到我脸上了，她有很多头发，而且闻起来都是椰子味。（菲比扬起眉毛）什么？！哦，那并不意味着我对瑞秋有感情！也许这意味着我对椰子有感情！

Phoebe Buffay: (taking his coffee) Okay, whatever you say. But just be careful, all right? Rachel’s not in the same place you are.

（端着咖啡）好吧，随你怎么说。但要小心，好吗？瑞秋和你不在同一个地方。

Ross Geller: (grabbing back his coffee) If the place you are referring too is being in love, then she is in the same place as me because I am not in that place!

（抓起咖啡）如果你指的地方也是恋爱，那么她和我在同一个地方，因为我不在那个地方！

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, I didn’t understand that, but y’know, maybe that’s ‘cause you were speaking the secret language of love!

好吧，我不明白，但是你知道，也许那是因为你说的是爱的秘密语言！

(She goes for his magazine and he grabs it away before she reaches it. But she was only using the magazine as a decoy because she grabs his cookie and coffee, takes a bite out of the cookie and drinks some of the coffee.)

（她去拿他的杂志，他在她够到之前就把它拿走了。但她只是把杂志当作诱饵，因为她抓了他的饼干和咖啡，咬了一口饼干，喝了一些咖啡。）

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Chandler and Monica are there as Joey enters.

🎬 乔伊进来时，莫妮卡和瑞秋、钱德勒和莫妮卡在那里。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey! (To Chandler) Dude, some guy just called for you.

嘿！（对钱德勒）老兄，刚才有人找你。

Chandler Bing: Who was it?

是谁？

Joey Tribbiani: I don’t know! How about, "Thanks for taking the message." Jeez! (Exits.)

我不知道！“谢谢你的留言”怎么样？天哪！（退出。）

Monica Geller: Okay listen, y’know when you move in Rachel’s room is gonna be empty, you wanna talk about what we want to do with it?

好吧，听着，你知道当你搬进瑞秋的房间时会空无一人，你想谈谈我们想用它做什么吗？

Chandler Bing: Sure!

当然！

Monica Geller: Okay, I was thinking we should have a beautiful guest room, right? With a mahogany sleigh bed and bedside tables with flowers on them all the time! And we could have a roll top desk with comment cards on them so people could say how much they loved staying here!! Okay, whatever, I really haven’t thought about it that much.

好吧，我在想我们应该有一间漂亮的客房，对吗？有一张桃花心木雪橇床，床头柜上总是放着鲜花！我们可以有一个带评论卡的翻盖办公桌，这样人们就可以说他们多么喜欢呆在这里！！好吧，不管怎样，我真的没怎么想过。

Chandler Bing: Well, I like that idea. Obviously! I was thinking maybe-maybe-maybe it could be a game room, y’know? I mean you can buy old arcade games like uh, like Space Invaders and Asteroids for $200, the real ones! The big-big ones!

嗯，我喜欢这个主意。明显地我在想可能是个游戏室，你知道吗？我的意思是你可以花200美元买到像《太空入侵者》和《小行星》这样的老街机游戏，真正的游戏！那些大的！

Monica Geller: No.

没有。

Chandler Bing: Okay so you mean no as in, "Gee Chandler, what an interesting idea. Let’s discuss it before we reject it completely."

好吧，你的意思是“不”，比如，“天啊，钱德勒，这是个多么有趣的主意。在我们完全拒绝之前，让我们先讨论一下。”

Monica Geller: Oh, I’m sorry. Of course I mean that. Interesting idea, umm, talk about it, but no.

哦，对不起。我当然是这个意思。有趣的想法，嗯，谈谈，但不是。

Chandler Bing: So, that’s it?

就这样？

Monica Geller: I just don’t think arcade games go in the beautiful guest room. The beautiful guest room is gonna be filled with antiques.

我只是觉得街机游戏不适合在漂亮的客房里玩。这间漂亮的客房里会摆满古董。

Chandler Bing: Which is why Asteroids is perfect! It’s the oldest game!

这就是为什么小行星是完美的！这是最古老的游戏！

Monica Geller: What do you have against the beautiful guest room?

你对这间漂亮的客房有什么不满？

Chandler Bing: I don’t have anything against the beautiful guest room, especially since everybody we know lives about 30 seconds away!

我并不反对这间漂亮的客房，尤其是因为我们认识的每个人都住在30秒之外！

Monica Geller: Are you mocking me?

你在嘲笑我吗？

Chandler Bing: No, I’m not mocking you, (in a mocking voice) or you beautiful guest room. (Exits.)

不，我不是在嘲弄你，（用嘲弄的声音）或者你漂亮的客房。（退出。）

🎬 Chandler and Joey's, Chandler is coming from across the hall.

🎬 钱德勒和乔伊家，钱德勒从大厅对面过来。

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, what’s up?

嘿，怎么了？

Chandler Bing: Nothing, Monica and I had a stupid fight.

没什么，莫妮卡和我吵了一架。

Joey Tribbiani: But you’re still moving in together, right? Because my ad came out today. (Shows him the paper.)

但你们还是住在一起，对吗？因为我的广告今天出来了。（给他看报纸。）

Chandler Bing: (reading the ad) "Wanted. Female roommate, non-smoker, non-ugly." Nice!

（读广告）“通缉。女室友，不吸烟，不丑。”很好！

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah? I just figured y’know, after living with you it’d be an interesting change of pace to have a female roommate, y’know? Someone I can learn from, someone-someone who’s different than me. And what’s more different than me; a guy who’s not 19 than say a girl who is 19? Enh? (Points to his head.) Not just a hat rack my friend!

是吗？我只是想你知道，和你住在一起后，有一个女室友会是一个有趣的变化，你知道吗？一个我可以学习的人，一个和我不同的人。还有什么比我更不同；一个不到19岁的男人比一个19岁的女孩更重要？恩？（指着他的头）不仅仅是一个帽架，我的朋友！

🎬 Central Perk, Phoebe is on the couch staring off into space as Rachel enters.

🎬 中央公园，瑞秋进来时，菲比坐在沙发上凝视着太空。

Rachel Green: Pheebs, I have to ask you…

菲比，我得问你…

Phoebe Buffay: Shhhhhh! I’m swamped right now.

嘘！我现在忙得不可开交。

Rachel Green: You’re just staring into space.

你只是在盯着太空看。

Phoebe Buffay: Umm, I’m trying to move that pencil. (There’s a pencil lying on the table.)

嗯，我在试着移动那支铅笔。（桌子上放着一支铅笔。）

Rachel Green: This one? (Picks it up.)

这个？（拿起它。）

Phoebe Buffay: It worked!

成功了！

Rachel Green: Pheebs, this whole apartment thing is just a nightmare! Every place I can afford comes with a roommate who is a freak. I mean, look at this; (Points to one and starts to read it.) "Wanted. Female roommate, non-smoker, non-ugly." It’s just, there is nothing! The city’s full!

菲比，整件公寓的事简直是一场噩梦！我能负担得起的每个地方都有一个怪胎室友。我是说，看看这个；（指着一个开始读。）“通缉。女室友，不吸烟，不丑。”只是，什么都没有！城市满座了！

Phoebe Buffay: Wait, no, look at this! (Points to one.) (Reading) "Two bedroom, two bath, must be non-smoker, Satan worshipers okay…" Oh, yeah, but it’s on the ground floor.

等等，不，看这个！（指向一个。）（读着）“两间卧室，两个浴室，必须是非吸烟者，撒旦崇拜者好的…”哦，是的，但在一楼。

Ross Geller: (entering) Hey!

（进来）嘿！

Rachel Green: Hey.

嘿。

Ross Geller: Rach, uh, you still looking for a place?

瑞秋，你还在找地方吗？

Rachel Green: Yeah! Why?

是啊！为什么？

Ross Geller: Okay, there’s this guy, Warren, from the museum and he’s going on a dig for like two years and he’s got this great place he needs to sublet. So uh, you interested?

好的，有一个叫沃伦的人，他来自博物馆，他正在进行一项为期两年的挖掘工作，他有一个很棒的地方需要转租。你有兴趣吗？

Rachel Green: That sounds great! I’d love to live at Warren’s!! I love Warren! Thank you!

听起来很棒！我很想住在沃伦家！！我爱沃伦！非常感谢。

Ross Geller: Don’t thank me! If you wanna thank something, thank the volcano that erupted thousands of years ago, killing but perfectly preserving an entire civilization. (Rachel just looks at him.) Here’s Warren’s number.

别谢我！如果你想感谢什么，那就感谢数千年前喷发的火山，它杀死了但完美地保存了整个文明。（瑞秋只是看着他）这是沃伦的电话号码。

Rachel Green: Oh, this is great! I am gonna call him right now! (Jumps up.) Oh, thank you!

哦，太棒了！我现在就给他打电话！（跳起来）哦，谢谢你！

(She hugs him and he starts to hug her back but notices the look Phoebe is giving him and pushes her away.)

（她拥抱他，他开始拥抱她，但注意到菲比的眼神，他推开了她。）

Ross Geller: Okay, you go grab it!

好吧，你去拿！

Phoebe Buffay: I saw it.

我看到了。

Ross Geller: I don’t know what you’re talking about.

我不知道你在说什么。

Phoebe Buffay: Umm, I’m talking about that which you already know but won’t admit. You love her again; you re-love her!

嗯，我说的是你已经知道但不愿承认的事情。你又爱她了；你爱她！

Ross Geller: Look, I do not re-love her.

听着，我不再爱她了。

Phoebe Buffay: I can’t believe you won’t just admit it! (Pause) Okay, just promise me that you won’t do anything stupid.

我不敢相信你不承认！（停顿）好吧，答应我你不会做傻事。

Ross Geller: Look, we’re just friends now! Okay? Why would I do anything stupid?

看，我们现在只是朋友了！可以我为什么要做傻事？

Rachel Green: (returning from calling Warren) Ugh!!! Well, the apartment is already subletted! I mean, this is just hopeless. I’m never gonna find anything.

（给沃伦打电话回来）啊！！！嗯，公寓已经转租了！我的意思是，这是毫无希望的。我什么也找不到。

Ross Geller: You can live with me.

你可以和我住在一起。

Rachel Green: What?!

什么？！

Phoebe Buffay: What?!

什么？！

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Commercial Break

商业休息

🎬 Central Perk, continued from earlier.

🎬 中央公园，从前面继续。

Rachel Green: Oh my God! Are you serious?!

我的天啊！你是认真的吗？！

Ross Geller: Uh-huh. (Phoebe grunts.)

嗯。（菲比咕哝着。）

Rachel Green: I would love to live with you Ross; that’s-that’s great! Thank you!

我很想和你住在一起，罗斯；太好了！非常感谢。

Ross Geller: Well, I’m-I’m just glad I could, y’know, help you out.

我很高兴能帮你。

Phoebe Buffay: Wow! I’m-I’m so happy for you guys. (To Ross) This is so-so, not stupid.

哇！我真为你们高兴。（对罗斯）这很简单，并不愚蠢。

Rachel Green: Ross-Ross, you have no idea what this means to me! I mean, I mean I was gonna be homeless. You just saved me! You’re my hero!

罗斯，你不知道这对我意味着什么！我是说，我是说我会无家可归。你刚刚救了我！你是我的英雄！

Ross Geller: Hero, I uh, I don’t know—well, all right.

英雄，我，呃，我不知道，好吧。

Rachel Green: Oh, I have to go tell Monica what a wonderful brother she has! (Kisses him on the cheek and exits.)

哦，我得去告诉莫妮卡她有一个多么好的哥哥！（亲吻他的脸颊，然后离开。）

Ross Geller: Oh please! (He sits down.) (To Phoebe) You’re gonna say things now, aren’t ya?

噢，求你了！（他坐下。）（对菲比）你现在要说话了，是吗？

Phoebe Buffay: No. No, I won’t. But I should tell you this, this exact same thing happened to my roommate Denise. She moved in with a guy who was secretly married to her and he said he didn’t love her, but he really did, and it just blew up! And that’s how she ended up living with me! (Ross looks at her.) (Pause) Okay, that’s a lie.

不，不，我不会。但我应该告诉你，我的室友丹尼斯也发生了同样的事情。她搬进了一个和她秘密结婚的男人家，他说他不爱她，但他真的爱她，事情就这样爆发了！这就是她最终和我住在一起的原因！（罗斯看着她。）（停顿）好吧，那是个谎言。

🎬 Monica and Rachel's, Monica is in the kitchen as Chandler enters.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，钱德勒进来时莫妮卡在厨房。

Chandler Bing: Hi.

嗨。

Monica Geller: Hi.

嗨。

Chandler Bing: Hi, listen, I’m sorry about before. I don’t need to have a game room. I mean when I was a kid I only played those games because I couldn’t get girls, and now I can ‘em—Now, I have you. (Monica glares at him.) Not-not that I think that I have you or think of you as property in any sort of way, I see women…

嗨，听着，我对以前的事感到抱歉。我不需要游戏室。我是说，当我还是个孩子的时候，我只玩那些游戏，因为我找不到女孩，现在我可以了，我有你了。（莫妮卡瞪了他一眼。）并不是说我认为我拥有你，也不是说我认为你是我的财产，我看到的是女人…

Monica Geller: Stop it Chandler. (Chandler is relieved) I’m sorry too.

别说了，钱德勒。（钱德勒松了一口气）我也很抱歉。

Chandler Bing: Really?

真的吗？

Monica Geller: Yeah! Oh yes!

是啊！哦，是的！

Chandler Bing: Ohh.

哦。

Monica Geller: Listen, we don’t have to make that a guest room, we can think of something to do with the room together.

听着，我们不必把那当成客房，我们可以一起想办法处理这个房间。

Chandler Bing: That’s a great idea! We can easily think of a way for us both to enjoy the room.

好主意！我们可以很容易地想出一个让我们俩都享受房间的方法。

Monica Geller: Totally!
