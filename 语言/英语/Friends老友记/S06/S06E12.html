The One with the Joke

有笑话的那个

The One With The Joke

讲笑话的那个

Teleplay by: Andrew Reich & Ted Cohen

安德鲁·里奇和特德·科恩

Story by: Shana Goldberg-Meehan

沙娜·戈德伯格·米汉

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Central Perk, Chandler, Phoebe, Rachel and Monica are there. Ross walks in with a magazine in his hand.

🎬 中央公园、钱德勒、菲比、瑞秋和莫妮卡在那里。罗斯手里拿着一本杂志走了进来。

Ross Geller: Hey, you’re not going to believe this. I made up a joke and sent it in to Playboy. They printed it!

嘿，你不会相信的。我编了一个笑话寄给《花花公子》。他们把它印出来了！

Phoebe Buffay: I didn’t know Playboy prints jokes.

我不知道《花花公子》会印笑话。

Ross Geller: Yeah, they print jokes, interviews, hard-hitting journalism. It’s not just about the pictures.

是的，他们刊登笑话、采访、重量级新闻。这不仅仅是关于照片。

Monica Geller: That didn’t work on mom, it’s not going to work on us.

这对妈妈没用，对我们没用。

Ross Geller: (showing them the page) Here, check it out. It’s the first one, too.

（给他们看页面）来，看看。这也是第一个。

(They all laugh indifferently, except Chandler, who’s a little angry.)

（他们都笑得无动于衷，除了钱德勒，他有点生气。）

Chandler Bing: That is funny. It was also funny when I made it up.

真有趣。我编出来的时候也很有趣。

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Chandler Bing: I made that joke up.

那个笑话是我编的。

Ross Geller: Uh, oh-oh, no you didn’t. I did.

哦，哦，不，你没有。是的。

Chandler Bing: Yes, I did. I told it to Dan at work, and he said it was the funniest joke he’d ever heard.

是的。我在工作时告诉丹，他说这是他听过的最有趣的笑话。

Ross Geller: Hey, tell Dan, ‘Thanks.’

嘿，告诉丹，‘谢谢。’

(Rachel is looking at the magazine and laughing.)

（瑞秋看着杂志笑了。）

Ross Geller: What?

什么？

Rachel Green: I’m sorry, I was just reading the joke below it. Man, that one is funny. (Ross grabs the magazine away from her.)

对不起，我只是在看下面的笑话。老兄，那件很有趣。（罗斯把杂志从她身边夺开。）

Chandler Bing: Monica, you remember me telling you that joke, right?

莫妮卡，你记得我跟你讲的那个笑话吧？

Monica Geller: No.

没有。

Chandler Bing: Seriously?

真的吗？

Monica Geller: Well, you tell a lot of jokes!

嗯，你讲了很多笑话！

Ross Geller: Look, Chandler, it’s my joke. But, hey, if it makes you feel any better they don’t print the name, so it doesn’t really matter who gets credit, right?

听着，钱德勒，这是我的玩笑。但是，嘿，如果这能让你感觉好一点的话，他们就不会打印名字，所以谁得到信用并不重要，对吧？

Chandler Bing: Yeah, I guess.

我想是的。

Joey Tribbiani: (entering) Hey guys.

（进来）嘿，伙计们。

Chandler Bing: (jumping up from his chair) Hey, Joey, Playboy printed my joke.

（从椅子上跳起来）嘿，乔伊，花花公子印了我的笑话。

Ross Geller: No, it’s my joke, it’s mine. You can call them, they’ll tell you.

不，这是我的玩笑，是我的。你可以打电话给他们，他们会告诉你的。

Chandler Bing: It’s my joke.

这是我的玩笑。

Ross Geller: It’s my joke.

这是我的笑话。

Joey Tribbiani: Whoa-whoa-whoa. Jokes? You guys know they have naked chicks in there, right?

哇，哇，哇。笑话？你们知道那里有裸女，对吧？

🎬 Joey’s apartment, Joey is sitting at the counter as Chandler enters.

🎬 乔伊的公寓，钱德勒进来时乔伊正坐在柜台旁。

我们去你家看吧。

不，莫妮卡在看烹饪节目。拜托，我不想错过他们瘦的时候。

什么？不，我想看这个。（他打开电视，屏幕完全被雪覆盖）。你的电缆断了吗？

不，那是VH-1。我得告诉你，这些孩子今天听的音乐。这对我来说就像是一个很大的噪音。我不知道…

Chandler Bing: Joey, why is your cable out?

乔伊，为什么你的有线电视断了？

Joey Tribbiani: I uh, oh! Because, uh, I haven’t really paid the bill

我，哦！因为，呃，我还没有付账

Chandler Bing: If you need money, will you please-please just let me loan you some money?

如果你需要钱，请让我借给你一些钱好吗？

Joey Tribbiani: No, Chandler. Look, forget about it, okay? Look, I know things have been a little tight since Janine moved out. Oh, was she hot.

不，钱德勒。听着，忘了它，好吗？听着，我知道自从Janine搬出去后事情就有点紧张了。哦，她很性感。

Chandler Bing: Whoa ho.

哇哦。

Joey Tribbiani: I know! Yeah, but, look I can handle it. All right? Look, I can listen to the radio, huh? And Ross gave me this great book (holds up the Playboy magazine).

我知道！是的，但是，看，我能应付。好吧听着，我可以听收音机，嗯？罗斯给了我这本很棒的书（拿着《花花公子》杂志）。

Chandler Bing: (picks up the phone) All right, you want to see if the joke stealer will let us watch the show at his place?

（拿起电话）好吧，你想看看那个偷笑话的人会不会让我们在他家看节目？

Joey Tribbiani: Sure.

当然。

Chandler Bing: (with phone to ear, obviously hearing no dial tone) Paid your phone bill?

（电话对耳朵，显然听不到拨号音）付了电话费？

Joey Tribbiani: Not so much.

没那么多。

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Monica and Rachel are on the couch looking at the Playboy magazine. When they hear someone coming, Monica goes to hide it under the sofa cushions.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，莫妮卡和瑞秋在沙发上看《花花公子》杂志。当他们听到有人来时，莫妮卡去把它藏在沙发垫子下面。

（进来）嘿。

（松了一口气）只有你。

你在干什么？

我们在看一个花花公子。

哦，我也想看看！（她跑过去坐下，看了看照片）。哎呀！

那么你认为这些照片是，它们是在讲故事吗？

Monica Geller: Well, she’s not going to find them lying in the grass like that.

嗯，她不会发现他们那样躺在草地上的。

(They flip through the pages to another picture.)

（他们将页面翻到另一张图片。）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, yeah. Aw, remember the days when you used to go out to the barn, lift up your shirt, and bend over?

哦，是的。噢，还记得你过去常去谷仓，提起衬衫，弯腰的日子吗？

Rachel and **Monica Geller: **Yeah.

瑞秋和莫妮卡·盖勒：是的。

Rachel Green: You see, now, I would date this girl. She’s cute, she’s outdoorsy, you know, and she knows how to build a fire. I mean, that’s got to come in handy

你看，现在，我要和这个女孩约会。她很可爱，喜欢户外活动，你知道，她知道如何生火。我是说，这一定会派上用场的

Monica Geller: Okay, I’ve got a question. If you had to pick one of us to date, who would it be?

好的，我有个问题。如果你必须选择我们中的一个约会，你会选谁？

Rachel Green: (thinks) I don’t know.

（想）我不知道。

Monica Geller: Me neither.

我也是。

Phoebe Buffay: Rachel.

瑞秋。

Monica Geller: What?!

什么？！

Phoebe Buffay: I don’t know. (Pause) Me neither.

我不知道。（停顿）我也是。

🎬 Central Perk, Joey, Chandler, and Ross are sitting on the couch.

🎬 中央公园、乔伊、钱德勒和罗斯坐在沙发上。

Joey Tribbiani: You forget how many great songs Heart had.

你忘了《心》有多少首好歌了。

Chandler Bing: Yeah.

是的。

Ross Geller: You know, Barracuda was the first song I learned to play on the keyboard.

你知道，梭鱼是我学会用键盘弹奏的第一首歌。

Chandler Bing: So, you heard it, you repeated it, so that must mean you wrote it.

所以，你听到了，你又重复了一遍，那一定是你写的。

Joey Tribbiani: Oh, you guys, with this joke. I gotta say, I know I cracked up, but I’m not even sure I got it.

哦，你们这些家伙，开这个玩笑。我得说，我知道我崩溃了，但我甚至不确定我是否得到了它。

Ross Geller: What, you didn’t get it? The doctor is a monkey.

什么，你没明白？医生是一只猴子。

(He and Chandler crack up.)

（他和钱德勒分手了。）

Chandler Bing: And monkeys can’t write out prescriptions.

猴子也不会开处方。

(He and Ross crack up again. Joey just sits there)

（他和罗斯又分手了，乔伊就坐在那里）

Chandler Bing: (stops laughing, to Ross) You are not allowed to laugh at my joke.

（别笑了，对罗斯）我的笑话不许你笑。

Ross Geller: Your joke? Well, I think ‘the Hef’ would disagree, which is why he sent me a check for one hundred ah-dollars.

你的笑话？嗯，我想“Hef”会不同意的，这就是为什么他寄给我一张一百美元的支票。

Chandler Bing: So, you stole my joke, and you stole my money.

所以，你偷了我的笑话，还偷了我的钱。

Ross Geller: Well, I was going to stick it in the ATM, but now I think I’ll show the sexy teller that I am a published writer.

嗯，我本来打算把它放在自动取款机上的，但现在我想我要让性感的出纳员知道我是一名出版作家。

Chandler Bing: Well, she is going to know that you stole the joke.

嗯，她会知道是你偷了这个笑话。

Ross Geller: Oh, what are you going to do, follow me down there?

哦，你要做什么，跟我下去？

Chandler Bing: Yeah!

是啊！

Ross Geller: Well, I’m not going to go now anyway (he goes to sit down).

嗯，反正我现在也不去（他去坐下）。

Chandler Bing: Okay (he goes to sit down).

好的（他去坐下）。

(Ross leaps out of his chair and runs out the door, with Chandler in hot pursuit.)

（罗斯从椅子上跳起来，跑出了门，钱德勒紧追不舍。）

Gunther: (handing Joey the bill) Here you go.

（把账单递给乔伊）给你。

Joey Tribbiani: Ah, Gunther, I can’t pay for this right now because I’m not working, so I’ve had to cut down on some luxuries like uh, payin’ for stuff.

啊，冈瑟，我现在付不起这笔钱，因为我没工作，所以我不得不减少一些奢侈品的消费，比如，呃，买东西。

嗯，如果你愿意，你可以在这里工作。

Joey Tribbiani: Uh, I don’t know. Ya see, it’s just, see I was a regular on a soap opera y’know? And to go from that to this, I just… Plus, I’d have to wait on all my friends.

呃，我不知道。你看，只是，我是肥皂剧的常客你知道吗？从那件事到这件事，我只是…而且，我得照顾我所有的朋友。

好吧，但是钱很好，而且你可以随意盯着瑞秋看。

什么？！

弹性工作时间。

也许我可以当个服务员。我能用一下电话吗？

🎬 Monica and Chandler’s bedroom, they are in bed together.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒的卧室，他们一起在床上。

选瑞秋。

哦，好吧，（笑）你不是一个容易上当受骗的人。

Monica Geller: (to Phoebe) You start working on that list. (She grabs her coat and leaves, too.)

（对菲比）你开始写那个名单。（她抓起外套也走了。）

[Cut to the hallway.]

[切到走廊。]

Rachel Green: I cannot believe her.

我真不敢相信她。

我知道。你想去哪里吃饭？

哦，哦，我喜欢那个日本的地方。
