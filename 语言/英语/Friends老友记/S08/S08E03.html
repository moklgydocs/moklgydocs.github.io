The One Where Rachel Tells…

瑞秋告诉我的那个…

Written by: Sherry Bilsing-Graham & Ellen Plummer

作者：Sherry Bilsing Graham&Ellen Plummer

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Monica and Chandler are getting ready to go on their honeymoon. Monica is entering from the bedroom.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，莫妮卡和钱德勒准备去度蜜月。莫妮卡从卧室走了进来。

Chandler Bing: Hey! Babe! Aren’t you excited we’re going on our honeymoon?

嘿！宝贝！我们要去度蜜月了，你不兴奋吗？

Monica Geller: Yeah I am!

是的，我是！

Chandler Bing: (singing) Bermuda, Bahama, come on pretty mama…

（唱）百慕大，巴哈马，来吧，漂亮的妈妈…

Monica Geller: That’s right. Get it out of your system while we’re alone.

没错。在我们单独的时候，把它从你的系统里弄出来。

Joey Tribbiani: (entering with Phoebe) Hey!

（和菲比一起进来）嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah! Have a great honeymoon!

是啊！祝你蜜月愉快！

Chandler Bing: I’d better go pack.

我最好去收拾行李。

Monica Geller: Oh no, I already packed. The only thing I couldn’t find though was your Speedo.

哦，不，我已经打包好了。我唯一找不到的就是你的Speedo。

(Joey looks at him, Phoebe tries not to smile, and Chandler is shocked.)

（乔伊看着他，菲比尽量不笑，钱德勒很震惊。）

Chandler Bing: A Speedo? Uh, I don’t have a Speedo. I’m gonna go pack my regular long bathing suit. (Goes into the bedroom.)

Speedo？呃，我没有Speedo。我要去收拾我的普通长泳衣。（走进卧室。）

Rachel Green: (entering) Oh good you’re still here!

（进来）哦，很好，你还在这里！

Monica Geller: Hey!

嘿！

Rachel Green: I want to tell you to have a good honeymoon! (Hugs Monica.)

我想告诉你要有一个愉快的蜜月！（拥抱莫妮卡。）

Monica Geller: Thank you.

谢谢。

Rachel Green: And I also wanted you guys to know that I am telling the father today. (They all look at her expectantly) What? What? What?

我还想让你们知道我今天要告诉父亲。（他们都期待地看着她）什么？什么？什么？

(Pause.)

（停顿。）

Joey Tribbiani: We know its Ross!

我们知道是罗斯！

Rachel Green: How?! How do you know?

怎么会？！你怎么知道的？

Phoebe Buffay: It was his sweater, but—Oh my God!

那是他的毛衣，但我的天啊！

Rachel Green: Oh, I so wanted Ross to know first, but I’m so relieved you guys know.

哦，我很想让罗斯先知道，但我很欣慰你们知道。

Monica Geller: This is so great! And I’m gonna be your baby’s aunt!

这太棒了！我要做你孩子的阿姨！

Rachel Green: I know! (They hug.)

我知道！（他们拥抱。）

Phoebe Buffay: Me too! (Joins the hug.)

我也是！（加入拥抱。）

Joey Tribbiani: I’m gonna be an uncle! Come here! (He joins the hug.)

我要当叔叔了！过来！（他加入了拥抱。）

Rachel Green: (breaking the hug) You’re all gonna be aunts and uncles.

（打破拥抱）你们都会成为叔叔阿姨。

Monica Geller: Yeah, but I’m the only one related by blood.

是的，但我是唯一有血缘关系的人。

Rachel Green: Okay. Great! So now that you guys all know you can help me. Give me some advice on how I’m gonna tell Ross!

好的。伟大的现在你们都知道你们可以帮我了。给我一些建议，我该怎么告诉罗斯！

Monica Geller: Well, what were you gonna say?

嗯，你想说什么？

Rachel Green: Well I was gonna tell him that I’m-I’m gonna have the baby and he can be as involved as he wants.

我本来想告诉他，我要生孩子了，他可以随心所欲地参与进来。

Joey Tribbiani: Well that, that sounds good.

嗯，听起来不错。

Rachel Green: Yeah but how do I start? I mean, what’s-what’s the first thing that I say? (They all pause to think.) Okay great! Thanks. (She starts to leave.)

是啊，但我该怎么开始呢？我是说，我说的第一件事是什么？（他们都停下来思考）太好了！谢谢（她开始离开。）

Monica Geller: Hey! Good luck!

嘿！祝你好运

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah, bye.

是的，再见。

Joey Tribbiani: Bye.

再见。

(Rachel exits.)

（瑞秋走了。）

Chandler Bing: (entering) Hey, what was that all about?

（进来）嘿，那是怎么回事？

Monica Geller: Well I guess there is no harm in telling you now, Rachel and Ross are gonna have a baby.

我想现在告诉你没什么坏处，瑞秋和罗斯要生孩子了。

Chandler Bing: (in a high pitched voice) What?! I didn’t even know that—Why didn’t you tell me?! (Pause) Why am I talking like this?!

（高声）什么？！我都不知道你为什么不告诉我？！（停顿）我为什么这样说话？！

Monica Geller: I didn’t think you could keep it a secret.

我以为你不能保守秘密。

Chandler Bing: (in the high pitched voice) What?! (Normal voice) I am an excellent secret keeper. I have kept all of our secrets.

（用高音）什么？！（正常的声音）我是一个优秀的保密员。我保守了我们所有的秘密。

Joey Tribbiani: What secrets?

什么秘密？

Chandler Bing: Oh no-no Joey, I am not going to tell you because I am an excellent secret keeper.

哦，不，不，乔伊，我不会告诉你的，因为我是一个出色的保密员。

(The girls walk away.)

（女孩们走开了。）

Joey Tribbiani: (whispering to Chandler) You’ll tell me later?

（对钱德勒耳语）你待会儿再告诉我？

Chandler Bing: You already know.

你已经知道了。

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Joey and Phoebe are entering.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，乔伊和菲比进来了。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, it's so romantic to send people off on their honeymoon.

哦，送人去度蜜月真是太浪漫了。

Joey Tribbiani: Y’know, Monica and Chandler are married. Ross and Rachel are having a baby. Maybe you and I should do something.

你知道，莫妮卡和钱德勒结婚了。罗斯和瑞秋要生孩子了。也许你和我应该做点什么。

Phoebe Buffay: All in good time my love. All in good time. Oh shoot! I left my guitar in their apartment. Well you can let me in later.

祝你一切顺利，亲爱的。一切都很及时。噢，见鬼！我把吉他忘在他们的公寓里了。好吧，你以后可以让我进去。

Joey Tribbiani: I don’t have a key, they took mine to give to you.

我没有钥匙，他们拿了我的给你。

Phoebe Buffay: What?! They took mine to give to you!

什么？！他们拿了我的给你！

Joey Tribbiani: Why would they take away our keys?

他们为什么要拿走我们的钥匙？

Phoebe Buffay: Maybe they don’t trust us.

也许他们不信任我们。

Joey Tribbiani: No that’s not it. They let me keep my key the last time they were out of town.

不，不是这样的。他们上次出城时让我保管钥匙。

Phoebe Buffay: You mean the time you broke the ketchup bottle and cleaned it up with Monica’s guest towels?

你是说那次你打破番茄酱瓶，用莫妮卡的客用毛巾把它清理干净？

Joey Tribbiani: Hey, I washed those!

嘿，我洗了那些！

Phoebe Buffay: No you didn’t.

不，你没有。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah that didn’t sound like me.

是啊，听起来不像我。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, what am I gonna do? I really need my guitar!

我该怎么办？我真的需要我的吉他！

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, I have stuff in there too.

是啊，我也有东西在里面。

Phoebe Buffay: What stuff?

什么东西？

Joey Tribbiani: Monica’s chicken parm! I’ll take care of it. (He picks up the phone) Hey Mr. Treeger, it’s Joey Tribbiani. Listen, I need to get into Monica and Chandler’s apartment. It’s an emergency. (Listens) Uhh, gas leak! Yeah oh, and bring garlic bread. (Hangs up.)

莫妮卡的鸡肉帕尔姆！我会处理的。（他拿起电话）嘿，特雷格先生，我是乔伊·特里比亚尼。听着，我要去莫妮卡和钱德勒的公寓。这是紧急情况。（听）啊，煤气泄漏！是啊，带大蒜面包来。（挂断。）

Ross Geller: (entering) Hey!

（进来）嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Did Rachel find you?

瑞秋找到你了吗？

Ross Geller: No why?

没有，为什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Oh she was looking for you.

哦，她在找你。

Ross Geller: Oh well, I guess I’ll catch up with her later.

哦，好吧，我想我以后会赶上她的。

Phoebe Buffay: Well, she really wanted to talk to you now.

她现在真的很想和你谈谈。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, it seemed pretty important.

是啊，看起来很重要。

Ross Geller: Oh no.

哦，不。

Phoebe Buffay: What?

什么？

Ross Geller: I think I might know what this is about.

我想我可能知道这是怎么回事。

(Phoebe and Joey trade looks)

（菲比和乔伊交换表情）

Joey Tribbiani: Really?

真的吗？

Ross Geller: Yeah. Uh, uh we promised we weren’t gonna tell anybody this but uh, about a month ago Rachel and I slept together.

是的。呃，呃，我们答应过不会告诉任何人的，但是，呃，大约一个月前，瑞秋和我睡在一起。

Phoebe Buffay: (deadpan) And?

（面无表情）然后呢？

Ross Geller: Wow! I thought you would be a little more shocked.

哇！我以为你会更震惊。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh sorry. (Shocked) And?!

哦，对不起。（震惊）然后呢？！

Ross Geller: Well, we-we said we’d just do it that one time but, but now I think she may wanna start things up again.

嗯，我们说过我们只会这么做一次，但现在我想她可能想重新开始。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, I don’t think that’s what it is.

是的，我不认为那是什么。

Ross Geller: Why? What-what else could it be?

为什么？还可能是什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Oh wow, I don’t feel well.

哦，哇，我感觉不舒服。

Ross Geller: I’m telling you. I’m telling you. That’s what it is. No wonder she was looking at me all funny during the wedding. She didn’t say anything to you?

我告诉你。我告诉你。就是这样。难怪婚礼上她看着我很滑稽。她什么都没跟你说？

Phoebe Buffay: (To Joey) Maybe it’s something you ate?

（对乔伊）也许是你吃的东西？

Joey Tribbiani: Please, just-just, just go and talk to Rachel.

求你了，去和瑞秋谈谈。

Ross Geller: Yeah, I guess I should. (Starts to leave.) Man, y’know what I have to realize? Maybe I’m just not the type of guy women can have just one night with. Y’know, they-they always seem to want a little bit more. I should remember that. (He pauses and then exits.)

是的，我想我应该。（开始离开）伙计，你知道我必须意识到什么吗？也许我不是那种女人能和我共度一夜的男人。你知道，他们似乎总是想要多一点。我应该记住这一点。（他停顿了一下，然后退出。）

🎬 The Airport Ticket Counter, Monica and Chandler are standing in line behind another couple kissing who are next in line to be served.

🎬 在机场售票处，莫妮卡和钱德勒站在另一对正在接吻的夫妇后面，这对夫妇是下一个排队接受服务的人。

Ticket Agent: Next?

下一个？

(The kissing couple doesn’t move.)

（接吻的夫妇一动不动。）

Monica Geller: They’re kissing let’s just go around them.

他们在接吻，我们绕着他们转吧。

Chandler Bing: Oh honey, leave them alone, they’re in love.

哦，亲爱的，别管他们，他们恋爱了。

Monica Geller: I’m in love too! But in an orderly fashion.

我也恋爱了！但是以一种有序的方式。

Ticket Agent: Next?

下一个？

Monica Geller: (to the couple) Hi! Can you do that and walk? ‘Cause she said, "Next."

（对这对夫妇）嗨！你能那样做并走路吗？”因为她说，“下一个。”

(The couple moves up to the counter.)

（这对夫妇走向柜台。）

Woman: Sorry. We didn’t hear you; we’re on our honeymoon.

对不起。我们没有听到你的声音；我们正在度蜜月。

Ticket Agent: Oh, let me see what I can do. (Checks the computer) There are some first class seats available.

哦，让我看看我能做些什么。（检查电脑）还有一些头等舱的座位。

Monica Geller: (To Chandler) Did you hear that?! They bumped them up to first class because they are on their honeymoon! Come on! Let’s act like we’re on our honeymoon.

（对钱德勒）你听到了吗？！因为他们正在度蜜月，他们把他们提升到头等舱！来吧让我们假装我们正在度蜜月。

Chandler Bing: We are on our honeymoon.

我们正在度蜜月。

Monica Geller: Grab my ass!

抓住我的屁股！

Ticket Agent: Next?

下一个？

(They go up to the counter.)

（他们走到柜台前。）

Monica Geller: Hi, sorry. I almost didn’t hear you, because y’know I’m just so in love with my new husband. We’re on our honeymoon.

嗨，对不起。我几乎没听见你说什么，因为你知道我太爱我的新丈夫了。我们正在度蜜月。

Ticket Agent: Congratulations. Okay, Mr. Bing you’ll be in 25J and Mrs. Bing you’ll be in 25K.

祝贺你。好的，宾先生，你坐25J，宾夫人，你坐25K。

Monica Geller: Oh no, you see we’re on our honeymoon. So umm, can you do your little thing and bump us up to first class?

哦，不，你看我们在度蜜月。那么，嗯，你能做点小事情把我们送到头等舱吗？

Ticket Agent: I’m sorry, all our first class seats are taken. That couple got the last two.

对不起，头等舱的座位都有人了。那对夫妇得到了最后两个。

Monica Geller: You see, if we’d gone around them like I said, we—She would’ve given us those tickets. Damnit!

你看，如果我们像我说的那样绕过他们，她会给我们那些票的。该死！

Chandler Bing: 25J and K, any chance those aren’t together?

25J和K，有没有可能他们不在一起？

🎬 Joey and Rachel's, Phoebe and Joey are playing Rock ‘em, Sock ‘em Robots.

🎬 乔伊和瑞秋家，菲比和乔伊在玩摇滚，给机器人打袜子。

我还是不敢相信他们拿走了我的钥匙。你相信我和你的。

我当然喜欢！我会在钥匙闪闪发光的地方把它还给你。

（他们听到走廊里传来敲门声，于是去调查。）

那到底是什么？

(They go into the hallway and see Mr. Treeger watching one of New York’s bravest breakdown Monica and Chandler’s door with an ax.]

（他们走进走廊，看到特雷格先生拿着斧头看着纽约最勇敢的人莫妮卡和钱德勒的门。）

哇！哇！哇！崔格，你在干什么？

你说这里有煤气泄漏。

那你为什么不用钥匙呢？

Treeger先生：因为当我在这个东西上找到它的时候（拿着一个巨大的钥匙圈，上面有一千把钥匙），整个地方可能已经爆炸了。如果这种情况发生在我管理的另一栋大楼，人们会开始问问题。（对消防队员）快点！快点。

(With a final swing the door gives way.)

（随着最后一次摆动，门让开了。）

Phoebe Buffay: Oh! We could have done that.

哦！我们本来可以这么做的。

🎬 The Airport, Chandler and Monica are following the previous couple through a tiny hallway that proves this is a set on a sound stage and not an actual airport, and see them enter the first class lounge.

🎬 机场，钱德勒和莫妮卡正跟随前一对夫妇穿过一个小小的走廊，证明这是一个舞台布景，而不是一个真正的机场，然后看到他们进入头等舱休息室。

没有。

就当你是属于你的。

哦，我的天啊！橙子！

嘘！（对柜台后面的人）很高兴再次见到你。（他们试图从他身边走过。）

Airline Employee: Uh sir, may I see your tickets please?

先生，我可以看一下您的机票吗？

Chandler Bing: Yes, of course. (Shows him the tickets.)

当然可以。（给他看票。）

Airline Employee: I’m sorry, would you move your thumb? I can’t see the seat number.

对不起，请你移动一下拇指好吗？我看不到座位号。

Chandler Bing: Oh that’s all right, I have it memorized. It’s 1A.

哦，没关系，我记下来了，是1A。

Airline Employee: (grabbing the ticket from him) Sir, this is not a first class ticket. I’m sorry.

（从他手里抢过机票）先生，这不是头等舱的票，对不起。

Chandler Bing: Apology accepted. Excuse us. (They try to enter again.)

接受道歉。请原谅。（他们试图再次进入。）

Airline Employee: Sir! I’m afraid I’m gonna have to ask you to leave.

先生！恐怕我得请你离开了。

Monica Geller: Fine. (Starts to walk away then she runs over and grabs an orange before she exits.) Go! Go! Go!

好。（开始走开，然后她跑过去抓了一个桔子，然后离开。）走！走！走！

🎬 The Hallway Outside Ross’s Apartment, Ross is walking towards his apartment and sees Rachel sitting in front of the door.

🎬 罗斯公寓外的走廊，罗斯正向他的公寓走去，看到瑞秋坐在门前。

Rachel Green: (seeing him) Hi!

（看着他）嗨！

Ross Geller: Hi.

嗨。

Rachel Green: Hi. (He helps her up.) Umm, I think there’s something that we really need to talk about.

嗨。（他扶她起来。）嗯，我想我们真的需要谈谈。

Ross Geller: (quietly confident) I think we do. Why don’t we go inside? (They go inside.) Look uh, I know why you’re here.

（平静地自信）我想是的。我们为什么不进去？（他们进去。）听着，我知道你为什么在这里。

Rachel Green: You do?

是吗？

Ross Geller: Yeah, and to save you from any embarrassment umm, I think maybe I should talk first.

是的，为了避免你尴尬，嗯，我想我应该先谈谈。

Rachel Green: (warily) Okay.

（小心地）好的。

Ross Geller: Okay. (He sits her down in a chair.) Uh, Ross and Rachel. Rachel and Ross. That’s been one heck of a see-saw hasn’t it?

好的。（他让她坐在椅子上。）嗯，罗斯和瑞秋。瑞秋和罗斯。那是一把非常棒的跷跷板，不是吗？

Rachel Green: (confused) What?

（困惑）什么？

Ross Geller: I mean look, that-that one night we had was fun and…and certainly passionate, but don’t you think it’s better if we just stayed friends?

我的意思是，看，那一晚我们玩得很开心，而且……当然还有激情，但你不觉得我们还是做朋友更好吗？

Rachel Green: Seriously. What?!

说真的，什么？！

Ross Geller: Okay. Okay. Y’know what? If you want to, we can do it one more time. I mean I’d-I’d be okay with that. In fact, I have some time right now.

好吧。好吧。你知道吗？如果你愿意，我们可以再做一次。我是说，我不会介意的。事实上，我现在有时间。
