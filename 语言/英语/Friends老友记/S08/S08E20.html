The One with the Baby Shower

带婴儿淋浴的那个

The One With The Baby Shower

有婴儿淋浴的那个

Written by: Sherry Bilsing-Graham & Ellen Plummer

作者：Sherry Bilsing Graham&Ellen Plummer

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Monica and Phoebe are preparing for Rachel’s baby shower.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，莫妮卡和菲比正在为瑞秋的婴儿派对做准备。

Rachel Green: (entering) Hi!

（进来）嗨！

Monica Geller: Hey!

嘿！

Phoebe Buffay: Hey!

嘿！

Rachel Green: So what’s the final head count on my baby shower?

那么我的婴儿沐浴的最终人数是多少？

Phoebe Buffay: About twenty, a couple people from work who had something else to do.

大约二十岁，一对上班的人，他们还有别的事要做。

Monica Geller: Also both of your sisters called and neither can make it.

你的两个姐姐都打电话来了，都来不了。

Rachel Green: What?! You mean they’re not coming to a social event where there’s no men and there’s no booze?! That’s shocking! I don’t care, as long as my mom’s here.

什么？！你是说他们不会来参加没有男人也没有酒的社交活动？！太令人震惊了！我不在乎，只要我妈妈在这里。

Monica Geller: Oh my God, your mother!

哦，天哪，你妈妈！

Rachel Green: What?! My mom’s not gonna be here?!

什么？！我妈妈不会在这里了？！

Monica Geller: Well, given that we forgot to invite her it would be an awfully big coincidence if she was.

嗯，考虑到我们忘了邀请她，如果她是，那将是一个非常大的巧合。

Rachel Green: My God!

我的天啊！

Monica Geller: Well it wasn’t my fault, Phoebe was in charge of the invitations!

这不是我的错，邀请是菲比负责的！

Phoebe Buffay: Well I don’t, I don’t have a mother so often I forget that other people…

嗯，我没有，我没有母亲，我经常忘记其他人…

Monica Geller: (interrupting her) Oh give it a rest!

（打断她）哦，休息一下！

Rachel Green: So my mother is not coming to my baby shower?!

所以我妈妈不来参加我的宝宝派对？！

Phoebe Buffay: No. (Pause) Neither is mine.

不。（停顿）也不是我的。

Monica Geller: Okay, y’know what? Don’t worry, okay? We’ll take care of it. We’ll call her. Just go home and get ready.

好吧，你知道吗？别担心，好吗？我们会处理好的。我们会打电话给她。回家准备吧。

Rachel Green: Please, make sure she comes. It’s really important to me, I mean it’s my mom!

求你了，一定要让她来。这对我来说真的很重要，我是说这是我妈妈！

Phoebe Buffay: I know. I know, what’s her number?

我知道。我知道，她的电话号码是多少？

Rachel Green: I don’t know.

我不知道。

Monica Geller: Go! I have it in my book. Go! (Rachel leaves and Monica calls Mrs. Green.) (To Phoebe) Wait a minute! If you’re in charge of the invitations why am I the one who has to call her—Hello Mrs. Green! Hi, it’s Monica Geller.

走！我的书上有。走！（瑞秋走了，莫妮卡打电话给格林太太。）（对菲比）等等！如果你负责邀请函的话，为什么我要叫她你好，格林太太！嗨，我是莫妮卡·盖勒。

Mrs. Green: Oh, hello Monica.

哦，你好，莫妮卡。

Monica Geller: (on phone) Hi, umm I know this is last minute, but we’ve decided to throw an impromptu baby shower for Rachel today.

（在电话里）嗨，嗯，我知道这是最后一分钟了，但我们决定今天为瑞秋举办一个即兴的婴儿淋浴会。

Mrs. Green: I know, my daughter’s told me about it when they received their impromptu invitations a month ago.

我知道，我女儿一个月前收到他们的即兴邀请时告诉我了。

Monica Geller: Yeah, I’m sorry. I’m-I’m so sorry.

是的，对不起。我很抱歉。

Mrs. Green: For what dear? For not inviting me or lying about it?

为了什么，亲爱的？因为没有邀请我或是撒谎？

Monica Geller: (To Phoebe) Oh my God, my ass is sweating! (on phone) Please! Please! Can you come? It’s today at four.

（对菲比）哦，我的天啊，我的屁股在出汗！（电话）请！请你能来吗？今天四点。

Mrs. Green: Well all right. I’ll see you at four.

好吧。四点钟见。

Monica Geller: Thank you. (Hangs up.)

谢谢。（挂断。）

Phoebe Buffay: Isn’t it at three?

不是三点吗？

Monica Geller: Son of a bitch! (Calls Mrs. Green again.)

狗娘养的！（又打电话给格林太太。）

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Joey's Apartment, Joey is reading a script as Ross and Chandler enter carrying a basketball.

🎬 乔伊的公寓，当罗斯和钱德勒拿着篮球走进时，乔伊正在读剧本。

Chandler Bing: Hey Joe! You wanna shoot some hoops?

嘿，乔！你想打篮球吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Oh no, I can’t go. I’m practicing; I got an audition to be the host of a new game show.

哦，不，我不能去。我在练习；我得到了一个新游戏节目主持人的试镜。

Ross Geller: Oh cool!

哦，酷！

Chandler Bing: That’s great.

太好了。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah-yeah, and if I get it by day I’ll (In a sexy voice) Dr. Drake Remoray, but by night I’ll be (In an announcer’s voice) Joey Trrrribbiani!

是的，是的，如果我白天得到它，我会（用性感的声音）德雷克·雷莫雷博士，但到了晚上我会（用播音员的声音）乔伊·特里比亚尼！

Chandler Bing: You’ll be perfect for this! That’s already your name!

你会做得很好的！那已经是你的名字了！

Joey Tribbiani: But the audition’s in a couple hours and I don’t even understand the game.

但试镜还有几个小时，我甚至不懂比赛。

Ross Geller: Well do you want some help?

你需要帮忙吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Oh really? That’d be great! You guys can be the contestants!

哦，真的吗？那太好了！你们可以成为参赛者！

Ross Geller: Awesome!

太棒了！

Chandler Bing: Okay, I guess we can lose to junior high girls some other time.

好吧，我想我们可以改天输给初中女生。

Joey Tribbiani: (announcer voice) All right! Let’s play Bamboozled!

（播音员声音）好的！让我们玩“迷惑”游戏吧！

Chandler Bing: Bamboozled?

迷惑？

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, isn’t that a cool name?

是啊，那不是个很酷的名字吗？

Ross Geller: (simultaneously with Chandler) Yeah!

（和钱德勒同时）耶！

Chandler Bing: (simultaneously with Ross) No!

（和罗斯同时）不！

Joey Tribbiani: All right. Uhh, okay. Our first contestant is Ross Geller. Why don’t you tell us a little something about you Ross?

好的。嗯，好的。我们的第一位选手是罗斯·盖勒。你为什么不告诉我们一些关于你的事，罗斯？

Ross Geller: Well uh, I-I’m a paleontologist. Umm, I-I live in New York. I have a son Ben. Uh, hi Ben! (Waves.) And uh…

嗯，我是古生物学家。我住在纽约。我有一个儿子本。嗨，本！（挥手）还有…

Joey Tribbiani: I said a little bit Ross. Now, how about you Chandler?

我说了一点，罗斯。你呢，钱德勒？

Chandler Bing: Well Joey, I’m a headhunter. I hook up out of work Soviet scientists with rogue third-world nations. Hi Rasputin! (Waves.)

乔伊，我是猎头。我把失业的苏联科学家和无赖的第三世界国家联系起来。嗨，拉斯普京！（挥手示意。）

Joey Tribbiani: Excellent! Let’s play Bamboozled! Chandler, you’ll go first. What is the capital of Columbia?

太好了！让我们玩“迷惑”游戏吧！钱德勒，你先走。哥伦比亚的首都是什么？

Chandler Bing: Bogota.

波哥大。

Joey Tribbiani: It’s Ba-go-ta, but close enough. Now, you can either pass your turn to Ross or pick a Wicked Wango card.

是巴哥塔，但离得够近了。现在，你要么把轮到你的牌传给罗斯，要么选一张邪恶的旺戈牌。

Chandler Bing: What does a Wicked Wango card do?

一张邪恶的旺戈牌能做什么？

Joey Tribbiani: I should know that. Let’s see, just one moment please. Umm, here we are, a Wicked Wango card determines whether you go higher or lower.

我应该知道的。让我看看，请稍等。嗯，我们到了，一张邪恶的旺戈牌决定你是走高还是走低。

Chandler Bing: Higher or lower than what?

比什么高还是比什么低？

Joey Tribbiani: This is embarrassing. (Looks it up.)

这太尴尬了。（查一查。）

Chandler Bing: (To Ross) Can you believe how lame this is?

（对罗斯）你能相信这有多差劲吗？

Ross Geller: I’m sorry, I don’t believe contestants are allowed to talk to each other.

对不起，我不相信参赛者可以互相交谈。

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Rachel’s baby shower is underway. Monica and Phoebe are working in the kitchen.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，瑞秋的婴儿沐浴正在进行。莫妮卡和菲比在厨房工作。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh, I told the stripper to be here at five. That’s good right?

哦，我告诉脱衣舞女五点到这儿来。那很好，对吗？

Monica Geller: You ordered a stripper for the shower?! That is totally inappropriate!

你叫了个脱衣舞女来洗澡？！那完全不合适！

Phoebe Buffay: What? He’s gonna be dressed as a baby! (Mrs. Green enters.) Oh hi Mrs. Green!

什么？他会穿得像个婴儿！（格林夫人进来）哦，嗨，格林夫人！

Monica Geller: Hi!

嗨！

Phoebe Buffay: I’m so glad you could make it.

我很高兴你能来。

Monica Geller: Yes, thank you so much. And again, we’re so sorry. We could not feel worse about it.

是的，非常感谢你。再一次，我们很抱歉。我们对此没有更糟的感觉。

Mrs. Green: Try. There’s my little girl. (Goes over to Rachel.)

试试看。那是我的小女儿。（走向瑞秋。）

Monica Geller: She’s still mad.

她还在生气。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah I know. Isn’t it great? One less person we have to make small talk with.

是的，我知道。这不是很棒吗？少了一个我们可以闲聊的人。

Monica Geller: Phoebe, Sandra’s mad at you too. It-it doesn’t bother you?

菲比，桑德拉也生你的气。你不介意吗？

Phoebe Buffay: No look, we’ve apologized twice! I can’t do anymore than that. I know you hate it when people are mad at you but you just have to be okay with it.

不，听着，我们已经道歉两次了！我再也做不到了。我知道你讨厌别人生你的气，但你必须接受。

Monica Geller: Okay. I can do that. (Pause) I gotta go powder my ass.

好的。我能做到。（停顿）我得去擦屁股了。

[Cut to Rachel and Mrs. Green.]

[切换到瑞秋和格林太太。]

Mrs. Green: Look at that face! Just like when you were in high school! If I didn’t know better I’d say you were a cheerleader in trouble. Come on, let’s get some tea.

看那张脸！就像你高中时一样！如果我不知道的话，我会说你是个有麻烦的啦啦队队长。来吧，我们去喝茶吧。

Rachel Green: Okay. (Mrs. Green helps her up and they walk over and get some tea.)

好的。（格林太太扶她起来，他们走过去喝茶。）

Mrs. Green: Oh my look at that. Only three weeks to go, now have you picked your nanny yet? Now I don’t want you to use your housekeeper ‘cause it would just split her focus.

哦，我的天，看那个。只剩下三个星期了，你已经选了你的保姆了吗？现在我不想让你用你的管家，因为这会分散她的注意力。

Rachel Green: Oh well actually gonna use a nanny and uh, I don’t even have a housekeeper.

哦，实际上我需要一个保姆，而我甚至没有管家。

Mrs. Green: It’s like you’re a cave person. Rachel, you must get a nanny. You don’t know how overwhelming this is going to be. I mean when you were a baby I had full time help, I had Mrs. Kay.

就像你是个穴居人一样。瑞秋，你必须找个保姆。你不知道这将是多么令人难以承受。我是说当你还是个婴儿的时候，我有全职的帮助，我有凯太太。

Rachel Green: Mrs. Kay! Oh yeah, she was sweet. She taught me Spanish. I actually think I remember some of it, tu madre es loca. (I think that’s your mother’s crazy.)

凯太太！哦，是的，她很可爱。她教我西班牙语。事实上，我想我还记得其中的一些，那是一种失水的感觉。（我想那是你妈妈疯了。）

Mrs. Green: Such a sweet woman.

真是个可爱的女人。

Rachel Green: Well, however great she was I just can’t afford that.

不管她有多棒，我都负担不起。

Mrs. Green: Oh Rachel!

哦，瑞秋！

Rachel Green: What?

什么？

Mrs. Green: I just had the greatest idea! I’m gonna come live with you!

我刚刚想到了一个好主意！我要和你一起住！

Rachel Green: Wh-wh-what? What?

什么？什么？

Mrs. Green: Oh, I’m so happy I’m gonna do this for my little girl. Aw, look at you. You have tears in your eyes.

哦，我很高兴能为我的小女儿做这件事。啊，看看你。你眼里含着泪水。

Rachel Green: Yes. Yes I do.

是的。是的。

🎬 Joey's Apartment, the guys are still playing Bamboozled.

🎬 乔伊的公寓，那些家伙还在玩骗局。

Joey Tribbiani: All right Ross you’re in the lead, would you like to take another question or spin the Wheel of Mayhem?

好吧，罗斯，你领先了，你是想再问一个问题还是想制造混乱？

Ross Geller: The wheel has not been my friend tonight Joey. Uh, I’ll take another question.

今晚方向盘不是我的朋友，乔伊。呃，我再问一个问题。

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, this is gonna be tough. Hold your breath.

好吧，这会很难。屏住呼吸。

Ross Geller: It’s okay, I’m ready.

没关系，我准备好了。

Joey Tribbiani: No dude, you gotta hold your breath until you’re ready to answer the question.

不，伙计，你得屏住呼吸，直到你准备好回答问题。

Chandler Bing: This is ridiculous, he’s not gonna hold his breath… (Ross cuts him off by taking a deep breath and holding it.)

这太荒谬了，他不会屏住呼吸的…（罗斯深深地吸了一口气，然后屏住呼吸，打断了他的话。）

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, what do you have a fear of if you suffer from this phobia, Tris…Holy cow, that’s a big word. Trisc… Seriously look at this thing. Chandler, how do you say that?

好吧，如果你患有这种恐惧症，你会害怕什么，崔斯……天哪，这是个大字。崔西…认真看看这件事。钱德勒，你怎么说？

Chandler Bing: Let me see that.

让我看看。

Joey Tribbiani: This one right here. (Ross whines.)

这个就在这里。（罗斯呜咽着。）

Chandler Bing: Triscadecaphobia.

三阶梯恐惧症。

Ross Geller: (exhaling) The fear of Triscuts!

（呼气）对崔斯切的恐惧！

Joey Tribbiani: No! No, fear of the number 13.

不！不，害怕数字13。

Chandler Bing: Fear of Triscuts?

害怕割伤？

Ross Geller: It’s possible, they have really sharp edges.

有可能，它们的边缘很锋利。

Joey Tribbiani: All right Chandler, you’re up.

好了，钱德勒，你起来了。

Ross Geller: Wait a minute, I-I believe I’m entitled to use my Angel Pass for a free turn?

等一下，我相信我有权免费使用我的天使通行证？

Chandler Bing: This game makes no sense!

这个游戏毫无意义！

Ross Geller: Y’know what? You’re just upset because you’re losing.

你知道吗？你只是因为输了而难过。

Chandler Bing: Oh come on Ross, I think we’re all losers here.

哦，别这样，罗斯，我想我们都是失败者。

Joey Tribbiani: All right. Chandler, you can either spin the wheel or pick a Google card.

好的。钱德勒，你可以旋转方向盘，也可以选择谷歌卡。

Chandler Bing: Let me think. Let me think—Oh! I don’t care.

让我想想。让我想想哦！我不在乎。

Joey Tribbiani: You-you must choose Mr. Bing.

你必须选择宾先生。

Chandler Bing: Either, it makes no difference.

也一样，没什么区别。

Joey Tribbiani: Choose, you jackass!

选择吧，你这个混蛋！

Chandler Bing: I’ll take a card.

我要一张卡片。

Joey Tribbiani: Okay, you picked the Gimmie card! You get all of Ross’s points!

好吧，你选了那张骗局卡！你得到了罗斯所有的分数！

Ross Geller: What?!

什么？！

Chandler Bing: This game is kinda fun.

这个游戏很有趣。

Ross Geller: (To Chandler) You don’t think it’s a little crazy that you get all my points just ‘cause you…

（对钱德勒）你不觉得因为你…

Chandler Bing: I don’t think the contestants are supposed to speak to each other.

我认为参赛者不应该互相交谈。

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, the baby shower is continuing as Rachel walks over to Monica and Phoebe.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，瑞秋走向莫妮卡和菲比时，婴儿淋浴还在继续。

Rachel Green: Why did you invite my mother?!

你为什么邀请我妈妈？！

Monica Geller: What?

什么？

Rachel Green: She wants to move in with me and Ross to help take care of the baby.

她想搬来和我和罗斯一起照顾孩子。

Phoebe Buffay: For how long?

要多久？

Rachel Green: Eight weeks. I mean I love my mother, but my God, a long lunch with her is taxing.

八周。我的意思是我爱我的母亲，但我的天，和她一起吃一顿长长的午餐真是累人。

Monica Geller: I personally would be honored if she wanted to live with me.

如果她想和我住在一起，我个人会很荣幸的。

Phoebe Buffay: She can’t hear you.

她听不见你说话。

Rachel Green: What? You guys, come on! What am I going to do?

什么？你们，快点！我该怎么办？

Phoebe Buffay: Well, if you don’t want your mother to move in with you, just tell her.

好吧，如果你不想让你妈妈和你一起住，就告诉她。

Rachel Green: You’re right. You’re right. I mean I’m about to have a baby, I can tell my mother that I don’t want her to just be sleeping on my couch! Oh my God! She’s gonna want to sleep in my bed with me. This cannot happen!

你说得对。你说得对。我的意思是，我就要生孩子了，我可以告诉我妈妈，我不希望她只是睡在我的沙发上！哦，我的上帝！她会想和我一起睡在我的床上。这不可能发生！

Monica Geller: That’s right. That is right, you go over there and tell her you don’t want her to live with you. Do not take no for an answer!

没错。没错，你去那边告诉她你不想让她和你住在一起。不要把“不”当作回答！

Rachel Green: Okay. (She goes over to tell her mother.)

好的。（她走过去告诉她妈妈。）

Monica Geller: (To Phoebe) This is great! Now she’s gonna be mad at Rachel! Y’know what? And I’m just gonna swoop in there and be like the daughter she never had.

（对菲比）这太棒了！现在她要生瑞秋的气了！你知道吗？我要冲进去，像她从未有过的女儿一样。

Phoebe Buffay: I have new respect for Chandler. All right everybody! It’s time to open the presents!

我对钱德勒有了新的尊重。好了，各位！是时候打开礼物了！

Monica Geller: Yes! Yes! And I think that the first gift that Rachel opens should be from the grandmother of the baby, because you’re the most important person in this room. And in the world!

是的！对我认为瑞秋打开的第一份礼物应该是孩子的祖母送的，因为你是这个房间里最重要的人。在这个世界上！

Mrs. Green: Well uh, I don’t have a gift because I wasn’t invited until the last minute, but thank you so much for bringing that to everyone’s attention.

嗯，我没有礼物，因为我直到最后一刻才被邀请，但非常感谢你让大家注意到这一点。

Phoebe Buffay: How about you less important people, let’s open your presents!

你们这些不那么重要的人呢，让我们打开你们的礼物吧！

(Mrs. Green goes into the kitchen and Rachel follows her.)
