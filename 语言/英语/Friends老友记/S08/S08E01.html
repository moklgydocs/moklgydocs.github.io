S08E01 The One After I Do

The One After I Do

在我之后的那个

🎬 The Wedding Hall, Monica and Chandler have just said "I do," and the photographer is taking the required pictures. First of Monica, Chandler, Ross and Joey.

🎬 在婚礼大厅，莫妮卡和钱德勒刚刚说了“我愿意”，摄影师正在按要求拍照。首先是莫妮卡、钱德勒、罗斯和乔伊。

Photographer: Great. (Takes a picture.) Great! Just give me a sec to change film.

太好了。（拍照）太好了！给我一点时间换胶卷。

Monica Geller: Okay.

好的。

Ross Geller: (To Monica) Okay, I know I’m not supposed to know, but I do. And I’m so excited for you!

（对莫妮卡）好吧，我知道我不应该知道，但我知道。我为你感到兴奋！

Joey Tribbiani: What? What’s going on?

什么？发生什么事？

Ross Geller: Monica’s pregnant!

莫妮卡怀孕了！

Joey Tribbiani: Oh my God! Is that why you guys had to get married?!

哦，我的天啊！这就是你们结婚的原因吗？！

Monica Geller: Guys! I’m not pregnant.

伙计们！我没有怀孕。

Joey Tribbiani: Ah. (To Chandler) Slow swimmers? (Chandler looks at him.)

啊。（对钱德勒）游泳慢的人？（钱德勒看着他。）

Ross Geller: What?! What do you mean? You-you-you’re not pregnant?

什么？！什么意思？你没有怀孕？

Monica Geller: You didn’t tell anybody I was did you?!

你没告诉任何人我是谁，是吗？！

Ross Geller: No! (Pause) I’ll be right back. (Exits.)

不！（停顿）我马上回来。（退出。）

Photographer: Now why don’t we get a shot of just Monica and the bloody soldier.

现在我们为什么不拍一张莫妮卡和那个该死的士兵的照片呢。

Monica Geller: (To Joey) Oh, about that. Joey, you have to change before the party.

（对乔伊）哦，关于这个。乔伊，你必须在派对前换衣服。

Joey Tribbiani: I can’t! I-I don’t have any other clothes here.

我不能！我这里没有别的衣服。

Monica Geller: Find some! Please! Anything that doesn’t say I-I died tragically in France. (Joey leaves to find clothes.)

找一些！请任何不说我在法国惨死的东西。（乔伊离开去找衣服。）

Photographer: Well then why don’t we see the bride and the groom and the bridemaids.

那我们为什么不看看新娘、新郎和新娘子呢。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay. (Phoebe and Rachel join Monica and Chandler on the altar.) Hey Mon, why did you tell the guys you weren’t pregnant?

好的。（菲比和瑞秋和莫妮卡、钱德勒一起站在祭坛上。）嘿，妈妈，你为什么告诉他们你没有怀孕？

Monica Geller: Because I’m not.

因为我不是。

Phoebe Buffay: We found your test in the trash, if you’re not pregnant—(She sees Rachel shaking her head)—It’s because I am.

我们在垃圾桶里发现了你的测试结果，如果你没有怀孕的话（她看到瑞秋摇着头），那是因为我怀孕了。

(Flash, the photographer takes a picture of Monica and Chandler’s stunned faces.)

（闪光灯下，摄影师拍下了莫妮卡和钱德勒惊呆的脸。）

Chandler Bing: What?! What are you talking about?

什么？！你在说什么？

Monica Geller: What are you talking about?

你在说什么？

Phoebe Buffay: Yes, I…I am with child. (Flash) And I didn’t want to say anything because it’s your day; I didn’t want to steal your thunder.

是的，我…我有孩子了。（闪光）我什么也不想说，因为今天是你的日子；我不想抢你的风头。

Monica Geller: Wait a minute! So you told people I was pregnant?! (Flash) Does this look like a conversation that I want to remember?!

等一下！你告诉别人我怀孕了？！（闪光）这看起来像是我想记住的对话吗？！

Chandler Bing: Who’s the father?

父亲是谁？

Monica Geller: Yeah!

是啊！

Phoebe Buffay: I can’t say.

我不能说。

Monica Geller: Why?!

为什么？！

Chandler Bing: Why not?

为什么不呢？

Phoebe Buffay: I can’t say because he’s famous.

我不能说，因为他很有名。

Rachel Green: Oh my God, who is it?! (Phoebe rolls her eyes.)

哦，天哪，是谁？！（菲比翻了翻眼睛。）

Monica Geller: Phoebe, come on, you have to tell us.

菲比，来吧，你得告诉我们。

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, okay. It’s James Brolin. James Brolin is the father of my baby.

好的，好的。我是詹姆斯·布罗林。詹姆斯·布罗林是我孩子的父亲。

Chandler Bing: As in Barbara Streisand’s husband James Brolin?

就像芭芭拉·史翠珊的丈夫詹姆斯·布罗林？

Phoebe Buffay: What?! Well he never said that to me!

什么？！他从来没对我说过！

Opening Credits

🎬 The Wedding Hall, continued from earlier.

那新娘子呢？

你知道我是新郎吧？有人告诉我这有点大不了。

给你。（钱德勒离开了。）

哦，我的天啊！

哦，谢谢你这么做。我现在还不能处理这件事。

所以你告诉我莫妮卡怀孕了。

你说她是，我只是不同意你的看法。

Phoebe Buffay: Sneaky.

鬼鬼祟祟的。

Rachel Green: Oh yeah.

哦，是的。

微笑吧，女士们。

Rachel Green: Oh! (They smile and the picture is taken.) Oh by the way?

哦！（他们微笑着拍照）哦，对了？

Phoebe Buffay: Uh-hmm.

嗯。

Rachel Green: James Brolin?

詹姆斯·布罗林？

哦，我知道。我只能想到两个名字，他和小埃德·贝格利，然后我想起他是同性恋。

Rachel Green: Ed Begley Jr. is not gay.

小埃德·贝格利不是同性恋。

Phoebe Buffay: (intrigued) Really?!

（好奇）真的吗？！

🎬 The Reception Hall, the party is in full swing.

🎬 接待大厅，派对正如火如荼地进行。

Bandleader: Thank you very much! Ladies and gentlemen, it gives me great pleasure to introduce to you for the very first time, Mr. and Mrs. Chandler Bing!

非常感谢！女士们，先生们，我非常高兴第一次向你们介绍钱德勒·宾先生和夫人！

(They enter.)

（他们进来了。）

Chandler Bing: Before we go out there I’ve got a present for ya.

在我们出去之前，我给你准备了一份礼物。

Monica Geller: Honey, I’m going to put my hand in your pocket!

亲爱的，我要把手伸进你的口袋！

Chandler Bing: No?

没有？

Monica Geller: No.

没有。

Chandler Bing: I’ve been taking dancing lessons.

我一直在上舞蹈课。

Monica Geller: What?!

什么？！

Chandler Bing: Yeah, the last six weeks. I wanted this to be a moment you will never forget.

是的，过去六周。我希望这是一个你永远不会忘记的时刻。

Monica Geller: Oh that is so sweet!

哦，太好了！

Chandler Bing: So? Would you care to join me in our first dance as husband and wife?

那么？你愿意和我一起跳我们的第一支舞吗？

Monica Geller: Yes.

是的。

(They walk onto the dance floor and Chandler slips and almost falls.)

（他们走到舞池上，钱德勒滑倒了，差点摔倒。）

Monica Geller: What’s the matter?

怎么了？

Chandler Bing: I don’t know, it’s these new shoes, they’re all slippery.

我不知道，是这双新鞋，它们都很滑。

Monica Geller: Well, are you going to be able to do this?

嗯，你能做到吗？

Chandler Bing: Not well.

不太好。

(They start dancing and Chandler starts slipping around.)

（他们开始跳舞，钱德勒开始四处溜达。）

Monica Geller: Well, the good news is, I don’t think anyone’s looking at us.

好消息是，我不认为有人在看我们。

[Cut to Phoebe and Rachel at their table.]

[切到菲比和瑞秋的餐桌旁。]

Phoebe Buffay: So, are you ready to talk about it?

那么，你准备好谈谈了吗？

Rachel Green: No.

没有。

(Pause)

（停顿）

Phoebe Buffay: Now?

现在？

Rachel Green: No!

不！

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, we’ll talk about something else then.

好吧，那我们再谈别的。

Rachel Green: Thank you.

谢谢你。

Phoebe Buffay: Who’s the father?!

父亲是谁？！

Rachel Green: Ugh! Look honey y’know what? I haven’t told him yet, so until I do I don’t think I should tell anybody else.

啊！亲爱的，你知道吗？我还没有告诉他，所以在我告诉他之前，我认为我不应该告诉任何人。

Phoebe Buffay: Yeah. That’s fine. That’s fair. Is it Tag?

是的。那很好。这很公平。是标签吗？

Rachel Green: Phoebe!!

菲比！！

Phoebe Buffay: Okay, I’m sorry. I’ll stop.

好吧，对不起。我停下来。

Rachel Green: Okay.

好的。

Phoebe Buffay: Is it Ross? It’s Ross isn’t it—Oh my God, it’s Joey!

是罗斯吗？是罗斯，不是吗天哪，是乔伊！

Rachel Green: Honey, stop it! I am not going to tell you until I tell him.

亲爱的，住手！在我告诉他之前我不会告诉你的。

Phoebe Buffay: Ah-hah! At least we know it’s a him.

啊哈！至少我们知道那是他。

[Cut to Joey entering wearing a preppy tennis outfit.]

[切到乔伊穿着大学预科网球衣入场。]

Monica Geller: (seeing him) Oh sweet Lord.

（看到他）哦，亲爱的上帝。

Joey Tribbiani: I’m sorry! Okay? I went down to the gift shop and it’s either this or a bathrobe! Look, what’s more important, the way I’m dressed or me being with you on your special day?

对不起！可以我去了礼品店，不是这个就是浴衣！听着，更重要的是，我的穿着还是我在你特别的日子和你在一起？

Monica Geller: Honey, I’m not even going to pretend I was listening. (Sees someone else.) Hey! Hey! (Goes over to that person.)

亲爱的，我甚至不会假装我在听。（看见别人）嘿！嘿（走向那个人。）

[Cut to the hallway outside the room, Ross is going to see which table he’s at and sees a beautiful woman doing the same thing.]

[切到房间外面的走廊，罗斯要去看他坐在哪张桌子上，看到一个漂亮的女人在做同样的事情。]

Ross Geller: Hi. I’m uh, I’m Ross. I don’t, I don’t believe we’ve met. I’m Monica’s older brother.

嗨。我是，我是罗斯。我不，我不相信我们见过面。我是莫妮卡的哥哥。

Woman: Oh hi, I’m, I’m Mona from her restaurant.

哦，嗨，我是，我是莫娜，来自她的餐厅。

Ross Geller: Oh! Hello uh, Mona from her restaurant. (He uses his card to mouth those words.) (Pause) Mona, wow what a, what a beautiful name.

哦！你好，莫娜在她的餐厅。（他用名片说出了这些话。）（停顿）莫娜，哇，多么漂亮的名字。

Mona: You think so? I’ve always kinda hated it.

你这么认为？我一直有点讨厌它。

Ross Geller: Aw come on, Mona Lisa?

噢，得了吧，蒙娜丽莎？

Mona: Uh-huh.

嗯。

Ross Geller: Mona umm…Clickclocken. The famous botanist? Huh? Oh no she’s uh—well she’s dead now. No, supposedly she was once quite the hottie of the plant world.

莫娜，嗯……点击时钟。著名的植物学家？嗯？哦，不，她，嗯，她现在死了。不，据说她曾经是植物界的热门人物。

Mona: Really?! Well see? I never knew about her.

真的吗？！好吧，明白了吗？我从来不知道她。

Ross Geller: Linda Clickclocken. (Pause) So what uh, what-what table are you at? (She shows him.) Oh, uh me too.

琳达·克利肯。（停顿）那你在哪张桌子上？（她指给他看）哦，嗯，我也是。

Mona: Oh good. Now there’ll be someone there who likes my name.

哦，很好。现在会有人喜欢我的名字。

Ross Geller: (sexily) Yes there will. (Mona leaves and Ross tries to find the same table.) Oh guess what, Molly Gilbert you’ve just been bumped up to table one. And if it’s all right with you I’m gonna take your place at table six—Martin Clickclocken.

（性感地）是的，会的。（莫娜离开了，罗斯试图找到同一张桌子。）哦，你猜怎么着，莫莉·吉尔伯特，你刚刚被推到了第一张桌子上。如果你不介意的话，我将代替你坐在六号桌马丁·克利克勒肯。

[Cut back inside to Joey giving a beautiful woman a tennis lesson by standing behind her.]

[镜头切换到乔伊站在美女身后给她上网球课。]

Joey Tribbiani: That’s better, now just bend your arms a little more. There you go. Okay, look straight ahead. Now this time I want you to really put your ass into it. (They do a practice swing and she really puts her ass into it.)

那好点了，现在把你的手臂再弯一点。好了。好的，向前看。这次我要你真的投入进去。（他们练习荡秋千，她真的很投入。）

[镜头切换到钱德勒坐在乔伊旁边，他妈妈走过来。他的生母，而不是最近去瑞典一家诊所就诊的母亲。]

钱德勒，亲爱的！看，我的约会对象终于到了。我想让你见见丹尼斯·菲利普斯。

祝贺你。

谢谢你。

丹尼斯是一位亲爱的老朋友，也是一位了不起的情人。

太好了，丹尼斯，谢谢你这么取悦我妈妈。

哦，很抱歉我错过了典礼，我被海选困住了。

哦，是的，丹尼斯正在导演一部新的百老汇剧。

我不相信我们见过面，乔伊·特里比亚尼。

Dennis Phillips: Dennis Phillips

丹尼斯·菲利普斯

哇，多年来我一直很欣赏你的工作。你做了一些非常了不起的事情。

哦，谢谢。如果你不介意的话，我要去喝一杯。马上回来。（走开了。）

哇，丹尼斯·菲利普斯！太好了！你们是怎么认识的？

Chandler Bing: What size shoes do you wear?

你穿多大号的鞋？

Joey Tribbiani: Uh, eleven, eleven and a half.

呃，十一，十一点半。

Chandler Bing: Great, because my shoes are giving me a little problem on the dance floor, can I borrow the boots from your costume?

太好了，因为我的鞋子让我在舞池里有点小问题，我能借用一下你服装里的靴子吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Uh, I don’t even really know where I left those. Sorry.

呃，我甚至都不知道我把那些东西放在哪里了。很抱歉

Chandler Bing: (looking at Joey’s feet) Those aren’t eleven and a half.

（看着乔伊的脚）那不是十一岁半。

Joey Tribbiani: Okay fine! I’m a seven! All right, I have surprisingly small feet. But the rest of me is good, I’ll show ya!

好的，很好！我是七岁！好吧，我的脚小得出奇。但我其余的都很好，我会让你看看！

[Cut to Monica and Rachel at their table.]

[切到莫妮卡和瑞秋的餐桌旁。]

Monica Geller: Can you believe Phoebe got pregnant?!

你能相信菲比怀孕了吗？！

Rachel Green: Oh y’know what honey? Let’s not talk about that right now?

哦，你知道吗，亲爱的？我们现在不谈这个吧？

Monica Geller: This is so huge.

这太大了。

Rachel Green: Sure, but come on, as big as your wedding?

当然，不过别这样，跟你的婚礼一样大？

Monica Geller: Of course not nothing is. Between me and you…

当然不是什么都不是。在我和你之间…

Rachel Green: Yeah.

是的。

Monica Geller: …in this day and age how dumb do you have to be to get pregnant?

……在这个时代，要怀孕你得有多笨？

Rachel Green: Hey! Y’know, sometimes you can do everything right, everyone can wear everything they’re supposed to wear, and one of those little guys just gets through!

嘿！你知道，有时候你可以把每件事都做好，每个人都可以穿他们应该穿的每件衣服，而那些小家伙中的一个就穿过去了！

Monica Geller: How?

怎么办？

Rachel Green: I don’t know! Maybe they have tools.

我不知道！也许他们有工具。

Monica Geller: Well I-I talked to and uh, she’s definitely going to have this baby. Y’know, she said she was gonna raise it on her own.

我和她谈过了，她肯定会生这个孩子的。你知道，她说她要自己养。

Rachel Green: Well, maybe that’s, maybe that’s really brave.

嗯，也许那真的很勇敢。

Monica Geller: Maybe. I just hope she realizes how hard it’s gonna be.

也许吧。我只是希望她意识到这有多难。

Rachel Green: Maybe she hasn’t really thought it through that well.
