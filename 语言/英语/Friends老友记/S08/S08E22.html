The One Where Rachel Is Late

瑞秋迟到的那个

Written by: Shana Goldberg-Meehan

作者：沙娜·戈德伯格·米汉

Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

转录人：埃里克·艾森

🎬 Central Perk, Joey is showing everyone a poster as Ross enters.

🎬 中央公园，当罗斯进来时，乔伊正在给大家看海报。

Ross Geller: Hey! What are you guys looking at?

嘿！你们在看什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Oh, it’s a poster for that World War I movie that I’m in, check it out.

哦，这是我演的那部一战电影的海报，看看吧。

Ross Geller: Yeah? Wow! It looks really violent!

是吗？哇！看起来真的很暴力！

Joey Tribbiani: Uh-huh! I know. I’m coming soon to a theater near you! I’m in THX! I’m unsuitable for children!

嗯哼！我知道。我马上就要来到你附近的一家剧院！我在THX！我不适合孩子！

Ross Geller: Now I cannot wait to see this.

我等不及要看这个了。

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah, yeah, it’s already generating Oscar buzz.

是的，是的，它已经引起了奥斯卡的轰动。

Phoebe Buffay: I started that!

是我开始的！

Joey Tribbiani: I thought I did! Oh hey guess what? The premiere is next week and you’re all invited! (They all gasp.)

我以为我做到了！你猜怎么着？下周将举行首映式，欢迎大家！（他们都喘着粗气。）

Monica Geller: Are we gonna take a limo?

我们要坐豪华轿车吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Sure! Why not?!

当然！为什么不呢？

Monica Geller: Oh I love taking limos when nobody died!

哦，我喜欢在没有人死的时候坐豪华轿车！

Rachel Green: Well obviously I won’t be able to come, for those of you who haven’t checked their calendars today is my due date. Well y’know, I just want to take a moment and thank you guys for how great you’ve been during this time. I really couldn’t have done it without you. And I have loved these last nine months! And even though I am so looking forward to the next part, I am really gonna miss being pregnant.

很明显我不能来了，因为今天是我的预产期，你们中那些还没查日历的人。好吧，我只想花点时间感谢你们在这段时间里表现得如此出色。没有你我真的做不到。过去九个月我一直很喜欢这些！尽管我非常期待下一部分，但我真的会怀念怀孕的日子。

🎬 Central Perk, time lapse, and Rachel is entering still pregnant.

🎬 中央公园，时间流逝，瑞秋进入时仍然怀孕。

Rachel Green: That’s right, still no baby! (To Monica, Joey, and Chandler on the couch) Come on people! Please make some room!

没错，还是没有孩子！（对沙发上的莫妮卡、乔伊和钱德勒）来吧，大家！请腾出一些空间！

Ross Geller: Uh sweetie, maybe you’d be more comfortable here? (Gets up from the green armchair.)

嗯，亲爱的，也许你在这里会更舒服些？（从绿色扶手椅上站起来。）

Rachel Green: You. Like you haven’t done enough.

你。好像你做的还不够。

Ross Geller: Look, I-I know how miserable you are, I wish there was something I can do. I mean I wish I were a seahorse. (She glares at him) Because with seahorses it’s the male, they carry the babies. And then also umm, I’d be far away in the sea. (He sits back down.)

听着，我知道你有多痛苦，我希望我能做点什么。我是说我希望我是一匹海马。（她瞪了他一眼）因为海马是雄性的，它们背着孩子。然后，嗯，我会远离大海。（他坐了下来。）

(Rachel turns and looks at the group on the couch and they move over. Chandler measures the room they’ve made with his arm and decides it’s not enough and they all move over again.)

（瑞秋转过身来，看着沙发上的一群人，他们走了过来。钱德勒用胳膊量了量房间，觉得不够，于是他们又走了过来。）

Rachel Green: God. (Sits down.) I have never been so uncomfortable in my entire life!

天啊。（坐下）我这辈子从来没有这么不舒服过！

Phoebe Buffay: Oh I know, I’ve been there. I remember toward the end…

哦，我知道，我去过那里。我记得快结束的时候…

Rachel Green: (interrupting her) Oh Phoebe, that’s a great story. Can you tell it to me when you’re getting me some iced tea? (Phoebe gets up and Rachel groans.) (To the baby) Oh God, get out! Get out!! Get out!! Get out!!

（打断她）哦，菲比，这是个很棒的故事。你给我拿冰茶的时候能告诉我吗？（菲比站起来，瑞秋呻吟。）（对婴儿）哦，上帝，出去！走出！！走出！！走出！！

Chandler Bing: Let’s. (Everyone gets up and leaves Rachel.)

我们走吧。（大家都站起来离开瑞秋。）

Opening Credits

期初学分

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Phoebe, Chandler, Joey, and Monica are there as Rachel enters.

🎬 瑞秋进来时，莫妮卡和钱德勒、菲比、钱德勒、乔伊和莫妮卡在那里。

Chandler Bing: Hey! Did you have the baby yet?

嘿！你有孩子了吗？

Rachel Green: Do you want me to come over there and sit on you? ‘Cause I’ll do it.

你想让我过来坐在你身上吗？”因为我会做的。

Monica Geller: What are you doing here so early?

这么早你在这里干什么？

Rachel Green: They sent me home from work. They were like, "Start your maternity leave now! Just rest, get ready for the baby." Well y’know what? Screw ‘em! If they don’t want me there, I’ll just hang out with you guys.

他们让我下班回家。他们说，“现在就开始休产假！好好休息，为孩子做好准备。”你知道吗？去他们的！如果他们不想我去，我就和你们一起出去。

Phoebe Buffay: Or you can do volunteer work.

或者你可以做义工。

(Joey’s cell phone rings and he answers it.)

（乔伊的手机响了，他接了。）

Joey Tribbiani: Hello?

喂？

Estelle: Joey! It’s Estelle! Great news, I was able to get you and one guest tickets to your premiere.

乔伊！是埃斯特尔！好消息，我能给你弄到一张首映式的客票。

Joey Tribbiani: One guest? You told me I can have six tickets!

一位客人？你告诉我我可以买六张票！

Estelle: Well, I sold four of them on Ebay. You’ll be sitting next to HotGuy372.

嗯，我在易趣上卖了四个。你会坐在辣妹372旁边。

Joey Tribbiani: Oh my God. So that’s it?! I only get to bring one guest?

哦，我的天啊。就这样？！我只带一个客人？

Estelle: Yeah, what time do you wanna pick me up? (Joey hangs up on her.) Hello?

是的，你想什么时候来接我？（乔伊挂断了电话）喂？

Joey Tribbiani: (to Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe) Did you hear that? I only get one extra ticket to my premiere. So some how I have to pick between you three and Ross.

（对莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比）你听到了吗？我只多得到一张首映式的票。所以我必须在你们三个和罗斯之间做出选择。

Rachel Green: (overhearing that) What-what about me?

（无意中听到）那我呢？

Joey Tribbiani: You said you didn’t want to go.

你说你不想去。

Rachel Green: I don’t. But I would still like to be acknowledged. What? Just because I’m pregnant you think I’m invisible.

我没有。但我仍然希望得到承认。什么？就因为我怀孕了，你就认为我是隐形人。

Joey Tribbiani: Definitely not invisible.

绝对不是隐形的。

Monica Geller: Well, well Ross didn’t care enough to be here, so I think he’s out. You snooze you lose.

好吧，好吧，罗斯不想在这里，所以我想他出去了。你打瞌睡，你就输了。

Chandler Bing: He’s not snoozing, he’s teaching a class.

他不是在打盹，他在上课。

Monica Geller: Well then somebody’s snoozing. Joey, not that this uh should affect you at all, but if you were to pick me, I was planning on wearing a sequined dress, cut down to here. (Points to her stomach just above her belly button.)

那么有人在打盹。乔伊，这一点也不影响你，但如果你选我，我打算穿一件亮片裙子，直接穿到这里。（指向肚脐上方的腹部。）

Chandler Bing: I haven’t seen this dress.

我没见过这件衣服。

Monica Geller: Star in a movie.

在电影里当明星。

Phoebe Buffay: Joey, you pick who ever you want. Okay? You just listen to your heart. What does it tell you? (Mimicking a heartbeat and tapping her chest.) Phoebe, Phoebe.

乔伊，你想选谁就选谁。可以你只需要倾听你的心声。它告诉你什么？（模仿心跳，轻拍胸部）菲比，菲比。

Joey Tribbiani: Well uh…I think I want to take Chandler.

嗯……我想我想带钱德勒去。

Phoebe Buffay: (still mimicking a heartbeat, only faster) Phoebe-Phoebe-Phoebe-Phoebe—Burrrrr! (Mimics the sound of a cardiac monitor going off.)

（仍在模仿心跳，只是速度更快）菲比菲比菲比伯勒！（模仿心脏监护仪发出的声音。）

Chandler Bing: You really want to take me?

你真的想带我去吗？

Joey Tribbiani: Yeah! Yeah! I mean I’m sorry, I wish I can take everybody, but y’know Chandler always supported my career. He’s paid for acting classes and head shots and stuff and well this will be my way of paying you back.

是啊！是 啊我是说，对不起，我希望我能把所有人都带走，但钱德勒一直支持我的事业。他为表演课和头像之类的东西付钱，这将是我回报你的方式。

Chandler Bing: So you’re never actually going to pay me back?

所以你永远不会还我钱了？

Monica Geller: Wait a minute, just because he paid for your head shots you’re gonna take him? Joey, I don’t think you’re comprehending just how slutty this dress is!

等一下，就因为他付了你的人头费，你就要带走他？乔伊，我想你不明白这件衣服有多淫荡！

Joey Tribbiani: It’s not just the stuff he paid for, I mean it’s-it’s everything. Y’know? He read lines with me. He-he went with me on auditions when I was really nervous, and then he consoled me after I didn’t get parts that I really wanted. You always believed in me man. Even, even when I didn’t believe in myself.

这不仅仅是他买的东西，我的意思是，这是一切。你知道吗？他和我一起读台词。他在我紧张的时候陪我去试镜，然后在我没有得到我真正想要的角色后他安慰我。你一直相信我，伙计。甚至，甚至当我不相信自己的时候。

Chandler Bing: I always knew you were gonna make it. I’m so proud of you.

我就知道你会成功的。我为你感到骄傲。

Joey Tribbiani: Thanks. That means a lot to me.

谢谢。这对我来说意义重大。

(They look at each other and smile for a while.)

（他们面面相觑，笑了一会儿。）

Phoebe Buffay: Mon, maybe one of these guys wants to wear your dress.

莫恩，也许这些家伙中有人想穿你的裙子。

Joey Tribbiani: (in a manly voice) I’m gonna go shave. (Gets up.)

（用男人的声音）我要去刮脸了。（站起来。）

Chandler Bing: (in a manly voice) Yeah well, I’m gonna go spit. (He goes into the bedroom. On his way out, Joey gives Rachel a wide berth.)

（用男人的声音）是的，我要去吐口水了。（他走进卧室。在出去的路上，乔伊给了瑞秋一个宽大的铺位。）

Rachel Green: Oh, I have to pee. If I don’t come out in five minutes it’s because I’ve choked to death on the potpourri stink. (Goes into the bathroom.)

哦，我得小便了。如果我五分钟内不出来，那是因为我被花香味呛死了。（走进浴室。）

Phoebe Buffay: When she comes out, you hold her nose, I’ll blow in her mouth, and the kid will just (makes a popping sound) right out of her.

当她出来的时候，你抱着她的鼻子，我会吹她的嘴，孩子就会（发出砰砰的一声）从她身上出来。

Monica Geller: She’s over a week late! She gotta have it today, right?

她晚了一个多星期！她今天一定要吃，对吗？

Phoebe Buffay: I don’t know. I-I think it’s still gonna be a while.

我不知道。我想还需要一段时间。

Monica Geller: Hmm, care to make it interesting? I’ll bet you that she’ll have it by this time tomorrow.

嗯，想让它有趣点吗？我跟你打赌，明天这个时候她会拿到的。

Phoebe Buffay: You’re on!

你上了！

Monica Geller: Okay, how much?

好的，多少钱？

Phoebe Buffay: One hundred thousand dollars!

十万美元！

Monica Geller: How about fifty bucks?

50美元怎么样？

Phoebe Buffay: Fine! I’ll call Zurich and move some money around.

很好！我会打电话给苏黎世，把钱搬来搬去。

Rachel Green: (calling from the bathroom) All right, who’s turn is it to help me get up!

（从浴室打电话）好吧，轮到谁帮我起床了！

(They both look at each other, then Phoebe gets an idea.)

（他们俩都看着对方，然后菲比有了主意。）

Phoebe Buffay: No one’s here! (Monica looks at her.) Oh damnit!

这里没人！（莫妮卡看着她）哦，该死！

🎬 The World Premiere of Over There, Joey and Chandler are arriving in a limo and are about to walk down the red carpet.

🎬 《那边》的全球首映式上，乔伊和钱德勒乘坐豪华轿车抵达，准备走上红地毯。

Chandler Bing: This is so exciting! It’s so glamorous! People taking our picture. How do I look?

这太令人兴奋了！太迷人了！给我们拍照的人。我看起来怎么样？

Joey Tribbiani: A little tall.

有点高。

Chandler Bing: What?

什么？

Joey Tribbiani: Do you mind crouching down a little bit, so that I look taller? (Chandler does so) There you go. (And they walk down the red carpet.)

你介意蹲下一点，让我看起来更高吗？（钱德勒这么做）好了。（他们走上了红地毯。）

Chandler Bing: It’s just so glamorous.

真是太迷人了。

🎬 Monica and Chandler's, Rachel is entering, still pregnant.

🎬 莫妮卡和钱德勒家，瑞秋进来了，还在怀孕。

Phoebe Buffay: Oh hey Mon? Rach is here! Ohh, you’re still pregnant. Oh, I’m sorry. I know how uncomfortable you are. Y’know what? You look great. Yeah, like fifty bucks.

哦，嘿，妈妈？瑞秋来了！哦，你还在怀孕。哦，对不起。我知道你有多不舒服。你知道吗？你看起来很棒。是的，大概五十美元。

Rachel Green: Oh, I have to go pee. Apparently this baby thinks that my bladder is a squeeze toy. (Goes to the bathroom.)

哦，我得去小便了。显然这个婴儿认为我的膀胱是个挤压玩具。（去洗手间。）

Monica Geller: Damnit! Damnit!! Here’s your fifty bucks! (Pays Phoebe.)

该死！该死的！！这是你的50美元！（菲比付款。）

Phoebe Buffay: It’s interesting that you lost. Now, I forget, do you like to lose?

有趣的是你输了。现在，我忘了，你喜欢输吗？

Monica Geller: Now stop it! Double or nothing that she has it by tomorrow!

现在停下来！要么加倍，要么什么都不做，她明天就能拿到！

Phoebe Buffay: Fine! You’re on!

很好！你上场了！

Monica Geller: Okay.

好的。

Phoebe Buffay: Until then, General Grant, why don’t you set up camp (She puts the bill in her bra) right there.

在那之前，格兰特将军，你为什么不在那里安营扎寨（她把账单放在胸罩里）。

Ross Geller: (entering) Hey is Rachel here? We have a doctor’s appointment.

（进来）嘿，瑞秋在吗？我们有医生的预约。

Monica Geller: She’s in the bathroom.

她在浴室里。

Ross Geller: Rach, we gotta go.

瑞秋，我们得走了。

Rachel Green: In a minute!!!

马上！！！

Ross Geller: People ask me why we’re not together, I just don’t know what to tell them.

人们问我为什么我们不在一起，我只是不知道该告诉他们什么。

Rachel Green: (entering) All right, all right. Let’s go!

（进来）好的，好的。走吧！

Ross Geller: Uh, do you wanna go change first? The doctor’s keeping the office open late for us, but if you hurry…

嗯，你想先换衣服吗？医生让我们的办公室一直开到很晚，但是如果你快点…

Rachel Green: No, I’m fine.

不，我很好。

Ross Geller: Really? You don’t think that’s a little inappropriate. (She’s wearing a tank top and has her belly sticking out.)

真的吗？你不觉得这有点不合适。（她穿着背心，腹部突出。）

Phoebe Buffay: Good God man don’t anger it.

老天爷，别生气。

Rachel Green: Ross, it is 100 degrees outside. For the first time in weeks, I am somewhat comfortable.

罗斯，外面100度。这是几周来我第一次感到舒服。

Ross Geller: Fine! Fine! Y’know what? Whatever you want. Okay? You’re the mommy.

很好！好的你知道吗？你要什么都行。可以你是妈妈。

Rachel Green: Oh uh-uh pal! Don’t call me mommy! It’s bad enough you call your own mother that. (He looks at Monica.)

哦，朋友！别叫我妈妈！你这样称呼自己的母亲已经够糟糕的了。（他看着莫妮卡。）

Monica Geller: I’m actually with her on this one.

这件事我真的同意她。

🎬 Inside Joey’s Premiere, he is intently watching the movie.

🎬 在乔伊的首映式中，他正聚精会神地观看电影。

Joey Tribbiani: (onscreen) "I thought I knew who the enemy was, but it was you all along."

（在银幕上）“我以为我知道敌人是谁，但一直都是你。”

Joey Tribbiani: (To Chandler) Okay, this is it. It’s my big fight scene coming up. (He looks over and Chandler and notices that he’s asleep.)

（对钱德勒）好了，就这样。接下来是我的大战场面。（他看了看钱德勒，发现他睡着了。）

🎬 Dr. Long’s Office, Ross and Rachel are waiting for the doctor. Ross is drumming his fingers on the bed.

🎬 朗医生的办公室，罗斯和瑞秋在等医生。罗斯在床上敲击手指。

Rachel Green: Ross.

罗斯。

Ross Geller: Yeah?

是吗？

Rachel Green: Can I ask you something?

我能问你件事吗？

Ross Geller: Uh-huh.

嗯。

Rachel Green: When Carol was pregnant with Ben…

当卡萝怀上本的时候…

Ross Geller: Mmm?

嗯？

Rachel Green: …were you this irritating?

……你有这么烦人吗？

Ross Geller: Wow!

哇！

Rachel Green: Excuse me?!

对不起？！

Ross Geller: Oh nothing. Nothing! Just uh, you’ve been a little short with me lately. I’m not trying to irritate you.

哦，没什么。没有什么！只是，你最近对我有点冷淡。我不是想激怒你。

Rachel Green: Well then you just must have a natural talent for it.

那你就必须有这方面的天赋。

Ross Geller: Y’know what? The doctor will be in soon, why don’t we not speak until then.

你知道吗？医生马上就要来了，我们何不在那之前谈谈呢。

Rachel Green: (silently) Okay. (Pause) Seriously, breathe louder Ross! That’s great!

（默默地）好的。（停顿）严肃地说，罗斯，大声呼吸！太好了！

Ross Geller: Y’know we should probably ask the doctor if she even knows how to deliver a baby that’s half human and half pure evil!

你知道，我们应该问问医生，她是否知道如何生产一个半人半恶的婴儿！

(Dr. Long enters.)

（龙博士进来了。）

Rachel Green: Hi Dr. Long, how are you?

嗨，朗博士，你好吗？

Ross Geller: (to Rachel) Oh, you’re nice to her.

（对瑞秋）哦，你对她很好。

Rachel Green: She has the drugs!

她有毒品！

Dr. Long: We’ll do a quick check.

我们会做一个快速检查。

Rachel Green: Okay. (Rachel lies back.)

好的。（瑞秋躺下。）

Dr. Long: So, eight days late huh?

那么，晚了八天，是吗？

Rachel Green: Yeah.

是的。

Dr. Long: You must be a little uncomfortable.

你一定有点不舒服。

Rachel Green: Eh, just a tad.

嗯，就一点点。

Dr. Long: You’re about 80 percent effaced, so you’re on your way. It still could last a little while longer. If you’re anxious there are a few ways to help things along.

你大约80%被抹去了，所以你已经上路了。它还可能持续一段时间。如果你感到焦虑，有几种方法可以帮助你解决问题。

Ross Geller: Do them!!
