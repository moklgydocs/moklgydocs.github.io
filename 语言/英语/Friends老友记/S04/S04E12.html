🎬 Monica and Rachel's, it’s 0-Dark:30, in other words it’s really, really early. Everyone’s asleep, and all through the apartments not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse. That is except for the chick, who turns out to be a rooster and is crowing in the sun. Needless to say, this awakens Monica and Rachel who rush into their living room, searching for the cause of the sound.

🎬 莫妮卡和瑞秋家，天黑了0:30，换句话说，现在真的很早。每个人都睡着了，公寓里没有任何生物在动，甚至没有老鼠。除了小鸡，小鸡原来是公鸡，在阳光下啼叫。不用说，这惊醒了莫妮卡和瑞秋，他们冲进了自己的房间m、 查找声音的原因。

Rachel Green: What the hell is that?!! (to Monica) What the hell is that? Is that you? (Monica nods her head no, and Rachel realizes what is making that sound.) Ohhhhhhh! (storms over to Chandler and Joey’s with Monica in trail.)

那到底是什么？！！（对莫妮卡）那到底是什么？是你吗？（莫妮卡点了点头，不，瑞秋意识到是什么发出了那个声音。）哦！（和莫妮卡一起冲向钱德勒和乔伊家。）

(Chandler opens the door, finally.)

（钱德勒终于打开了门。）

那是什么声音？

你！

什么样的变化？

好吧，我们昨晚在那里玩了个鬼脸。

好吧！十美元！叉过去！咳出来！付账！给我。
