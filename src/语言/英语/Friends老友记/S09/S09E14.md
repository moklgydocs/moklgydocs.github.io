🎬 *Joey and Rachel's apartment. Rachel is in the kitchen as Joey enters from his bedroom.*
> 🎬 *乔伊和瑞秋的公寓。当乔伊从卧室进入时，瑞秋在厨房。*

**Joey Tribbiani:** Morning, roomie!
> 早上好，室友！

**Rachel Green:** Hey! You remembered to put clothes on this morning.
> 嘿！你记得今天早上穿衣服。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Fifth day's a charm.
> 第五天很有魅力。

**Rachel Green:** Oh, Joey, it's so great to be back here. I gotta tell you, you're making it so easy on me and Emma.
> 哦，乔伊，回到这里真是太好了。我得告诉你，你让我和艾玛很容易相处。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Hey, it's great having you back. You know, stay as long as you want, and when does she stop crying all night?
> 嘿，你回来了真是太好了。你知道，想呆多久就呆多久，她什么时候停止整夜哭泣？

*(Ross enters.)*
> *（罗斯走了进来。）*

**Ross Geller:** Hey, you're not naked! So hey, Rach, when will we expect to see you tonight?
> 嘿，你不是裸体的！嘿，瑞秋，我们今晚什么时候能见到你？

**Rachel Green:** Well, I'll probably be back to pick her up around six, but she's in the bedroom all ready to go. But she did actually fall back to sleep, so...
> 嗯，我大概六点左右会回来接她，但她已经在卧室里准备好出发了。但她确实睡着了，所以。。。

**Joey Tribbiani:** She's probably exhausted from all that adorable screaming she did last night.
> 她可能因为昨晚那可爱的尖叫声而筋疲力尽了。

**Rachel Green:** Bye!
> 再见！

*(She leaves.)*
> *（她离开了。）*

**Ross Geller:** Bye! Hey, I hope Emma isn't making it too hard on you.
> 再见！嘿，我希望艾玛没有对你太苛刻。

**Joey Tribbiani:** No, hey, it's been great.
> 不，嘿，太棒了。

**Ross Geller:** Yeah?
> 是吗？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yeah. And look, I just want you to know that with Rachel staying here and everything, all my feelings from before are totally over, okay? And even if they weren't, when you accidentally walk in on a woman using a breast pump...
> 是的。听着，我只是想让你知道，随着瑞秋留在这里，我以前的所有感觉都结束了，好吗？即使不是，当你不小心碰到一个用吸奶器的女人时。。。

**Ross Geller:** Yeah, that'll do it.
> 好的，就这样。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Wow! So, how are you?
> 哇！那么，你好吗？

**Ross Geller:** I'm, I'm okay.
> 我，我没事。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Really?
> 真的吗？

**Ross Geller:** Sure, I mean, do I wish me and Rachel living together would have worked out? Of course. You know, I'm disappointed, but it's not like it's a divorce.
> 当然，我的意思是，我希望我和瑞秋一起生活会有结果吗？当然你知道，我很失望，但这不像是离婚。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well, actually it...
> 嗯，实际上。。。

**Ross Geller:** No, it's not a divorce, it is not a divorce! Anyway, I think Rachel and I need to, you know, get on with our lives, maybe, maybe start seeing other people.
> 不，这不是离婚，这不是离婚！不管怎样，我想瑞秋和我需要，你知道，继续我们的生活，也许，也许开始和其他人交往。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Wow, really?
> 哇，真的吗？

**Ross Geller:** Yeah, sure, why not? In fact, if you know anyone that would be good for me...
> 是的，当然，为什么不呢？事实上，如果你认识对我有好处的人。。。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Sure, I know lots of girls.
> 当然，我认识很多女孩。

**Ross Geller:** Yeah? Any names come to mind?
> 是吗？我想到什么名字了吗？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Ooh, names?
> 哦，名字？

Opening credits.
> 期初学分。

🎬 *Joey and Rachel's apartment. Joey is there as Phoebe enters.*
> 🎬 *乔伊和瑞秋的公寓。菲比进来时乔伊在那里。*

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hey.
> 嘿。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Hey. I was just gonna get something to eat. You want something?
> 嘿。我正要去吃点东西。你想要点什么？

**Phoebe Buffay:** What you got?
> 你有什么？

Joey (checks the refrigerator): Okay, let's see, we got strained peas, strained carrots... Ooh! Strained plums. We haven't tried that yet.
> 乔伊（检查冰箱）：好的，让我看看，我们有滤过的豌豆，滤过的胡萝卜。。。哦！滤过的李子。我们还没有试过。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Goodie! Thanks. So, how is it living with Rachel again? I mean, apart from the great food.
> 好极了！谢谢那么，和瑞秋一起生活怎么样？我是说，除了美味的食物。

**Joey Tribbiani:** I'm fine, I'm fine, it's just, it's just weird what's happening with her and Ross. You know, yesterday he asked me to fix him up with somebody.
> 我很好，我很好，只是，她和罗斯之间发生的事很奇怪。你知道，昨天他让我帮他找个人。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh my god, Rachel asked me if I knew anyone for her too.
> 哦，天哪，瑞秋问我是否也认识她。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Why are they doing this?
> 他们为什么这么做？

**Phoebe Buffay:** I don't know. They're so perfect for each other; it's crazy.
> 我不知道。他们是如此完美的一对；这太疯狂了。

**Joey Tribbiani:** You know what's crazy? These jars. What is it, like two bites in here?
> 你知道什么是疯狂的吗？这些罐子。怎么了，像是被咬了两口？

**Phoebe Buffay:** I just wish they'd realise they should be together.
> 我只是希望他们能意识到他们应该在一起。

**Joey Tribbiani:** I know, I know. And when they moved back in together, I figured y'know, that's where things were headed.
> 我知道，我知道。当他们搬回来的时候，我想你知道，事情就是这样发展的。

**Phoebe Buffay:** I know. They should be a family. They should get married and have more children.
> 我知道。他们应该是一家人。他们应该结婚生子。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yes, and they should name one of their kids Joey. I may not have kids; someone's gotta carry on the family name.
> 是的，他们应该给他们的一个孩子起名叫乔伊。我可能没有孩子；总得有人继承这个姓氏。

**Phoebe Buffay:** You know what? Maybe once they start dating, and they see what's out there, they'll realise how good they are for each other.
> 你知道吗？也许一旦他们开始约会，看到外面发生了什么，他们就会意识到他们对彼此有多好。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yeah, because it is slim pickings. I had this date last night: Yuck! But we should probably keep it down; she's still in the bedroom.
> 是的，因为这是很难的选择。我昨晚有个约会：恶心！但我们可能应该保持低调；她还在卧室里。

**Phoebe Buffay:** So, what are we gonna do? Are we just gonna go ahead and set them up with people?
> 那么，我们该怎么办？我们是不是要继续和别人一起安排他们？

**Joey Tribbiani:** I know; that just pushes them further and further apart.
> 我知道；这只会把他们推得越来越远。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah. (BEAT) Oh, I know what we can do. We could set Ross and Rachel up on horrible dates, so that they'll realise how good they are together.
> 是的。（拍）哦，我知道我们能做什么。我们可以安排罗斯和瑞秋进行可怕的约会，这样他们就会意识到他们在一起有多好。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Ooh, that's a great plan!
> 哦，这是个很棒的计划！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah, you know what the best part of it is? I get to do my "plan-laugh." (A maniacal laugh follows.)
> 是啊，你知道最棒的部分是什么吗？我开始做我的“计划笑”（接着是疯狂的笑）

*(They both start laughing really loudly.)*
> *（他们俩都开始大笑起来。）*

**Joey Tribbiani:** Shhh! Not so loud, we don't wanna wake up, uh...
> 嘘！别那么大声，我们不想醒来，呃。。。

*(He looks at his bedroom door, but he can't remember the name of the girl.)*
> *（他看着卧室的门，但记不起那个女孩的名字。）*

🎬 *Monica and Chandler's apartment. They're in the kitchen as Rachel enters.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡和钱德勒的公寓。瑞秋进来时，他们在厨房里。*

> 嘿！

**Monica Geller:** Hey!

**Rachel Green:** Well, Phoebe set me up on a date.

**Monica Geller:** Oh my god.

**Chandler Bing:** Wow.
> 哇。

**Rachel Green:** Why? What's the big deal?

**Monica Geller:** Just figured, 'cause you and Ross are...

**Rachel Green:** What, slept together a year and a half ago? Yeah, I'm all set.
> 什么，一年半前睡在一起？是的，我准备好了。

**Chandler Bing:** Well, I think it's great that you're going on a date, you know? I mean, it sounds healthy. I mean, you have needs. Embrace your womanhood!
> 嗯，我觉得你去约会很好，你知道吗？我是说，听起来很健康。我是说，你有需要。拥抱你的女性！

**Monica Geller:** You want a job? Turn off "Oprah," and send out a resume!
> 你想要份工作吗？关掉“奥普拉”，发一份简历！

**Rachel Green:** So I'll bring her by around seven? Is that okay?
> 那我七点左右带她来？可以吗？

**Monica Geller:** Oh, it's perfect.
> 哦，太完美了。

**Rachel Green:** Oh, you guys are gonna have so much fun! She's at such a cute age. Oh, a couple things. Now that she's eating solid food, she poops around the clock. And watch out for your hair, 'cause she likes to grab it. And oh, she's also in this phase where if you leave the room, she screams bloody murder, but ah... Thanks, you guys. Have fun!
> 哦，你们会玩得很开心的！她正处于如此可爱的年龄。哦，有几件事。既然她吃固体食物，她就不停地大便。小心你的头发，因为她喜欢抓头发。哦，她也在这个阶段，如果你离开房间，她会尖叫血腥的谋杀，但是啊。。。谢谢你们。玩得高兴

Chandler (reading the newspaper): Suddenly I wish I was reading my own name.
> 钱德勒（看报）：突然间，我希望我读的是我自己的名字。

🎬 *Central Perk. Joey is drinking coffee by the counter as Phoebe enters.*
> 🎬 *中央公园。菲比进来时，乔伊正在柜台边喝咖啡。*

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ooh, Joey.
> 哦，乔伊。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Hey.
> 嘿。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hey. I'm so excited; I just set up Rachel with the worst guy tonight.
> 嘿。我太激动了；我刚刚安排瑞秋和今晚最坏的家伙在一起。

**Joey Tribbiani:** All right! Who is he?
> 好吧！他是谁？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Well, it's this guy I used to massage. And by massage, I mean hold down so he wouldn't turn over and flash me.
> 嗯，就是我以前按摩的那个人。我所说的按摩，是指按住，这样他就不会翻过身来向我闪现。

Joey (gives a thumbs up sign): Okay, okay. Wait till you hear who I got for Ross.
> 乔伊（竖起大拇指）：好的，好的。等你听到我给罗斯找了谁。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh, yeah.
> 哦，是的。

*(They sit down on the couch.)*
> *（他们坐在沙发上。）*

**Joey Tribbiani:** She's this really boring woman. She's a teacher!
> 她真是个无聊的女人。她是老师！

**Phoebe Buffay:** A teacher?
> 老师？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yeah, yeah, she's really into history and foreign movies... And oh, oh, she loves puzzles. Huh? Come on, who loves puzzles?
> 是的，是的，她对历史和外国电影都很感兴趣。。。哦，哦，她喜欢拼图。嗯？拜托，谁喜欢拼图？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Well, Ross does. What... You're - you're ruining the plan! Joey, you've - you've fixed him up with his perfect woman!
> 嗯，罗斯喜欢。什么。。。你毁了计划！乔伊，你已经-你已经给他安排了一个完美的女人！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Oh my god, you're right!
> 我的天啊，你说得对！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah.
> 是的。

**Joey Tribbiani:** She even reads for pleasure!
> 她甚至读书是为了消遣！

**Phoebe Buffay:** How do you even know a woman like that?
> 你怎么会认识这样的女人？

**Joey Tribbiani:** What? I'm not allowed to know smart women?
> 什么？不允许我认识聪明女人？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Joey.
> 乔伊。

**Joey Tribbiani:** I met her at the library. I went in to pee.
> 我在图书馆见过她。我进去小便。

**Phoebe Buffay:** So now what do we do?
> 那现在我们该怎么办？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well, okay, I'll - I'll just call her and tell her the date's cancelled, and find him somebody else.
> 好吧，好吧，我给她打电话，告诉她约会取消了，然后给他另找个人。

**Phoebe Buffay:** What if we don't find him somebody else? We'll just tell her the date's off, but we don't tell Ross, and he goes to the restaurant and gets stood up!
> 如果我们找不到其他人呢？我们只会告诉她约会取消了，但我们不告诉罗斯，他去了餐厅，站了起来！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Ooh...I hear that's bad.
> 哦…我听说那很糟糕。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ooh, so this is great! Rachel's gonna have a terrible date, Ross gets stood up, and then they'll realise how good they have it together.
> 哦，这太棒了！瑞秋会有一个糟糕的约会，罗斯站起来，然后他们会意识到他们在一起有多好。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Ah, yes, The Plan! (Laughs loudly again, but he sounds more like Santa Claus.)
> 啊，是的，计划！（再次大笑，但他听起来更像圣诞老人。）

**Phoebe Buffay:** It's not Santa's plan. No, it's... (Laughs the real "plan-laugh.")
> 这不是圣诞老人的计划。不，是。。。（笑真正的“计划笑”。）

*(They both start laughing again.)*
> *（他们俩又开始笑了。）*

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yeah, you know, it's not that fun.
> 是的，你知道，这没那么有趣。

**Phoebe Buffay:** No, I think we killed it.
> 不，我想我们杀了它。
