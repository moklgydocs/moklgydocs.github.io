**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E05.mp3" start="0.0" duration="24.648" />

**原句**：Oh right, it's that girl's phone number. There it is, just a phone number a really hot girl gave me. It's no big deal. I almost lost this baby!

**层次划分**：

- ⚠️ 原文 `its that girl's phone number` 应为 **it's**（it is 的缩略，its 是所有格）；场景描述里 `tying to get Rachel's attention` 应为 **trying**（转写笔误）
- **there it is**：There be 存在句的"找到了"用法——配合捡起来炫耀的动作（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **just a phone number (that) a really hot girl gave me**：定语从句省略关系代词（作 gave 的直接宾语）"一个辣妹给我的电话号码"；a really hot girl 名词词组（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **It's no big deal**：惯用语"没什么大不了的"（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **I almost lost this baby**：**almost + 过去式**"差点弄丢"；this baby 指"这张宝贝纸条"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **gives it to Phoebe for her to sneeze into**（舞台提示）：**for + 宾语 + 不定式**（台：不定词）的复合结构"递给她（让她）打喷嚏用"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）

**翻译**：哦对了，是那个姑娘的电话号码。——就在这儿呢，一张辣妹给我的电话号码。没什么大不了的……哇，差点把宝贝弄丢了！

**语法要点**：定语从句宾语省略（Ch.14）；almost + 过去式（Ch.8）；for sb to do（Ch.4）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役动词">

**本集句型一：使役/感官动词 + 宾语 + 原形或分词补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）：

- **make + O + 原形**：It makes them like me / That cold makes you sound so great / Treeger made you cry
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">makes</span> <span class="o">them</span> <span class="c">like me</span></p>
- **have + O + 原形/分词**：he used to have me rehearse / all of them had us making out
- **口诀**：主动句里 make/have/let 后的补语**用原形不加 to**；要强调"正在进行"就换**现在分词**（had us making out）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,动词时态">

**本集句型二：现在完成式三兄弟——经验 / 持续 / 完成进行**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **表经验（ever/never/before）**：nothing that sad has ever happened to me / he's never set foot in a museum
- **表持续到现在（for/since）**：I've been trying to go out with this guy for a month / I haven't stopped thinking about her since the moment I met her
- **完成进行式（一直……着）**：you've been avoiding her ever since we started going out / I've been feeling guilty
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve been trying</span> <span class="o">to go out with this guy</span> <span class="a">for a month</span></p>
- **口诀**：有 for/since 时段 + 动作还在进行 → **have been + V-ing**；只讲"有没有过" → **have + p.p.**

</GrammarTip>
