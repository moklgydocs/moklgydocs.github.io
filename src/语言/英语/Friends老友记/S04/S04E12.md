🎬 *Monica and Rachel's, it’s 0-Dark:30, in other words it’s really, really early. Everyone’s asleep, and all through the apartments not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse. That is except for the chick, who turns out to be a rooster and is crowing in the sun. Needless to say, this awakens Monica and Rachel who rush into their living room, searching for the cause of the sound.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡和瑞秋家，天黑了0:30，换句话说，现在真的很早。每个人都睡着了，公寓里没有任何生物在动，甚至没有老鼠。除了小鸡，小鸡原来是公鸡，在阳光下啼叫。不用说，这惊醒了莫妮卡和瑞秋，他们冲进了自己的房间m、 查找声音的原因。*

<GrammarTip points="文化梗,惯用语">

- **0-Dark:30**：军营俚语时间写法"凌晨漆点半"——形容天没亮的任何时刻（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse**：戏仿圣诞诗 *'Twas the Night Before Christmas* 名句——"万籁俱寂，连老鼠都没动静"；否定名词词组作倒装式强调（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
- **Needless to say**：插入语"不用说也知道"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **searching for the cause**：现在分词作伴随状语"寻找声源"（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）

</GrammarTip>

**本集打赌句型大全**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）——从叫价到成交：

- **金额 + says (that)...**：Ten bucks says that we can name every item in that bag.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">ten bucks</span> <span class="v">says</span> <span class="o">(that) we can name every item...</span></p>
- **I bet (that) + 从句**：I bet you can't guess what color my tonsils are. / I bet it works.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">bet</span> <span class="o">(that) you can't guess...</span></p>
- **Wanna bet? / How about we play for more money? / Deal!**：挑战、加码、成交三连
- **Raise the stakes / spice it up / fork it over / cough it up / pay the piper**：加注与掏钱的花式说法
- **口诀**：bet + 从句用来"断言"，bet + 金额/物品用来"下注"（I'll bet the apartment!）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,短语动词">

**本集短语动词丰收**（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）——两队斗嘴全靠它们：

- **清理类**：get rid of the rooster / give up the birds / throw in the duck
- **情绪类**：back off / get riled up / get mad at us / freaked (out) / go nuts
- **搞钱类**：fork it over / cough it up / pay the piper / pay you back
- **身体类**：come down with something / pass out / grab on（着床）
- **口诀**：动词 + 小品词，意思看小品词——up 表"完/起"、off 表"离开"、out 表"出来/完"、down 表"倒下/患上"
