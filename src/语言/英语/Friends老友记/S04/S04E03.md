- **Wait right there**：祈使句；right 加强 there"就在那儿等着"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E03.mp3" start="11.136" duration="3.696" /> **Chandler Bing:** Oh no-no-no, I’m, <Note>I’m paddling away</Note>!

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E03.mp3" start="50.688" duration="7.416" /> **Ross Geller:** Come on, I think this is a good thing. <Note>I don’t think Mom would’ve hired you if she didn’t think you were good at what you do</Note>.
> 拜托，我觉得这是件好事。我不认为妈妈会雇佣你，如果她不认为你擅长你的工作。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,否定前移,虚拟语气">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E03.mp3" start="50.688" duration="7.416" />

**原句**：I don't think Mom would've hired you if she didn't think you were good at what you do.

**层次划分**：

- **I don't think...**：**否定前移**——语义上否定的是宾语从句（= I think Mom **wouldn't** have hired you...）（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **Mom would've hired you if she didn't think...**：**与过去事实相反的虚拟**变体——would've hired（would have + 过去分词）配 if 从句过去式 didn't think，表"（要不是……）就不会雇你"的推测（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **be good at what you do**：at 后接 **what 引导的名词从句**作介词宾语——"擅长你干的那一行"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：别这么说，我觉得这是好事。我是说，要是妈觉得你手艺不行，她才不会雇你呢。

**语法要点**：否定前移（Ch.12）；would have done + if 过去式（Ch.9）；at + what 从句（Ch.12）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E03.mp3" start="58.104" duration="4.584" /> **Monica Geller:** You don’t have to <Note>stick up for her</Note>. She can’t <Note>here</Note> you.

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,半被动">

**本集 get 家族大盘点：get + 过去分词（半被动/状态变化）**（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）：

- **get + 过去分词（表被动/遭遇）**：I almost **got fired** / I'm gonna **get fired** / The phone **gets cut off** / we're **broken up**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">almost got</span> <span class="c">fired</span></p>
- **get + 过去分词（表状态变化）**：I'm **getting dressed** / you're **gonna get** it dirty 类
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m getting</span> <span class="c">dressed</span></p>
- **get + 宾语 + 过去分词（使役"让……被……"）**：**get you locked up** back / they **get handcuffs taken off** them
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve got to get</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">locked up</span></p>
- **注意**：get 被动比 be 被动更口语、更强调"遭遇/结果"；使役式 get + O + pp 里宾语是动作的承受者

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,情态动词">

**本集情态动词"推测/假设"全家福**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **must have done（对过去的肯定推测）**：that **must've been** awkward / these **must be** Chandler's pants
- **must + have + 宾 + pp（推测 + 使役）**：You **must have me confused** with the Amazing Chandler
- **may/might（可能）**：I **may be** a little while longer / you **might** even **say**...
- **might as well（倒不如）**：you **might as well** just let me go
- **would（假设/委婉）**：that **would make** this a success / it still **wouldn't be** clean / that **would be** something
- **could（可能）**：she **could be** gone for hours / **Could** we **be** more white trash?（修辞问句）
- **强调助动词表反驳**：I **did break** up with her / I **did borrow** your Walkman / I have **so** got it

</GrammarTip>
