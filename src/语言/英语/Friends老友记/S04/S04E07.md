**层次划分**：

- **Is that why you bought all this stuff?**：why 引导**表语从句**作 is 的补语——"这就是你买这些东西的原因？！"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,虚拟与推测">

**本集"虚拟/推测"句型大盘点**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **第三条件句（与过去相反）**：if you **had told** him..., he **would've stepped** aside.
<p class="svoc"><span class="a">if you had told him</span> <span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">would've stepped</span> <span class="a">aside</span></p>
- **must have done（对过去的肯定推测）**：it **must've fallen** out a few blocks back / that **must've been** awkward 类
- **could/would（假设与意愿）**：it **could not get** any worse / I **would never do** that to you / **would it be** okay if I wrote...?（if 从句用过去式表委婉）
- **强调助动词（反驳/坦白）**：I **do have** a couple more / I **sure did** / I really **did forget** my purse
- **口诀**：if 从句往前推一格时态（过去→过去完成），主句用 would have done；推测过去用 must have done

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集名词从句（台：名词从句）三种角色大集合**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **作宾语**：I know **what you're thinkin'** / pretend **that nothing happened** / I think **I know who the other guy is** / I figured **we hit a dog**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">who the other guy is</span></p>
- **作补语（表语从句）**：the craziest thing **is that I just ate a whole pizza** / **that's why** I let people watch / **that's what** I wanted to talk to you about / Is **that why** you bought all this stuff?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">why you bought all this stuff</span></p>
- **同位语从句（that 不可省）**：I got this **sense that** she's smart / the **fact that**... / it's bad enough **that** I'm in love with...
- **口诀**：that 在宾语从句里可省，在表语/同位语从句里**不省**；wh- 从句一律陈述语序

</GrammarTip>
