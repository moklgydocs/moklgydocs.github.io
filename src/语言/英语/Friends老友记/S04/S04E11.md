<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,关系从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E11.mp3" start="48.576" duration="13.584" />

**原句**：They say that our only chance to have a baby is that if they take my sperm, her egg and put it together in a dish and then put it into another girl. So we were wondering if you could be the girl that we could put it into.

**层次划分**：

- **they say (that) our only chance to have a baby is...**：say + 宾语从句；**our only chance to have a baby**——**不定式**（台：不定词）to have a baby 作 chance 的后置定语"我们唯一能有孩子的机会"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **is that if they take my sperm, her egg and put it together in a dish and then put it into another girl**：表语从句内嵌 **if**（口语里 that...if 连用）；从句内 take / put together / put into 三个动词原形**排比**讲试管流程（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **we were wondering if you could be the girl**：**we were wondering if...** 委婉请求的固定模板"不知道你能不能……"；if = whether"是否"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **the girl (that) we could put it into**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）——关系代词作介词 **into** 的宾语故省略，into 滞留句尾"那个能把它放进去的人"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）

**翻译**：医生说我们要孩子唯一的办法就是：把我的精子、她的卵子放在培养皿里结合，再放进另一个女人身体里。所以我们就想，不知道你愿不愿意做那个"另一个女人"。

**语法要点**：chance to do（Ch.4）；we were wondering if（Ch.9）；介词尾置定语从句（Ch.14）

</GrammarTip>

- **I'm really okay with this**：**be okay with**"我能接受"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **'Cause look at them, and I made that**：'cause 原因从句单独成句；**look at them** 祈使句"你看看他们（多开心）"；**I made that**"那份（快乐）是我给的"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **it's gonna be like a million times harder to give up a baby**：it **形式主语**，**不定式** to give up a baby 作真主语；**倍数 + 比较级** a million times harder"难一百万倍"（呼应妈妈的原话）（[Ch.18](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter18.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）
- **it's gonna feel like a million times better**：**feel like + 名词性比较**"那感觉也会好上一百万倍"（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **I wanna carry your baby**：wanna = want to；**carry a baby**"怀（孩子）"——代孕的正式表态（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

**翻译**：不，我是真想通了。知道为什么吗？因为你看他们俩——那份快乐是我给的。我知道送走一个孩子要难上一百万倍，可是天啊，那种感觉也会好上一百万倍，对吧？我想做这件事。——我想替你们怀这个孩子。

**语法要点**：形式主语 + 不定式（Ch.18/Ch.4）；倍数比较级（Ch.7）

**本集句型一：give up / put through / fill in / back up……短语动词的情感负载**（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）：

- **give up**（放弃/送养）：give up a baby / I can't give him up / give up the puppy
- **put...through**（让经受）：putting your body through an awful lot

- **口诀**：代词宾语夹中间（give **him** up / think **it** through），名词宾语可前可后

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,虚拟语气">

**本集句型二：wish / should have / if...were——"事与愿违"虚拟三件套**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **wish + 过去式**（与现在/过去相反）：I only wish I had someone there that had given up babies（真希望当时有人……）
- **should(n't) have + 过去分词**（该做没做/不该做做了）：I really shouldn't have given you the puppy first
- **if + were / would have**（假想）：you might if it were anything like 7! / I would have thought it was the other way around
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">wish</span> <span class="o">I had someone there...</span></p>
- **口诀**：现在相反用**过去式**（wish I had），过去相反用**过去完成**（shouldn't have given）——时态一律"往后退一步"

</GrammarTip>
