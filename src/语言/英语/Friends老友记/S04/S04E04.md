<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,情态动词">

**本集句型二：口语半助动词家族——gonna / wanna / gotta / had better / be supposed to**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **be gonna + 原形**（将要）：you're gonna go up there and apologize / they're gonna take 50 bucks / I'm not gonna be
- **wanna = want to**：I wanna quit the gym / you wanna come / you wanna use our place
- **gotta = (have) got to**（必须）：you gotta be strong / I gotta catch the bus
- **had better + 原形**（最好）：he'd better come up here and apologize / you'd better tell his other wife
- **we'd like to**（礼貌版 want）：We'd like to close our accounts
- **注意**：gonna/wanna/gotta 只用于口语；否定式 not gonna；**don't have to**（不必）≠ **must not**（不准）

</GrammarTip>
