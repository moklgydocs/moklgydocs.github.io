**本集双宾语句型**（S+V+O+O，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）——"给谁什么东西"：

- **make**：I made her dinner. / Chandler 的 make her dinner 同款
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">made</span> <span class="o">her</span> <span class="o">dinner</span></p>

- **show**：she's showing Monica and Rachel (it) / showed her the bill

**本集委婉语气工具箱**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）——把话说得soft一点：

- **would rather (not)**：I'd rather not.（我宁愿不）
