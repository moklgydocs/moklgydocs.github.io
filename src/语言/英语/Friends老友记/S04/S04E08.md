<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,不定式,动词时态">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E08.mp3" start="1.584" duration="10.176" />

**原句**：I know you can't stand to be in the same room as me, so I just thought I'd try and apologize over the phone.

**层次划分**：

- **I know (that) you can't stand to be...**：know + 宾语从句（台：名词从句）；**can't stand + 不定式**（台：不定词）"受不了（跟我同处一室）"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **the same room as me**：**the same...as + 宾格 me**——口语用 me（规范语法派主张 as I，但口语已固定用宾格）（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **I just thought I'd try and apologize**：**I'd = I would**——过去将来"（我就想）不如在电话里道歉"；**try and + 原形** = try to do 的口语（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **over the phone**："通过电话"，over 表"借助……（媒介）"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：嘿，是我。我知道你现在没法忍受跟我待在一个屋里——所以我想，要不我就在电话里道歉吧。我所……

**语法要点**：can't stand to do（Ch.4）；the same...as + 宾格（Ch.7）

</GrammarTip>

**语法要点**：since 原因从句（Ch.13）；who gets who 名词从句（Ch.12）

- **get + O + to do**：trying to get him to talk to me / I'm gonna get you to talk to Chandler
- **want + O + to know**：I want you to know how sorry I am
- **口诀**：want/tell/get/ask 四类都接"宾语 + to do"；换成被动时 to 才显现（I was told **to** follow...）
