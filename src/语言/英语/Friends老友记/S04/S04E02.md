<GrammarTip points="动词时态,副词">

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E02.mp3" start="5.448" duration="7.728" /> **Joey Tribbiani:** <Note>How many times do I have to tell you</Note>! Ya, <Note>turn and sliiiide</Note>! Y'know, turn and slide.

<GrammarTip points="感叹句,对等连接词">

- **How many times do I have to tell you!**：how many times 开头的**感叹句**（不是疑问句）——"我都跟你说过多少遍了！"；have to"不得不"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **turn and slide**：and 连接两个并列原形动词，动作一气呵成"一转、一滑"（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E02.mp3" start="13.176" duration="7.848" /> **Chandler Bing:** <Note>You don’t turn and slide</Note>, <Note>you throw it out</Note>! <Note>I’m tired of having to get a tetanus shot every time I get dressed</Note>!
> 你不会转身滑倒，你会把它扔出去！我厌倦了每次穿衣服都要打破伤风针！
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,动名词,副词从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E02.mp3" start="13.176" duration="7.848" />

**原句**：You don't turn and slide, you throw it out! I'm tired of having to get a tetanus shot every time I get dressed!

**层次划分**：

- **You don't turn and slide, you throw it out**：对比两个口语小句——"正确的做法不是转身滑，是直接扔出去！"；**throw out**"扔掉"，代词 it 夹中间（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **I'm tired of having to get a tetanus shot**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m tired of</span> <span class="o">having to get a tetanus shot</span></p> **be tired of + 动名词**"受够了……"；动名词短语内再套 have to get"不得不去（打针）"（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **every time I get dressed**：**every time** 作连接词引导时间**状语从句**（台：副词从句）"每次我穿衣服（都）"；get dressed"穿衣服"（get + 过去分词）（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）

**翻译**：不是叫你转身滑——是把它扔出去！我受够了每回穿件衣服都得去打破伤风针！

**语法要点**：be tired of doing（Ch.5）；every time 引导时间从句（Ch.13）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E02.mp3" start="21.024" duration="5.472" /> **Joey Tribbiani:** Look, <Note>we’re not throwing it out</Note>! <Note>I built this thing with my own hands</Note>!

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E02.mp3" start="34.896" duration="5.424" /> **Chandler Bing:** (nods his head) <Note>Do you mind if we stick you in another cabinet</Note>? (to Joey) <Note>They seem all right with it</Note>!

**层次划分**：

**本集"动词 + 宾语 + 补语"句型家族**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **let + O + 原形**：Let it go! / I never let her eat. / let one rip
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Let</span> <span class="o">it</span> <span class="c">go</span></p>
- **make + O + 形容词**：it's made her so happy / it's making...jealous
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">'s made</span> <span class="o">her</span> <span class="c">so happy</span></p>
- **have + O + 过去分词**（使役"请人做"）：they had to have that specially made
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">they</span> <span class="v">had</span> <span class="o">that (uniform)</span> <span class="c">specially made</span></p>
- **find + O + 过去分词**（发现状态）：what I just found tacked up on a telephone pole
- **口诀**：补语"用什么词"看动词——let 用原形、make 用形容词、have/find 用过去分词

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集名词从句（台：名词从句）四兄弟**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **think/believe/say + (that) 从句**：I don't think any of them are gonna work out / I believe this is my Mother
- **wonder/know + what/where/how 从句**：I wonder what he wants / I don't know what to say / you know what that means
- **be sure/sorry/worried + (that) 从句**：Are you sure she's in the cat / she's worried that she's gonna be replaced
- **名词 + that 同位语从句**：the feeling that this cat is my Mother / the fact that there's a new mom
- **口诀**：that 在宾语从句里可省、在同位语从句里**不省**；wh- 词开头的从句一律陈述语序
