<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E01.mp3" start="0.0" duration="7.392" /> **Joey Tribbiani:** (lying on a beach towel, recapping what happened in the last episode) Okay, so we went to the beach, because Phoebe <Note>found out about this lady who knew her mom and dad</Note>, and I don’t really know <Note>what happened with that</Note>.
> （躺在沙滩浴巾上，回顾上一集发生的事情）好吧，我们去了海滩，因为菲比发现了一位认识她父母的女士，我真的不知道发生了什么。

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E01.mp3" start="0.0" duration="7.392" />

**原句**：We went to the beach, because Phoebe found out about this lady who knew her mom and dad, and I don't really know what happened with that.

**层次划分**：

- **because + 从句**：原因状语从句（台：副词从句），交代去海滩的缘由（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **find out about + 名词**："打听到、发现（关于……的事）"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **this lady who knew her mom and dad**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 lady，关系代词 who 在从句中作主语，**不可省略**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- 主干2：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">don't know</span> <span class="o">what happened with that</span></p> **what happened with that**：what 引导**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），what 本身作从句主语"那事儿后来怎样了"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- Joey 的回顾套路：每条八卦都以"我也不知道后来咋样了"收尾

**翻译**：话说我们去了海滩，因为 Phoebe 打听到一个认识她爸妈的女士——至于后来怎么样，我是真不知道。

**语法要点**：who 作主语不可省（Ch.14）；what 引导宾语从句（Ch.12）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,关系从句">

**本集定语从句（台：关系从句）省略与不可省**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **作宾语，可省**：some things (that) I've been thinking about / something (that) you can do / all that sleep (that) you're gonna miss
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">some things (that)</span> <span class="v">I've been thinking about</span></p>
- **作主语，不可省**：this lady **who** knew her mom and dad / everything **that** went wrong / the guy **who** peed on me
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">everything (that)</span> <span class="v">went wrong</span></p>
- **介词悬空，关系代词可省**：the way (that) you owned up to everything / which you took full responsibility **for** / nothing to worry **about**
- **口诀**：关系代词在从句里当主语→必须留；当宾语（含介词宾语）→口语常省

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,语气">

**本集"对过去的虚拟与回顾"：wish/if + 过去式·过去完成式**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **wish + 过去完成式**（悔不当初）：I just wish we **hadn't lost** those four months.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">wish</span> <span class="o">we hadn't lost those four months</span></p>
- **if + 过去式**（与现在相反）：if we **were** a couple, we **could play** this game naked / if time **was** what you needed...
- **过去完成式叙事**（过去的过去）：my Dad **had run off** / I'**d seen** this thing on The Discovery Channel / you **had rambled on** for 18 pages
- **口诀**：从现在往后退一步用过去式，从过去再退一步用 had done

</GrammarTip>
