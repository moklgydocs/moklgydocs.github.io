- **pay/offer/hand 类**：you **paid me 50 bucks** / he **offered me the job** / **Hand me those tomatoes** / I **got us a job**
- **tell 类**：**tell your father** how much we're gonna miss him
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">offered</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="o">the job</span></p>
- **口诀**：两个宾语都是"人 + 物"（先人后物）；换成"物"在前要加 **to/for**——offer the job **to** me

</GrammarTip>
