**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E10.mp3" start="25.512" duration="11.328" />

**原句**：I sat near the door, so she’d have to pass by me if she wanted to switch cars.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">’d have to pass by</span> <span class="o">me</span></p> she’d = she would，**would 表"（当时看来）将要"**——站在过去时间点看未来；have to"不得不、只能"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **if she wanted to switch cars**：if 条件**状语从句**（台：副词从句）用过去式 wanted，与主句 would 时态呼应；want to + 原形，**不定式**（台：不定词）作 want 的宾语；switch cars"换车厢"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **so** 此处是对等连接词"所以、这样一来"，连接两个并列分句（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **it was just me and her**：口语以宾格 me/her 作补语（规范写法为 she and I，口语通例）；at the back of"在……尾部"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **at my mercy**：习语"任我摆布"，at + 名词构成的介词短语（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：我坐在靠门的位置，这样她想换车厢就必须从我面前走过。她完全落在我的掌控之中。

**语法要点**：would 表过去将来（Ch.9/Ch.3）；if 从句与主句时态呼应（Ch.13）；so 作对等连接词（Ch.15）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,语气">

- **Why don’t you + 原形**：Why don’t you just yell at them? / why don’t you fire me?

</GrammarTip>

- **It’s been a long time since + 完成式/过去式**：it’s been a long time since I’ve been flung / It’s been a long time since I had... 327 + 238 dollars!
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">It</span> <span class="v">’s been</span> <span class="c">a long time</span> <span class="a">since I’ve been flung</span></p>
- **口语变体**：it’s been a long time (that) I’ve been single——用 that 顶替 since
- **注意**：主句用**完成式 has been**（从过去持续到现在的时长）；since 从句用过去式或现在完成式都可；中文译作"自从……已经（多久）了"
