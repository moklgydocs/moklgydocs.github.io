**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E13.mp3" start="68.616" duration="6.576" />

**原句**：What is that? Like where you walk around with snooty rich people and tell them what to buy?

**层次划分**：

- **Like (it's) where you walk around...**：口语 like 引出解释"就是那种感觉……"；where 引导**表语性质的名词从句**（台：名词从句），where = the situation in which（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- 从句内主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">walk around</span> <span class="a">with snooty rich people</span> <span class="conj">and</span> <span class="v">tell</span> <span class="o">them</span> <span class="o">what to buy</span></p> and 连接两个并列谓语（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）

- **snooty**：形容词"目中无人的"修饰 people

**翻译**：私人购物？那是干嘛的？是不是就像——你陪着那些拽哄哄的有钱人逛，然后告诉他们该买什么？

**语法要点**：where 名词从句（Ch.12）；疑问词 + 不定式作宾语（Ch.18）；并列谓语（Ch.15）

- **seems to have left**：**不定式完成式 to have done**——"似乎已经落下了"，动作发生在 seems 之前（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E13.mp3" start="1099.176" duration="24.216" />

**原句**：I left my wallet here on purpose. Yes, I just wanted to see you again. Rachel, I'd like to say something to you. How you doin'?

**层次划分**：

- **I left my wallet here on purpose**：**on purpose**"故意地"；leave + 物 + 地点"把……落在某处"（呼应场景二 Joey 的 left a donut up here）（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **I just wanted to see you again**：**wanted to + 原形**用过去式把心意说得含蓄；just"只是"降低唐突感（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **I'd like to say something to you**：**would like to + 原形**"我想要……"的礼貌版；say something to sb"对某人说些话"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **How you doin'?**：Joey 金句压轴返场——Rachel 终究活成了 Joey 的样子（场景八已详解）

**翻译**：（一人分饰两角）"你好，Rachel。""嗨，Joshua。""我是故意把钱包落在这儿的。""真的吗？""真的，我就是想再见你一面。""哦，我好开心。""Rachel，我有话想对你说。""什么话？""How you doin'?"

**语法要点**：on purpose（Ch.10）；wanted to / would like to 的委婉过去式与礼貌式（Ch.9/Ch.4）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,邀请与建议">

**本集邀约/建议句型大盘点**——Rachel 全剧都在练"怎么开口"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **Would you like to + 原形...?**（最正式）：Would you like to go to a basketball game with me?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">would like</span> <span class="o">to go...</span></p>
- **Why don't you + 原形...?**（最顺口）：Why don't you ask him out? / why don't you call me when you grow up!
<p class="svoc"><span class="a">Why</span> <span class="v">don’t</span> <span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">ask</span> <span class="o">him</span> <span class="a">out</span></p>
- **I've got + 票/东西, if you're interested**（最委婉）：I have two tickets to the Knicks game tonight if you're interested.
- **What if + 过去式...?**（假设推演）：What if I was wrong? / what if she didn't actually sleep with the guy?
- **口诀**：邀请用 would like to，怂恿用 why don't you，递台阶用 if you're interested，脑内小剧场用 what if

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,现在完成式">

**本集现在完成式两大高频用法**（台：现在完成式，[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **表经历（常配 never / ever / before）**：I've never asked a guy out before. / have I ever had chemistry on stage? / You've never done that. / we've had our first fight
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">’ve never asked</span> <span class="o">a guy</span> <span class="a">out before</span></p>
- **表"至今结果"（常与 just / already / yet 呼应）**：he has not asked me out（到现在还没约我）/ I've just been fiddling around（一直在鼓捣）/ They've suffered enough
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">has not asked</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="a">out</span></p>
- **对照过去式**：Did she call?（那一刻打了没）vs Has she called?（到现在打了没）——美式口语常混用，但 never/ever/before 只认完成式

</GrammarTip>
