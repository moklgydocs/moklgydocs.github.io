<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,存在句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E15.mp3" start="3.6" duration="14.4" />

**原句**：I can't believe I'm getting my nails done! You said it was gonna be fun! Which it kinda is. You said there would be other guys here. There are no other guys here!

**层次划分**：

- **I can't believe (that) I'm getting my nails done**：believe + 宾语从句（台：名词从句）；从句内 **get my nails done**——get + 宾语 + 过去分词"（请人）做指甲"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **you said it was gonna be fun**：said + 宾语从句；was gonna 过去将来（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **Which it kinda is (fun)**：**which 非限定定语从句**（台：关系从句）指代上句整件事——"结果还真有点意思"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **you said there would be other guys here**：从句内 **there would be**——There be 的过去将来式"（你说）这儿会有别的男的"（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **There are no other guys here!**：There be 存在句 + no"一个别的男的都**没有**！"（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

**翻译**：我真不敢相信我居然在做指甲！你说会好玩的！——好吧确实有点。还有，你说这儿会有别的男人——这儿根本没有别的男人！

**语法要点**：get sth done（Ch.6）；which 指代整句（Ch.14）；there would be（Ch.3）

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S04/S04E15.mp3" start="28.008" duration="12.816" />

**原句**：This doesn't make you any less of a guy! That does! What am I sitting on? I hate to think what this woman was scratching when this broke off.

**层次划分**：

- **This doesn't make you any less of a guy**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">This</span> <span class="v">doesn't make</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">any less of a guy</span></p> **make + 宾语 + 名词补语**"这并不会让你**少一分**男子气"；any less of 用于否定（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **That does!**：**代动词 does** = makes you less of a guy"那（吹指甲）才真叫娘！"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **What am I sitting on?**：疑问句中介词 on **滞留**句尾"我这是坐到啥了？！"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **I hate to think what this woman was scratching when this broke off**：hate + **不定式**（台：不定词）to think；think 后接 **what 引导的宾语从句**（台：名词从句）；从句内过去进行式 was scratching + **when this broke off** 时间状语从句"这（指甲）崩断的那一刻她在挠什么"——broke off"折断"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）

**翻译**：别担心！做指甲不会折损你的男子气概！（Chandler 开始学女人吹指甲）——**那个**才会！……我屁股底下坐了个啥？（发现一根巨型指甲片）我真不敢想，这玩意儿崩掉的时候，那女的在挠什么。

**语法要点**：any less of（Ch.8）；hate to think + what 从句（Ch.12）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役感官">

**本集"使役/感官动词 + 宾语 + 补语"句型**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **let + O + 原形**：I am not letting you get away this time / let me help you
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m not letting</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">get away</span></p>
- **make + O + 形容词/原形**：This doesn't make you any less of a guy / it makes me look taller / I made a man twice my size cry
- **see/hear + O + 原形或分词**：see you take-off / hear the actor sing Old Man River
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">wanna see</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">take off</span></p>
- **get + O + 过去分词**：getting my nails done
- **口诀**：看动词选补语——let/see/hear 用原形，make 用形容词或原形，get/have 表"请人做"用过去分词

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,时间状语从句">

**本集时间/条件从句的"现在式代未来"**（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）：

- **until**：I'm not leaving until you **get** on that plane / until that moment **comes**
- **before**：I'm going to end it with her before it **starts**
- **when**：when you **come** over / When we **get** to Yemen / when I **turn** it on
- **if**：if things **work** out / if you **insist** on doing this / if you **go** back out there
- **after**：right after I **got** on the plane（过去叙事则正常用过去式）
<p class="svoc"><span class="a">until you get on that plane</span> <span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m not leaving</span></p>
- **口诀**：主句是将来（will/be going to），从句动词一律"退回"现在式——until/when/before/if 全都适用

</GrammarTip>
