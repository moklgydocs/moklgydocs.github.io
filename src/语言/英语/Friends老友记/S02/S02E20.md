<GrammarTip points="口语缩略,动词时态">

- **Watcha guys doin?** = **What are you guys doing?** 的口语连读缩略——whatcha = what are you，doin' = doing（词尾 g 脱落）；现在进行式问"正在干嘛"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- 本剧高频缩略一家亲：**gonna** = going to、**wanna** = want to、**gotta** = got to，本集将反复出现，见到就认

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E20.mp3" start="2.256" duration="2.208" /> **RICHARD:** <Note>Monica's making us watch</Note> Old Yeller.
> 莫妮卡让我们看老大喊大叫。
<GrammarTip points="使役动词,基本句型">

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">Monica</span> <span class="v">'s making</span> <span class="o">us</span> <span class="c">watch Old Yeller</span></p>

- **make + 宾语 + 原形动词**：使役动词 make 的经典句型 S+V+O+C，补语（台：宾语补足语）一律用**原形、不加 to**——"逼/让我们看"（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **'s making** = is making，现在进行式表示"眼下正在（逼我们）看"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

</GrammarTip>

- **It's Old Yeller, it's a happy movie**：代词 it 回指片名，重复主语加强语气——口语里常见的"先说再评"结构

<GrammarTip points="介词,口语缩略">

- **What're you talkin' about?** = What are you talk**ing** about?——talk about"谈论"，**介词**（台：介系词）about 的宾语正是句首的 what：You are talking about **what** 倒装成疑问句（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- 疑问词作介词宾语时，介词留在句尾是口语常态：Who are you talking **to**? / What are you looking **for**?

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E20.mp3" start="12.432" duration="3.36" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** C'mon, <Note>happy family gets a dog</Note>, frontier fun.

<GrammarTip points="动词时态,省略">

<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,情态动词">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E20.mp3" start="25.296" duration="4.464" />

**原句**：That's when my mother would shut off the TV and say 'The end'.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">That</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">when my mother would shut off the TV and say 'The end'</span></p>
- **when my mother would shut off...**：when 引导**表语从句**（台：名词从句），整个从句作 is 的补语，意思是"那就是……的时刻"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **would shut off...and (would) say**：would 在此表**过去的习惯性动作**"（那时）总会"，相当于 used to shut off（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **shut off**：短语动词"关掉（电器）"，宾语 the TV 可放中间（shut the TV off）也可放后面（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：就是那时候——我妈总会关掉电视说"剧终"。

**语法要点**：when 表语从句（Ch.12）；would 表过去惯常（Ch.9）；短语动词宾语位置（Ch.10）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E20.mp3" start="29.76" duration="2.904" /> **MONICA GELLER:** What about the part <Note>where he has rabies</Note>?
> 那他患狂犬病的那部分呢？
<GrammarTip points="关系从句">

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">the part</span> <span class="a">where he has rabies</span></p>

- **where he has rabies**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 the part——关系副词 where = in which，"他（狗）得狂犬病的那一段"；从句内 he has rabies 主谓齐全，所以不能用关系代词 that/which（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **have rabies**：have 表"患（病）"；rabies 狂犬病，形为复数实为单数疾病名（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E20.mp3" start="32.664" duration="4.176" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** <Note>He doesn't have rabies, he has babies</Note>. <Note>That's what my mom said</Note>.

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">That</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what my mom said</span></p>

- **That's what my mom said**：what 引导名词从句作补语，what = the thing that"我妈说的（内容）"；从句内用陈述语序 my mom said（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **doesn't have / has**：第三人称否定 doesn't + 原形 have；rabies / babies 谐音，是菲比被"保护"多年的笑点所在

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E20.mp3" start="36.84" duration="4.416" /> **RICHARD:** Uh, Phoebe, <Note>I don't think</Note> your mom <Note>would want you to see</Note> <Note>what's about to happen</Note>.
> 嗯，菲比，我想你妈妈不会想让你看到会发生什么。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,否定前移,不定词">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E20.mp3" start="36.84" duration="4.416" />

**原句**：I don't think your mom would want you to see what's about to happen.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">don't think</span> <span class="o">(that) your mom would want you to see...</span></p> think 后接省略 that 的宾语从句
- **I don't think...**：**否定前移**——语义上否定的是从句（I think your mom **wouldn't** want...），习惯上把 not 提到主句；think/believe/suppose 都这么用（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **would want you to see**：want + 宾语 + **不定式**（台：不定词）to see 作宾语补足语；would 让推测语气更委婉（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **what's about to happen**：what 名词从句作 see 的宾语；从句内 **be about to + 原形**"马上就要发生"（最近将来）（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

**翻译**：呃，菲比，我觉得你妈妈当年是不想让你看到接下来要发生的事。

**语法要点**：否定前移（Ch.12）；want sb to do（Ch.4）；be about to（Ch.3）

</GrammarTip>

**本集使役/感官句型：make / let / have / see + 宾语 + 原形（或分词）补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **make + O + 原形**：Monica'**s making** us **watch** Old Yeller. / the mean pharmacist **made** his ear **bleed** / I'**ll make** you **feel** like one of the guys
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">Monica</span> <span class="v">'s making</span> <span class="o">us</span> <span class="c">watch Old Yeller</span></p>
- **let + O + 原形**：he **let** us **drive** his Jaguar / that'**s** why we don't **let** her **play**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">let</span> <span class="o">us</span> <span class="c">drive his Jaguar</span></p>
- **see + O + 原形/分词**：I'm **seeing** him **go** off to college / Didn't you **see** it **coming**?
- **have + O + 过去分词/现在分词**：I **had** a whole speech **prepared** / don't want to **have** everything **decided** for me / We'**ve got** a cab **waiting** downstairs
- **口诀**：主动让/看 → 补语用**原形**；宾语是"被做"的一方 → 补语用**过去分词**；宾语正在做 → **现在分词**

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,动词时态">

**本集现在完成式家族：never...before / yet / since / the first time**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **never...before**：I'**ve never seen** this part before. / he'**s never waved** before——"有史以来头一回"
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve never seen</span> <span class="o">this part</span> <span class="a">before</span></p>
- **yet（疑问/否定句尾）**：**Has** he **spoken** yet? / He can't quite say mama **yet**
- **since + 过去式从句**：I **haven't been** around them **since I was one**
- **the first time + 完成式**：that'**s** the first time we'**ve said** that
- **完成进行式 + for 时段**：we'**ve been dating** for six weeks / this hole I'**ve been digging**
- **对比记忆**：有了 last week / a moment ago 这类**具体过去时间**，就退回简单过去式：he already **did** it last week（× has done...last week）

</GrammarTip>
