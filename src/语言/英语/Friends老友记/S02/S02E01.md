**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E01.mp3" start="1.152" duration="34.392" />

**原句**：This is pretty much what's happened so far. ... What she didn't know was, that Ross was getting off the plane with another woman.

**层次划分**：

- 主干①：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">this</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="c">what's happened so far</span></p> **what's happened so far**：what 引导**表语从句**（台：名词从句），what = the thing that"到目前为止发生的事"；从句内用**现在完成式** has happened，so far"到目前为止"是完成式的标志状语（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **pretty much**：口语副词"差不多、基本上"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **Ross was in love with Rachel since, you know, forever**：be in love with sb"爱着某人"（状态动词，过去式追叙上一季）；since 此处口语化"打从……起"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **every time he tried to tell her**：every time 引导**时间状语从句**（台：副词从句），="每当……"；tried to tell——**不定式**（台：不定词）to tell 作 tried 的宾语（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **something kind of got in the way**：kind of 口语"有点"；get in the way"碍事、挡道"——**介词**（台：介系词）in 构成固定短语（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **like cats, and Italian guys**：like 此处为介词"比如、像"，用于举例（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **Chandler was like "forget about her"**：口语 **be like** = say"（他当时）说"；forget about 短语动词（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **Chandler let it slip that Ross was in love with Rachel**：let it slip that..."说漏了……"——**it 是形式宾语**，真正的宾语是 that 从句；使役动词 let + 宾语 + 原形 slip（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- 主干②：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">what she didn't know</span> <span class="v">was</span> <span class="c">that Ross was getting off the plane...</span></p> **what 主语从句** + **that 表语从句**双层嵌套；表语从句内用**过去进行式** was getting off 描绘"正在下飞机"的画面（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **everything you need to know**：everything + **定语从句**（台：关系从句），关系代词 that 作 know 的宾语故省略——"你需要知道的一切"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **how've you been** = how have you been：**现在完成式**问候"你最近怎么样"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **enough about us**：enough 作表语 + about 介词——"我们的事说得够多了"，话锋一转把话题抛给对方（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：这就是到目前为止的大致经过。……但她不知道的是，罗斯正带着另一个女人走下飞机。

**语法要点**：what 名词从句（Ch.12）；let it slip that 形式宾语（Ch.1）；完成式 + so far（Ch.3）；省略 that 的关系从句（Ch.14）

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：You deserve to be with someone who appreciates you, and who gets how funny and sweet and amazing, and adorable, and sexy you are.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">You</span> <span class="v">deserve</span> <span class="o">to be with someone...</span></p> **deserve + 不定式**"值得/应该……"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **who appreciates you, and who gets...**：**两个定语从句并列**修饰 someone，第二个 who **重复、不可省**——"欣赏你、并且懂得……的人"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **gets how funny and sweet and amazing, and adorable, and sexy you are**：gets 的宾语是 **how 引导的感叹式名词从句**——语序为 how + 形容词罗列 + **主语 you + be**（不倒装！）——"懂你有多有趣、多甜、多了不起、多可爱、多性感"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **Someone who wakes up every morning thinking "..."**：定语从句内再套**现在分词短语 thinking...作伴随状语**——"每天醒来都在想……"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **someone who makes you feel good**：**make + 宾语 + 原形** feel——使役句型 S+V+O+C（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **the way I am with Julie**：**the way + 从句**作方式状语——"就像我和朱莉在一起那样"；**这句话是整集的情感扳机**：Rachel 刚要靠近，听见 Julie 的名字又退回去（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）

**翻译**：你应该跟一个欣赏你、懂得你有多风趣多甜多棒、多可爱多性感的人在一起。一个每天醒来都想"天哪，我居然和 Rachel 在一起"的人。一个让你感觉良好的人——就像我和 Julie 在一起时那样。

**语法要点**：并列关系从句（Ch.14）；how + 形容词 + 主语 + be 的感叹式名词从句（Ch.12）；make + O + 原形（Ch.1）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,虚拟语气">

**本集条件句家族：if 虚拟两连发**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **与现在/将来相反：if + 过去式 → would + 原形**：If I could go back in time and do it again, I **wouldn't**. / If I **had** a nickel for every guy I wish I hadn't... (I'd be rich)
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">wouldn't</span> <span class="o">(do it)</span></p>
- **与过去事实相反：if + 过去完成式 → would have + 过去分词**：I never **would've gone** for it with her if it **hadn't been** for you. / Even Mary Tyler Moore **would've been** better.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">never would've gone</span> <span class="a">for it with her</span></p>
- **固定句型**：**if it hadn't been for sb**"要不是你"；**wish + 过去完成式**"真希望当初没……"（I wish I hadn't）
- **口诀**：现在虚拟往后退一步（过去式），过去虚拟往后退两步（过去完成式）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集 how / the way 方式从句三连**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **how 表语从句（陈述语序！）**：This is **how he wears it**. / That's **how they do pants**. / That's not **how they measure pants**.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">how they do pants</span></p>
- **the way + 定语从句（关系词省略）**：the **way** Ross felt about you / the **way** I am with Julie
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve never felt</span> <span class="a">about anyone</span> <span class="a">the way...</span></p>
- **口诀**：从句里永远用陈述语序——how they do（√），how do they do（×）

</GrammarTip>
