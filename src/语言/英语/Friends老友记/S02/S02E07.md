🎬 *Monica and Rachel's apartment. Everyone is sitting at the couches, Chandler enters.*

🎯 **本场景语法重点**：疑问词作主语 / 情态动词 + 完成式 / 现在完成式 / if 条件句与比较级

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E07.mp3" start="0.0" duration="6.288" /> **CHAN:** OK, <Note>what is it about me</Note>? Do I not look fun enough? Is there <Note>something. . . repellant. . . about me</Note>?
> 好吧，我是怎么了？我看起来不够有趣吗？有什么。排斥剂。关于我？
<GrammarTip points="特殊疑问句,存在句,主谓一致">

- **what is it about me?**：疑问词 **what 作从句主语**，语序不倒装（= What is it about me that turns people off?）；"what is it about + 人"是自问"我到底哪点……"的固定句式（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
- **Do I not look fun enough?**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">do not look</span> <span class="c">fun enough</span></p> 否定疑问的完整式（口语常说 **Don't I look...**）——**look 在此为系动词**（台：连缀动词）"看起来"，后接形容词 fun 作主语补语；enough 修饰形容词**后置**（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **Is there something repellent about me?**：There be 存在句的疑问式；**主谓一致**（台：主语动词一致性）看 be 后面的真正主语 something → 用 is；形容词 repellent 作不定代词 something 的**后置定语**（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- ⚠️ 原文 `repellant` 标准拼写为 **repellent**

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E07.mp3" start="6.288" duration="1.344" /> **RACH:** So, how was the party?
> 派对怎么样？
<GrammarTip points="疑问句,动词时态">

- **How was the party?**：**How was + 名词？** 问"……怎么样／还顺利吗"（= How did it go?）；派对已结束 → be 用**过去式** was（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **So**：句首承接词"好了、那么"——切换话题的信号（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E07.mp3" start="7.632" duration="7.512" /> **CHAN:** Well <Note>it couldn't have been worse</Note>. A woman literally <Note>passed through me</Note>. OK, so what is it, am I <Note>hideously unattractive</Note>?
> 嗯，不可能更糟了。一个女人从我身边走过。好吧，那又是什么呢，我是不是很难看？
<GrammarTip points="情态动词,比较结构,副词">

- **it couldn't have been worse**：**情态动词 + 完成式**对过去下结论——"（当时）不可能更糟了"；否定词 + 比较级 = 最高级含义（= It was the worst）（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）
- **a woman literally passed through me**：pass through"从……中间穿过"，**介词**（台：介系词）through 表"贯穿"；literally 强调"真的、毫不夸张"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **hideously unattractive**：程度副词 hideously"吓人地"修饰形容词 unattractive（Ch.8）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E07.mp3" start="15.144" duration="9.336" /> **PHOE:** No, you are not, you are very attractive. You know what, I go through <Note>the exact same thing</Note>. <Note>Every time I put on a little weight</Note>, I start questioning everyting.

<GrammarTip points="副词从句,短语动词,名词词组">

- **every time I put on a little weight**：**every time + 从句**引导时间副词从句"每次……（就）"，主从句都用现在式表习惯（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **put on weight**：短语动词"长胖"；a little 修饰不可数名词 weight（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **the exact same thing**：exact 强调 same"一模一样的（经历）"；same 前通常带 the（Ch.7）
- ⚠️ 原文 `everyting` 标准拼写为 **everything**

</GrammarTip>

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve put on</span> <span class="o">a little weight</span></p>

- **I've put on a little weight?**：**现在完成式**——过去的动作（变胖）与现在状态挂钩"（我什么时候）胖了？"；全句用**陈述句语序 + 升调**表惊讶复述对方的言外之意（回声问句 echo question）（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

</GrammarTip>

- ⚠️ 原文 `wieght` 标准拼写为 **weight**（口诀：i before e, except after c）

- **I'm unemployed and in dire need of a project**：unemployed 过去分词形容词作补语"失业的"；**be in (dire) need of + 名词**"急需……"，in need of 是固定介词短语（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **Ya wanna work out?**：wanna = want to，后接**不定式**（台：不定词）to work out 作 want 的宾语；**work out** 短语动词"健身锻炼"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

<GrammarTip points="省略,语气,动名词">

- **might get in the way of**：might 表不肯定的推测"也许会"；**get in the way of + 名词**"妨碍……"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **my lying around time**：**my + 动名词** lying around 构成名词词组"我闲躺（的时间）"；lie around"无所事事地躺着"（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)）

</GrammarTip>

- **Please.**：独词恳求句——由 if you please 缩减而来；此处用**升调**读，表"求你了（就答应我吧）"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E07.mp3" start="39.984" duration="2.256" /> **ALL:** C'mon. <Note>Let her</Note>. Yeah.

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E07.mp3" start="42.24" duration="5.592" /> **CHAN:** Alright, OK, alright. But <Note>if we put on spandex</Note> and <Note>my boobs are bigger than yours</Note>, <Note>I'm goin' home</Note>.

<GrammarTip points="副词从句,比较结构,动词时态">

<p class="svoc"><span class="a">If we put on spandex and my boobs are bigger than yours</span> <span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m going</span> <span class="a">home</span></p>

- **if we put on spandex and my boobs are bigger than yours**：if 条件副词从句用**现在式代未来**；从句内 and 连接两个并列分句（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **bigger than yours**：比较级 + than；**yours 名词性所有格代词** = your boobs（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **I'm goin' home**：goin' = going；**现在进行式表最近的将来**"我就回家"；home 此处为副词，前不加 to（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

</GrammarTip>

**层次划分**：

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役动词与get">

**本集句型一：make / let / get / have + 宾语 + 补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）——四种补语一次分清：

- **make/let + O + 原形**：don't **make** me **do** anything / **let** her / can't **make** hands **meet**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">makes</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">do...</span></p>
- **get + O + 不定式**：he **got** you **to beg** / **got** you **to say**...（说服某人做）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">got</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">to beg</span></p>
- **have/get + O + 现在分词**：she'**s got** me **doing** butt clenches / I'**ve got** a cab **waiting** / **got** you **thinking**...（使处于持续状态）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">'s got</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">doing...</span></p>
- **get + O + 过去分词**：**get** the lock **undone** / **get** this **straight**（使完成）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">can't get</span> <span class="o">the lock</span> <span class="c">undone</span></p>
- **口诀**：make/let 后**用原形**，get 后**加 to**；V-ing 表"持续中"，过去分词表"被完成"

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,完成式家族">

**本集句型二：完成式家族三连**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **现在完成式**（过去→现在的经验/结果）：I'**ve put on** a little weight / you **haven't had** any closure / this ship **has sailed** / now I'**ve got** closure
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve had</span> <span class="o">feelings</span> <span class="a">for you</span></p>
- **现在完成进行式**（一路持续到现在）：I'**ve been playing** the movie in my head / I'**ve been doin'** it since the ninth grade
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve been doing</span> <span class="o">it</span> <span class="a">since the ninth grade</span></p>
- **情态动词 + 完成式**（回头看过去）：it **couldn't have been** worse（推测）/ I never **should have said** anything（懊悔）/ you **should have said** something（责备）
- **口诀**：完成式问"到现在为止怎样"；完成进行式强调"一直没停"；should/could + have done 全是"马后炮"

</GrammarTip>
