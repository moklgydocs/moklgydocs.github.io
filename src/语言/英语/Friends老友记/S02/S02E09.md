**原句**：Yeah, we were gonna give fifty, but if you guys gave more, we don't wanna look bad.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">we</span> <span class="v">were gonna give</span> <span class="o">fifty</span></p> **were gonna = were going to**，**过去将来式**"本来打算给"——以过去某时刻为基准看将来（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **if you guys gave more**：**条件状语从句**（台：副词从句）；从句用过去式 gave，把假设推到"未必如此"的委婉距离——"要是你们给得多呢"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **we don't wanna look bad**：wanna = want to，**不定式**（台：不定词）to look bad 作 want 的宾语；look 在此作系动词"看起来"，后接形容词 bad 作表语（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）

**翻译**：对，我们本来打算给五十，可要是你们给得更多，我们可不想显得寒酸。

**语法要点**：过去将来式 was/were going to（Ch.3）；if 从句过去式表委婉假设（Ch.13）；wanna = want to（Ch.4）

</GrammarTip>

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E09.mp3" start="29.496" duration="8.952" />

**原句**：Money is so impersonal. Cookies says someone really cares. . . Alright, we're broke, but cookies do say that.

**层次划分**：

- <p class="svoc"><span class="s">Money</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="c">so impersonal</span></p> S+V+C；程度副词 so 修饰形容词 impersonal"太没人情味"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- <p class="svoc"><span class="s">Cookies</span> <span class="v">says</span> <span class="o">someone really cares</span></p> Cookies 此处当"饼干（这东西）"整体看待，动词用单数 says——**主谓一致**（台：主语动词一致性）按"意念单数"处理（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）
- **someone really cares**：**宾语从句**（台：名词从句）作 says 的宾语，省略连接词 that；从句内 someone 配单数 cares（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）
- **cookies do say that**：**do + 原形**强调动词"确实那么说"；此句 cookies 又当复数用 do——与前句 says 对照，正是口语单复数随感觉摇摆的活例（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **we're broke**：broke 形容词"身无分文的"，S+V+C（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）

**翻译**：钱太没人情味了。饼干才能说明有人真的在乎……好吧，是我们没钱了，但饼干确实能表达那份心意。

**语法要点**：意念单/复数的主谓一致（Ch.11）；宾语从句省略 that（Ch.12）；do 强调动词（Ch.9）

**本集双宾语句型：give / get / show / tell / wish + 间宾 + 直宾**（S+V+O+O，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **give + O + O**：we were gonna give fifty / You gave him cookies? / we just didn't give him enough / give him cash
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">gave</span> <span class="o">him</span> <span class="o">cookies</span></p>
- **get + O + O**：I got you a little present / I didn't get you anything / You got me a cola drink? / I should get you another sweater
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">got</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="o">a little present</span></p>
- **show / tell / wish + O + O**：show us what you bought / let me ask you something / Wish me luck
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">show</span> <span class="o">us</span> <span class="o">what you bought</span></p>
- **换算口诀**：间宾是人、直宾是物；间宾后置时要加介词——give sth. **to** sb. / get sth. **for** sb.，介词选择由动词决定

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集"do you think / 疑问词从句"语序三连**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）：

- **do you think + 陈述语序**：do you think we **could have** a new one by 6? / do you think there's a chance you **could fix** that radiator now? / do you think it'**d be** alright if I went in...?

- **疑问词从句保持陈述语序**：who he **was** / where he **lived** / what she's goin' through / what you bought
