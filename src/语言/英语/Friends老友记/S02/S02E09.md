🎬 *Phoebe's grandmother's place. Phoebe's grandmother is sitting at the table, reading the obituaries, and crossing out names in the phonebook.*
> 🎬 *菲比的祖母的地方。菲比的祖母坐在桌子旁，读讣告，在电话簿上划掉姓名。*

**GRANDMOTHER:** Esther Livingston. [scratches out name] Gone.
> 埃丝特·利文斯顿[划掉名字]走了。

[Phoebe enters.]
> [菲比进来了。]

**GRANDMOTHER:** Hi, Phoe.
> 嗨，菲。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hi Gram. Whatcha doin'?
> 嗨，格兰，你在干什么？

**GRANDMOTHER:** Oh, just updating the phonebook.
> 哦，只是更新电话簿。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Um, gram, um, can I see the pictures of my dad again?
> 嗯，克，嗯，我能再看看我爸爸的照片吗？

**GRANDMOTHER:** [nervously] Oh. Oh, sure, sure, uh, uh, how come?
> （紧张地）哦，哦，当然，当然，呃，呃，怎么会呢？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Just, you know, to see... um.
> 只是，你知道，看看……嗯。

**GRANDMOTHER:** Oh, sure, yeah. [gets a box with the pictures] This is the one of you father in a meadow, and, uh, helping a little boy fly a kite, and here he is at a graduation. . . another graduation. . . another graduation.
> 哦，当然，是的。[拿一个有照片的盒子]这是你父亲在草地上，帮助一个小男孩放风筝，他在这里参加一个毕业典礼…另一个毕业典礼…另一个毕业典礼。

**Phoebe Buffay:** OK, is this really my father?
> 好吧，这真的是我父亲吗？

**GRANDMOTHER:** Is it really your fa--I can't... well of course it is.
> 真的是你的父亲吗？我不能……当然是。

**Phoebe Buffay:** OK, I smell smoke. Maybe that's 'cause someone's pants are on fire.
> 好吧，我闻到烟味。也许是因为有人的裤子着火了。

**GRANDMOTHER:** Look, I. . .
> 看，我。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ya know, in all the years that we have been grandmother and granddaughter, you have never lied to me.
> 你知道，在我们做祖母和孙女的这些年里，你从未对我撒谎。

**GRANDMOTHER:** Alright, that is not your father, that's just a picture of a guy in a frame.
> 好吧，那不是你父亲，那只是一张相框里一个人的照片。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh God.
> 哦，天啊。

**GRANDMOTHER:** It was your mother's idea. Ya know, she didn't want you to know your real father because it hurt her so much when he left, and, I didn't want to go along with it, but, well then she died and, and it was harder to argue with her. Not impossible, but harder.
> 这是你母亲的主意。你知道，她不想让你认识你的亲生父亲，因为他离开时她很伤心，我也不想同意，但是，后来她死了，很难和她争论。不是不可能，而是更难。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Alright, so, what, he's not a famous tree surgeon? And then, I guess, OK, he doesn't live in a hut in Burma where there's no phones?
> 好吧，那么，什么，他不是一个著名的树木外科医生？然后，我猜，好吧，他不住在缅甸一个没有电话的小屋里？

**GRANDMOTHER:** Last I heard, he was a pharmacist somewhere upstate.
> 我最后听说，他是州北部某个地方的药剂师。

**Phoebe Buffay:** OK, that makes no sense. Why would the villagers worship a pharmacist?
> 好吧，那没道理。为什么村民会崇拜药剂师？

**GRANDMOTHER:** Honey.
> 亲爱的。

**Phoebe Buffay:** [realizes] Oh.
> （意识到）哦。

**GRANDMOTHER:** Anyway, that's all I know. That, and this. [pulls apart a frame and pulls a picture out] This is the real him.
> 不管怎样，我只知道这些。那个，还有这个。[拆开一个相框，拿出一张照片]这才是真正的他。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh.
> 哦。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah, um, in Albany, can I have the number of Frank Buffay. . . OK, um, in Ithica. . . alright, um, Saratoga. . . Oneonta. Alright, you know what, you shouldn't call youself information. [hangs up]

> [菲比的祖母进来]

**GRANDMOTHER:** Hey.
> 嘿。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hello grandma, if that is in fact your real name.
> 你好，奶奶，如果那是你的真名的话。

> 来吧，菲，别再生我的气了。怎么样？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Well, not so good. Upstate's pretty big, he's pretty small, you do the math.
> 嗯，不太好。北州很大，他很小，你来算吧。

**GRANDMOTHER:** Well, I think you're better off without him. Oh honey, I know he's your daddy but, but to me he's still the irresponsible creep who knocked up your mom and stole her Gremlin.
> 嗯，我想没有他你会过得更好。哦，亲爱的，我知道他是你爸爸，但对我来说，他仍然是一个不负责任的混蛋，把你妈妈打翻，偷走了她的小精灵。

> 不，我只是想知道他是谁。

> 哇。非常感谢。
