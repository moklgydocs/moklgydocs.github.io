🎯 **本场景语法重点**：省略助动词的口语疑问句 / what 名词从句 / 完成式 + for 时段 / next time 时间从句

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E15.mp3" start="0.0" duration="6.696" /> **JOEY TRIBBIANI:** Man this is weird. <Note>You ever realize</Note> <Note>Captain Crunch's eyebrows are actually on his hat</Note>?
> 伙计，这太奇怪了。你有没有意识到克朗奇船长的眉毛其实在他的帽子上？
<GrammarTip points="口语省略,名词从句">

- **(Do) you ever realize...?**：口语疑问句常省略句首助动词 Do；ever"曾经"是疑问句/否定句常用的频率副词（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **(that) Captain Crunch's eyebrows are actually on his hat**：realize 后接**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），连接词 that 口语中省略；Captain Crunch's 名词所有格作定语（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E15.mp3" start="6.696" duration="6.72" /> **CHANDLER BING:** That's <Note>what's weird</Note>? Joey, <Note>the man's been captain of a cereal</Note> <Note>for the last 40 years</Note>.
> 这就是奇怪的地方？乔伊，这个人在过去的40年里一直是一种麦片粥的船长。
<GrammarTip points="名词从句,动词时态">

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">That</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what's weird</span></p>

- **That's what's weird?**：is 是**系动词**（台：连缀动词），what's weird 是 what 引导的名词从句作补语，what = the thing that"奇怪的地方"；整句用陈述语序 + 升调表反问（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **the man's been captain ... for the last 40 years**：man's = man has，**现在完成式** has been + for + 时段，表"从过去持续到现在"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- 讽刺点：眉毛长在哪不重要，"当了 40 年麦片船长"这件事本身才奇怪

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,否定疑问句,动名词">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E15.mp3" start="24.504" duration="9.864" />

**原句**：Well don't you see how gross that is? I mean that's like you using my toothbrush. You used my toothbrush?

**层次划分**：

- **Don't you see how gross that is?**：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">Don't</span> <span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">see</span> <span class="o">how gross that is</span></p> 否定疑问句"难道你看不出来"；see 后接 how 引导的感叹式名词从句"那有多恶心"，从句内用陈述语序（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **that's like you using my toothbrush**：like 此处作介词"就像"，后接**动名词复合结构** you using my toothbrush——you 是 using 的逻辑主语（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)）
- **You used my toothbrush?**：陈述句语序 + 升调 = 回声式疑问，表达震惊（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

**翻译**：难道你没觉得那很恶心吗？我是说，那就好比你用我的牙刷。（乔伊一脸心虚）你用过我的牙刷？

**语法要点**：how 感叹式名词从句（Ch.12）；动名词复合结构（Ch.5）；陈述语序表疑问（Ch.9）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,进行式表将来">

**本集高频句型一：进行式表"已定的将来"**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- He's **havin'** a brunch. / you guys are actually **getting** tattoos / I'**m getting** a lily for my Mom
- Rich is **goin'** to the party too / I'**m movin'** to a bigger place / I'**m movin'** out like we talked about / you'**re** just **moving** uptown
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m moving</span> <span class="a">uptown</span></p>
- **对比 be gonna**（打算/预测，未必已安排）：What're you **gonna** get? / I'm really **gonna** miss you / who's **gonna** eat all our food
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">who</span> <span class="v">'s gonna eat</span> <span class="o">all our food</span></p>
- **口诀**："日程已定用进行，打算预测用 gonna"——进行式往往隐含"已经安排好了"
- **注意**：此句型多用于移动/安排类动词（go / come / move / get / have），且常带将来时间状语

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集高频句型二：名词从句（台：名词从句）的三种角色**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **作宾语**：I can't believe **(that) you guys are getting tattoos**. / I don't even know **how serious he is about me**. / did you know **they do this with needles**?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">can't believe</span> <span class="o">(that) you guys are getting tattoos</span></p>
- **作补语**：That'**s what**'s weird. / that'**s how** I feel. / it's not **what you think**. / this is exactly **what I wanted**.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">how I feel</span></p>
- **作介词宾语**：'cause of **what Ross said** / sorry about **what happened** / About **how I never lived alone**
- **感叹式变体**：how + 形容词引导——I don't even know **how serious he is about me**（"他有多认真"）
- **口诀**：what = the thing that；从句内一律陈述语序，不倒装；连接词 that 引导宾语从句时口语中常省略

</GrammarTip>
