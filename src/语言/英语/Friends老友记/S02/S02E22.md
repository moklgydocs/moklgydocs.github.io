**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E22.mp3" start="0.0" duration="9.168" />

**原句**：So, I'll get candles and my mom's lace tablecloth, and since it's Rachel's birthday, I mean, we want it to be special, I thought I'd poach a salmon.

**层次划分**：

- 主干一：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ll get</span> <span class="o">candles and my mom's lace tablecloth</span></p> 一般将来式 will get"我要去拿/买"；my mom's 名词所有格作定语（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **since it's Rachel's birthday**：since 引导原因**状语从句**（台：副词从句）"既然/因为……"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- 主干二：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">we</span> <span class="v">want</span> <span class="o">it</span> <span class="c">to be special</span></p> **want + 宾语 + 不定式补语**"希望它……"——**不定式**（台：不定词）to be special 作**宾语补足语**（台：补语）（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **I thought I'd poach a salmon**：thought 后接省略 that 的**宾语从句**（台：名词从句）；I'd = I would，would 让打算显得更委婉；poach"（小火）水煮"，与下文 Ross 拿它开玩笑呼应（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **I mean**：口语插入语"我的意思是"，不参与句法（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

**翻译**：这样吧，我去拿蜡烛和我妈的蕾丝桌布；既然是瑞秋的生日，我是说，咱们都想让它特别一点——所以我打算清煮一条三文鱼。

**语法要点**：since 原因从句（Ch.13）；want + O + to do（Ch.4）；thought + (that) 从句 + would 委婉（Ch.12/Ch.9）

**原句**：I think fancy parties are only fun if you're fancy on the inside and I'm just not sure we are.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">think</span> <span class="o">(that) fancy parties are only fun</span></p> think 后接省略 that 的宾语从句；从句内 S+V+C = fancy parties are fun（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

- **I'm just not sure (that) we are**：sure 后再接一个省略 that 的宾语从句；句尾 we are = we are fancy (on the inside)，补语承前省略，只留系动词 are（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

**语法要点**：think/sure + (that) 从句（Ch.12）；if 条件从句（Ch.13）；补语承前省略

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,短语动词">

**本集短语动词（动 + 介 / 动 + 副）大盘点**（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）：

- **动 + 介（不可拆，宾语永远在后）**：deal **with** it / rely **on** a program / listen **to** them / care **about** / think **about** / get **through** this / look **at** the bright side / scream **at** us
- **动 + 副（可拆，代词必须夹中间）**：take the trash out → take **it** out / keep **it** down / fire **it** up / put the caps back **on** / call **her** back / get my cigarettes **out** of my jacket
- **动 + 副（整体新义）**：drop **by** 顺访 / pop **out** 蹦出 / sneak **off** 偷溜 / shut **up** 闭嘴 / throw **up** 呕吐 / dry **out** 干掉 / get **out** 出去
- **句型提醒**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">keep</span> <span class="o">it</span> <span class="c">down</span></p> 代词作宾语时**只能**说 keep it down，不能说 keep down it

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,宾语从句">

**本集"省略 that 的宾语从句"三连**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **think / guess / believe / hope + (that) 从句**：I think fancy parties are only fun / I guess we don't invite her parents / I can't believe this is happening / I hope you guys had fun
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">think</span> <span class="o">(that) fancy parties are fun</span></p>
- **否定前移**：I didn't think anyone'd buy that = 我认为没人**会**信——not 从从句搬到主句 think 前
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">didn't think</span> <span class="o">(that) anyone'd buy that</span></p>
- **时态呼应（主句过去式 → 从句后移）**：you didn't tell me your boyfriend **smoked** / I thought you **were** Rachel
- **口诀**：从句一律**陈述语序**，不倒装；that 作连接词不作成分，口语十之八九省略

</GrammarTip>
