**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E19.mp3" start="28.56" duration="15.744" />

**原句**：I don't think you're being fair. What about all the other nights when you don't see me? What about last night when you went and got a drink of water and I was nice enough to hide behind the door?

**层次划分**：

- **I don't think you're being fair**：**否定前移**——语义是 I think you're **not** being fair，think 接宾语从句时 not 习惯移到主句（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）；**be being fair** 用进行式表"（眼下这一刻的）表现公平"，是 be + 形容词少见的进行式用法（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **what about + 名词短语**："……又怎么说？"口语反问句型，后接名词或动名词（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **the other nights when you don't see me**：when 引导**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 nights，when = on which，从句内用现在式表"通常的那些晚上"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **you went and got a drink of water**：went 与 got 对等谓语，and 连接两个过去式表连续动作（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）；a drink of water"一口水"是名词词组（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **I was nice enough to hide behind the door**：形容词 + **enough + 不定式**"好心得足以……"，enough 后置修饰形容词（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）

**翻译**：我觉得你这不公平。你只撞见过我一晚就吓坏了——那其他那么多没撞见我的晚上怎么说？就说昨晚吧，你起来喝水，我还好心地躲在门后呢，这又怎么说？

**语法要点**：否定前移（Ch.12）；when 关系从句修饰时间名词（Ch.14）；be being + adj（Ch.3）；adj + enough + to do（Ch.7）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,want句型">

**本集 want 句型全家族**（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **want + 宾语 + 不定式**：You want **me to sing**? / you want **me to move out**? / you want **me to help you unpack** your stuff?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">You</span> <span class="v">want</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">to move out</span></p>
- **want + 宾语 + 副词/介词短语补语**：I want **you out** / I want **you out of the apartment** / you want **me to go**?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">want</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">out</span></p>
- **want + 不定式（自己做）**：I don't ever want **to suck your wind** again / I want **to hear you say it**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">want</span> <span class="o">to hear you say it</span></p>
- **口诀**：want 后"谁做"决定结构——自己做用 to do；让别人做用 want sb **to do** 或 want sb **+ 方位补语**（out / back / home）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役动词,感官动词">

**本集"使役/感官 + 宾语 + 原形补语"大集合**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **watch / see / hear + O + 原形**（感知全过程）：watch **me sleep** / just watchin' **you sleep** / I actually saw **him leave** / hear **you say** it
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">saw</span> <span class="o">him</span> <span class="c">leave</span></p>
- **let / make / have + O + 原形**（使役）：if you won't let **me blow** / you let **him wash** his feet / let **me finish** / have **the cab crash**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">let</span> <span class="o">him</span> <span class="c">wash his feet</span></p>
- **make + O + 名词补语**（使成为）：the wind can make **us Goddesses**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">the wind</span> <span class="v">can make</span> <span class="o">us</span> <span class="c">Goddesses</span></p>
- **注意**：补语一律用**原形、不加 to**；感官动词换用现在分词（see him leav**ing**）则强调"正在进行的画面"（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）

</GrammarTip>
