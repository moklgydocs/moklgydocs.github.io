**层次划分**：

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,情态动词">

**本集 have got to 句型：have got to + 原形 = "必须"**（半情态，[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **I got to get to the museum.**（have/'ve 整个省略，只剩 got to）
- **You've got to get back out there**, it's your party.
- **I've got to set up the music.**
- **We have got to get you lazy boys out of these chairs.**
- **you gotta promise** you won't get all big-brothery（gotta = got to 的连写）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">'ve got to get</span> <span class="o">back out there</span></p>
- **注意**：have got to 没有过去式，表过去要用 had to（I'm sorry I had to work tonight）；否定"不必"是 don't have to，**不是** haven't got to（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,不定词">

**本集 be supposed to 句型：be supposed to + 原形 = "按理应该"**（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）：

- **肯定**：Tonight **you're supposed to waitress for me**.（按约定该给我帮工）
- **否定疑问表惊讶**：**Aren't you supposed to be out with Rachel?**（你不是该跟瑞秋出去吗？）
- **否定**：australopithicus **isn't supposed to be in that display**.（按理不该摆在那儿）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">'re supposed</span> <span class="c">to waitress for me</span></p>
- **注意**：be supposed to 自带"规定/期待"意味，与现实相反时常含埋怨——对比 should（主观应该）与 have to（客观必须）（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

</GrammarTip>
