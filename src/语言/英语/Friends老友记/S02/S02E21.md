<GrammarTip points="疑问句语序,口语缩略">

- **watchin'** = watching：口语里 -ing 的尾音 /ŋ/ 常读成 /n/，书写用 ' 代替 g，全文还有 kiddin' / tryin' / standin' 等
- **why are we watching...?**：特殊疑问句 = 疑问词 + 助动词提前的一般疑问句语序（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
- **the business channel**：名词 business 直接作定语修饰 channel"商业频道"（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E21.mp3" start="3.648" duration="13.176" /> **MONICA GELLER:** <Note>'Cause I was going by it the other day</Note> and <Note>I saw that there was a stock with my initials, MEG, on it</Note> and, well, sometimes I have to watch for two or three hours <Note>before it comes up again</Note> but <Note>when it does</Note>, it's pretty exciting.
> 因为前几天我路过那里，我看到那里有一只股票，上面有我名字的首字母，梅格。嗯，有时候我得看两三个小时，它才会再次出现，但当它出现的时候，我会非常兴奋。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,状语从句,名词从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E21.mp3" start="3.648" duration="13.176" />

**原句**：'Cause I was going by it the other day and I saw that there was a stock with my initials, MEG, on it and, well, sometimes I have to watch for two or three hours before it comes up again but when it does, it's pretty exciting.

**层次划分**：

- **'Cause I was going by it the other day**：'Cause = because 的口语缩略，引导**原因状语从句**（台：副词从句）；go by"路过"；the other day"前几天"，从句用**过去进行式**描述那几天发生的背景动作（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">saw</span> <span class="o">that there was a stock with my initials, MEG, on it</span></p> saw 后接 that 引导的**宾语从句**（台：名词从句）；从句内部是 there be 存在句；**with my initials, MEG, on it** 是介词（台：介系词）短语作后置定语修饰 stock，MEG 作 initials 的同位语（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **sometimes I have to watch for two or three hours before it comes up again**：have to"不得不"；**before** 引导时间状语从句，从句用**现在式 comes 代替将来式**——时间/条件从句里不用 will（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）；come up"（行情）跳出来"
- **but when it does, it's pretty exciting**：when 时间状语从句里 **does 是替代动词** = comes up again，避免重复；it 指那只股票（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

**翻译**：因为前几天我路过那地方，看见有只股票挂着我名字的缩写 MEG。有时候我得盯上两三个小时它才会再跳出来一次，可它一出现，就特别让人兴奋。

**语法要点**：时间状语从句现在式代将来（Ch.13）；that 宾语从句套 there be（Ch.12）；does 替代前文动词（Ch.8）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E21.mp3" start="19.896" duration="5.448" /> **ROSS GELLER:** Mon, <Note>speaking of which</Note>, dad says <Note>he knows someone you can call for an interview</Note>.

- **says (that) he knows someone**：says 后省略 that 的宾语从句，从句里 knows 又套一层宾语 someone（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E21.mp3" start="35.016" duration="10.992" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** No, 'cause you just said dad and <Note>everywhere I go today</Note> <Note>I keep getting signs telling me to go see my father</Note>. > 不，因为你刚才说爸爸，今天无论我走到哪里，都会收到指示牌，告诉我去见我爸爸。就像当我走到这里，经过一个自助餐…这是我父亲的姓。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,状语从句,分词">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E21.mp3" start="35.016" duration="10.992" />

**原句**：No, 'cause you just said dad and everywhere I go today I keep getting signs telling me to go see my father. Like when I was walking over here and I passed a buffet...which is my father's last name.

**层次划分**：

- **'cause you just said dad**：because 缩略引导原因状语从句；just"刚才"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **everywhere I go today**：**everywhere 直接当连词**引导地点状语从句"无论我走到哪里"，从句用现在式 go（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">keep getting</span> <span class="o">signs</span></p> **keep + V-ing**"不断地……"（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)）
- **telling me to go see my father**：现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 signs = signs **that tell** me...（从句简化，[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)/[Ch.16](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter16.md)）；tell sb **to do**——**不定式**（台：不定词）作宾语 me 的**宾语补足语**（台：补语）（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）；go see 是口语"go + 原形" = go and see
- **when I was walking over here and I passed a buffet**：when 时间状语从句内**过去进行式 + 一般过去式**并置——was walking 是进行的背景，passed 是插入的动作（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **which is my father's last name**：which 引导定语从句，口语里松接地指代前面整个 buffet"自助餐（这个词）正是我生父的姓 Buffay"（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）

**翻译**：不对，因为你刚说了"爸爸"，而我今天走到哪儿都能看到叫我去看生父的征兆。比如我往这儿走的时候路过一家 buffet（自助餐）……那正是我爸的姓。

**语法要点**：everywhere 作连词（Ch.13）；分词定语 = 关系从句简化（Ch.16）；tell sb to do 的不定式宾补（Ch.4）

</GrammarTip>

**本集使役句型：make / let + 宾语 + 原形（或形容词）补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **make + O + 原形**：Don't **make** me **come** down there and kick your wall street butt.
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Don't make</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">come down there...</span></p>
- **let + O + 原形**：**let** him **know** that his dog is ok / never **let** it **settle** / you can't **let** him **look** up too long
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">let</span> <span class="o">him</span> <span class="c">know</span></p>
- **make + O + 形容词**：that would **make** it **gross** / we didn't **make** it **clear** enough / Did we not **make** ourselves **clear**?
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">make</span> <span class="o">it</span> <span class="c">clear</span></p>
- **注意**：make/let 后的补语**用原形，不加 to**；改成被动后 to 才还回来（He was made **to** wear...）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集 wh- 名词从句：一律陈述语序，不倒装**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **作宾语**：I know **what we can do**. / I know **where your dog is**. / You know **what I keep wondering**?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">what we can do</span></p>
- **作补语**：that's **why we're here**. / here's **what we'll do**.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">why we're here</span></p>
- **作介词宾语**：despite **what she says** / thanks for bringing back **what's left of him**
- **口诀**：疑问句才倒装（Where is your dog?），从句里永远"主语 + 动词"（where your dog is）；what = the thing that

</GrammarTip>
