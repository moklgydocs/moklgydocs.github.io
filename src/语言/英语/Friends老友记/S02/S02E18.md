🎯 **本场景语法重点**：it 虚主语句型 / 省略关系代词的定语从句 / 代动词 do / where 引导的定语从句

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E18.mp3" start="0.0" duration="0.936" /> **AMBER:** Oh Drake.
> 哦，德雷克。

DR. REMORE: I'm sorry Amber. <Note>It's just like Brad</Note> <Note>to have to have the last word</Note>.
> 对不起，琥珀。就像布拉德必须说最后一句话一样。
<GrammarTip points="形式主语,惯用语">

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">It</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="a">just like Brad</span> <span class="s">to have to have the last word</span></p>

- **It's (just) like sb to do sth**："某人不就是这样嘛"——it 作虚主语（形式主语），真正主语是后面的**不定式**（台：不定词）短语 to have to have...（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **have to have the last word**：前一个 have 属情态词组 have to"不得不"，后一个 have 是实义动词"获得"；**have the last word** 惯用语"说最后一句话、占上风"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

</GrammarTip>

[Ross enters]
> [罗斯进来]

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E18.mp3" start="0.936" duration="3.0" /> **ROSS GELLER:** I'm sorry <Note>I'm late</Note>, <Note>what happened</Note>?
> 对不起，我迟到了，发生什么事了？
<GrammarTip points="名词从句,疑问句">

- **I'm sorry (that) I'm late**：sorry 后接省略 that 的**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），形容词 sorry/sure/afraid 后的从句都属此类（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **what happened?**：疑问词 what 本身就是主语，疑问句**不倒装**——"发生了什么"；happen 用过去式表刚发生（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip points="口语缩略">

- **wanna** = want to（口语缩略），后接原形动词 see；书写语中应还原为 want to see（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E18.mp3" start="6.816" duration="1.104" /> **AMBER:** I want you Drake.

DR. REMORE: I know <Note>you do</Note> but <Note>you and I</Note> can never be together that way.
> 我知道你知道，但你和我永远不能这样在一起。
<GrammarTip points="代动词,人称代词">

- **I know (that) you do**：do 是**代动词**，替代前面的实义动词 want me——"我知道你想要我"；避免重复用 do 代打（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **you and I**：并列主语用主格 I（口语常误说 you and me，此处是正式肥皂剧台词故用主格）；can never be 情态动词 + 否定副词 never（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E18.mp3" start="7.92" duration="0.72" /> **AMBER:** What?

DR. REMORE: <Note>There's something</Note> <Note>I never told you</Note> Amber. I'm actually your half- brother.
> 有件事我从来没有告诉过你，琥珀。我实际上是你同父异母的兄弟。
<GrammarTip points="存在句,关系从句">

<p class="svoc"><span class="a">There</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="s">something (that) I never told you</span></p>

- **There be 存在句**：真正主语是 something，后面跟着修饰它的从句（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **something (that) I never told you**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）——关系代词 that 在从句中作 told 的宾语（tell sb sth 的双宾结构里缺直接宾语），故**可省略**；先行词是 something 这类不定代词时习惯用 that 而不用 which（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **half-brother**：half- 前缀"同父异母/同母异父的"，复合名词（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

</GrammarTip>

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E18.mp3" start="10.968" duration="14.952" />

**原句**：I get the medical award for separating the siamese twins. ... And that's where I find the world's biggest emerald.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">get</span> <span class="o">the medical award</span> <span class="a">for separating the siamese twins</span></p> **for + 动名词**短语表原因"因为分离了连体婴而获奖"（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **that's where I find the world's biggest emerald**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">where I find...</span></p> where 引导**表语从句**（台：名词从句），where = the place where"就在那里"；从句内嵌定语从句 (where) I find...，关系副词 where 兼作两处桥梁（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **the world's biggest emerald**：形容词最高级 biggest + 名词所有格 world's"世界上最大的"（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）

- 全段用**现在式**讲未来剧情——编故事、讲梗概时的"历史性现在式"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

**翻译**：我因为成功分离连体婴拿到医学大奖……就在那儿，我找到了世界上最大的翡翠。

**语法要点**：for + V-ing 表原因（Ch.5）；where 表语从句（Ch.12）；最高级（Ch.7）；历史性现在式（Ch.3）

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：I just thought that it would be fun if the three of us had some beers and got to know each other.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">thought</span> <span class="o">that it would be fun...</span></p> **I thought (that)...**：宾语从句"我（刚才）想……"；用过去式 thought 让提议听起来更试探、更客气（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **it would be fun**：从句内主干——it 虚主语，真正主语是后面的 if 从句所设想的事；**would** 表假设"（要是……）会好玩"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **if the three of us had some beers and got to know each other**：if **条件状语从句**（台：副词从句），用**过去式 had / got** 与主句 would 呼应——这是"与现在事实不太沾边的设想"的客气假设语气；**the three of us**"我们三个人"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,关系从句">

**本集定语从句（台：关系从句）高频句型**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **省略关系代词（作宾语）**：something (that) I never told you / everyone (that) I've been with / the guys (that) Monica has been with / the best (that) I have ever had
<p class="svoc"><span class="o">something</span> <span class="o">(that) I never told you</span></p>
- **who/that 作主语（不可省）**：someone who slept with her / a conversation that terrified me to my very soul / the only doctor that could have saved me
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">someone who slept with her</span> <span class="v">would say</span></p>
- **最高级/only 后只用 that**：the greatest thing **that** ever happened to me（不用 which）
- **口诀**：从句缺宾语 → that 可省；缺主语 → who/that 必须留

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,比较结构">

**本集比较句型三兄弟**（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）：

- **比较级 + than 从句（省略重复部分）**：I'm so much happier with you than I ever was (happy) with him / you are so much better for me than Paolo ever was
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m</span> <span class="c">so much happier</span> <span class="a">than I ever was</span></p>
- **as + 原级 + as（同等比较）**：it's never gonna be as good as you thought it would be
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">as good as you thought</span></p>
- **最高级 + 完成式定语从句**：this is the best I have ever had / the greatest thing that ever happened to me——"有史以来最……的"
- **口诀**：so much / even / still 常用来给比较级"加码"

</GrammarTip>
