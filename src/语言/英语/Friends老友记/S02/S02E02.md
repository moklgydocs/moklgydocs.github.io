**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E02.mp3" start="1.128" duration="7.824" />

**原句**：Ok, these were unbelievably expensive, and I know he's gonna grow out of them in like, 20 minutes, but I couldn't resist.

**层次划分**：

- 分句一：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">these</span> <span class="v">were</span> <span class="c">unbelievably expensive</span></p> S+V+C，**系动词**（台：连缀动词）were + 形容词补语；unbelievably 副词修饰形容词"贵得离谱"（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- 分句二主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">(that) he's gonna grow out of them...</span></p> know 后接**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），连接词 that 在口语中省略（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **he's gonna grow out of them in like, 20 minutes**：**be gonna + 原形** = be going to 的口语缩略，表最近将来；短语动词 **grow out of** "长大而穿不下"；**in + 时间段**表"过……之后"；like 此处是口语填充词"大约"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **I couldn't resist**：情态动词过去式 could + 否定——"（当时）忍不住"，resist 后省略宾语 buying them（resist 后接动名词，见 [Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)）

**翻译**：好吧，这玩意儿贵得离谱，而且我知道他大概 20 分钟就穿不下了，可我就是忍不住（买了）。

**语法要点**：know + (that) 宾语从句（Ch.12）；be gonna 表将来（Ch.3）；grow out of 短语动词（Ch.10）

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：This is my son having lunch, ok? It's gonna happen a lot, so you'd better get used to it. Now if you have any problem with it, if you're uncomfortable, just ask questions.

**层次划分**：

- 句一：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">This</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="c">my son having lunch</span></p> **having lunch 现在分词短语作后置定语**修饰 son——"我儿子（正在）吃午饭这件事"（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）

- **if you have any problem with it, if you're uncomfortable**：两个 if 条件**副词从句**（台：状语从句）叠加，从句用**现在式代未来**；主句 **just ask questions** 是祈使句"尽管问"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

**语法要点**：名词 + 分词（Ch.6）；had better（Ch.9）；if 从句现在式代未来（Ch.13）

**本集反意疑问句（tag question）**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **前肯后否**：It's gonna be a problem, **isn't it**?——注意 's gonna = is going to，反问只留助动词 **isn't**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">It</span> <span class="v">'s gonna be</span> <span class="c">a problem</span>, <span class="a">isn't it?</span></p>
- **前否后肯**：You didn't microwave that, **did you**?——主句否定 didn't，反问用肯定 did

- **口语变体**：Your territory, **huh**?——huh/eh/right 可顶替正式 tag
- **口诀**：反问部分 = 助动词 + 代词，肯否与主句相反；降调=求认同，升调=真疑问

**本集"过去形式表委婉"句型**——过去式/过去进行式不表过去，表客气（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **I was wondering if...**：I was just wondering **if** Joey **could** ask you a question / I was wondering **if**...you and I **could** go grab a cup of coffee——过去进行式 + if 从句（从句内 could 呼应）

- **对比记忆**：would/could/should/might 开头问句比 will/can/shall/may 更客气——Would you give that a chance, for me?
