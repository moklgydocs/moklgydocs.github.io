**层次划分**：

- **I gotta side with Chandler**：gotta = have got to"得、必须"，后接原形动词；**side with sb**"站在某人一边"；on this one"在这件事上"，one 指代前面的话题（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **When I first moved to the city**：when 时间从句；first 副词"刚……的时候、初次"修饰 moved（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- 主句：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">went out</span> <span class="a">a couple of times with this girl</span></p> **go out with sb**"与某人约会"；a couple of + 复数名词"两三（次）"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **really hot, great kisser**：省略句 = (she was) really hot, (a) great kisser——口语罗列特征常省掉主谓（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **the biggest Adam's apple**：**最高级** the biggest + 名词；Adam's apple"喉结"，名词所有格 's 作定语（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **It made me nuts**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">It</span> <span class="v">made</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">nuts</span></p> make + 宾语 + 形容词**宾语补足语**（台：补语）"使我抓狂"，S+V+O+C（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）

**翻译**：打住打住，这事儿我得站钱德勒。我刚搬到纽约那会儿，跟一个姑娘约会过几次——特辣、接吻一流——可她长了老大的喉结，真把我逼疯了。

**语法要点**：when 过去时间从句（Ch.13）；最高级 the biggest（Ch.7）；make + O + 形容词补语（Ch.1）

**原句**：His whole life was in this apartment, and now it's gone. I think it would be nice if we just took a few moments, for Mr. Heckles. He was kind of a pain, he was, but he was a person.

**层次划分**：

- **His whole life was in this apartment, and now it's gone**：and 连接对等分句；**gone** 为过去分词作形容词"（已经）没了的"，作主语补语（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）

- **he was kind of a pain, he was**：**kind of a + 名词**"有点算是个……"；句尾重复 he was 是口语强调尾（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

**语法要点**：it would be nice if + 过去式（Ch.9）；gone 作补语（Ch.6）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集 what/how 名词从句（台：名词从句）句型**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **作宾语**：You know **what we gotta do**? / You know **what you want** now. / Look **what he wrote** on them.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">You</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">what we gotta do</span></p>
- **作补语**：That's **what it says**. / This is **what I do**. / That's **how much you wanted to be with someone**!
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">That</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what it says</span></p>
- **口诀**：what = the thing that；从句内一律**陈述语序**，不倒装——说 what does he do ❌，说 what he does ✅

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,关系从句">

**本集定语从句（台：关系从句）"省与不省"规则**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **关系代词作宾语 → 可省**：one woman (that) you broke up **with** / the air (that) we breathe / every man (that) we have ever gone out **with** / things (that) you like about her
- **关系代词作主语 → 不可省**：fossils **that** actually show the evolution（化石展示……）/ anyone **who**'s crazy enough / that guy **who** eats his own face / somebody **who** rents a room
- **关系副词 where/when**：a time **when** the brightest minds believed...（= in which）/ The One **Where** Heckles Dies（剧集命名）
- **口诀**：从句里"缺主语"就不能省，"缺宾语"才能省

</GrammarTip>
