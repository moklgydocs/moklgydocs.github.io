<GrammarTip points="语气,动词时态">

- **would you slow down?**：would + 原形表委婉请求，比 will 更客气（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **they're not gonna be sold out of papers**：gonna = going to 表将来"（不）会"；**be sold out of**"卖光……"，被动式 + 介词 of 的固定搭配（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **at one o'clock in the morning**：时刻用 at、上午/下午/晚上用 in——**介词**（台：介系词）的时间搭配层级（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip points="动词时态,分词">

- **I've never gotten reviewed before**：**现在完成式 + never...before**"以前从未……"——before 把时段括到说话此刻；**get + 过去分词** reviewed 是口语被动（= be reviewed"被评论"）（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **I'm excited**：excited 过去分词作形容词"感到兴奋的"（Ch.6）

**原句**：The only thing worse than the mindless, adolescent direction was Joseph Tribbiani's disturbingly unskilled portrayal of the king.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">The only thing</span> <span class="v">was</span> <span class="c">Joseph Tribbiani's portrayal of the king</span></p> S+V+C；主语核心词 thing 是单数，故用 was（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）
- **worse than the mindless, adolescent direction**：比较级形容词短语**后置修饰** thing——"比……更糟的唯一一点"；bad 的比较级 worse + than 介词短语（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）
- **disturbingly unskilled portrayal**：副词 disturbingly 修饰形容词 unskilled"令人不安地生涩的"；Joseph Tribbiani's 名词所有格作定语（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **Here it is**：here 置于句首时，主语是**代词**则不倒装（Here it is）；若主语是名词才倒装（Here comes the bus）（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）

**翻译**：（读报）唯一比那无脑、幼稚的导演手法更糟的，是约瑟夫·特里比亚尼对国王这个角色令人不安的生涩演绎。

**语法要点**：比较级短语作后置定语（Ch.7）；长名词词组作主补（Ch.2）；主谓一致看核心词 thing（Ch.11）

</GrammarTip>

**层次划分**：

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,基本句型">

**本集 S+V+O+C 句型：宾语后面跟"补语"交代宾语的状态/动作**（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **make + O + 原形**：make Joey **feel** better / makes me **feel** so good / made him **quit** drinking
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">makes</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">feel so good</span></p>
- **make + O + 形容词**：make these coffees **Irish** / she **was all over** me（介词短语作补语）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">we</span> <span class="v">make</span> <span class="o">these coffees</span> <span class="c">Irish</span></p>
- **find / see + O + 形容词**：I find his stories much more **amusing** / have you ever seen me **ecstatic**?
- **let / set + O + 补语**：let me **finish** / set it **free**
- **口诀**：make/let 后动词补语用**原形不加 to**；find/see 后用**形容词**描述宾语状态

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集名词从句（台：名词从句）全家福**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **作宾语**：they don't know **what they're talking about** / I believe **this will change your mind** / wonder **if it was because of my talent** / did you see **who she was dating**?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">what your problem is</span></p>
- **作表语**：all she said was **that I remind her too much of somebody** / Joey, **that's who**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">All she said</span> <span class="v">was</span> <span class="c">that I remind her...</span></p>
- **作同位语**：the fact **that I will not be dating Ross** / the feeling **that she was coming on to me** / any idea **who she's talking about**
- **口诀**：wh- 从句一律**陈述语序**不倒装；that 同位语从句的 that **不可省**；think/know 的否定常**前移**到主句
