<GrammarTip points="祈使句,疑问句,口语">

- **Don't cry**：否定祈使句 = Don't + 原形，对第二人称下指令（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **Why is he still crying?**：特殊疑问句倒装——疑问词 why + 助动词 is 提到主语 he 之前；现在进行式 is crying 配 still"还在哭"（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **Who da wenny-Benny boy?**：哄婴儿的儿语——da = the，wenny-Benny 是 Ben 的叠音昵称，属语音游戏

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E06.mp3" start="11.016" duration="6.72" /> **ROSS GELLER:** <Note>Let me hold him</Note> for a sec. There. (Ben stops crying) Huh? <Note>There we are</Note>.
> 让我抱他一会儿。那里（本停止哭泣）嗯？我们到了。
<GrammarTip points="使役动词,基本句型">

<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Let</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">hold him</span></p>

- **Let me hold him**：使役动词 let + 宾语 me + 原形动词补语 hold——"让我抱他"，补语**用原形、不加 to**（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md) S+V+O+C）
- **for a sec**：= for a second，口语缩略
- **There we are**：固定感叹句"好了/搞定了"（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md) 倒装式惯用语）

</GrammarTip>

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E06.mp3" start="48.768" duration="6.072" /> **MONICA GELLER:** <Note>What if my own baby hates me</Note>? Huh? <Note>What am I gonna do then</Note>?

- **What if + 从句**：固定假设句型"要是……怎么办"，从句用现在式 hates 表未来可能（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **What am I gonna do then?**：be gonna = be going to 的口语缩略，表最近将来"那我该怎么办"；疑问句助动词 am 倒装到主语 I 前（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）

</GrammarTip>

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E06.mp3" start="54.84" duration="14.856" />

**原句**：Do you know how long it's gonna be before you actually have to deal with this problem?

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">how long it's gonna be before...</span></p> 一般疑问句助动词 do 提前（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
- **how long it's gonna be**：**宾语从句**（台：名词从句）作 know 的宾语——疑问词 how long 引导，从句内用**陈述语序** it's gonna be，不倒装（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **before you actually have to deal with this problem**：before 引导时间**状语从句**（台：副词从句），从句用现在式 have to 表未来（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **deal with**：短语动词"处理/应对"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **she does not look fat**：借 does 构成否定，系动词 look"看起来"退回原形（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）

**翻译**：你知道距你真正必须面对这个问题还有多久吗？

**语法要点**：名词从句陈述语序（Ch.12）；before 从句现在式代未来（Ch.13）；否定借 do（Ch.9）

</GrammarTip>

**本集口语缩略与特殊发音汇总**（听音频时对照）：

- **助动词缩略**：gonna = going to / wanna = want to / gotta = got to / kinda = kind of / 'cause = because / outta = out of / 'bout = about / gimme = give me / lemme = let me

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役动词">

**本集使役句型：let / make + 宾语 + 原形补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

带娃场景里命令、请求满天飞，使役句型出现率全剧第一：

- **let + O + 原形**：Let me hold him / Let me see that / he won't let me work / let her go on / Let go of my hand
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Let</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">hold him</span></p>
- **make + O + 原形**：she makes me want to put my finger... / it made me happy / to make myself feel better
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">makes</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">want to put...</span></p>
- **句型拆解**：使役动词的补语是**原形动词**——因为补语描述的是"宾语做的动作"，不是另一个限定谓语；两个谓语动词不能裸奔相撞，必须有一个退化（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **感官动词同型**：pay to hear me **play** / I see someone'**s having** an allergic reaction——hear/see 后接原形（看全程）或 V-ing（看进行）（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">see</span> <span class="o">someone</span> <span class="c">'s having...</span></p>
- **want + O + to 不定式补语**对比：You want Daddy **to change** your diaper / He wants **to talk** to you——want 不是使役动词，补语必须带 to（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **help + O + 原形/to 两可**：help me (to) carry——本集未出现，顺带对比（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **注意**：换成被动式 to 要还回来——He was made **to** wait. / She was heard **to** sing.

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集宾语从句（台：名词从句）的高频两件套**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

本集几乎每位角色都在"动词 + 从句"——这是口语里出现率最高的从句类型，务必练到脱口而出：

- **that 省略**：I thought it was a key lime pie / I assume we're talking about the baby / I'm assuming one of you is the father / I hope Ben has a little sister / I'm sorry I almost broke your hand / I know it's not your big money song / Are you sure he didn't break it
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">hope</span> <span class="o">(that) Ben has a little sister</span></p>
- **否定前移**：I don't think we brought enough stuff / I don't think everybody gets Smelly Cat / I don't think so——语义否定从句，形式上 not 住主句
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">don't think</span> <span class="o">(that) we brought enough</span></p>
- **mean/guess 同用法**：I **meant** (that) he's gonna be paying that other woman / I **guess** that's out the window——同样省 that（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **疑问词从句用陈述语序**：Do you know how long it's gonna be...? / I was wondering what someone might do / you know which one is our baby——从句内部**不倒装**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">which one is our baby</span></p>
- **if = whether**：My brother would like to know **if there's any way to treat this orally**——if 引导宾语从句表"是否"，从句同样用陈述语序（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **what 引导的名词从句**：That's **what makes it so special** / that's **what's gonna kill me** / I was wondering **what someone might do**——what = the thing that，从句内陈述语序（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">That</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what makes it so special</span></p>
- **口诀**：think/assume/hope/know/say/be sure/be sorry + 从句，口语一律省 that；要否定从句时，not 搬家到主句；疑问词开头的从句，语序照常陈述

</GrammarTip>
