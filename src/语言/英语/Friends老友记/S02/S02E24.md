<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E24.mp3" start="45.936" duration="11.088" /> **JOEY TRIBBIANI:** See after the scene, Mr. Beatty <Note>comes up to me and says</Note> 'good actor, bad kisser'. <Note>Can you believe that</Note>, me not a good kisser, <Note>that's like</Note>, like Mother Theresa, not a good mother.
> 看完戏后，比蒂先生走到我跟前说‘好演员，坏接吻者’。你能相信吗，我不是一个好的接吻者，就像特里萨修女，不是一个好母亲。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,历史现在式,类比">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E24.mp3" start="45.936" duration="11.088" />

**原句**：See after the scene, Mr. Beatty comes up to me and says 'good actor, bad kisser'. Can you believe that, me not a good kisser, that's like, like Mother Theresa, not a good mother.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">Mr. Beatty</span> <span class="v">comes up to</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="conj">and</span> <span class="v">says</span> <span class="o">'good actor, bad kisser'</span></p>
- **comes up to me and says**：叙述刚发生的事却用**现在式**——历史现在式（historic present），让画面更生动；comes 与 says 并列（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **after the scene**：介词短语作时间状语"那场戏一完"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **'good actor, bad kisser'**：引语是省略的平行结构 = (You're a) good actor, (but a) bad kisser（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **Can you believe that, me not a good kisser**：believe 的宾语 that 指被评价这件事；me, not a good kisser 是同位语式惊叹补充"我，居然不是接吻高手"
- **that's like Mother Theresa, not a good mother**：like 介词"就像"，类比句 = that's like (saying) Mother Theresa (is) not a good mother（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：结果那场戏一完，比蒂先生走过来跟我说"戏好，吻差"。你能信吗？我，居然不是接吻高手——这就好比说特蕾莎修女不是个好母亲。

**语法要点**：历史现在式（Ch.3）；并列省略（Ch.15）；like 表类比（Ch.10）

</GrammarTip>
