**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S02/S02E13.mp3" start="23.568" duration="7.632" />

**原句**：I don't know what Big Leon told ya but it's an even thousand if you want me for the whole night. What is this for?

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">don't know</span> <span class="o">what Big Leon told ya</span></p> **what Big Leon told ya**：what 引导**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），从句内 what 作 told 的直接宾语——tell sb sth 是 S+V+O+O 双宾句型；ya = you 的口语（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **it's an even thousand**：even 程度副词"整整"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **if you want me for the whole night**：if 条件**副词从句**（台：状语从句），现在式代未来（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **What is this for?**：疑问句中介词 for 滞留句尾（= This is for **what**?）；口语 What...for? ≈ Why...?"这是干什么的？"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：我不知道大里昂跟你说了什么，但整晚包我是一千整。这钱是什么意思？

**语法要点**：what 名词从句作宾语（Ch.12）；if 条件句现在式代未来（Ch.13）；介词滞留句尾（Ch.10）

**文化梗**：Chandler 假装误会 Joey 是付"过夜费"的男伴游掮客。

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：That's the beauty part, it goes with everything. You put this on, you're good to go. Ohhh man, you are so wearin' that bracelet.

**层次划分**：

- **it goes with everything**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">goes with</span> <span class="o">everything</span></p> go with"与……相配"；主语 it 第三人称单数 → goes，**主谓一致**（台：主语动词一致性）（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **You put this on, you're good to go**：口语**条件并立句** = **If** you put this on, you're good to go——两个独立句并排，前句暗含条件；good to go 惯用语"一切就绪"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **you are so wearin' that bracelet**：so + 现在进行式——这里的进行式不表"正在"，而是强调"你铁定得戴"，带强烈情绪色彩（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

**翻译**：不不，妙就妙在它百搭。你一戴上，立马精神焕发。哦老兄，这手镯你非戴不可。

**语法要点**：并立句暗含条件（Ch.13）；so + 进行式的强调用法（Ch.8/Ch.3）；go with（Ch.10）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集 wh- 名词从句（台：名词从句）三种角色**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **作宾语**：I don't know **what Big Leon told ya**. / tell me **what you're doing** / Guess **what Gunther found**? / I can't believe **you did that**.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">don't know</span> <span class="o">what Big Leon told ya</span></p>
- **作补语**：that'**s what they used to cover Connecticut**. / That'**s what they'll call us**. / that'**s what it's there for**.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what they'll call us</span></p>
- **作同位语**：the possibility **that I won't make rent** / the fact **that I wore the bracelet** / the fact **that we're best buds**——that 不作成分、不可换成 which
- **口诀**：wh- 从句一律陈述语序不倒装（what you're doing ✅ / what are you doing ❌ 只在独立问句成立）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,语气">

**本集"情态动词 + have + 过去分词"回顾过去**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **should have + p.p.**＝本该做（却没做）：you **should have just said** so.（你本该直说）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">should have said</span> <span class="o">so</span></p>
- **could have + p.p.**＝本来能做（却没做）：he **coulda gotten** me a VCR.（coulda = could have）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">could have gotten</span> <span class="o">me a VCR</span></p>
- **must have + p.p.**＝肯定做了（推测）：It **must have cost** you quite a few debloons.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">It</span> <span class="v">must have cost</span> <span class="o">you quite a few debloons</span></p>
- **对比**：must **be thinking** of someone else＝对"此刻正在"的推测（have 换 be + V-ing，时间从过去换成现在）

</GrammarTip>
