# The One with All the Thanksgivings

The One With The Thanksgiving Flashbacks

**Written by: Greg Malins**

**Transcribed by: Eric Aasen**

> 🎬 *莫妮卡和瑞秋家，每个人都刚吃完感恩节晚餐，都在为自己的丰满而呻吟。*

**Phoebe Buffay:** Monica your remote doesn't work.

**Monica Geller:** That's very nice.
> 那太好了。

**Chandler Bing:** That's sweet, Joey.

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yeah, the other day I was at the bus-stop and this lovely fall breeze came in out of nowhere and blew this chick's skirt right up. Oh! Which reminds me, I'm also thankful for thongs. (Note: Actually, I think every guy is thankful for thongs. That and spandex. J )
> 是的，前几天我在公共汽车站，一阵秋风不知从哪里吹来，吹得这小妞的裙子直冒。哦这提醒了我，我也很感谢皮带。（注：事实上，我认为每个男人都会感谢丁字裤和氨纶）
