🎬 *Monica and Rachel's, Rachel has just gotten home and is going through the mail. She finds something that's Monica's and goes over and knocks on her closed bedroom door.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡和瑞秋家，瑞秋刚到家，正在看邮件。她找到了莫妮卡的东西，走过去敲她关着的卧室门。*

**Monica Geller:** (In a sexy voice) Come in. I've been waiting for you.
> （用性感的声音）进来。我一直在等你。

*(Rachel enters.)*
> *（瑞秋进来了。）*

**Rachel Green:** Hi! I just wanna-(sees Monica)-Ahhh!!! Oh my God! (She runs out in horror.) Oh my God!
> 嗨！我只想-（见莫妮卡）-啊哈！！！哦，我的上帝！（她惊恐地跑了出来。）哦，天哪！

**Monica Geller:** (pulling on a robe) Okay, I'm sorry! I'm sorry! I-I-I was um, I was taking a nap.
> （穿上长袍）好吧，对不起！我很抱歉！我，我，我在小睡一会儿。

**Rachel Green:** Since when do take naps in that position. Oh God Monica, tell me you were waiting for a guy! Please tell me you were waiting for a guy!
> 从什么时候开始，你用那种姿势打盹。天哪，莫妮卡，告诉我你在等一个人！请告诉我你在等一个人！

**Monica Geller:** Yes. Yes, I was. A guy. From work. (Thinks) I'm seeing a guy from work! Ha!
> 是的。是的，我是。一个人。从工作中。（想）我要去看一个下班的家伙！哈

**Rachel Green:** (Gasps) That cute waiter guy from your restaurant, the one that looks like a non-threatening Ray Liotta?
> （喘息）你们餐厅那个可爱的服务员，看起来像个没有威胁的雷·利奥塔？

**Monica Geller:** Uh-huh, that one!
> 嗯，那个！

**Rachel Green:** Y'know what, just give me a second and I'll be out of your hair. I'm just gonna grab a jacket. When I get back, I want every little detail. (There's a knock on the door.) Maybe that's him. (Goes to answer the door.)
> 你知道吗，只要给我一秒钟，我就不烦你了。我去拿件夹克。当我回来的时候，我想要每一个细节。（有人敲门）也许是他。（去开门。）

**Monica Geller:** (Panicking) Okay, umm, okay, umm… (Rachel opens the door.) It's just Joey and Ross.
> （惊慌失措）好吧，嗯，好吧，嗯…（瑞秋开门）只有乔伊和罗斯。

**Rachel Green:** Why aren't you guys at the movie?
> 你们为什么不去看电影？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well, we were! But Ross was talking so loud on his phone they threw us out!
> 嗯，我们是！但是罗斯在电话里说得太大声了，他们把我们赶了出去！

**Ross Geller:** I had to talk loud because the movie was loud!
> 我不得不大声说话，因为电影太吵了！

**Joey Tribbiani:** (to Monica and Rachel) He's talking to London!
> （对莫妮卡和瑞秋）他在跟伦敦说话！

**Monica Geller:** But why?! Did he get in touch with Emily?
> 但是为什么？！他和艾米丽联系了吗？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well no, not yet. He's calling everyone on her side of the family hoping that someone will help him get in touch with her.
> 不，还没有。他打电话给她家里的每一个人，希望有人能帮助他与她取得联系。

**Ross Geller:** (on phone) I-I-I don't care if I said some other girl's name you prissy, old twit!
> （在电话里）我，我，我不在乎我是否说了别的女孩的名字，你这个百里茜，老笨蛋！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Ross! Way to suck up to the family.
> 罗斯！讨好家人的方式。

**Chandler Bing:** (entering, happily, with a bottle of champagne, thinking that Monica is the only one there) Ha-ha-ha-(sees everyone)-enh-enh. I'm so glad you guys are all here! My office finally got wrinkle free fax paper!
> （兴高采烈地端着一瓶香槟进来，心想莫妮卡是那里唯一的一个）哈哈哈-（见到大家）-恩恩恩。我很高兴你们都来了！我的办公室终于有了无皱纹的传真纸！

🎬 *Monica and Rachel's, the next morning, Phoebe, Chandler, Ross, and Monica are eating breakfast.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡和瑞秋家，第二天早上，菲比、钱德勒、罗斯和莫妮卡正在吃早餐。*

**Joey Tribbiani:** (entering, wearing a tux) Hey!
> （进来，穿着燕尾服）嘿！

**Chandler Bing:** Oh no-no-no-no-no-no, vomit tux! No-no, vomit tux!
> 哦，不，不，呕吐的礼服！不，不，吐燕尾服！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Don't worry, I had it dry-cleaned.
> 别担心，我已经干洗过了。

**Monica Geller:** Vomit tux? Who vomited on—y'know what, what you up to Joe?
> 吐燕尾服？谁吐了你知道吗，你在跟乔干什么？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well, I'm doing this telethon thing on TV and my agent got me a job as co-host!
> 嗯，我在电视上做这个电视节目，我的经纪人给我找了份共同主持的工作！

**Monica Geller:** Oh that's great!
> 哦，太好了！

**Joey Tribbiani:** A little uh, good deed for PBS and a little TV exposure, now that's the kind of math Joey likes to do!
> 一点，嗯，PBS的好事和一点电视曝光，这就是乔伊喜欢做的数学！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ugh, PBS!
> 啊，PBS！

**Monica Geller:** What's wrong with PBS?
> PBS怎么了？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ugh, what's right with them?
> 呃，他们怎么了？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Why don’t you like PBS, Pheebs?
> 菲比，你为什么不喜欢PBS？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Okay, 'cause right after my mom killed herself, I was just in this really bad place, y'know personally. So, I just thought that it'd make me feel better if I wrote to Sesame Street, 'cause they were so nice when I was a little kid! No one ever wrote back.
> 好吧，因为就在我妈妈自杀之后，我就在这个非常糟糕的地方，你知道的。所以，我只是觉得如果我给芝麻街写信会让我感觉更好，因为我小时候他们都很好！没有人回信。

**Chandler Bing:** Well y'know a lot of those Muppets don't have thumbs.
> 你知道很多布偶都没有拇指。

**Phoebe Buffay:** All I got was a lousy key chain! And by that time I was living in a box. I didn't have keys!
> 我得到的只是一个糟糕的钥匙链！那时我已经住在一个盒子里了。我没有钥匙！

**Joey Tribbiani:** I'm sorry Pheebs, I just, y'know, I just wanted to do a good deed. Like-like you did with the babies.
> 对不起，菲比，我只是，你知道，我只是想做一件好事。就像你对待孩子一样。

**Phoebe Buffay:** This isn't a good deed, you just wanted to get on TV! This is totally selfish.
> 这不是好事，你只是想上电视！这完全是自私的。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Whoa! Whoa! Whoa! What about you, having those babies for your brother? Talk about selfish!
> 哇！哇！哇！那你呢，给你弟弟生孩子？说自私！

**Phoebe Buffay:** What-what are you talking about?!
> 你在说什么？！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well, yeah, it was a really nice thing and all, but it made you feel really good right?
> 嗯，是的，这是一件非常好的事情，但它让你感觉非常好，对吗？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah. So?
> 是的。所以

**Joey Tribbiani:** It made you feel good, so that makes it selfish. Look, there's no unselfish good deeds, sorry.
> 它让你感觉很好，所以它很自私。听着，没有无私的善行，对不起。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yes there are! There are totally good deeds that are selfless.
> 是的，有！有完全无私的善行。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well, may I ask for one example?
> 我可以问一个例子吗？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah, it's… Y'know there's—no you may not!
> 是的，是……你知道，不，你不可以！

*(They are standing on either side of Chandler as they discuss the point. Chandler, meanwhile, is disgusted with the whole argument.)*
> *（他们站在钱德勒一边讨论这一点。与此同时，钱德勒对整个争论感到厌恶。）*

**Joey Tribbiani:** That's because all people are selfish.
> 那是因为所有人都是自私的。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Are you calling me selfish?!
> 你说我自私吗？！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Are you calling you people? (Chandler rolls his eyes.) Yeah, well sorry to burst that bubble, Pheebs, but selfless good deeds don't exist. Okay? And you the deal on Santa Clause right?
> 你是在给你们打电话吗？（钱德勒转动眼睛）是的，很抱歉打破了这个泡沫，菲比，但无私的善行是不存在的。可以你也同意圣诞老人条款，对吗？

**Phoebe Buffay:** I'm gonna find a selfless good dead. I'm gonna beat you, you evil genius.
> 我会找到一个无私的好人。我要打败你，你这个邪恶的天才。

*(Ross's phone rings and he answers it.)*
> *（罗斯的电话响了，他接了。）*

**Ross Geller:** (on phone) Hello.
> （打电话）你好。

**Emily:** (on phone from London) Hello, Ross?
> （从伦敦打来的电话）你好，罗斯？

**Ross Geller:** Emily? Emily! Oh my God! Oh my God, it's Emily! (He picks up a lamp and hands it to Chandler, for no reason.) It's Emily everyone! Shush-shush-shhst! (to Emily) Hi!
> 艾米丽？艾米丽！哦，我的上帝！天哪，是艾米丽！（他拿起一盏灯，无缘无故地递给钱德勒。）大家都是艾米丽！嘘嘘嘘嘘！（对艾米丽）嗨！

**Emily:** Ross, I'm only ringing to say stop harassing my relatives. Good-bye!
> 罗斯，我打电话只是想说别再骚扰我的亲戚了。再见！

**Ross Geller:** No wait! Look, wait! Okay, you can hang up, but I'm gonna keep calling! I'm gonna, I'm gonna call everyone in England if that's what it takes to get you to talk to me!
> 不，等等！看，等等！好的，你可以挂断，但我会继续打电话！我要，我要打电话给英格兰的每个人，如果你能和我说话的话！

**Emily:** Really? About what?
> 真的吗？关于什么？

**Ross Geller:** Look you're my wife. We're-we're married. Y'know? I-I love you. I-I really miss you.
> 看，你是我妻子。我们结婚了。你知道吗？我爱你。我真的很想你。

**Emily:** I miss you to. Well, at least I think I do.
> 我想念你。嗯，至少我想我是这样。

**Ross Geller:** (to the gang, whispering) She's talking.
> （对那帮人耳语）她在说话。

**All:** (subdued) Yay! (He motions for them to keep quiet, including Chandler who is still holding the lamp Ross handed him, before he goes off to talk to Emily in private.)
> （被制服）耶！（他示意他们保持安静，包括钱德勒，他仍然拿着罗斯递给他的灯，然后他就去和艾米丽私下谈话。）

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hey, Joey, when you said the deal with Santa Clause, you meant?
> 嘿，乔伊，当你说和圣诞老人的交易条款时，你的意思是？

**Joey Tribbiani:** That he doesn't exist.
> 他根本不存在。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Right. (She turns and opens her eyes in shock.)
> 对。（她转过身，震惊地睁开眼睛。）

🎬 *Central Perk, Chandler, Monica, and Rachel are there.*
> 🎬 *中央公园、钱德勒、莫妮卡和瑞秋在那里*

> 罗斯，还有一件事真的让我害怕。
