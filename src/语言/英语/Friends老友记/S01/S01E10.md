<GrammarTip points="存在句,关系从句">

<p class="svoc"><span class="a">There</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="s">a somebody (I'd like you to meet)</span></p>

- **There's a somebody**：There be 存在句“有那么一个人”；somebody 前加 a，把“某人”说成一个具体的“某位”（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,动名词,关系从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E10.mp3" start="44.088" duration="12.072" />

**原句**：I think you reach a certain age, having a roommate is kinda pathe- ... that's 'pathet', which is Sanskrit for 'really cool way to live'.

**层次划分**：

- **I think (that)...**：think 后接**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），that 在口语中常省略（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **(when) you reach a certain age**：口语把时间从句压进 think 的从句里——“人到了某个年纪”（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **having a roommate is kinda pathetic**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">having a roommate</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="c">kinda pathetic</span></p> **动名词**词组作主语，**主谓一致**（台：主语动词一致性）上视为单数，动词用 is；Ross 把 pathetic（可悲的）紧急刹成 pathe-（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)/[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）
- **which is Sanskrit for 'really cool way to live'**：非限定关系从句，which 指代生造词 pathet——Ross 说漏嘴后现场瞎编“这是梵语，意思是超酷的生活方式”挽尊（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）

**翻译**：不会吧，我不知道……我是说，人到了一定年纪，还跟室友合住就有点可悲——（猛然意识到面前两位正合租）……呃抱歉，那是“pathet”，梵语，意思是“超酷的生活方式”。

**语法要点**：动名词作主语用单数（Ch.5/Ch.11）；非限定 which 从句（Ch.14）；think + 省略 that 的宾语从句（Ch.12）

</GrammarTip>

- **I wanted this to work**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">wanted</span> <span class="o">this</span> <span class="c">to work</span></p> want + 宾语 + 不定式补语“我多想让它（这段关系）成啊”（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **I'm still in there, changing his diapers, pickin' his fleas**：be in there (doing)“还在坚持付出”；两个现在分词短语 changing.../pickin'... 作伴随状语（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **he's just phoning it in**：phone it in（惯用语）“敷衍了事、出工不出力”——字面“打个电话交差”（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **(It's) just so hard to accept the fact that...**：句首省略形式主语 It's；**the fact that...** 是同位语从句，that 不可省（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **something (that) you love so much doesn't love you back**：关系从句修饰 something，that 作 love 的宾语省略；love you back“回爱你”（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）

**翻译**：我太想让我们之间好好的了。我是说，我还在掏心掏肺——给他换尿布、替他捉跳蚤……可他只是在敷衍了事。最难接受的就是这个事实：你爱得那么深的（东西），却不回爱你。

**语法要点**：the fact + that 同位语从句（Ch.12）；want + O + to do（Ch.4）；分词伴随状语（Ch.6）

</GrammarTip>

**本集句型总复习一：将来时间的四种说法**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **be going to / gonna（打算）**：Is he gonna live with you? / I'm gonna be the only one standing there alone?
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">is gonna live</span> <span class="a">with you</span></p>
- **现在进行式（已定安排）**：I'm doing all new material tonight. / Paolo's catching an earlier flight. / you are so going to Minsk!
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">Paolo</span> <span class="v">'s catching</span> <span class="o">an earlier flight</span></p>
- **will / 'll（预测、承诺）**：I'll never forget you. / it'll be midnight / no-one'll know who's with who
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ll never forget</span> <span class="o">you</span></p>
- **现在式（时刻表、既定日程）**：when do you leave? / the diet starts tomorrow
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">the diet</span> <span class="v">starts</span> <span class="a">tomorrow</span></p>
- **口诀**：打算 gonna、定好用进行、预测承诺 will、时刻表用现在式

</GrammarTip>

**本集句型总复习二：动词 + (that) 宾语从句**（台：名词从句）（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **that 口语常省略**：I think (that) you reach a certain age / I assume (that) it's important enough / I figured (that) you taught him that / I can't believe (that) he hasn't kissed you yet
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">can't believe</span> <span class="o">he hasn't kissed you yet</span></p>
