- **be going to**（打算／可以预见）：What are you going to make? / He's not going to hurt you! / how're you going to talk dirty to her?
- **will / 'll**（断言／临场的决定）：you'll get there / you will care about it / I'll see you on Monday! / we'll share
- **will be + V-ing**（将来进行，流程在走）：we'll be serving some delicious onion tartlets
- **be + V-ing**（已敲定的安排）：I'm cooking dinner for him Monday night.
- **时间／条件从句里一律现在式代未来**：when he gets here / if you just wait another six and a half minutes / if you can't talk dirty to me
