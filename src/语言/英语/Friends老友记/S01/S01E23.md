🎯 **本场景语法重点**：现在进行式 / be going to 将来 / 现在完成式 / used to / 疑问词 + the hell

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E23.mp3" start="8.088" duration="6.912" /> **Ross Geller:** She's not here yet. She's not here. <Note>She's having my baby</Note> and she's not here.
> 她还没来。她不在这里。她生了我的孩子，但她不在这里。
<GrammarTip points="动词时态,副词">

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">She</span> <span class="v">'s having</span> <span class="o">my baby</span></p>

- **She's having my baby**：现在进行式表"正在/即将发生"——have a baby"生孩子"，这里用进行式强调"这一刻就要生了"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **not...yet**："还没……"，yet 用于否定句句尾（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- 三个短句连发，把准爸爸的焦虑推到顶

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E23.mp3" start="15.0" duration="4.848" /> **Monica Geller:** I'm sure everything's fine. <Note>Has her water broke yet</Note>?
> 我相信一切都很好。她的水破了吗？
<GrammarTip points="名词从句,动词时态,原文勘误">

- **I'm sure (that) everything's fine**：sure 后接宾语从句（台：名词从句），口语常省 that（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- ⚠️ **Has her water broke yet**：完成式应用过去分词 **broken**——`broke` 是过去式，此处属口语不规范/抄写问题；**her water breaks** 是"破羊水"的惯用语（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E23.mp3" start="19.848" duration="5.712" /> **Ross Geller:** I don't know, but <Note>when I spoke to her</Note>, she said <Note>she had already passed the mucus plug</Note>.
> 我不知道，但当我和她说话时，她说她已经通过了粘液塞。
<GrammarTip points="副词从句,名词从句,动词时态">

- **when I spoke to her**：时间状语从句（台：副词从句），交代主句动作的时间（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **she said (that) she had already passed the mucus plug**：said 后接宾语从句（台：名词从句），省略 that；从句用**过去完成式** had passed——"说"是过去，"通过粘液塞"发生在"说"之前（过去的过去）；already 与完成式搭配"已经"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

</GrammarTip>

- **to know**：不定式（台：不定词）；know about"了解（详情）"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）

---

**Q1**：Lydia 说 *He couldn't hit water if he was standing on a boat.* 为什么从句里用过去式 **was standing**，而不用 is standing？

<GrammarTip points="查看答案">

**答**：这是**虚拟语气**（与现在事实相反）：他又没站在船上，所以 if 从句的时态"后退一步"用过去式 was standing，主句配 couldn't/wouldn't——整句意思是"他就是站在船上，也投不中水"。正式文体用 if he **were** standing，口语里 was 很常见。同集另一句：if someone **was havin'** my baby somewhere, I'**d** wanna know about it.（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md) 语气）

</GrammarTip>

**Q2**：Monica 说 *How come they get two?* 为什么不说 How come **do** they get two？

<GrammarTip points="查看答案">

**答**：**How come 后面永远用陈述语序，主谓不倒装**。它可以理解成 How does it come (that)... 的固化简化，从句部分保持"主语 + 动词"的陈述句骨架。同集：How come you never **mentioned** Ben before?（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md) 倒装）

</GrammarTip>

**Q3**：Chandler 说 *if neither one of us are married*，neither one 到底是单数还是复数？

<GrammarTip points="查看答案">

**答**：**neither one (of us) 按语法规则是单数**，正式文体应说 neither one of us **is** married——"两者中没有一个"。口语里常受 of us（复数）影响而说成 are，这就是本句的情况。**主谓一致**（台：主语动词一致性）看真正的主语，不看介词短语里的名词。（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md) 主谓一致）

</GrammarTip>

**Q4**：Joey 说 *I don't think that's his name.* 他明明想否定的是"那不是他的名字"，为什么 not 跑到 think 前面？

<GrammarTip points="查看答案">

**答**：这是**否定前移（否定转移）**：think/believe/suppose 等动词接宾语从句时，语义上否定从句的 not 习惯移到主句——I don't think that's his name = I think that **isn't** his name，前者更地道。（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md) 名词从句）

</GrammarTip>

**Q5**：Phoebe 说 *your negative fighting noises are not the first thing they should be hearing*，thing 后面为什么没有 that/which？

<GrammarTip points="查看答案">

**答**：关系代词在**定语从句**（台：关系从句）中**作宾语时可以省略**——补全是 the first thing (that) they should be hearing，that 作 hearing 的宾语，故省略。对比本集不可省略的情况：anybody **who's** not an ex-husband——who 在从句中作主语，必须保留。（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md) 关系从句）

</GrammarTip>

**Q6**：护士说 *anybody who's not an ex-husband or a lesbian life partner, out you go!* 为什么是 **out you go** 而不是 out go you？医生却说 **Here he comes**？

<GrammarTip points="查看答案">

**答**：两句都是**方向副词提前的倒装惯用语**，规则是：**主语是名词时全倒装**（Here comes the doctor），**主语是代词时不倒装**——所以 out **you** go、Here **he** comes 都保持"副词 + 代词 + 动词"的语序。对比：There goes the train（名词主语，全倒装）。（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md) 倒装）

</GrammarTip>

**Q7**：Ross 说 *you get to go home with the baby*，Susan 说 *You get to be the baby's father*——get to 是什么意思？和 have to 一样吗？

<GrammarTip points="查看答案">

**答**：**get to + 原形 = 得以、有机会做**（通常是好事），和 have to（不得不）意思几乎相反。Ross 酸 Susan"你**能**带孩子回家"，Susan 回敬"你**能**当名正言顺的爸爸"——同一个 get to，两边都觉得自己吃亏。同集还有：you **get to** sleep with her, I get the cramps.（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md) 不定词）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,动词时态">

**本集将来时句型：be going to / will + when、if 从句现在式代未来**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）：

- **be going to（gonna）表打算/即将**：What are you gonna do when you have a baby? / I'm gonna be in the waitin' room, handing out cigars. / It's gonna be ok.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m gonna be</span> <span class="a">in the waitin' room</span></p>
- **will 表预测/承诺**：You'll get one. / They wouldn't miss this. / I'll see you guys later.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">You</span> <span class="v">'ll get</span> <span class="o">one</span></p>
- **will 的否定 won't / wouldn't**：Why won't I be married when I'm 40? / they wouldn't miss this——"才不会/绝不会"的口吻
- **时间/条件从句里用现在式代替未来式**：When we'**re** 40, if neither one of us **are** married... / when I'**m** 40 / When this day **is** over——从句中不说 will be
- **口诀**：主句将来（will/gonna），从句现在；when、if、until、before 一律照此办理

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,疑问句">

**本集特色疑问句四种**（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **疑问词 + the hell 加强语气**：Where the hell have you been? / Who the hell is Jordie?——"到底/究竟"
- **How come + 陈述语序（不倒装）**：How come they get two? / How come you never mentioned Ben before?——永远不说 How come do they...
<p class="svoc"><span class="a">How come</span> <span class="s">they</span> <span class="v">get</span> <span class="o">two</span></p>
- **What do you mean + 从句/引语**：What do you mean, back to Jordie? / What do you mean this might be your only chance?——mean 后一律陈述语序
- **惯用句型**：What's with the guitar?（带这干嘛）/ What's the deal with you and doctors?（怎么回事）/ What does he look like?（长什么样）
