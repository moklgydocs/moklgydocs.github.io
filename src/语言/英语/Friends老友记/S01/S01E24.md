🎬 *Central Perk, the whole gang is there, Ross is showing pictures of his new baby boy, Ben, to the group.*

🎯 **本场景语法重点**：名词所有格 / have got 表"有" / 情态动词 must·will·would / 短语动词 help out·pay back

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E24.mp3" start="0.0" duration="2.832" /> **Ross Geller:** And <Note>here's little Ben</Note> <Note>nodding off</Note>...

<GrammarTip points="存在句,分词">

<p class="svoc"><span class="a">Here</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="s">little Ben</span> <span class="c">nodding off</span></p>

- **here's + 名词**：Here be 句型"瞧，（这）有……"，与 there be 存在句同族，here 前置引人注意（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **nodding off**：现在分词短语作主语补语，描写 Ben 正在进行的动作"打瞌睡"；nod off = "打盹"（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **And 起句**：口语叙事爱用 And 开头承接上文，相当于"（还有呢，）你瞧……"（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）

</GrammarTip>

- **Aunt Monica's little boy**：名词所有格 's 作定语——"莫妮卡姑姑的（小宝贝）"；小孩子面前自称 Aunt Monica 是英语哄娃习惯（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E24.mp3" start="8.232" duration="8.52" /> **Rachel Green:** Oh, let me see! (grabs picture) Oh, God, <Note>is he just the sweetest thing</Note>? <Note>You must just want to kiss him</Note> <Note>all over</Note>!

<GrammarTip points="形容词比较,情态动词,介词">

- **let me see**：let + 宾语 + 原形动词作**宾语补足语**（台：补语）"让我看看"（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **the sweetest thing**：形容词最高级 the + -est"最可爱的小人儿"，thing 是对婴儿的昵称用法（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）
- **must just want to kiss him**：情态动词 **must 表推测**"你准是、肯定想……"，不是"必须"；不定式（台：不定词）to kiss 作 want 的宾语（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E24.mp3" start="72.336" duration="15.336" />

**原句**：It's not that big a deal. I just go down there every other day and make my contribution to the project. But at the end of two weeks, I get seven hundred dollars.

**层次划分**：

- **it's not that big a deal**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">'s not</span> <span class="c">that big a deal</span></p> 程度副词 **that**（= so）插在 big 前"没那么大不了"；注意语序是 that + 形容词 + a + 名词，不是 that big deal（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **every other day**："每隔一天"，every other + 单数时间名词（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **make my contribution to the project**：make a contribution to..."为……作贡献"，contribution 后固定接介词 to（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **at the end of + 时段**："在……结束时"；**I get seven hundred dollars**：一般现在式叙述既定安排"（到时候）就能拿到七百块"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

**翻译**：没什么大不了的。我就是隔天去一趟……给项目"出一份力"。嘿，两周一到手，七百美元就到账了。

**语法要点**：that + adj. + a + n. 的程度结构（Ch.8）；contribution to 的固定搭配（Ch.10）；现在式表既定安排（Ch.3）

</GrammarTip>

**本集双宾语句型：S + V + 人 + 物**（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **give 系**：give her this for me / give him a message / give us a chance
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">give</span> <span class="o">him</span> <span class="o">a message</span></p>
- **tell / show / wish 系**：tell me you're only donating... / show it to Ben / wish her a happy birthday
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">wish</span> <span class="o">her</span> <span class="o">a happy birthday</span></p>
- **buy / cost / owe 系**：bought her that crystal duck / cost him a fortune / owe me 17 jillion dollars
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">owe</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="o">17 jillion dollars</span></p>
- **注意**：间接宾语（人）在前、直接宾语（物）在后；人若后移要加介词——give a message **to** him / buy a duck **for** her（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,关系从句">

**本集定语从句（台：关系从句）关系代词"省与不省"**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **作宾语 → 可省**：a girl (whom) I like / that guy (whom) she met / the blouse (that) you got me / the most generous man (that) I ever met / the response (that) I got
<p class="svoc"><span class="o">a girl</span> (<span class="o">whom</span>) <span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">like</span></p>
- **作主语 → 不可省**：guys **who**'re always 'me, me, me' / the thing **that** usually makes me great / the kind of thing **that** can wait
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">makes</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">great</span></p>
- **口诀**：关系代词在从句里"有活儿干"（作主语）就不能省；"只是挂名宾语"口语里统统可省
