**层次划分**：

**本集关系从句句型：关系代词"作宾语可省、作主语不可省"**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **作宾语 → 可省**：the stuff (that) you bought / everything (that) I want / plays (that) I wanna see / people (that) I don't know / a coupla things (that) I want to say
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">bought</span> <span class="o">(that = the stuff)</span></p>
- **作主语 → 不可省**：a name **that**'s more neutral / the woman **that** stole your credit card / a movie **that** changed my life / the person **who** needs to fluff the pillows
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">(that = the woman)</span> <span class="v">stole</span> <span class="o">your credit card</span></p>
- **口诀**：把从句单独拎出来看——关系代词在从句里"当主语就不能省，当宾语随便省"

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,强调句">

**本集强调句型：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who...**（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）：

- **It was my credit card (that) you were using.**——强调的是宾语"我的信用卡"
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">It</span> <span class="v">was</span> <span class="c">my credit card</span> <span class="a">(that) you were using</span></p>
- **It wasn't me who turned you in.**——强调的是主语"我"；强调人时可用 who
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">It</span> <span class="v">wasn't</span> <span class="c">me</span> <span class="a">who turned you in</span></p>
- **判断方法**：把 It is/was...that/who 整体拆掉，剩下的部分能拼回一个完整句（You were using my credit card / I didn't turn you in）——能拼回去的就是强调句，拼不回去的才是定语从句或名词从句
