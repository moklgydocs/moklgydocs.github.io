<GrammarTip points="简化从句,动词用法">

- **while whistling the theme...**：while + 现在分词，是**副词从句简化**（台：状语从句简化）——= while they were whistling...，从句主语与主句相同故省略（[Ch.19](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter19.md)）
- **helping Rachel (to) mail out resumes**：help + 宾语 + 原形/不定式"帮某人做……"；mail out"寄出"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **The Bridge on the River Kwai**：电影《桂河大桥》，大家口哨吹的正是该片著名的进行曲

</GrammarTip>

🎯 **本场景语法重点**：短语动词 run low on / 不定式作后置定语（悬空介词）/ 动名词被动式 / 插入式疑问句

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E18.mp3" start="7.848" duration="4.848" /> **Chandler Bing:** Well, if <Note>you're gonna work for mechanics</Note>, those are <Note>the ones to work for</Note>.
> 嗯，如果你要为机械师工作，那就是你要为之工作的人。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,不定式,副词从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E18.mp3" start="7.848" duration="4.848" />

**原句**：Well, if you're gonna work for mechanics, those are the ones to work for.

**层次划分**：

- **if you're gonna work for mechanics**：if 条件**副词从句**（台：状语从句），**现在式代未来**；gonna = going to 的口语缩略（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">those</span> <span class="v">are</span> <span class="c">the ones to work for</span></p> those 指上文 mechanics，**系动词**（台：连缀动词）are 接名词补语（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **the ones to work for**：**不定式**（台：不定词）短语 to work for 作后置定语修饰 the ones；for 的宾语正是被修饰的 the ones——介词悬空在句尾（= the mechanics (that) you should work for）（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **双关**：mechanics 明指"机械师"，暗扣上文杂志名 Popular Mechanics——"要投就投这一行里最对口的那家"

**翻译**：嗯，反正都是给机械师打工，那就该给这一家打。

**语法要点**：if 条件从句现在式代未来（Ch.13）；不定式后置定语 + 悬空介词（Ch.4）

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：I'm going for anything here, OK? I cannot be a waitress anymore, I mean it. I'm sick of the lousy tips, I'm sick of being called 'Excuse me...'

**层次划分**：

- **I'm going for anything here**：go for"努力争取"；肯定句里的 anything 表"任何（工作）都行"（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **I cannot be a waitress anymore**：not...anymore"不再……"；**系动词**（台：连缀动词）be + 名词补语 a waitress（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **I mean it**：插入短句"我是认真的"，it 指代上面整句话
- **I'm sick of the lousy tips**：be sick of + 名词"受够了……"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **being called 'Excuse me...'**：of 后接**动名词被动式** being + 过去分词，"被人叫……"；'Excuse me...' 是顾客使唤服务员的口头禅，作 called 的**宾语补足语**（台：补语）（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）

**翻译**：嘿，听着，各位，我现在什么工作都愿意干，好吗？我再也不当服务员了，我是认真的。我受够了那点可怜的小费，受够了被人喊"喂，那个谁……"

**语法要点**：动名词被动式 being done（Ch.5/Ch.6）；not...anymore（Ch.8）；be sick of（Ch.10）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,形容词比较">

**本集句型：否定/疑问 + 比较级 = "再……不过"**（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **Could you want her more?**：你还能更想要她吗 = 你想要她想得无以复加
- **Couldn't be more out.**：退得不能再退 = 打死也不跟
- **I couldn't be inner.**：跟得不能再跟 = 铁定跟注（inner 为顺着 in 生造的"比较级"）
- **not as bad as Ross**：as...as 同级比较的否定式——我没罗斯那么过分

口诀：can't/couldn't be more + 形容词 ≈ 最高级；疑问版 Could you... more? 同效。

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役动词">

**本集宾补句型：make / let / get / want + 宾语 + 补语**（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md) S+V+O+C）：

- **make + O + 原形**：I made you look.（骗你低头看）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">made</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">look</span></p>
- **let + O + 原形**：Let me see! / Let me tell you something.
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Let</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">see</span></p>
- **get + O + 现在分词**：who gets my heart pounding（让我心跳加速）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">who</span> <span class="v">gets</span> <span class="o">my heart</span> <span class="c">pounding</span></p>
- **want/expect + O + 不定式**：do you want me to shuffle those? / don't expect me to be a 'nice guy'
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">want</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">to shuffle those</span></p>
- **注意**：make/let 后补语用原形、不加 to；get 后接分词表"使……处于某状态"；want/expect 后必须带 to
