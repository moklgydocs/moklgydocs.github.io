<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E12.mp3" start="4.488" duration="13.248" /> **Monica Geller:** Aunt Syl, stop yelling! <Note>All I'm saying is</Note> <Note>that if you had told me vegetarian lasagna</Note>, <Note>I would have made vegetarian lasagna</Note>. > 西尔阿姨，别喊了！我想说的是，如果你告诉我素食千层面，我会做素食千层面。（停顿一下，听电话里的人说）嗯，肉只有三层，也许你可以刮一刮。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,虚拟语气,名词从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E12.mp3" start="4.488" duration="13.248" />

**原句**：All I'm saying is that if you had told me vegetarian lasagna, I would have made vegetarian lasagna.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">All (that) I'm saying</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="c">that 从句</span></p> all + **定语从句（台：关系从句）**——关系代词 that 作 saying 的宾语，故省略（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）；that 引导**表语从句（台：名词从句）**作主语补语（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **if you had told me..., I would have made...**：**虚拟语气·与过去事实相反**——if 从句用过去完成式 had told，主句用 would have + 过去分词："当时要是说了，我就会做了"（实则没说、没做）（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **the meat's only every third layer**：meat 为不可数名词，动词用单数 's——**主谓一致（台：主语动词一致性）**（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）；**every third layer**"每隔两层（就有一层）"，every + 序数词 + 单数名词（Ch.2）
- **maybe you could scrape**：could 表委婉建议"你不妨刮一刮"；scrape 后省略宾语 it（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

**翻译**：Syl 阿姨，别嚷了！我要说的只是：你当初要是告诉我要做素的，我就会做素的千层面。其实肉也只隔两层才有一层，要不你刮掉算了？

**语法要点**：all (that)...is that...（Ch.14/Ch.12）；虚拟过去 if had done...would have done（Ch.9）；every + 序数词（Ch.2）

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：What do you want me to do with a dozen lasagnas?

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="o">What</span> <span class="v">do</span> <span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">want</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">to do with a dozen lasagnas</span></p> **want + 宾语 + 不定式**——**不定式（台：不定词）** to do 作**宾语补足语（台：补语）**"要我做……"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,疑问句">

**本集句型一：陈述语序疑问句（升调表疑问）**——不动助动词、不换语序，单靠语调把陈述句变成问句，表"惊讶确认"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **You kiss Uncle Freddie with that mouth?**（你就用那张嘴亲 Freddie 叔叔？）
- **you know that right now, your baby's only this big?**（你知道宝宝现在才这么大吗？）
- **You knew about that?**（那事儿你居然知道？）
- **it's just because it was my table, I have to buy a new one?**（就因为是我的桌子，就得我买新的？）
- **I'm having a boy?**（我要生的是儿子？）
- **口诀**：语序不动、句尾升调；语气 = 惊讶 / 难以置信 / 求确认

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,动词时态">

**本集句型二：be going to（= gonna）表将来**——本集出现率最高的将来表达（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **表既定安排**：my sister'**s giving** us her place（进行式同义）/ it'**s going to be** a... / not all guys **are going to be** a Paolo / **I'm having** a boy
- **表预测**：She'**s gonna hate** me / what'**s it gonna be**? / what are you **gonna do**? / people are **gonna come** in and think
- **叠加其他结构**：you'**re going to have to** stop that（+ have to）/ is not **going to grow up to be** one（+ 不定式）
- **口诀**：gonna 是 going to 的口语缩略，前面必须有 be；正式写作请还原为 going to

</GrammarTip>
