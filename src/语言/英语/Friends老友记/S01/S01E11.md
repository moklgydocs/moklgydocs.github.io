<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">think</span> <span class="o">(that) they have yesterday's daily news</span></p>

- **Do you think (that)...?**：think 后接**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），口语中连接词 that 常省略；从句内部保持陈述语序 they have...，不随主句倒装（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

- **see if it was right**：if = whether"是否"，引导宾语从句作 see 的宾语；从句用过去式 was——星座运势是昨天印的，验证它"说对了没有"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

**原句**：Don't look now, but behind us is a guy who has the potential to break our hearts and plunge us into a pit of depression.

**层次划分**：

- 主干一：<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Don't look</span> <span class="a">now</span></p> 祈使句否定式 = Do not + 原形（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- 主干二：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">behind us</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="s">a guy</span></p> **地点状语前置 + 完全倒装**：正常语序 A guy is behind us；为了与上文衔接把 behind us 提到句首，主谓整组倒装（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
- **who has the potential...**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 a guy，关系代词 who 在从句中作主语，**不可省略**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **to break our hearts and (to) plunge us into...**：两个**不定式**（台：不定词）并列作后置定语修饰 potential"有……的可能"；并列不定式第二个省略 to（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **a pit of depression**：a pit of + 名词"……的深渊"，**介词**（台：介系词）into 表"陷入"（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：现在别看，但咱们身后站着一个有可能让我们心碎、把我们推进抑郁深渊的男人。

**语法要点**：地点状语前置完全倒装（Ch.22）；关系代词作主语不可省（Ch.14）；并列不定式省 to（Ch.4）

**层次划分**：

- **接过去式**：I can't believe **you did that**! / I can't believe **you said woowoo**. / I can't believe **you did this**.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">can't believe</span> <span class="o">(that) you did that</span></p>
- **接进行式**：I cannot believe **we're having this conversation**.（事情正在进行）
- **接单宾语 it/this**：I can't believe **it**. / I can't believe **this**!（不展开细节时）
- **口诀**：believe 后的 that 一律可省；从句时态跟着事实走，不受 can't 影响

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,虚拟语气">

**本集高频句型二：wish + 过去式（虚拟语气）**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）——"要是……就好了"，与现在事实相反：

- **I wish there was something we could do.**（事实：我们无能为力）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">wish</span> <span class="o">there was something we could do</span></p>
- **I wish we at least knew his name.**（事实：不知道）/ **I wish all guys could be like him.**（事实：并非如此）
- **规则**：wish 后的从句，动词一律**倒退一个时态**——表现在的事实用过去式（was/knew/could）；表与过去相反用过去完成式（had done）
- **扩展**：本集 Joey 的 what **woulda** happened = what would have happened，正是"与过去事实相反"的虚拟过去

</GrammarTip>
