- **That is it!**：惯用语"够了！到此为止！"——that 指代之前所有糟心事，is 重读表忍无可忍（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

**原句**：That's a relatively open weave and I can still see your... nipular areas.

**层次划分**：

- 两个独立分句由 **and** 并列（对等连接词，[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">That</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">a relatively open weave</span></p> + <p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">can see</span> <span class="o">your... nipular areas</span> <span class="a">still</span></p>

**原句**：Tell them the story about your patient who thinks things are, like, other things. Like, the phone rings and she takes a shower.

**层次划分**：

- 主干为祈使句：<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Tell</span> <span class="o">them</span> <span class="o">the story about your patient</span></p> tell + 间接宾语 them + 直接宾语 the story（S+V+O+O，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **about your patient**：介词短语作后置定语修饰 story（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **who thinks things are other things**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 patient；关系代词 who 在从句中作主语，**不可省略**；从句里 thinks 又带宾语从句 (that) things are other things——从句套从句（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **the phone rings and she takes a shower**：and 并列两分句举例；一般现在式讲惯常反应（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **like**：口语填充词"好比/就像"，无实义

**翻译**：亲爱的，跟他们讲讲你那个病人的故事——她觉得什么东西都是别的东西。你懂吧？好比电话铃一响，她就去洗澡。

**语法要点**：who 作主语的关系从句不可省（Ch.14）；think + 宾语从句（Ch.12）；祈使句双宾语（Ch.1）

- **in some ways I think (that) he is so right for me**：in some ways"在某些方面"作状语；I think 后接宾语从句；be right for sb"适合某人"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **it's just... I hate that guy**：it's just (that)... "只是……"欲言又止；口头禅 I hate that guy 收尾，连环反转（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

**翻译**：我不知道。我是说，他人很好，有时候真的特别甜，而且在某些方面，我觉得他特别特别适合我，只是……我讨厌那家伙！

**语法要点**：can 表可能性（Ch.9）；I think + 宾语从句（Ch.12）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,答代句">

**本集句型总复习①：助动词答代句**——用 do / will / would / have 等助动词代替整个谓语，避免重复（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **do / don't / does / did**：even though she **does**（= knows）/ But I **don't**.（= don't think so）/ I know you **did**, cookie.（= did what I wanted）/ I really **do**.（= do think so）
- **will / won't**：No you **won't**. / No we **won't**.（= won't go to a hotel）
- **have / haven't**：Then y'**haven't**.（= haven't been in love）
- **口诀**：答代句里**助动词必须与前句的时态/语态对得上**；前句无助动词时，按人称时态补 do/does/did

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集句型总复习②：what / where / why / how 引导的名词从句**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）——从句内部一律**陈述语序**：

- **作补语**：That's **what he does**. / that's **where you're wrong** / that's not **what I was saying** / that's **why you're so great**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what he does</span></p>
- **作宾语**：I just did **what I thought you'd want** / I don't care **how old you are** / about **how I'm always seeing girls...**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">did</span> <span class="o">what I thought you'd want</span></p>
- **易错**：从句内不倒装——说 how old you **are**、what you **were doing**，不说 how old are you / what were you doing

</GrammarTip>
