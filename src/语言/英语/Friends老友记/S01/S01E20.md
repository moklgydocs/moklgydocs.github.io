<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">would rather be</span> <span class="c">Mr.Peanut</span></p>

- **I can't believe (that) you would say that**：believe 后接**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），连接词 that 口语中常省略；从句里 would 表"竟然会"的惊讶语气（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **would much rather be A than B**："宁愿做 A 也不愿做 B"——would rather + 原形动词，than 后接平行成分；much 加强语气（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

</GrammarTip>

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S01/S01E20.mp3" start="1096.608" duration="10.464" />

**原句**：That whole thing about her coming all the way down here, just to see if I was okay? How needy is that?

**层次划分**：

- **she seems very nice and everything**：seem 系动词 + 形容词；and everything"……什么的"口语尾缀（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **that whole thing about her coming all the way down here**：about 后接**动名词复合结构** her coming——her 是 coming 的逻辑主语（宾格所有格均可）"她大老远跑下来这事"；all the way"一路、大老远"（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **just to see if I was okay**：**不定式目的状语**"就为了看看"；see 后接 **if 宾语从句**（= whether）"我是否还好"；if 从句用过去式 were... 实为 was 配合当时语境（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **how needy is that?**：**how + 形容词**感叹式问句"那得有多饥渴啊？"——Chandler 用自己的名言回敬自己（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)/[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）

**翻译**：她是挺好的，可大老远专程跑下来，就为了确认我没事？这……这也太饥渴了吧？

**语法要点**：动名词复合结构 her coming（Ch.5）；不定式目的 + if 从句（Ch.4/12）；how + adj 反问（Ch.8）

**本集时态句型：完成式 / 完成进行式 + 时段或 since 从句**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **现在完成式 + in/for + 时段**：I haven't heard from her in seven months. / We haven't been on a second date.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">haven't heard</span> <span class="a">from her in seven months</span></p>
- **现在完成进行式**：That's what you've been working on for the past two hours. / I've been honing! / I've been calling you.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">'ve been working on</span> <span class="o">it</span></p>
- **ever since + 过去式从句，主句完成式**：Ever since I ran out on Barry..., I have wondered... / Ever since we announced the engagement, he's been acting really weird.
- **口诀**：since 从句用过去式、主句用完成式；for/in + 时段配完成式；强调"一直不停"就用完成进行式

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集 think/know/hope + 宾语从句与 what 从句**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **that 省略型**：I can't believe you would actually say that. / I know it's stupid! / I think Barry is seeing someone. / I'm pretty sure I'm still gonna marry him.
- **if/whether 型**：I don't know if you tried to call me. / I have wondered whether I made the right choice. / just to see if I was okay
- **what 型**：That's what you've been working on. / that's what I was afraid of / he's just gonna do to you what he did to Rachel
- **注意**：think 的否定要**前移**（Y'don't think that makes me seem...），hope 不前移（I hope Barry doesn't kill you）

</GrammarTip>
