**本集"太……"与"如此……"两大程度结构**（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）：

- **too + 形容词 + to do（太……而不能）**：I was too immature to get married / you're too young to really know what you want / he's a little young to get married（变体：a little young to do）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">'re</span> <span class="c">too young</span> <span class="a">to really know what you want</span></p>
- **so + 形容词/副词 + (that) 结果从句（如此……以至于）**：your laundry just smelled so good, (that) I thought I'd curl up in it / he was so good, and I really, really want this part 类 / so much better than...
- **对比记忆**：too...to 表"**超过限度**（否定结果）"；so...(that) 表"**程度引发结果**"
