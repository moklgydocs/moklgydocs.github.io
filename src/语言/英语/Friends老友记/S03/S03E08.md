🎬 *Monica and Rachel's, Phoebe, Chandler, and Ross are there, Rachel is serving brownies.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡和瑞秋家，菲比，钱德勒和罗斯在那里，瑞秋在供应巧克力饼。*

<GrammarTip points="场景说明,动词时态">

- **Rachel is serving brownies**：场景说明用**现在进行式**描画"正在分发布朗尼"；serve"招待、端上"可接双宾语（serve sb sth）（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）

</GrammarTip>

🎯 **本场景语法重点**：Neither 倒装 / every time 时间从句 / 表语从句 That's why / 不定式目的状语

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="0.0" duration="5.52" /> **Rachel Green:** <Note>Here you go</Note> Pheebs. <Note>Who else wants one of my special homemade brownies</Note>?
> 给你，菲比。还有谁想要我特制的布朗尼？
<GrammarTip points="惯用语,名词词组">

- **Here you go**：递东西时的固定口语"给你"——here 开头的惯用句，主语是代词 you，不倒装（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
- **Pheebs**：称呼语独立在句外，位置灵活——Here you go, Pheebs / Pheebs, here you go 均可（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **Who else wants...?**：**else** 紧跟疑问词 who 之后"还有谁"；who 作主语，动词用**单数** wants（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）
- **one of my special homemade brownies**：one of + 复数名词"……之一"；**homemade**"自制的"是复合形容词（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="5.52" duration="1.944" /> **Chandler Bing:** <Note>I will have one</Note>. (Ross and him both take one.)
> 我要一个。（罗斯和他都拿了一个。）
<GrammarTip points="情态动词,代词">

- **I will have one**：**will + 原形**此处表"愿意、要"（不是将来）——"我来一个"；**one** 作代词代替 brownie（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **(Ross and him) both take one**：舞台指示里 **both** 作"Ross and him"的**同位语**"两人都"（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）

</GrammarTip>

*(Phoebe takes a bite and spits it out and screams.)*
> *（菲比咬了一口，吐了出来，尖叫起来。）*
<GrammarTip points="短语动词,舞台指示">

- **takes a bite (and) spits it out**：舞台指示三连动作用**现在式**；**spit out**"吐出来"——代词宾语 it 必须放在短语中间：spits **it** out（√），spits out it（×）（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="7.464" duration="2.76" /> **Chandler Bing:** Okay, <Note>I’m not gonna have one</Note>.
> 好吧，我不会要的。
<GrammarTip points="动词时态">

- **I'm not gonna have one**：**be going to 的否定**——看到 Phoebe 吐出来，立马改口"我不要了"；gonna = going to（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **对照**：上句刚说 I will have one——will 与 gonna 在本集反复互换，口语里表"要/不要"几乎无差别（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="10.224" duration="1.92" /> **Ross Geller:** <Note>Neither will I</Note>. (they both put back the brownies.)
> 我也不会。（他们都把布朗尼放回去了。）
<GrammarTip points="倒装句,副词">

<p class="svoc"><span class="a">Neither</span> <span class="v">will</span> <span class="s">I</span></p>

- **Neither will I**：**否定副词 Neither 置于句首，助动词倒装**——"我也不（要）"；相当于 I won't have one either。口诀：Neither/Nor + 助动词 + 主语（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **对照**：肯定附和用 **So will I**"我也要"——So + 助动词 + 主语；否定附和才用 Neither/Nor（Ch.22）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="12.144" duration="1.944" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** No, no, <Note>it’s just my tooth</Note>.
> 不，不，只是我的牙齿。
<GrammarTip points="副词">

- **it's just my tooth**：**just** 作限定副词"只是、不过是"——"不是布朗尼难吃，是我牙疼"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- 本句为下一句 Chandler 的反复改口埋伏笔：先 I'm not gonna have one，听说只是牙疼，又 All right I'll have one——情态动词随信息秒变（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="14.088" duration="2.232" /> **Chandler Bing:** All right <Note>I’ll have one</Note>. (he and Ross take another brownie,)
> 好吧，我要一个。（他和罗斯又吃了一块布朗尼）
<GrammarTip points="限定词">

- **(take) another brownie**：**another + 单数名词**"再来一块"——another = an + other，后只能接单数（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **All right**：话头词"那好吧"，表让步后的重新决定（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="16.32" duration="4.992" /> **Ross Geller:** So <Note>what’s a matter</Note>, <Note>you need a dentist</Note>? <Note>I’ve got a good one</Note>.
> 怎么了，你需要一个牙医？我有一个好的。
<GrammarTip points="原文勘误,疑问句">

- ⚠️ 原文 `what's a matter` 应为 **What's the matter**（漏定冠词 the）——"怎么了？"（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **you need a dentist?**：**陈述语序 + 升调**构成疑问——"你需要看牙医？"；完整疑问式是 Do you need a dentist?（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）
- **I've got a good one**：= I have got...——have got 表"有"；**one** 代 dentist（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="21.312" duration="4.08" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** <Note>No thanks</Note>, I have a good one too. I just, I, I <Note>can’t see him</Note>.
> 不用了，谢谢，我也有一个好的。我只是，我，我看不见他。
<GrammarTip points="惯用语,双关">

- **No thanks**：婉拒固定用语 = No, thank you（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **I can't see him**：双关笑点——see 既有"看（病）"也有"看见"：Phoebe 表面说"我不能找他看牙"， Chandler 故意听成"看不见他"（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **I have a good one too**：**too**"也"置于句尾；a good one = a good dentist（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="25.392" duration="3.6" /> **Chandler Bing:** See <Note>that is the problem with invisible dentists</Note>.
> 看，这就是隐形牙医的问题。
<GrammarTip points="名词词组,介词">

- **See that is the problem...**：句首 **See** 作插入语"你瞧、你看"，引起注意，不作句子成分（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **the problem with + 名词**：**介词**（台：介系词）with 表"（某事物）固有的毛病"——"隐形牙医的问题就在于此"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **invisible dentists**：invisible"看不见的"——Chandler 顺着"看不见他"开玩笑（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="28.992" duration="3.48" /> **Ross Geller:** Why? <Note>Why can’t you go to him</Note>?
> 为什么？你为什么不能去找他？
<GrammarTip points="疑问句,短语动词">

- **Why can't you go to him?**：**否定疑问句**表不解——"你为什么不能去找他（看牙）呢"；go to sb 此处 = "找某人（看病）"（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **Why?**：单问一句 Why? 先行——口语里常先抛疑问词再补完整问句（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="32.472" duration="3.288" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** Because, <Note>every time I go to the dentist, somebody dies</Note>.
> 因为，每次我去看牙医，都会有人死。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,副词从句,主谓一致">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="32.472" duration="3.288" />

**原句**：Every time I go to the dentist, somebody dies.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">Every time I go to the dentist</span> <span class="s">somebody</span> <span class="v">dies</span></p>
- **every time I go to the dentist**：**every time (that)** 作连词引导**时间副词从句**"每次……都"，从句用**现在式**表习惯性动作（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **somebody dies**：**somebody** 作主语视为**单数**，故动词加 -s：dies（die-dies-died 注意拼写）（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **go to the dentist**："去看牙医"——go to + 职业名词表"找某人办事"：go to the doctor / go to the barber（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：因为啊，每次我去看牙医，就会死一个人。

**语法要点**：every time 引导时间从句（Ch.13）；somebody 单数主谓一致（Ch.11）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="35.76" duration="6.384" /> **Chandler Bing:** That is so weird, because <Note>every time I go to the dentist</Note>, <Note>I look down the hygienist’s blouse</Note>.
> 那太奇怪了，因为每次我去看牙医，我都会看不起卫生员的衬衫。
<GrammarTip points="副词从句,短语动词">

- **That is so weird**：**so + 形容词**表程度"太奇怪了"——Chandler 嘴上附和，其实要抢话头（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **every time I go to the dentist**：与上句完全相同的 every time 从句——Chandler 故意用同句型说自己的"怪事"，形成平行对照的笑点（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **look down the hygienist's blouse**：**look down** 短语动词"往……下看"——down the blouse"往衣领里看"；hygienist's 名词所有格（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

</GrammarTip>

- **Phoebe, what?**：先呼唤名字再抛疑问词——称呼语 + 单词问句，是听说意外消息时的标准反应（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **what? ... what?!**：疑问词独立成句——第二遍加 **?!** 语气从"没听清"升级为"震惊"；口语里语调承担疑问与感叹的双重功能（[Ch.22](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter22.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="46.008" duration="6.336" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah, yeah, <Note>first there was my aunt Mary</Note>, and then there was umm, John, my mailman, and then my, my cowboy friend ‘Albino Bob’.

<GrammarTip points="存在句,同位语">

- **John, my mailman / my cowboy friend ‘Albino Bob’**：**同位语**——名词后接名词词组补充说明身份（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

<GrammarTip points="限定词,副词">

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="55.08" duration="8.376" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** Yes, <Note>while I was in the chair</Note>! <Note>That’s why I take such good care of my teeth now</Note>, y'know, <Note>it’s not about oral hygiene</Note>, <Note>I floss to save lives</Note>!
> 是的，当我坐在椅子上的时候！这就是为什么我现在要好好保养我的牙齿，你知道，这不是关于口腔卫生，我用牙线来拯救生命！
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,不定词">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="55.08" duration="8.376" />

**原句**：While I was in the chair! That's why I take such good care of my teeth now. It's not about oral hygiene, I floss to save lives!

**层次划分**：

- **while I was in the chair**：**while**"正当……时"引导**时间副词从句**，**过去进行式** was——"（他们就死）在我躺上诊疗椅的那会儿"；the chair 特指牙医椅（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">That</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">why I take such good care of my teeth now</span></p> **That's why + 从句**：why 引导**表语从句**（台：名词从句）——"这就是我为什么……"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **take such good care of my teeth**：**take (good) care of**"好好照料"；**such + 形容词 + 名词**（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **it's not about oral hygiene**：**It's not about...**"这事儿不在于……"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **I floss to save lives**：**不定式**（台：不定词）**to save lives 作目的状语**——"我用牙线是为了救命"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **y'know**：= you know，口语插入语"你懂吧"，不占句子成分（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

**翻译**：对啊，就在我躺上椅子的时候！所以我现在才把牙齿保养得这么好。你懂吗，这跟口腔卫生无关——我用牙线，是在拯救生命！

**语法要点**：That's why 表语从句（Ch.12）；while + 过去进行（Ch.13）；不定式表目的（Ch.4）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="63.456" duration="10.968" /> **Ross Geller:** Pheebs, come on, <Note>you didn’t kill anybody</Note>, these people <Note>just happened to die when you went to the dentist</Note>. It’s, it’s, it’s just ah, a coincidence.

<GrammarTip points="动词时态,副词从句">

- **you didn't kill anybody**：**过去式否定**；否定句里用 **anybody**（不用 somebody）（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **come on**：短语动词此处表"拜托、得了吧"（劝慰语气），不是"过来"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **just happened to die**：**happen to + 原形**"碰巧……"——"只是恰好那会儿死了"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **when you went to the dentist**：when 引导**时间副词从句**，**过去式** went——与主句 happened 时态呼应（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **a coincidence**："巧合"，可数名词（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **It's, it's, it's just ah...**：主语反复重启三次 + 填充词 ah，Ross 搜肠刮肚找委婉说法（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E08.mp3" start="74.424" duration="3.624" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** Well <Note>tell that to them</Note>. Oh! You can’t, <Note>their dead</Note>.

- **You can't**：= You can't (tell them)——谓语承前省略（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）

**本集感官动词句型：see / watch / hear + 宾语 + 原形或现在分词作补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）：

- **see + O + 现在分词**（进行中）：Joey **saw Janice kissing** her ex-husband / he **saw you two kissing**

- **watch + O + 原形**（全过程）：I **watched Monica bang** his head against that thing
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">watched</span> <span class="o">Monica</span> <span class="c">bang his head against that thing</span></p>
- **口诀**：**现在分词 = 正在发生的片段；原形 = 目睹全程**；hear/notice/feel 同此句型（Did you hear that?）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,副词从句">

**本集时间/条件副词从句的时态规则：现在式代未来**（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）：

- **every time**：every time I go to the dentist, somebody dies.（习惯）
- **if**：if you're my next victim, don't come back... / if you don't tell about our little bonking incident
- **until ('til)**：you could wait 'til I go to the dentist
- **the second**：The second that Ross walks in that door, I want you...
- **before**：before I can say 'good-bye'
- **对照（过去叙事）**：Whenever I **would** see him, I was always think...——过去惯常改用 would
- **口诀**：主句将来/祈使/情态，从句**现在式**；主句过去惯常，从句可换 would

</GrammarTip>
