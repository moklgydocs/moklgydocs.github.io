# The One with Frank Jr.
> *小弗兰克的那一集。*

The One With Frank Jr.
> 和小弗兰克在一起的那个。

<GrammarTip points="名词词组,后缀">

- **The One with Frank Jr.**：《老友记》命名传统——**The One + 介词（台：介系词）短语**（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **Frank Jr.**：后缀 **Jr.**（= Junior）直接跟在人名后表"小弗兰克"（父子同名），不加所有格（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **Jr.** 读作 Junior，中文习惯译"小……"；对应的父辈后缀是 Sr.（Senior）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,基本句型">

**本集头号句型：get / make / drive / call + 宾语 + 补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **get + O + 过去分词**：how do you guys get anything done / I almost got my arm broken / has a paint lid stuck to his pants
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you guys</span> <span class="v">get</span> <span class="o">anything</span> <span class="c">done</span></p>
- **get + O + 不定式**：get her eyes to pop out of her head / get one of the other girls to do it
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ll get</span> <span class="o">one of the other girls</span> <span class="c">to do it</span></p>
- **drive / make + O + 形容词**：drive each other crazy / makes our doors look smaller / make you mad at me
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">we</span> <span class="v">used to drive</span> <span class="o">each other</span> <span class="c">crazy</span></p>
- **call + O + 名词**：we don't call it that / we'll call her an alternate
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">we</span> <span class="v">'ll call</span> <span class="o">her</span> <span class="c">an alternate</span></p>
- **口诀**：get 的补语用 **to do** 或**过去分词**；make/drive/let 的补语用**原形或形容词**；call 的补语用**名词**——补语永远回答"宾语怎么样/被叫什么"

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集疑问词从句四连：what / where / why / if**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **what 从句**：I know **what I wanted to ask you** / that is **what the game is** / that's **what you thought I did**（what = the thing that）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what you thought I did</span></p>
- **where 从句**：the couch is usually **where we keep the varnish** / that's **where the waistband goes over your head**（where = the place where）
- **why 从句**：that's **why you won't get Isabella Rossellini**（why = the reason why）
- **if/whether 从句**：I was wondering **if I could buy you a cup of coffee** / ask Mr. Whiffler **if he can wait**（if = 是否）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">was wondering</span> <span class="o">if I could buy you a cup of coffee</span></p>
- **口诀**：从句内一律**陈述语序**不倒装；what 自带"东西"、where 自带"地方"、why 自带"原因"、if 自带"是否"

</GrammarTip>
