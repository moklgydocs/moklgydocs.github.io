🎯 **本场景语法重点**：if 宾语从句 / would like 委婉语气 / before·since·now that 状语从句 / what 名词从句作补语

> （在他的脑子里）说瑞秋，我想知道你是否愿意找个时间和我一起去看电影。作为我的爱人！恩，去外面。也许你只是想找个时间和我一起吃点东西？作为我的爱人。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,语气">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E19.mp3" start="2.616" duration="11.616" />

**原句**：I was wondering if you'd like to go to a movie with me sometime.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">was wondering</span> <span class="o">if you'd like to go to a movie with me sometime</span></p> **was wondering** 过去进行式表试探、客气，比 I wonder 更委婉（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **if you'd like to go to a movie with me sometime**：**宾语从句**（台：名词从句）作 wondering 的宾语，if = whether"是否"，从句内陈述语序不倒装；**would like to + 原形**"想要……"，比 want 委婉（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **As my lover!**：**介词**（台：介系词）短语独立成句，= (Come) as my lover"以我恋人的身份"，as + 身份名词（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **get something to eat**：**不定式**（台：不定词）to eat 作后置定语修饰 something——"吃点东西"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- ⚠️ 原文 `Nnnsch, to out there` 应为 **too out there**"太露骨了"，抄写漏字母

**翻译**：瑞秋啊，我在想你要不要哪天跟我去看场电影。以我恋人的身份！呃，太露骨了。要不你哪天跟我去吃点什么？以我恋人的身份。

**语法要点**：wonder + if 从句（Ch.12）；过去进行式表委婉（Ch.3/9）；something + 不定式后置定语（Ch.4）

</GrammarTip>

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E19.mp3" start="24.888" duration="12.504" />

**原句**：I've kinda of had this crush on you. But since you were with Ross, I didn't do anything about it. But now that you're not, I'd really like to ask you out sometime. So that's what I'm doing now.

**层次划分**：

- 主干1：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ve had</span> <span class="o">this crush on you</span></p> 现在完成式"（从过去到现在）一直暗恋你"；**have a crush on sb** 固定短语"迷恋某人"；kinda = kind of"有点儿"，原文 `kinda of` 是口语叠用（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **since you were with Ross**：since 此处表"因为"，引导**原因状语从句**（台：副词从句），非"自从"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **now that you're not**：now that + 从句"既然……"；you're not = you're not (with Ross)，避免重复而省略介词短语（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **ask you out**：短语动词 ask sb out"约某人出去"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- 主干2：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what I'm doing</span></p> **what I'm doing**：what 引导**表语从句**（台：名词从句）作主语补语，what = the thing that（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

**翻译**：我一直有点暗恋你。（瑞秋震惊）但因为当时你跟罗斯在一起，我什么都没做。可既然你们现在不在一起了，我真的很想哪天约你出去。所以——这就是我现在正在做的事。

**语法要点**：since/now that 表原因（Ch.13）；have a crush on（Ch.10 短语）；what 表语从句（Ch.12）

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：That's why, within a few years, voice recognition is gonna be pretty much standard on any computer you buy. It's not gonna be able to do any of those things, but it'll understand what you're saying.

**层次划分**：

- 主干1：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">why voice recognition is gonna be standard</span></p> **that's why + 从句**：why 引导**表语从句**（台：名词从句），"这就是……的原因"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- 从句内部：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">voice recognition</span> <span class="v">is gonna be</span> <span class="c">(pretty much) standard</span> <span class="a">on any computer you buy</span></p> **voice recognition** 复合名词"语音识别"（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）；**is gonna be** = is going to be，be going to 表预言式未来，gonna 为口语缩略（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）；**pretty much** 副词"几乎"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **any computer (that) you buy**：定语从句省略关系代词 that（作 buy 的宾语）（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **be able to do**：be going to 后接 be able to，两层助动叠用"将能够做"；**any of those things**：any of + 复数名词（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **what you're saying**：what 引导**宾语从句**作 understand 的宾语，从句内陈述语序不倒装（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

**语法要点**：that's why 表语从句（Ch.12）；be going to 未来（Ch.3）；关系代词作宾语可省（Ch.14）；what 宾语从句（Ch.12）

**本集"动词 + 宾语 + 补语"句型**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）——补语用什么形式，由动词决定：

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集省略 that 的宾语从句（台：名词从句）**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）——think / know / guess / mean / say 后面，that 口语里基本全省：

- **I guess (that)** he musta gotten the part.（must have + p.p. 推测）
- **she realises (that)** life without me sucks.（时态呼应）
- **I don't think (that)** I told you this.（**否定前移** = I think I didn't）
- **maybe that means (that)** you're not over Ross yet.
- **I think (that)** somebody has a crush on somebody.
- **I say (that)** we **get** back at him right on this couch.（表建议，从句用原形虚拟）
- **口诀**：连词 that 不作成分、无词义，宾语从句中可省；但**从句内的陈述语序不能乱**
