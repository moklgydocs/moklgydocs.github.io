<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E17.mp3" start="49.488" duration="5.976" /> **Joey Tribbiani:** Well, <Note>I guess he says that because they were on a break when it happened</Note>, <Note>that she should of forgiven him by now</Note>.
> 嗯，我猜他是这么说的，因为事情发生时他们正在休息，所以她现在应该原谅他了。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,副词从句,语气,原文勘误">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E17.mp3" start="49.488" duration="5.976" />

**原句**：I guess he says that because they were on a break when it happened, that she should have forgiven him by now.

**层次划分**：

- **I guess (that) he says that...that...**：guess + 宾语从句；says 后面其实带两个 that 从句（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **because they were on a break when it happened**：**because 原因从句**内嵌 **when 时间从句**"因为那件事发生时他们正处于'分手冷静期'"；**be on a break**"（恋爱）暂停期"——本集核心梗（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **she should have forgiven him by now**：**should have + 过去分词**"（他认为）她到现在'早该'原谅他了"；**by now**"到如今"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- ⚠️ 原文勘误：**should of** forgiven 应为 should **have** forgiven（of/have 同音误写）

**翻译**：我猜他的说法是：那事儿发生的时候咱俩正在"冷静期"，所以到现在她早该原谅他了。

**语法要点**：should have done 表"早该"（Ch.9）；be on a break（Ch.10）；because 套 when

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E17.mp3" start="55.464" duration="15.936" />

**原句**：Although I think I understand what he means. This is like 60 Minutes: at first you're really mad at that pharmaceutical company for making the drug, and then you just feel bad for the people because they needed to make their hair grow.

**层次划分**：

- **although I think I understand what he means**：**although 引导让步副词从句**"虽然我（嘴上骂他不讲理，但）";**what he means** 是 what 引导的宾语从句（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **this is like 60 Minutes**：be like"这就像《60 分钟》（调查报道节目）里的桥段"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **at first...and then...**：**at first / then** 叙事序列"一开始……然后……"（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **you're really mad at that pharmaceutical company for making the drug**：**be mad at sb for doing sth**"因某事生某人的气"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)）
- **you just feel bad for the people**：**feel bad for sb**"替……难过"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **they needed to make their hair grow**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">they</span> <span class="v">needed</span> <span class="o">to make their hair grow</span></p> need + 不定式；不定式内 **make + 宾语 + 原形补语** grow"让头发长出来"——原形作**宾语补足语**（台：补语）（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）

**翻译**：哇！他也太不讲理了！……不过，我想我懂他的逻辑。天哪，这简直像《60 分钟》——你知道吧，一开始你气那个制药公司造了这种药，可看到后来你又同情那些人，因为他们只是想让自己的头发长出来啊。

**语法要点**：although 让步从句（Ch.13）；be mad at sb for doing（Ch.10）；make + O + 原形（Ch.1）

**本集万能动词 get 的六种用法**：

- **get = 获得/拿到**：get a cup of coffee / get a tow truck / I got you the best one I could

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">got</span> <span class="a">home safely</span></p>
- **get = 变得（+ 过去分词/形容词）**：get divorced / get married / it's getting... / get this open（使役"弄开"）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">my parents</span> <span class="v">got</span> <span class="c">divorced</span></p>

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">mine</span> <span class="v">get</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">out of tickets</span></p>
- **get = 搞懂/深入**：get into it（深入聊）/ I don't get it（S03E03 联动）
- **口诀**：get 后面跟什么词性就是什么意思——跟名词是"拿"，跟方位是"去"，跟形容词/分词是"变"，跟 out of 是"摆脱"

- **Why don't you + 原形（强烈建议/反讽）**：why don't you put that on your answering machine!

- **might as well（退而求其次）**：I might as well offer to stay.
- **口诀**：从 how about 到 might as well，语气一个比一个"怂"——建议越弱，越像自言自语
