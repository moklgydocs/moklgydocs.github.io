<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E06.mp3" start="18.744" duration="7.176" /> **Janice:** I’m sorry <Note>I find it hard to believe</Note> <Note>that a group of people who spends as much time together as you guys do has never bumped uglies</Note>.
> 很抱歉，我发现很难相信像你们一样花很多时间在一起的一群人从来没有碰到过丑八怪。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,基本句型,关系从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E06.mp3" start="18.744" duration="7.176" />

**原句**：I'm sorry I find it hard to believe that a group of people who spends as much time together as you guys do has never bumped uglies.

**层次划分**：

- **I find it hard to believe that...**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">find</span> <span class="o">it</span> <span class="c">hard</span></p> **find + it + 形容词 + 不定式**——it 是**形式宾语**，hard 是**宾语补足语**（台：补语），真正宾语是后面的**不定式**（台：不定词）to believe that...（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **a group of people who spends as much time together as you guys do**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 a group of people——who 的先行词理解为集合名词 a group，故从句动词用单数 **spends**（口语里也常听到 spend）；从句内 **as much time together as you guys do** 是 as...as 同级比较从句，do 代替 spend（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)/[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）
- **has never bumped uglies**：主句谓语用**现在完成式** has never bumped"（至今）从未"；bump uglies 是"上床"的粗俗俚语（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）

**翻译**：抱歉啊，我实在难以相信：像你们这样成天腻在一起的一群人，居然从来没有谁跟谁上过床。

**语法要点**：find it + 补语 + 不定式（Ch.1）；定语从句套 as...as 比较从句（Ch.14/Ch.7）；完成式 + never（Ch.3）

</GrammarTip>
