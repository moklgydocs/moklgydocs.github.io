**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E13.mp3" start="42.048" duration="10.488" />

**原句**：That's because I was just grabbing some things out of the dryer, and it's static cling. Or maybe it's just that God knew I'd be running into you and saw an opportunity.

**层次划分**：

- **That's because I was just grabbing some things out of the dryer**：**That's because + 从句**"那是因为……"；**过去进行式** was grabbing"（我）刚才正从烘干机里掏衣服"；**grab...out of...**"从……里抓出"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **it's static cling**：**static cling**"静电吸附"——复合名词作表语（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **it's (just) that God knew (that) I'd be running into you and (He) saw an opportunity**：**It's (just) that + 从句**"只不过是……"——**主语从句/表语从句**结构；knew 后接**宾语从句**（台：名词从句）省略 that；从句内 **I'd be running into you** 是**过去将来进行式**"（上帝早）知道我会撞见你"；**run into sb**"偶遇"；**saw an opportunity**"看到了机会"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：那是因为我刚从烘干机里掏了几件衣服，那是静电吸附。要么呢，就是上帝早知道我今儿会撞见你，就顺手安排了一个机会。

**语法要点**：That's because / It's (just) that 从句句型（Ch.12/Ch.13）；过去将来进行 would be doing（Ch.3）；run into（Ch.10）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,动名词">

**本集动名词四大句型**（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)）：

- **spend + 时间 + doing**：she spent the last six months **getting over** him / I spent years **learning not to squish** them
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">spent</span> <span class="o">the last six months</span> <span class="a">getting over him</span></p>
- **end up + doing**：he ends up **marrying** Amy（结局出人意料）
- **keep + doing**：**keep squishing** / **keep reading** / Why does this **keep happening** to me?（持续不停）
- **go on doing 变体**：by **going on** a date / celebrated that **by doing**——介词后一律用动名词
- **口诀**：介词后、固定动词后（spend/keep/end up/finish/worth），动词一律加 -ing

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,关系从句">

**本集定语从句（台：关系从句）省略与否的判定**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **作主语——不可省**：a way **that's proved** very popular / friends **who sleep together** / friends **who lived together** / the guy **who stood in the way**（E08 呼应）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">the guy</span> <span class="v">who stood in the way of that</span></p>
- **作宾语——可省**：the first book (that) **he's ever loved** / everyone (that) I know / something (that) **I'm putting out there** / the blind date (that) I told you **about**（介词尾置时 that 常省）
<p class="svoc"><span class="o">the book (that) he's ever loved</span></p>
- **嵌套双层**：the first book (that) he's ever loved **that didn't star** Jack Nicholson——前省后不省
- **口诀**：从句缺主语→留 that/who；从句缺宾语→可省略

</GrammarTip>
