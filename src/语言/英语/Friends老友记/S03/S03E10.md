**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E10.mp3" start="109.056" duration="10.32" />

**原句**：I am against innocent trees being cut down in their prime, and their corpses (being) grotesquely dressed in like tinsel and twinkly lights. How do you sleep at night?

**层次划分**：

- **I am against innocent trees being cut down in their prime**：be against + **动名词复合结构**——innocent trees 是逻辑主语，**being cut down** 是动名词的**被动式**"反对无辜的树在盛年被砍倒"；in their prime"正当盛年"（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **their corpses (being) grotesquely dressed in like tinsel and twinkly lights**：与前面并列的动名词结构，being 省略——"（反对）它们的尸体被花里胡哨地挂满金属丝和彩灯"；**dressed in**"被穿上/挂满"；grotesquely"怪诞地"（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **how do you sleep at night?**："你晚上怎么睡得着（良心不痛吗）？"——道德质问惯用语；at night"在夜里"（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：不不，我反对的是：无辜的树在最好的年纪被砍掉，它们的尸体还要被花里胡哨地缠满金属丝和彩灯。喂——你晚上睡得着吗？

**语法要点**：against + 动名词被动（Ch.5）；in one's prime（Ch.2）；how do you sleep at night（Ch.9）
