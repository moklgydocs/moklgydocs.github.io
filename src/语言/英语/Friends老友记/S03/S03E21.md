<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,将来时间">

**本集将来时间表达法大集合**（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）：

- **be gonna + 原形**（口语最常用，表已定安排）：I**'m gonna be** on TV!! / It'**s gonna film** it / You'**re never gonna get** it!
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m gonna be</span> <span class="a">on TV</span></p>
- **现在进行式表将来**（近期已约好的事）：I**'m quitting**!! / I'**m meeting** some of the cast / I'**m checking out** the restaurant
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'m checking out</span> <span class="o">the restaurant</span></p>
- **will + 原形**（单纯预测/随即决定）：I **will go** to the hospital tomorrow / it'**ll** still **be** broken then
- **过去将来 was/were gonna**（"当时以为/打算"）：I **was gonna tell** you / I thought you **were gonna take** her back
- **将来进行式 be gonna be + V-ing**：I'**m gonna be doing** it by myself now（强调从此持续）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,补语">

**本集"动词 + 宾语 + 补语"（S+V+O+C）句型盘点**（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **使役 make/let + 宾语 + 原形**（不加 to）：you'd **make** me **go** / not gonna **let** that **happen** / **let** me just **see** something
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">'d make</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">go</span></p>
- **want/would like + 宾语 + 不定式**：**want** me **to kick** his ass / would **like** you **to be** the head chef / don't **want** you **to see** me naked
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">would like</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">to be the head chef</span></p>
- **want + 宾语 + 现在分词**（持续意味）：don't **want** you **thinking** of me like that
- **help + 宾语 + 原形/to do**（to 可省）：**help** me **get dressed** / **help** me **with** this
- **感官 see + 宾语 + 形容词**：**seen** you **naked** a million times
- **make + 宾语 + 形容词**：**make** it **even** / **make** you more **sophisticated**
- **口诀**：make/let 后裸不定式；want/expect 后带 to；被动时 to 才回来
