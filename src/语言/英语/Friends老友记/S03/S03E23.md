<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E23.mp3" start="0.0" duration="6.24" /> **Joey Tribbiani:** Hey, <Note>wouldn't be cool if our duck and chick had a little baby</Note>? We could <Note>call it Chuck</Note>.
> 嘿，如果我们的小鸭和小鸡生了一个小宝宝会不会很酷？我们可以叫它查克。
<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,语气,原文勘误">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E23.mp3" start="0.0" duration="6.24" />

**原句**：Wouldn't it be cool if our duck and chick had a little baby? We could call it Chuck.

**层次划分**：

- ⚠️ 原文 `wouldn’t be cool` 漏了形式主语 **it**，应为 **Wouldn't it be cool if...**
- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">if our duck and chick had a little baby</span> <span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">wouldn't be</span> <span class="c">cool</span></p> **虚拟语气第二条件句**：if 从句用过去式 had，主句用 would be——鸭和鸡根本不会生孩子，纯属假设（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **it 是形式主语**，真正的主语是 if 从句描述的那件事（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **could call it Chuck**：could + 原形表"（如果成真）就可以"；**call + 宾语 + 名词**："管它叫 Chuck"，名词作宾语补足语（台：补语）（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- Chuck = Chick（小鸡）+ Duck（鸭子）的合成名

**翻译**：哎，咱家鸭子和小鸡要是能生个宝宝，岂不酷毙了？咱们就管它叫 Chuck。

**语法要点**：Wouldn't it be cool if + 过去式（Ch.9）；call + O + 名词补语（Ch.1）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,关系从句,语气">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E23.mp3" start="29.04" duration="9.792" />

**原句**：It's kinda in a place that's not... It's not visually accessible to me, and I was hoping maybe you guys could help me out.

**层次划分**：

- **a place that's not (visually accessible)**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 place，关系代词 that 作主语不可省；话说到一半改口，从句补语悬空没说完（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **be accessible to sb**："对某人来说看得见/够得着"，介词用 to（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）；visually 副词修饰形容词（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">was hoping</span> <span class="o">(that) maybe you guys could help me out</span></p> **过去进行式 was hoping 表委婉**——比 I hope 更试探、更不好意思，与 I was wondering 同法（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **help sb out**："帮某人一把"，短语动词（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- hoping 后接省略 that 的**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），从句内 maybe 直接放在主语前（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

**翻译**：呃，我也说不清，它长在……一个我自己看不太见的地方，所以我想着你们俩能不能帮我瞧瞧。（开始脱裤子）

**语法要点**：was hoping 委婉（Ch.3/9）；be accessible to（Ch.10）；定语从句半途改口（Ch.14）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,语气">

**本集虚拟假设句型：if + 过去式 → would/could + 原形**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）：

- **Wouldn't it be cool if** our duck and chick **had** a little baby?
<p class="svoc"><span class="a">if...had a baby</span> <span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">wouldn't be</span> <span class="c">cool</span></p>
- **If** Joey and I **played** with matches, we **could get** you up to an even hundred.
- **If I wanted** to see a fireman, **I would date** one.
- You'**d say** no **if he asked** you.
- That **could've been** really awkward.（对过去：could have + 过去分词）
- **口诀**：与现在事实相反，从句退一步用过去式、主句 would/could + 原形；假设过去的事，再退一步用 had done / would have done

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,动名词">

**本集动名词高发区**（[Ch.5](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter05.md)）——介词之后、特定动词之后，动词一律变 -ing：

- **miss + V-ing**：I'll never miss **doing** it.
- **形式主语 it's + 形容词 + V-ing**：it's pretty cool **knowing** that... / it's kinda cool **seeing** you up there
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">it</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">cool</span> <span class="s">knowing that...</span></p>
- **feel like + V-ing**：it doesn't feel like **playing** anymore
- **介词 + V-ing**：without **being** able to identify it / talk about **getting** married / what happened to **playing** the field / my way of **telling** you
- **be worth + 名词/V-ing**：it's worth a try
- **口诀**：记住几个"只接 -ing"的动词（miss/feel like/enjoy/finish），其余看介词

</GrammarTip>
