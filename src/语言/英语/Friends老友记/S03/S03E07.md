**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E07.mp3" start="0.0" duration="10.584" />

**原句**：I told Carl, "Nobody, no matter how famous their parents are, nobody is allowed to climb on the dinosaur." But of course this went in one ear and out.

**层次划分**：

- **I told Carl (that)...**：tell + 间接宾语 + 宾语从句"我告诉 Carl 说……"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **no matter how famous their parents are**：**no matter how + 形容词**引导让步状语从句"无论父母多有名"——从句用现在式（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **nobody is allowed to climb on the dinosaur**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">Nobody</span> <span class="v">is allowed</span> <span class="c">to climb on the dinosaur</span></p> nobody 作主语动词用单数——**主谓一致**（台：主语动词一致性）；**be allowed + 不定式**（台：不定词）to climb"被允许攀爬"，被动句中不定式作主语补足语（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **this went in one ear and out (the other)**：习语"左耳进右耳出"——and 连接两个方向副词，括号内常省略（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）

**翻译**：所以我跟 Carl 说："任何人——不管他爸妈多大牌——都不许爬上恐龙。"结果呢，人家当然是左耳进右耳出。

**语法要点**：no matter how 让步从句（Ch.13）；be allowed to do（Ch.4）；nobody 单数（Ch.11）

**原句**：There will come a time in each of your careers when you'll have a chance to screw over another soap opera actor. I'm ashamed to say that I took it. **层次划分**：

- **There will come a time in each of your careers when you'll have a chance to screw over another soap opera actor**：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">There</span> <span class="v">will come</span> <span class="s">a time</span></p> **there be 的将来式变体** there will come"总会有那么一天"；**when you have a chance...** 定语从句修饰 time；have a chance to + 原形；screw over"坑"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **in the recent, present**：Joey 想说 in the recent past"最近（过去）"，嘴瓢成 present——时间状语错误本身就是笑点（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **I'm ashamed to say that I took it**：**be ashamed to + 原形**"惭愧地承认"；say + that 宾语从句；took it = took the opportunity（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **I advised a fellow actor to play a role, homosexually**：**advise sb to do**"建议某人做某事"；play a role"演角色"；**homosexually** 副词"以同性恋方式"——生硬副词戳中笑点（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）
- **as it turned out, they liked the stupid gay thing and cast him**：**as it turned out**"结果（没想到）"——插入语；**cast him**"选他（出演）"（cast-cast-cast 三同形）（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **he's got a two year contract opposite Susan Lucci, the first lady of daytime television**：**opposite**"与……演对手戏"（介词用法）；**the first lady of daytime television** 同位语"日间剧第一夫人"；⚠️ 原文 `Susan Luchhi` 应为 **Susan Lucci**（美国肥皂剧女王，抄写误）（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **I'm stuck here teaching a bunch of people, most of whom are too ugly to even be on TV**：**be stuck (here) + 分词**"被困在这儿（教）"；a bunch of"一帮"；**most of whom** 非限定定语从句——whom 作介词 of 的宾语必须用宾格；**too + 形容词 + to + 原形**"丑到连电视都上不了"（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)/[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

**翻译**：（黯然）各位的演艺生涯里，总有一天，你会有机会去坑另一个肥皂剧演员。最近……现在吧，我就摊上这么一回。我惭愧地说，我干了——我建议一位同行，把这个角色，用同性恋的方式来演。没错，我俩都去试了镜，结果呢，他们偏偏喜欢那套愚蠢的 gay 把戏，选了他。现在人家拿到两年合约，跟日间剧第一夫人 Susan Lucci 演对手戏；而我呢，我，我却被困在这儿教你们这一帮——其中大多数人丑得连电视都上不去。对不起，对不起，对不起。（全班热烈鼓掌）谢谢。

**语法要点**：there will come a time + when 从句（Ch.14）；advise sb to do（Ch.4）；most of whom 非限定从句（Ch.14）；too...to（Ch.8）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,语气">

**本集"情态动词 + have + 过去分词"三兄弟**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）——对过去事情的推测与评论：

- **must have done（肯定推测："准是"）**：your Dad must've added wrong / you must've won like a contest or something
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">must've won</span> <span class="o">a contest</span></p>
- **might have done（没底气的可能："兴许"）**：I-I might've said supergay
- **shouldn't have done（懊悔的评论："真不该"）**：Monica kinda trusted me with something and she shouldn't have (trusted me)
- **口诀**：对过去下结论，情态动词后接 have + 过去分词；肯定程度 must ＞ might ＞ shouldn't（表"不该做而做了"）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,形容词">

**本集比较级 / 最高级花式用法**（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）：

- **比较级 + than**：Mr. Big Shot is better than 'wethead' / I tip more than that / one leg shorter than the other / my right leg is two inches shorter（度量 + 比较级）
- **惯用比较级**：be the bigger man（做度量更大的人）/ much more attractive（much 加强）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">have got to be</span> <span class="c">the bigger man</span></p>
- **最高级**：the biggest man / one of the most important things（one of the + 最高级 + 复数，动词随 one 用单数）
- **递进修辞**：the bigger man → the biggest man → a big, huge, giant man（比较级→最高级→同义词堆叠）
