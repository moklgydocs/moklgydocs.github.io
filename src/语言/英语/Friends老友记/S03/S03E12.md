**原句**：You should have seen the hug she gave him when she got the job.

**层次划分**：

- ⚠️ 原文 `you should of seen` 的 `of` 应为 **have**——should have seen 的快读同音致误
- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">should have seen</span> <span class="o">the hug she gave him</span></p> **should have + 过去分词**——"你真该看看（当时那个拥抱）"，带"没看见可惜/你不懂我多难受"的埋怨（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **the hug (that) she gave him**：**定语从句**修饰 hug，that 作 gave 的宾语（直接宾语）故省略；give sb sth 双宾（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **when she got the job**：**when 时间从句**"她拿到 offer 那会儿"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **he's really good looking**：**good looking**"（长得）好看"——复合形容词（[Ch.7](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter07.md)）

**翻译**：她在跟他吃午饭。在跟他吃午饭！你真该看看她拿到工作那天给他的那个拥抱。还有——他还长得特别帅。我怎么办啊？

**语法要点**：should have seen（Ch.9）；双宾 + 定语从句（Ch.1/Ch.14）

</GrammarTip>

**本集头号句型：动词 + 宾语 + 补语的四种补位**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **形容词补语**：which makes him even sexier / you make me so sad / keep it inside
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">which</span> <span class="v">makes</span> <span class="o">him</span> <span class="c">even sexier</span></p>
- **名词补语**：you name a kid that / he's becoming her confidant
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">name</span> <span class="o">a kid</span> <span class="c">that</span></p>
- **原形补语**：let's watch everybody watch Joey / Let it be me
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">watch</span> <span class="o">everybody</span> <span class="c">watch Joey</span></p>
- **不定式补语**：I need you to come to this bachelor party / want people to know / expect them to grow up to be
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">need</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">to come to this party</span></p>
- **口诀**：make/keep 后形容词，call/name 后名词，let/watch 后原形，want/need/expect 后 to

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,关系从句">

**本集关系代词"省略规则"实战**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **作宾语 → 可省略**：the hug (that) she gave him / stuff (that) Ross has sent her / the best (that) I could get out of them / a kiss (that) he won't forget
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">the hug she gave him</span> <span class="v">should have been seen</span></p>
- **作主语 → 不可省略**：things **that** move me / the guy **who**'s in an audition / trick poems **that** seem to be... but are...
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">things that move me</span> <span class="v">are...</span></p>
- **介词悬空时**：the woman (that) I interviewed with / the guy (that) she goes to to complain——介词留在句尾，关系词照样省
- **口诀**：宾语位，that 走；主语位，that 留；介词悬空不影响

</GrammarTip>
