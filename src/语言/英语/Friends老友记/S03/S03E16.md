- **I can't imagine my life without you**：imagine + 名词短语；**without + 名词/代词**"（无法）想象没有你的人生"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **Without these arms, and your face, and this heart**：**Without... 排比省略**——"（无法想像没有）这双臂、这张脸、这颗心"；**this heart** 指 Rachel 的善良（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：一定有办法让我们跨过这道坎的。我无法想象——我无法想象没有你的人生。没有这双臂，没有你的脸，没有这颗心……你这颗善良的心，Rach……

**语法要点**：there's got to be（Ch.1/Ch.9）；imagine...without（Ch.10）；Without 排比（Ch.15）

- **it doesn't matter what you say, or what you do**：**It doesn't matter + wh-从句**"（你说什么、做什么）都（再）无济于事"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,虚拟语气">

**本集是"虚拟语气"全家桶：与过去/现在事实相反**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）：

- **与过去事实相反：if + 过去式/过去完成，主句 would have done**：
  - none of this **would've happened** if I **didn't think**... you **weren't having** sex with Mark
  - Let's say I **had slept** with Mark. **Would** you **have been** able to forgive me?
  - I **would have been devastated**, but I **would** still **want** to be with you
<p class="svoc"><span class="a">if I had slept with Mark</span> <span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">would have forgiven</span> <span class="o">me</span></p>
- **与现在事实相反：wish + 过去式**：I **wish** I **could take** it back（我要是能收回就好了）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">wish</span> <span class="o">I could take it back</span></p>
- **对过去的推测/责备：情态动词 + have done**：how insane **must** I **have been** / what you **shoulda**(=should have) done
- **口诀**：if 从句退一级（现在→过去、过去→过去完成），主句 would have done；wish 后直接退一级

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,短语动词">

**本集"感情危机"短语动词一网打尽**（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）：

- **分**：**break up**（分手）/ **bail on sb**（撂下）/ **get outta here**（滚）/ **take a break**（冷静期）
- **合**：**make up with sb**（和好）/ **get back together**（复合）/ **work on this**（修补）/ **work it out with her**（理顺）/ **fight for us**（争取）
- **熬**：**get through this**（挺过）/ **work past it**（跨过）/ **get over**（走出）/ **take it back**（收回）
