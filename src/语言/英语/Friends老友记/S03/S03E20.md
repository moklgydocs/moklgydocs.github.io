**原句**：You have no idea. When we're on stage I get to kiss her and touch her, but then she goes home with the director, and it's like somebody's ripping out my heart!

**层次划分**：

- **You have no idea**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">You</span> <span class="v">have</span> <span class="o">no idea</span></p> have no idea"根本不知道"，no 直接否定名词（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

- **I get to kiss her and touch her**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">get to kiss / touch</span> <span class="o">her</span></p> get to do"得以、有机会做"，不定式作 get 的补足成分；kiss 与 touch 并列（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)）
- **she goes home with the director**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">goes</span> <span class="a">home with the director</span></p> 单三 goes；go home"回家"，home 副词不加 to（[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)/[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

**语法要点**：when 时间从句（Ch.13）；get to do（Ch.4）；it's like + 进行式表感受（Ch.3）

- **will → would**：He said he'**d** call.（Joanna 转述：站在过去看将来）
- **is going to → was going to**：he said he **was going to** call / I'm sorry I said I **was going to** when I'm not
- **未来仍未发生时可保留 will**：He said he'**ll** call.（Rachel 模仿 Joanna 原话，call 还没发生）
- **疑问词从句照保留语序**：kinda told her **how things were** with us
- **口诀**：主句动词是过去时（said/told），从句时态"退一步"；若说的内容到现在还没兑现，口语里 will 可以不退
