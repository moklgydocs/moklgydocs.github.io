**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E04.mp3" start="10.92" duration="8.736" />

**原句**：Has this ever happened to you? You go to the refrigerator to get a nice glass of milk, and these darn cartons are so flingin'-flangin' hard to open.

**层次划分**：

- **has this ever happened to you**：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">has</span> <span class="s">this</span> <span class="v">ever happened</span> <span class="a">to you</span></p> 现在完成式 + ever"曾经……过吗"——ever 把时间跨度拉到"过去至今"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）；happen to sb"发生在某人身上"，介词（台：介系词）to 引出承受者（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **You go to the refrigerator to get a nice glass of milk**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">You</span> <span class="v">go</span> <span class="a">to the refrigerator</span> <span class="a">to get a nice glass of milk</span></p> **不定式**（台：不定词）to get 表目的"去拿"；a glass of milk"一杯牛奶"——量词 + of + 名词（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **struggling to open a cartoon of milk**：现在分词短语作伴随状语；struggle to do"挣扎着做"；⚠️ 原文 `cartoon`（卡通）应为 **carton**（纸盒）（抄写形近误）（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **so flingin'-flangin' hard to open**：so + 形容词 + 不定式"难得打不开"；flingin'-flangin' 是广告主持人的自造强调词（后文 Chandler 会复读），仅起加强语气作用（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

**翻译**：各位，这种事你们遇到过吗？你去冰箱想倒杯好牛奶，结果这些破纸盒死活就是打不开。

**语法要点**：完成式 + ever（Ch.3）；不定式表目的（Ch.4）；so + adj + to do（Ch.8）

</GrammarTip>

**原句**：There is a revolutionary new product that guarantees that you'll never have to open up milk cartons again.

**层次划分**：

- 主干：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">There</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="s">a revolutionary new product</span></p> there be 存在句，真正主语 product 是单数，故用 is（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)/[Ch.11](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter11.md)）
- **that guarantees...**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 product，关系代词 that 在从句中作主语，**不可省略**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **that you'll never have to open up milk cartons again**：guarantees 的宾语，that 引导**名词从句**（台：宾语从句）；从句内 you'll never have to + 原形 open up"将再也不必打开"，have to 的否定由 never 承担（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **Meet the Milk Master 2000**：祈使句"请见识一下"，广告腔（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

**语法要点**：定语从句 that 作主语不可省（Ch.14）；动词 + that 宾语从句（Ch.12）；have to + 原形（Ch.9）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,动词时态,关系从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E04.mp3" start="44.976" duration="8.328" />

**原句**：Keep in mind, he's never used this product before, you're gonna see how easy this is to do. It is a spout that you jab into a paper milk carton so that you don't have to rip it open.

**层次划分**：

- **Keep in mind (that)...**：祈使句 + 省略 that 的宾语从句"请记住……"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **he's never used this product before**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">he</span> <span class="v">'s never used</span> <span class="o">this product</span> <span class="a">before</span></p> 现在完成式 + never...before"以前从未用过"——before 把时间括弧拉到现在（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **how easy this is to do**：see 的宾语从句，how + 形容词 easy 提前成感叹式语序，从句主谓不倒装（this is）；easy 后的不定式 to do 表"做起来容易"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **it is a spout that you jab into a paper milk carton**：**定语从句**（台：关系从句）修饰 spout"壶嘴"，that 在从句中作 jab 的宾语（jab a spout into a carton"把嘴子戳进纸盒"），口语保留不省（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）
- **so that you don't have to rip it open**：so that 引导目的副词从句"这样就不必……"；don't have to"不必"（≠ must not"不准"）（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **This works with any milk carton**：work with"对……管用"；any + 单数名词表"任何一个"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）

**翻译**：各位记住，他以前从没用过这东西，你们马上会看到它有多简单。请吧。这个小嘴子适用于任何牛奶纸盒——往盒上一戳就行，再也不用撕了。

**语法要点**：完成式 + never...before（Ch.3）；how 引导的感叹式宾语从句（Ch.12）；so that 目的从句（Ch.13）

</GrammarTip>

- **What are you being such a weenie for?**：= Why are you being such a weenie?——**what...for** 句型"为了什么/干嘛"；**be being + 名词**（weeny"怂包"）表"（一时）装怂、犯怂"，与上文 Are you being British? 同一进行式用法（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **So he has a Barbi, big deal**：so"不就是……嘛"带出不屑；big deal 反语"有什么了不起"（[Ch.15](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter15.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **You used to dress up like a woman**：**used to + 原形**"你过去常常……"（现在不了）；dress up like + 名词"打扮成……的样子"（[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：你搁这儿犯什么怂呢？他不就玩个芭比吗，有什么大不了。你小时候还老穿女装呢。

**语法要点**：what...for = why（Ch.9）；used to + 原形（Ch.3）；be being + 名词（Ch.3）

</GrammarTip>

**本集祈使句四种面孔**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **基本型（原形动词开头）**：Get the woman a pad! / Give daddy the Barbi! / Drop the Barbi! / face your fear / Go for it!
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Get</span> <span class="o">the woman</span> <span class="o">a pad</span></p>
- **否定型 Don't + 原形**：don't go! / don't get me wrong / stop naming dwarves
- **Be + 形容词（命令摆出某种状态）**：Be sleepy. / we said be 'aloof' not 'a doof'
- **保留主语 you（加重叮嘱或对比）**：You go get yourself another appointment. / you go shoe shopping, you get your butt in a bubble bath
- **变体**：Let's move in together.（建议）/ let there be a song（祈愿）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,副词从句">

**本集 if 条件句三级跳**（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）：

- **真实条件（从句现在式代未来）**：If you feel yourself reaching for that phone, (then) you go shoe shopping / if I get it I'm going to have to see her tonight / If you're afraid of bugs, get a bug
- **与现在事实相反（从句退成过去式，主句 would）**：if Janice were a guy, she'd be sleeping with somebody else by now / If we ever did what you did, a man would never call
<p class="svoc"><span class="a">if Janice were a guy</span> <span class="s">she</span> <span class="v">'d be sleeping</span> <span class="a">with somebody else by now</span></p>
- **口诀**：真实条件"现在式管将来"；虚拟条件"动词往后退一格"（am → were、will → would、do → did）
