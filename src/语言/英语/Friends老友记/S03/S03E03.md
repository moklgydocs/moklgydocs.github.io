<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役动词">

**本集使役句型：make / let / help / get + 宾语 + 补语**（S+V+O+C，[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）：

- **make + O + 原形**：It makes me ache. / I made you quit cold turkey.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">made</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">quit cold turkey</span></p>
- **let + O + 原形**：Let me see. / or simply let hang（S01E06 联动）
<p class="svoc"><span class="v">Let</span> <span class="o">me</span> <span class="c">see</span></p>
- **help + O + 原形（或 to do）**：I'll help you get over my sister. / help him get 'de-Ursula-ized'.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">'ll help</span> <span class="o">you</span> <span class="c">get over my sister</span></p>
- **get + O + to do**（唯一要带 to 的）：get them to put my 'Days of Our Lives' on here.
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I should</span> <span class="v">get</span> <span class="o">them</span> <span class="c">to put...on here</span></p>
- **口诀**：make / let / have 后补语用原形；get 后必须 to do；help 两可（美式多省 to）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集 that's + wh- 表语从句**（台：名词从句作补语，[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **that's why + 从句（原因）**：that's why your parents told you not to jump / that's why he couldn't just come up and talk to me
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">why he couldn't come up...</span></p>
- **that's how + 从句（方式）**：that's how you bought it
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">how you bought it</span></p>
- **that's what + 从句（事物，what 兼任从句成分）**：that's what's stupid / that's what I was wearing / that's what I would do
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what I would do</span></p>
- **that's when + 从句（时刻）**：that's when I came up with the baby plan
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">when I came up with...</span></p>
- **口诀**：why 问原因、how 问方式、when 问时间、what 指事物——从句内一律陈述语序，what 本身还要在从句里"兼职"作主语或宾语

</GrammarTip>
