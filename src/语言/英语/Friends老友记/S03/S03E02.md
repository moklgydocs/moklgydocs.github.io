**原句**：I'd feel a whole lot better if you got dressed now.

**层次划分**：

- **if you got dressed now**：if 条件副词从句用**过去式** got dressed——明明说的是 now"现在"，动词却退后一个时态，这正是虚拟语气的标志："（可惜你们现在并没有穿）要是现在穿上就好了"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

**翻译**：说实话，你们要是现在就把衣服穿好，我心里会踏实得多。

**语法要点**：虚拟语气主从句时态搭配 would V / if + 过去式（Ch.9）；比较级的程度修饰（Ch.7）

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,名词从句">

**本集 what 名词从句（台：名词从句）四种角色**（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

- **作主语**：**What you do on your own time**...（你私下干什么随你）/ know **what's weird**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">What you do on your own time</span> <span class="v">...</span></p>
- **作宾语**：figure out **what I'm wearing** / you know **what gets out hummus** / I don't know **what you're trying to do to me** / Let's see **what else he'll do**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">know</span> <span class="o">what gets out hummus</span></p>
- **作补语**：If that is **what it takes**... / Then that's **what I'll do**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what I'll do</span></p>
- **口诀**：what = the thing that；从句内一律**陈述语序**，what 在从句里要兼任主语或宾语

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,使役动词">

**本集 make + 宾语 + 补语句型：S+V+O+C**（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）——"look fat"梗三连：

- **make + O + 形容词补语**：this is the outfit that **makes my calves look fat** / I don't care if they **make your ankles or your knees or your earlobes look fat**
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">the outfit</span> <span class="v">makes</span> <span class="o">my calves</span> <span class="c">look fat</span></p>
- **make + 形式宾语 it + 形容词 + 不定式**：that will **make it easy to get a cab**——it 顶替后面的不定式
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">will make</span> <span class="o">it</span> <span class="c">easy</span> <span class="a">to get a cab</span></p>
- **注意**：make 作"使得"讲时是使役动词，补语用**原形/形容词**，不加 to；同类句型还有 want + O + 地点补语（I want you there / I want everyone down stairs）

</GrammarTip>
