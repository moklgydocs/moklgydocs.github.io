> 哦，菲比，你还在等吗？我应该在两小时前给我爸爸回电话的。
<GrammarTip points="不定词,短语动词">

- **be supposed to + 原形**："应该、本该……"——**不定式**（台：不定词）to call 跟在 supposed 后；用过去式 was supposed to 常含"本该做却没做"的意味（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)/[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）
- **call my Dad back**：可分离短语动词 call back"回电话"，名词宾语 my Dad 可放中间（代词则必须放中间：call him back）（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **still on hold**：介词（台：介系词）短语 on hold"（电话）持线等待中"；like 此处是口语"大约"= about（[Ch.8](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter08.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E22.mp3" start="6.984" duration="4.488" /> **Phoebe Buffay:** Oh, yeah, he clipped on, he said <Note>call him</Note> <Note>as soon as you get a chance</Note>, he’s at Flimby’s.
> 哦，是的，他突然说，他说一有机会就给他打电话，他在弗林比家。
<GrammarTip points="名词从句,副词从句">

- **he said (that you should) call him**：said 后接**宾语从句**（台：名词从句），口语省略连接词 that；从句内直接用原形 call，相当于间接命令 = he told you **to call** him（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）
- **as soon as you get a chance**：as soon as"一……就……"引导时间**状语从句**（台：副词从句），虽然指将来，从句用**现在式 get 代未来**（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）
- **clip on**：（电话里）突然插话/接通，短语动词（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,副词从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E22.mp3" start="23.04" duration="12.144" />

**原句**：That's what they want me to do. My warranty expires tomorrow, if I don't get through, they're not gonna fix my crappy, broken phone for free! We cannot let them win! It's us versus them!

**层次划分**：

- **that's what they want me to do**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">that</span> <span class="v">'s</span> <span class="c">what they want me to do</span></p> what 引导**表语从句**（台：名词从句），what = the thing that"他们想要的（事）"；从句内部是 want + 宾语 me + **不定式作宾语补足语**（台：补语）to do（S+V+O+C）（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **if I don't get through, they're not gonna fix my crappy, broken phone for free**：<p class="svoc"><span class="a">if I don't get through</span> <span class="s">they</span> <span class="v">'re not gonna fix</span> <span class="o">my crappy, broken phone</span> <span class="a">for free</span></p> if 条件副词从句用**现在式代未来**；主句 be gonna + 原形表将来；get through"（电话）接通"；crappy（口语"烂"）与过去分词 broken 并列作定语；for free"免费"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)/[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）
- **We cannot let them win**：<p class="svoc"><span class="s">We</span> <span class="v">cannot let</span> <span class="o">them</span> <span class="c">win</span></p> 使役动词 let + 宾语 + **原形补语**（不加 to）（[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **It's us versus them**：口语用宾格 us / them 作补语（正式文体应为 we / they）；versus 介词"对垒"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）

**翻译**：不！瑞秋，他们要的就是我挂断。我的保修明天到期，要是打不通，他们就不给免费修这部破手机了！我们不能让他们赢！这是我们和他们的战争！

**语法要点**：what 表语从句 + want O to do（Ch.12/Ch.4）；if 条件句现在式代未来 + be gonna（Ch.13/Ch.3）；let + O + 原形（Ch.1）

</GrammarTip>

**本集句型一：think / say / hope / guess / swear + 省略 that 的宾语从句**（台：名词从句）（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）：

<p class="svoc"><span class="s">I</span> <span class="v">think</span> <span class="o">(that) he's bad news</span></p>
- **注意**：口语中 that 几乎一律省略；否定要**前移**——I **don't think** you should be seeing Tommy（= 我认为你不该再见他，not 挂在 think 上）

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,关系从句">

**本集句型二：定语从句（台：关系从句）——关系代词"作宾语可省、作主语不可省"**（[Ch.14](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter14.md)）：

- **作宾语 → 可省**：the word (that) I use / the girl (that) you like / the guy (that) Rachel's dating / the guy (that) Rachel is with / all that training (that) they go through / the next play (that) I'm gonna see / things (that) you have to do / the number (that) you can call
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">the guy (that) Rachel is with</span> <span class="v">is</span> <span class="c">crazy</span></p>
- **作主语 → 不可省**：people **who** couldn't make it / some guy **who** may or may not scream / a little chick **who**'s disgusting
- **口诀**：关系代词后面**紧跟主语**（I / you / Rachel…）→ 可省；**紧跟动词**（couldn't / may / 's…）→ 不可省

</GrammarTip>
