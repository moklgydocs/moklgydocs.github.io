<GrammarTip type="long" points="长难句,名词从句,副词从句">

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E24.mp3" start="7.2" duration="10.632" />

**原句**：Do you think that there's a town in Missouri or some place called Sample? And as you're driving into town there's like a sign, and it says "You're in Sample."

**层次划分**：

- **Do you think (that) there's a town...?**：think + 宾语从句（台：名词从句），从句是 There be 存在句；a town in Missouri or some place 并列主语（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)/[Ch.1](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter01.md)）
- **some place called Sample**：called Sample 是过去分词短语作后置定语"（一个）叫 Sample 的地方"（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)/[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)）
- **as you're driving into town**：as 时间**状语从句**（台：副词从句）+ 现在进行式"当你正开车进城时"（[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)/[Ch.3](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter03.md)）
- **it says "You're in Sample"**：say + 宾语从句"（牌子上）写着……"；笑点：**in Sample** 谐音 urine sample（尿样）——"你尿（样）里了"（[Ch.12](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter12.md)）

**翻译**：你说密苏里会不会有个小镇叫 Sample？你开车进城的时候，路边立着个牌子，写着"你正身处 Sample（尿样）之中"。

**语法要点**：think/say + that 从句（Ch.12）；as + 进行式时间从句（Ch.13）

</GrammarTip>

**原句回听**：<AudioButton src="/audio/episodes/S03/S03E24.mp3" start="1083.024" duration="9.216" />

**原句**：This is ironic. Of your last two boyfriends, Richard didn't want to have kids, and from the looks of it, now Pete can't.

**层次划分**：

- **Of your last two boyfriends**：**of 部分格**"在你前两任男友当中"——of + 范围引出话题（[Ch.2](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter02.md)/[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **Richard didn't want to have kids**：want + **不定式**（台：不定词）to have kids"不想生小孩"（[Ch.4](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter04.md)）
- **from the looks of it**：惯用语"从目前迹象看来"（[Ch.10](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter10.md)）
- **now Pete can't (have kids)**：**省略句**——can't 后省去整个不定式短语；毒舌笑点：刚才那一击之后，Pete（身体上）不能生了（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)）

**翻译**：这可真讽刺。你最近两任男友：Richard 是不想要孩子——而 Pete 呢，照刚才那下看，是要不了了。

**语法要点**：情态动词后不定式短语整段省略（Ch.9/Ch.4）；of 部分格（Ch.2）

**本集 get + 过去分词被动句型**（[Ch.6](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter06.md)）：

- **get hurt**：I don't want you to get hurt.（= be hurt"受伤"）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">you</span> <span class="v">get</span> <span class="c">hurt</span></p>
- **get smacked**：We never get smacked.（"被拍屁股"——本集主题词）
<p class="svoc"><span class="s">We</span> <span class="v">never get</span> <span class="c">smacked</span></p>
- **get tanked**：I got tanked myself last night.（"喝醉"）
- **feel left out**：You're not feeling left out, are ya?（"被冷落"——left out 已是形容词化补语）
- **口诀**：get + 过去分词 = be 被动的口语版，强调"遭遇、变化"，比 be 被动更有动作感

</GrammarTip>

<GrammarTip type="pattern" points="句型总复习,虚拟语气">

**本集虚拟/假设句型一览**（[Ch.9](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter09.md)/[Ch.13](../../语法俱乐部/Chapter13.md)）：

- **What if + 过去式**：What if Joey **were** president?（与现在事实相反）
- **if + 过去，主句 would**：I'**d** fight tonight, if they'**d** let me.（今晚就能打，如果他们肯让的话）
- **would it be okay...if + 现在式**：would it be okay with you if I **set** Ross up...?（委婉请求，不是虚拟）
- **would have done**：that **would have been** really hard to describe（对过去的假设）
- **couldn't have done...without**：(I) **couldn't have done** it without ya.（对过去的虚拟否定 = 最大肯定）
- **口诀**：if 从句动词"退一步"（现在→过去），主句配 would/could；辨真伪看事实——真有可能是真实条件（用现在式），绝无可能才是虚拟（退一步）

</GrammarTip>
