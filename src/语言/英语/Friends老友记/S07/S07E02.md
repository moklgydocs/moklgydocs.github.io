# The One with Rachel's Book
> *有瑞秋书的那本*

The One With Rachel’s Book
> 拿着瑞秋书的那个

**Written by: Andrew Reich & Ted Cohen**

**Transcribed by: Eric Aasen**

🎬 *Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's, everyone is there having breakfast and Joey enters carrying a loaf of bread.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比家，每个人都在那里吃早餐，乔伊拿着一条面包进来。*

**Joey Tribbiani:** Hey!
> 嘿！

**Ross Geller:** Hi!
> 嗨！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Who wants French toast?
> 谁想要法式吐司？

**Ross Geller:** Oh, I’ll have some!
> 哦，我要一些！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Good, me too. (Tosses him the loaf.) Eggs and milk are in the fridge. Thanks.
> 我也是。（把面包扔给他。）鸡蛋和牛奶在冰箱里。谢谢。

**Monica Geller:** (entering from her room) Oww!
> （从她的房间进来）噢！

**Chandler Bing:** What’s the matter honey?
> 怎么了，亲爱的？

**Monica Geller:** I don’t know, my hand feels weird. I guess it’s because, I’m engaged! (Shows off the ring.) How long before it starts getting annoying?
> 我不知道，我的手感觉怪怪的。我想这是因为，我订婚了！（炫耀戒指）多久它才会变得烦人？

**Phoebe Buffay:** It starts?
> 开始了？

**Rachel Green:** Yeah, so let’s get started on the wedding plans!
> 是啊，那我们就开始婚礼计划吧！

**Monica Geller:** Okay! (Runs off.)
> 好的！（跑掉了）

**Chandler Bing:** (incredulous) Already?!
> （怀疑）已经？！

**Rachel Green:** Yeah, we got a lot to do! We gotta think about the flowers, the caterers, the music…
> 是啊，我们有很多事要做！我们得想想鲜花、餐饮、音乐…

**Chandler Bing:** Oh, I got some thoughts on that.
> 哦，我有一些想法。

**Rachel Green:** Oh wait Chandler, too many cooks…
> 哦，等等，钱德勒，厨师太多了…

**Ross Geller:** Take from me, as the groom all you have to do is show up and try to say the right name.
> 听我说，作为新郎，你所要做的就是出现并试着说出正确的名字。

**Monica Geller:** (returning) Okay! (Sets down a huge 3" 3-ring binder on the table.)
> （返回）好的！（在桌上放了一个巨大的3英寸3环活页夹。）

**Chandler Bing:** What in God’s name is that?!
> 那到底是什么？！

**Ross Geller:** Oh my God, the wedding book?! I haven’t seen that since the forth grade!
> 哦，天哪，结婚书？！我从四年级就没见过了！

**Monica Geller:** This baby has got everything. Take y’know, locations for instance. (She opens up the binder to the locations chapter.) First, organized alphabetically, then geographically, then by square footage.
> 这孩子什么都有了。以你知道的地点为例。（她打开活页夹，打开位置章节。）首先，按字母顺序排列，然后按地理位置排列，然后按平方英尺排列。

**Phoebe Buffay:** That is so smart! (To Chandler, under her breath) Break it off. Break it off now.
> 太聪明了！（对着钱德勒，屏住呼吸）别说了。现在把它关掉。

> 这应该可以证明我首先想到了侏罗纪公园！现在让我们来看看……（菲比冲进来）。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hey! Ross!
> 嘿！罗斯！

> 菲比，我的天啊！你在这里干什么？

> 我需要和你谈谈，事情很紧急。是关于莫妮卡和钱德勒的。

**Ross Geller:** Oh my God! Of course, of course. (To the class.) Umm, would you please excuse me for a moment? Umm, do you know each other’s hometowns? Why don’t you… (Motions that they should learn everyone’s hometown.) (To Phoebe) Wh-what’s going on?
> 哦，我的天啊！当然，当然。（对全班）嗯，请原谅我一会儿好吗？嗯，你们知道彼此的家乡吗？你为什么不…（提议他们应该了解每个人的家乡。）（对菲比）发生了什么事？

> 嗯，不多。但是，我只是在想，既然那些家伙刚刚订婚，如果他们有一些隐私的话，也许会更好，你知道吗？我能和你住几天吗？

**Ross Geller:** Umm, okay, yeah, sure. But wh-what’s wrong with Monica and Chandler?
> 嗯，好的，是的，当然。但是莫妮卡和钱德勒怎么了？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Nothing—Why?!
> 没什么为什么？！

**Ross Geller:** Phoebe, you said it was urgent!
> 菲比，你说有急事！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh yeah it is! I’m going to the movies and it starts in like five minutes.
> 哦，是的！我要去看电影，大概五分钟后就开始了。

**Ross Geller:** Do you realize I have a classroom full of students?
> 你知道我的教室里挤满了学生吗？

**Phoebe Buffay:** (to the students) Oh, I’m sorry. I’m so rude. Does anyone want to come to the movies?
> （对学生们）哦，对不起。我太粗鲁了。有人想来看电影吗？

🎬 *Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's, Rachel and Monica are pretty much telling Chandler what the wedding plans are.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡、钱德勒、菲比、瑞秋和莫妮卡几乎都在告诉钱德勒婚礼的计划。*

**Monica Geller:** All right, so I haven’t cleared the budget with my parents yet, but tell me how this is for music.
> 好吧，我还没有和我父母结清预算，但是告诉我这是怎么用于音乐的。

**Rachel Green:** Okay.
> 好的。

**Monica Geller:** All right umm, a string quartet for the procession.
> 好吧，嗯，游行的弦乐四重奏。

**Rachel Green:** Aw.
> 噢。

**Monica Geller:** A jazz trio for cocktails. The Bay City Rollers for dancing. Wait, that was from my sixth grade wedding.
> 鸡尾酒爵士三重奏。海湾城市为跳舞而狂欢。等等，那是我六年级的婚礼。

**Chandler Bing:** Well, you couldn’t get them anyway. Ian doesn’t plan anymore and Derrick… (Off of Rachel and Monica’s looks) And Derrick is a name I shouldn’t know.
> 嗯，反正你也买不到。伊恩不再计划了，德里克…（从瑞秋和莫妮卡的表情来看）而德里克是一个我不应该知道的名字。

**Joey Tribbiani:** (sitting up from the couch) Hey Mon, do you have another pillow? (Holds up one.) Y’know, something a little snugglyer?
> （从沙发上坐起来）嘿，妈妈，你还有枕头吗？（举起一个）你知道，有点依偎吗？

**Chandler Bing:** Why are you napping over here instead of over at your place?
> 你为什么在这里打盹而不是在你家？

**Joey Tribbiani:** Well, the duck…
> 嗯，鸭子…

**Rachel Green:** What?! The duck?! What the hell did the damn duck do now?!
> 什么？！鸭子？！这该死的鸭子现在干了什么？！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Uh, well he did not get sick somewhere in there and it was immediately found and properly cleaned up!
> 嗯，他在那里什么地方没有生病，而且很快就被发现了，并得到了妥善的清理！

**Chandler Bing:** Now, do I get to look at this book or is it just for people who are actually involved in the wedding?
> 现在，我是要看这本书，还是只为那些真正参与婚礼的人看？

**Monica Geller:** Of course you can look at it! Yeah, I want your opinion too!
> 你当然可以看！是的，我也想听听你的意见！

**Chandler Bing:** Okay.
> 好的。

**Monica Geller:** Here you go! What do you think about centerpieces?
> 给你！你觉得中心件怎么样？

**Chandler Bing:** Centerpieces!
> 中心！

**Monica Geller:** Yeah! Roses or Lilies? (Holds up a picture of each.)
> 是啊！玫瑰还是百合？（每个人都有一张照片。）

**Chandler Bing:** Definitely roses. (Monica and Rachel exchange a look.) Well, I just think they’re a little more weddingy. (Monica holds the Lily picture closer to him.) But Lilies are the clear choice.
> 当然是玫瑰。（莫妮卡和瑞秋交换了一个眼神。）嗯，我只是觉得他们结婚了一点。（莫妮卡把百合花的照片拿得离他更近）但百合花是最好的选择。

**Monica Geller:** Oh my God! It’s like one mind.
> 哦，我的天啊！这就像一颗心。

**Chandler Bing:** Uh-huh!
> 嗯！

**Joey Tribbiani:** (sitting up again) Guys! Guys!! You gotta let me nap! Ugh, I’m gonna get cranky!
> （又坐起来）伙计们！伙计们！！你得让我睡一觉！啊，我要发疯了！

**Rachel Green:** Joey, there is a perfectly good couch across the hall!
> 乔伊，大厅对面有一张非常好的沙发！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yes it is perfectly good, and it is not one of the places the duck got sick!
> 是的，非常好，而且这不是鸭子生病的地方之一！

**Rachel Green:** What?!
> 什么？！

**Joey Tribbiani:** All right, I’m gonna go! (Gets up and heads for the door.)
> 好吧，我要走了！（起身向门口走去。）

**Rachel Green:** Now Joey, what did the duck do?!
> 乔伊，鸭子做了什么？！

**Joey Tribbiani:** I don’t know! But he did not eat your face cream!
> 我不知道！但是他没有吃你的面霜！

[Cut to Joey and Rachel’s, Joey enters and heads for his bedroom. He pushes open the door to find the duck.]
> [切到乔伊和瑞秋家，乔伊走进房间，朝他的卧室走去。他推开门找到了鸭子。]

**Joey Tribbiani:** Hey little buddy, how are you feeling? (The duck does not get sick and Joey recoils in horror and heads for the couch.) What the hell is in that face cream? (He’s about to try out the couch but notices the bed in Rachel’s room. He walks into her room and feels the bed.) That’s so soft. (He pulls back the comforter.) Pillowcases! (He climbs in and groans in delight. Suddenly, he feels something under him and pulls out a little beat up paperback book. He opens it and starts to read from it.) (In his head.) Zelda looked at the chimney sweep. Her father, the vicar…(Stops reading and thinks.) The vicar? (Continues reading) …wouldn’t be home for hours. Her loins were burning. She threw caution to the wind and reached out and grabbed his…(Out loud.) Whoa! (Reads on in silence.) Whoa-ho-ho-ho! This is a dirty book! (Continues to read.)
> 嘿，小家伙，你感觉怎么样？（鸭子没有生病，乔伊吓得往后退，朝沙发走去。）那面霜里到底是什么？（他正要试用沙发，但注意到了瑞秋房间的床。他走进她的房间，摸了摸床。）太柔软了。（他拉开被子）枕套！（他爬了进去，高兴地呻吟着。突然，他感到身下有什么东西，拿出一本破旧的平装书。他打开书，开始读。）。她的父亲，牧师…（停止阅读并思考）牧师？（继续阅读）…几个小时都不在家。她的腰在燃烧。她不顾一切地伸出手抓住了他的…（大声说）哇！（默念）哇，呵呵！这是一本脏书！（继续阅读。）

> 那我开始和钱德勒约会的时候呢？

> 是钱德勒！我们没想到他会求婚！
