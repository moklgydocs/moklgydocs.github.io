# The One with the Holiday Armadillo
> *带假日犰狳的那只*

The One With the Holiday Armadillo
> 带假日犰狳的那个

**Written by: Greg Malins**

**Transcribed by: Eric Aasen**

🎬 *Monica, Chandler, and Phoebe's, Chandler is on the phone, Rachel and Monica are sitting in the kitchen.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡、钱德勒和菲比家，钱德勒在打电话，瑞秋和莫妮卡坐在厨房。*

**Chandler Bing:** Buh-bye. (Hangs up the phone) I just got us reservations at Michelle’s and tickets to the Musicman to celebrate our first holiday season as a betroughed couple.
> 再见。（挂断电话）我刚拿到米歇尔家的订票和音乐人的票，以庆祝我们作为一对情侣的第一个假期。

**Monica Geller:** Betrothed… (Corrects him)
> 订婚了…（纠正了他）

**Chandler Bing:** …betrothed couple.
> ……订婚夫妇。

**Phoebe Buffay:** (entering carrying a skull) Hey!
> （拿着头骨进来）嘿！

**Chandler Bing:** Hey!
> 嘿！

Rachel and **Monica Geller: **Hi!

> 瑞秋和**莫妮卡·盖勒：**嗨！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Haaaa... (Puts the skull on the table) ... ahhhh!
> 哈哈。。。（把头骨放在桌子上）。。。啊！

**Chandler Bing:** Pheebs?
> 菲比？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Huh?
> 嗯？

**Chandler Bing:** Skull?
> 骷髅？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh, yeah, it's my mom's.
> 哦，是的，是我妈妈的。

**Rachel Green:** (freaking out) Oh my god!!
> （发疯）哦，我的天啊！！

**Phoebe Buffay:** No, no, no. It's not! It's not my mom. It belonged to mom. Yeah, no, she used to put it out every Christmas to remind us, that even though it's Christmas, people still die. And, you can put candy in it. (She grabs the skull, pulls out a stick of licorice, and takes a bite.)
> 不，不，不，不是！不是我妈妈。它是妈妈的。是的，不，她每年圣诞节都会把它放出来提醒我们，即使是圣诞节，人们仍然会死去。而且，你可以在里面放糖果。（她抓起头骨，拿出一根甘草，咬了一口。）

**Ross Geller:** (entering) Hey!
> （进来）嘿！

**All:** Hey!
> 嘿！

**Monica Geller:** (Offering Ross the skull) Licorice?
> （给罗斯头骨）甘草？

**Ross Geller:** (Thinking it over) Sure! (Takes one) Hey, I just found out, I get Ben for the holidays this year.
> （仔细考虑）当然！（拍了一张）嘿，我刚发现，今年的假期我请到了本。

**All:** Ohh! That's great!
> 哦！太好了！

**Monica Geller:** Are you gonna dress up as Santa?
> 你要打扮成圣诞老人吗？

**Ross Geller:** Nope. I mean, I know Susan does every year, but I think I wanna take this year to teach him all about Hanukkah.
> 没有。我是说，我知道苏珊每年都这样做，但我想今年我要教他所有关于光明节的知识。

**Phoebe Buffay:** And maybe I could teach Ben about the Christmas skull and how people die.
> 也许我可以教本关于圣诞骷髅和人们是如何死去的。

**Rachel Green:** You may need to use this year to teach Ben about Phoebe.
> 你可能需要用今年的时间来教本菲比。

**Ross Geller:** Hmm.
> 嗯。

*(Joey comes out of the bathroom reading a newspaper)*
> *（乔伊从浴室出来看报纸）*

**Joey Tribbiani:** Hey. (He exits)
> 嘿。（他退出）

**Rachel Green:** (to Monica) Did you know he was in there?
> （对莫妮卡）你知道他在里面吗？

**Monica Geller:** No.
> 没有。

**Chandler Bing:** How long have we been home?
> 我们在家多久了？

**Monica Geller:** About a half an hour.
> 大约半小时。

**Chandler Bing:** Lovely!
> 真可爱！

🎬 *Central Perk, Chandler is getting a cup of coffee and sits down next to Monica.*
> 🎬 *中央公园，钱德勒正在喝咖啡，坐在莫妮卡旁边。*

**Chandler Bing:** Hey, you know what I was thinking? When we get married, are you gonna change your last name to Bing?
> 嘿，你知道我在想什么吗？我们结婚后，你会把你的姓改成宾吗？

**Monica Geller:** No.
> 没有。

**Chandler Bing:** Why not?
> 为什么不呢？

**Monica Geller:** Bing's weird.
> 宾很奇怪。

**Phoebe Buffay:** (entering) Oh, hey, you guys!
> （进来）哦，嘿，你们！

**Chandler Bing:** Hey!
> 嘿！

**Monica Geller:** Hey!
> 嘿！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hey. Guess what! My landlord just called and my apartment is gonna get ready soon, so I guess I'll be moving out.
> 嘿。猜猜看！我的房东刚打电话来，我的公寓马上就要准备好了，所以我想我要搬出去了。

**Monica Geller:** Ahh, Phoebe, I'm gonna miss you!
> 啊，菲比，我会想你的！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yes, you will be very sad. All right, well I gotta go tell Rachel the good news.
> 是的，你会很难过的。好吧，我得去告诉瑞秋这个好消息。

**Chandler Bing:** Ohh! You guys gonna be living together again?
> 哦！你们还会住在一起吗？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah, why not?
> 是啊，为什么不呢？

**Chandler Bing:** Well, she's just so much fun with Joey, I just assumed, she'd still be living with him.
> 嗯，她和乔伊在一起很开心，我只是想，她还会和他住在一起。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Why do you think, she's having so much fun living with Joey?
> 你觉得为什么，她和乔伊住在一起很开心？

**Chandler Bing:** No reason, except…she…told…me.
> 没有理由，除了……她……告诉了我。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Really? So she said, she didn’t wanna live with me anymore?
> 真的吗？所以她说，她不想再和我住在一起了？

**Chandler Bing:** No! No, she didn't say that. I-I-I think you should talk to Monica now.
> 不！不，她没那么说。我想你现在应该和莫妮卡谈谈。

**Monica Geller:** Phoebe, don't worry about it. I'm sure she wants to live with you.
> 菲比，别担心。我肯定她想和你住在一起。

**Phoebe Buffay:** You're sure? You're absolutely sure?
> 你确定？你绝对肯定？

**Monica Geller:** Well, no. But, um, I bet she probably does.
> 嗯，没有。但是，嗯，我打赌她可能有。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Probably? Yeah, I don't like that word. (Chandler and Monica look surprised) Kind of what ‘probably’ really means. Yeah, uh-huh. Yeah, oh, "Your mom probably won't kill herself," y’know? I'm sorry, but I'm not hanging all my hopes of Rachel and I living together on-on "Probably!" Y’know? You gotta take care of yourselves! (She starts to walk out) In this world history teaches us nothing! (Exits)
> 可能吧？是的，我不喜欢这个词。（钱德勒和莫妮卡看起来很惊讶）这就是“可能”的真正含义。是的，嗯。是的，哦，“你妈妈可能不会自杀，”你知道吗？我很抱歉，但我不会把我和瑞秋住在一起的希望都寄托在“可能”上，你知道吗？你得照顾好自己！（她开始走出去）在这个世界上，历史什么也教不了我们！（出口）

**Chandler Bing:** Bing doesn't seem so weird now, does it?
> 冰现在看起来不那么奇怪了，是吗？

🎬 *Michelle's, Chandler and Monica enter.*
> 🎬 *米歇尔、钱德勒和莫妮卡走进来。*

**Chandler Bing:** (to the Maitre d') Hi, could we get two burritos to go, please? (Laughs.)
> （对领班说）嗨，我们能买两个墨西哥煎饼吗？（笑）

**Monica Geller:** I'm sorry. But not that sorry, 'cause you don't have to live with it. Um, we have a reservation under the name Chandler Bing.
> 对不起。但不是很抱歉，因为你不必忍受它。嗯，我们以钱德勒·宾的名字预订了房间。

Maitre d': Oh-kay, we'll have a table for you in about 45 minutes.
> 哦，凯，45分钟后我们会给你安排一张桌子。

**Chandler Bing:** Forty-five minutes? We have tickets to the Musicman at 8:00.
> 四十五分钟？我们有8点去音乐人的票。

Maitre d': I'm sorry. Christmas is a very busy time, sir.
> 对不起。先生，圣诞节很忙。

**Chandler Bing:** Is this because of the burrito thing?
> 这是因为玉米煎饼的事吗？

**Monica Geller:** (pulling Chandler away from the Maitre d') You need to give him money.
> （把钱德勒从领班身边拉开）你得给他钱。

**Chandler Bing:** Give him money? It was a joke!
> 给他钱？这是个笑话！

**Monica Geller:** No, to get a table! Places like are always shakin’ you down. Everybody wants to be paid off.
> 不，找张桌子！像这样的地方总是让你心烦意乱。每个人都想得到报酬。

**Chandler Bing:** Right, calm down, O'Mally. I'll slip him some money.
> 好的，冷静点，奥马利。我会给他一些钱。

**Monica Geller:** You've got to be smooth about it.
> 你得坦然面对。

**Chandler Bing:** Hey, I can be smooth. (Walks back to the Maitre d', very smoothly) Listen, we're a little bit in a hurry, so, if you can get us a table a little quicker, I'd appreciate it. (Shakes his hand)
> 嘿，我可以很顺利。（非常顺利地走回领班）听着，我们有点赶时间，所以，如果你能快点给我们弄张桌子，我将不胜感激。（与他握手）

Maitre d': Of course, sir.
> 当然可以，先生。

**Chandler Bing:** Okay. (Walks back to Monica)
> 好的。（走回莫妮卡身边）

**Monica Geller:** How did it go?
> 怎么样？

**Chandler Bing:** Had the money in the wrong hand. (Shows her his left hand with the money in it)
> 钱拿错了手。（向她展示了他的左手和里面的钱）

🎬 *Ross's apartment, Ross is whirling Ben around.*
> 🎬 *罗斯的公寓，罗斯在和本周旋。*

**Ross Geller:** Wooooooo, hehehe. Hey, ahh, you don't feel like you're gonna throw up, do ya?
> 哦，呵呵。嘿，啊，你不会觉得你要吐了吧？

**Ben:** No.
> 没有。

**Ross Geller:** Well, I do, so let's... (Ben and Ross sit down on the couch) So, Ben, you uh, you know what holiday is coming up, don't ya?
> 嗯，我知道，所以让我们。。。（本和罗斯坐在沙发上）那么，本，你，呃，你知道什么节日即将到来，是吗？

**Ben:** Christmas.
> 圣诞节。

**Ross Geller:** Yep, and you know what other holiday is coming up?
> 是的，你知道还有什么节日吗？

**Ben:** Christmas eve.
> 平安夜。

**Ross Geller:** Yes, but also (Pauses to let Ben answer, but he doesn’t.) Hanukkah! See, you're part Jewish, and-and Hanukkah is a Jewish holiday.
> 是的，但是（停下来让本回答，但他没有回答）光明节！看，你有一部分是犹太人，光明节是犹太人的节日。

**Ben:** Santa has reindeers that can fly!
> 圣诞老人有会飞的驯鹿！

**Ross Geller:** Right, um, but, on Hanukkah, Hanukkah is a celebration of a miracle. See, years and years ago there were these people called the Maccabees.
> 是的，嗯，但是在光明节上，光明节是对奇迹的庆祝。看，很多年以前，有一些人叫马卡比人。

**Ben:** (singing) Jingle bells, jingle bells...
> （唱）叮当铃，叮当铃。。。

**Ross Geller:** (interrupts him) Okay, that's right, yes, but on Hanukkah, uh, we sing, uh (Sings) Dreidel, dreidel, dreidel, I made you out of clay.
> （打断他）好的，没错，是的，但是在光明节，我们唱，呃，唱，德雷德尔，德雷德尔，我用粘土做了你。

**Ben:** (singing) Rudolph, the red-nosed reindeer...
> （唱）鲁道夫，红鼻子驯鹿。。。

**Ross Geller:** (interrupts him again) Okay, it's not a contest.
> （再次打断他）好吧，这不是比赛。

**Ben:** When is Santa coming?
> 圣诞老人什么时候来？

**Ross Geller:** Well, how about this year, instead of Santa, we have fun celebrating Hanukkah?
> 那么，今年我们庆祝光明节而不是圣诞老人怎么样？

**Ben:** No Santa? Was I bad?
> 没有圣诞老人？我很坏吗？

**Ross Geller:** No! Oh, no-no-no. Hey, you weren't bad, you've been very good, Ben.
> 不！哦，不，不，不。嘿，你不坏，你一直都很好，本。

**Ben:** Santa's mad at me.
> 圣诞老人生我的气了。

**Ross Geller:** No, hey-hey, come on, (He grabs Ben and sits him on his lap) Ben, Santa is not mad at you, okay? Hey, you're-you're his favorite little guy!
> 不，嘿，嘿，来吧，（他抓住本，让他坐在腿上）本，圣诞老人没有生你的气，好吗？嘿，你是他最喜欢的小家伙！

**Ben:** So Santa's coming?
> 那么圣诞老人要来了？

**Ross Geller:** (disappointed) Yes! Santa's coming!
> （失望地）是的！圣诞老人来了！
