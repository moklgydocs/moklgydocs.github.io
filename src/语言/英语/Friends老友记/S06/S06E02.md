🎬 *Central Perk, it’s the same scene from the end of last week’s show. Phoebe and Rachel are sitting on the couch as Ross enters.*
> 🎬 *中央公园，与上周节目结束时的场景相同。罗斯进来时，菲比和瑞秋坐在沙发上。*

**Ross Geller:** Hey!
> 嘿！

**Rachel Green:** Hey, so did everything go okay with the annulment?
> 嘿，取消婚约一切顺利吗？

**Ross Geller:** Oh, yeah, no problems. It’s all taken care of.
> 哦，是的，没问题。一切都搞定了。

**Rachel Green:** Ross, thank you. Hey, do you guys wanna go see a movie?
> 罗斯，谢谢你。嘿，你们想去看电影吗？

**Ross Geller:** Oh yeah, why not?
> 哦，是的，为什么不呢？

**Rachel Green:** Okay, umm, I’m gonna get my sweater. (Walks away.)
> 好吧，嗯，我去拿毛衣。（走开了。）

**Ross Geller:** Okay. (Sits down next to Phoebe.) You uh, you wanna hear something weird?
> 好的。（坐在菲比旁边）你想听听奇怪的事吗？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Always.
> 总是。

**Ross Geller:** I didn’t get the annulment.
> 我没有被取消婚约。

**Phoebe Buffay:** What?!
> 什么？！

**Ross Geller:** We’re still married! Don’t tell Rachel. See you later. (He gets up leaving Phoebe in shock.)
> 我们还是结婚了！别告诉瑞秋。待会儿见。（他站起来，留下菲比震惊。）

[Cut to outside Central Perk.]
> [切至中央公园外。]

**Ross Geller:** (To Rachel) Hey, do you have any gum?
> （对瑞秋）嘿，你有口香糖吗？

**Rachel Green:** Oh? Yeah! Sure!
> 哦？是 啊当然

**Phoebe Buffay:** (running out) Wait! Wait! Hi! Listen, Ross can I just talk to you for just a second?
> （跑出去）等等！等待你好听着，罗斯，我能和你谈谈吗？

**Rachel Green:** Oh but Phoebe, we’re gonna be late for the movie.
> 哦，但是菲比，我们看电影要迟到了。

*(A cab pulls up.)*
> *（一辆出租车停了下来。）*

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh, there’s a cab! Taxi!! (The cab stops and she opens the door.) Good timing, my God, huh? Here you go. (Pushes Rachel in and closes the door.)
> 哦，有一辆出租车！出租车（出租车停了下来，她打开了门。）好时机，天哪，是吗？干得好。（把瑞秋推进去，关上门。）

**Rachel Green:** Yeah, we’re, we’re actually just gonna walk ‘cause it’s right up there at the Angelica.
> 是的，我们，我们实际上只是走路，因为它就在安吉丽卡。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh, the Angelica!! Go! Go! (She bangs on the cab’s roof and it pulls away.) (To Ross) You didn’t get the annulment?!!
> 哦，安吉丽卡！！走！走！（她砰的一声撞上了出租车的车顶，车开走了。）（对罗斯说）你没有拿到驾照吗？！！

**Ross Geller:** I know.
> 我知道。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ross?!
> 罗斯？！

**Ross Geller:** Well, I tried! But when I got to my lawyer’s office all I could hear was, "Three divorces. Three divorces!" Look, I just don’t want my tombstone to read, Ross Gellar, Three Divorces.
> 我试过了！但当我到律师办公室时，我听到的只是“三次离婚。三次离婚！”听着，我只是不想让我的墓碑上写着，罗斯·盖勒，三次离婚。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Don’t be worried about that! Your tombstone can say whatever you want it to say! It could say, Ross Geller, Good at Marriage! Y’know? Mine’s gonna say Phoebe Buffay, Buried Alive.
> 别担心！你的墓碑可以说任何你想说的话！可以说，罗斯·盖勒，擅长婚姻！你知道吗？我的名字是菲比·布菲，被活埋了。

**Ross Geller:** Look, all I know is I-I can’t have another failed marriage!
> 听着，我只知道我不能再有一次失败的婚姻了！

**Phoebe Buffay:** So okay what? You’re gonna be married to a girl who doesn’t even know about it?!—Op, woman! Sorry.
> 那好吧，什么？你会嫁给一个连这事都不知道的女孩-啊，女人！很抱歉

**Ross Geller:** Well, okay so, I don’t have it all worked out quite yet. Just don’t say anything to Rachel, please?
> 嗯，好吧，我还没有完全解决。别跟瑞秋说什么好吗？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Rachel is one of my closest friends. (Pause) Although, being the only one who knows anything about this does makes me feel special. Okay!
> 瑞秋是我最亲密的朋友之一。（停顿）不过，作为唯一一个知道这件事的人确实让我觉得很特别。可以

*(Rachel’s cab backs up.)*
> *（瑞秋的出租车倒车。）*

**Rachel Green:** (to the driver) Okay, stop-stop! Phoebe?!
> （对司机）好的，停下来！菲比？！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hey Rach!
> 嘿，瑞秋！

**Rachel Green:** What was that?!
> 那是什么？！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Sorry, mix up. Hey, how was the movie?
> 对不起，弄错了。嘿，电影怎么样？

**Rachel Green:** I haven’t seen it yet!
> 我还没看到呢！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Well then you’d better hurry! The Angelica! Go! Go! (Bangs on the roof again and off they go.)
> 那你最好快点！当归！走！走！（砰砰的一声砸在屋顶上，然后就掉了。）

**Rachel Green:** Noooooooo!!
> 不！！

🎬 *Monica and Rachel's, Chandler and Monica are cuddling on the couch.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡和瑞秋的，钱德勒和莫妮卡在沙发上拥抱。*

**Chandler Bing:** Y’know when we move in together, can I get a gumball machine?
> 你知道我们一起住的时候，我能买个口香糖机吗？

**Monica Geller:** Of course! Joey wouldn’t let you have one?
> 当然！乔伊不让你有一个？

**Chandler Bing:** No. When it comes to sweets, he’s surprisingly strict.
> 不。说到糖果，他出奇地严格。

**Monica Geller:** Hey, have you figured out a way to tell him you’re moving out?
> 嘿，你有没有想办法告诉他你要搬出去？

**Chandler Bing:** No, no, I keep trying, y’know? I can get out, "Joey, I have too…" but then I lose my nerve and I always finish with, "…go to the bathroom." He may think I’m sick.
> 不，不，我一直在努力，你知道吗？我可以走出去，“乔伊，我也有……”但是我失去了勇气，我总是以“……去洗手间”结束。他可能会认为我病了。

**Monica Geller:** Y’know, I really have to tell Rachel, but I… We just have to get it over with! Y’know, the next time we see them we’re just gonna tell them. Okay? That’s it.
> 你知道，我真的必须告诉瑞秋，但我…我们必须结束这一切！你知道，下次我们见到他们的时候，我们会告诉他们的。可以就这样。

**Chandler Bing:** Oh, so that’s this is gonna work now? You’re just gonna order me around all the time?
> 哦，那么这就行了吗？你要一直命令我吗？

**Monica Geller:** Pretty much.
> 差不多。

**Chandler Bing:** All right.
> 好吧。

**Joey Tribbiani:** (entering) Hey Monica!
> （进来）嘿，莫妮卡！

**Monica Geller:** Hi!
> 嗨！

**Joey Tribbiani:** (To Chandler) Hey man, you feeling any better? (Chandler answers him with some guttural sounds that only he can make and that no human can transcribe.)
> （对钱德勒）嘿，伙计，你感觉好些了吗？（钱德勒用一些只有他才能发出的、任何人都无法转录的喉音回答他。）

**Monica Geller:** Joey, we have something to tell you.
> 乔伊，我们有事要告诉你。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Oh my God! You’re pregnant!
> 哦，我的天啊！你怀孕了！

**Chandler Bing:** No-o-o! (To Monica) No? (She nods no.) No-o-o! Look Joey, here’s the thing, Monica and I have decided to live together, here. So, I’m gonna be moving out man.
> 不！（对莫妮卡）没有？（她点了点头）不！听着，乔伊，事情是这样的，莫妮卡和我决定住在一起。所以，我要搬出去了，伙计。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Wow! (Tearing up) Well, uh… Hey! I’m really happy for you guys! Congratulations! (Kisses Monica on the cheek) See you later. (Starts for the door on the verge of tears as Monica stops him.)
> 哇！（撕扯）嗯……嘿！我真为你们高兴！祝贺（亲吻莫妮卡的脸颊）再见。（莫妮卡拦住他时，他几乎要哭了。）

**Monica Geller:** Wait! Joey! Joey! Are you okay?!
> 等等！乔伊！乔伊！你没事吧？！

**Joey Tribbiani:** Yeah, I gotta go! I got an acting job. (Turns towards the door, pauses, and turns back.) Like you’d believe that. This sucks!
> 是啊，我得走了！我找到了一份表演工作。（转向门，停下来，然后转身）就像你相信的那样。这太糟糕了！

**Chandler Bing:** Look, I-I’m just gonna be right across the hall and I promise you, the minute Monica and I break up I’m moving right back in with you!
> 听着，我就要穿过大厅，我向你保证，我和莫妮卡分手的那一刻，我马上就搬回来和你住！

**Joey Tribbiani:** (To Monica) Okay! Look-look-look, uh, if you’re gonna be moving in with him I feel it’s my responsibility to tell you the truth about him! Okay? He’s a terrible roommate! Terrible! He uh, forgets to umm… Oh-oh he always, he always umm—Oh, who am I kidding! He’s the best roommate ever! (Hugs Chandler.)
> （对莫妮卡）好的！听着，如果你要和他一起住我觉得我有责任告诉你关于他的真相！可以他是个糟糕的室友！可怕的！他，呃，忘了……哦，哦，他总是，他总是，哦，哦，我在跟谁开玩笑！他是有史以来最好的室友！（拥抱钱德勒。）

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ooh! That’s good! Wow! But now if you were pregnant, what would you name it? Hint: Phoebe.

**Ross Geller:** Hey! Hi!

**Phoebe Buffay:** Guys, I’m happy too.
> 伙计们，我也很高兴。

**Monica Geller:** Okay, come here! (Phoebe joins them in the hug.)
> 好的，过来！（菲比和他们一起拥抱。）

**Phoebe Buffay:** Wow! Big day huh? People moving in, people getting annulled… (Winks at Ross.)
> 哇！大日子啊？人们搬进来，人们被宣告无效……（向罗斯眨眼。）

**Monica Geller:** Okay, I gotta go find Rachel but umm, if you guys see her could you please try to give her some really bad news so that mine doesn’t seem so bad? (Exits.)
> 好吧，我得去找瑞秋，不过，嗯，如果你们见到她，能不能试着告诉她一些非常坏的消息，这样我的消息就不会那么糟糕了？（退出。）

**Ross Geller:** Bye.
> 再见。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Hmm, something bad to tell Rachel… Bad news for Rachel, what could that be?!
> 嗯，有件不好的事要告诉瑞秋…瑞秋的坏消息，那会是什么？！

**Ross Geller:** Could ya just, could ya just lay off, please? All right? My life is an embarrassment! I should go live under somebody’s stairs!
> 你能不能，你能不能停下来？好吧我的生活很尴尬！我应该住在别人的楼梯下！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ross, it’s not that big a deal! So you’ll been divorced three times, you’ll still have a life, you’ll go on dates…
> 罗斯，没什么大不了的！所以你会离婚三次，你仍然有生活，你会约会…

**Ross Geller:** (interrupting) No! No, I won’t! I’ll be at the bottom of the dating barrel now. The only guys below me will be Four Divorce Guy uh, Murderer Guy, and-and, Geologists.
> （打断）不！不，我不会！我现在就在约会桶的底部。我下面的人只有四个，离婚的，杀人犯，还有地质学家。

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ross, you’re being ridiculous! Okay? You are cute and smart and sweet and that is much more important than three stupid divorces!
> 罗斯，你太可笑了！可以你可爱、聪明、可爱，这比三次愚蠢的离婚更重要！

**Ross Geller:** Oh yeah? Have you ever dated anyone who has been divorced three times?
> 哦，是吗？你有没有和离婚三次的人约会过？

**Phoebe Buffay:** Y’know that’s really fair. Y’know? Most guys who have been divorced three times are like 60. Ross, nobody cares about this except you! This-this embarrassment thing is all in your head! Here, I’ll show you! Come here.
> 你知道这很公平。你知道吗？大多数离婚三次的男人都像60岁。罗斯，除了你没人关心这个！这件尴尬的事全在你脑子里！来，我给你看！过来。

**Ross Geller:** What?! Why?
> 什么？！为什么？

*(She grabs his arm and drags him over to a table where three beautiful women are sitting. Now, I’m going to go out on a limb and say that their names are Stephanie, Karin, and Meg. Okay, so I looked at the credits. Of course, only Meg is named later on, so I’ll have to guess who is Stephanie and who is Karin. But, we’ll cross that bridge when we come to it.)*
