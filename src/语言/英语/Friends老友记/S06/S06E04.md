🎬 *Monica and Rachel's, Rachel is packing her belongings to move to Ross’s. She’s standing in the kitchen.*
> 🎬 *莫妮卡和瑞秋家，瑞秋正在收拾行李搬到罗斯家。她站在厨房里。*

**Rachel Green:** So, which of this kitchen stuff is mine?
> 那么，厨房里的哪些东西是我的？

**Monica Geller:** This bottle opener. (She grabs it off of the freezer door.)
> 这个开瓶器。（她把它从冰箱门上拿下来。）

**Rachel Green:** And?
> 还有？

**Monica Geller:** And it’s a magnet!
> 而且是磁铁！

**Rachel Green:** Look at that!
> 看那个！

> 这有多奇怪？你知道吗？你要搬来和我一起住，你有我没有的东西。这就像，嗯，在某种程度上，你完成了我（菲比瞪着他）的厨房。

**Rachel Green:** What?!
> 什么？！

**Ross Geller:** (in an Australian accent) You complete me kitchen, matey!
> （用澳大利亚口音）伙计，你把我的厨房弄得一团糟！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Ross, I know what you’re thinking.
> 罗斯，我知道你在想什么。

**Ross Geller:** What?
> 什么？

**Phoebe Buffay:** That she’s gonna move in with you and maybe then she’ll fall in love with you and then when she finds out you’re already married, she’ll just be happy. Y’know? You’re just, you’re very sad.
> 她会搬进你家，也许然后她会爱上你，然后当她发现你已经结婚了，她会很开心的。你知道吗？你只是，你很伤心。

**Ross Geller:** Oh…my…God! I-I see what this is! You are in love with Rachel!
> 哦…我的…天哪！我明白了！你爱上了瑞秋！

**Phoebe Buffay:** What?!!
> 什么？！！

**Ross Geller:** Of course! It all adds up! I mean you you’re obsessed with her. It’s always, "Ross, what are you gonna do about Rachel?" "Ross, why-why are you moving in with Rachel?" "When are you gonna confess your secret marriage to Rachel?" You want her!
> 当然！这一切加起来！我是说你被她迷住了。总是说，“罗斯，你打算怎么处理瑞秋？”“罗斯，你为什么要和瑞秋同居？”“你什么时候要向瑞秋坦白你的秘密婚姻？”你想要她！

**Phoebe Buffay:** No! (Ross’s phone rings.)
> 不！（罗斯的电话响了。）

**Ross Geller:** (answering it) Uh-oh, saved by the bell. (On phone) Hello?
> （回答）哦，铃救了我。（电话）喂？

[Cut to the living room where Monica is helping pack a box.]
> [切到起居室，莫妮卡在那里帮着收拾箱子。]

**Monica Geller:** Hey Rach, aren’t these candlesticks (holds up a pair) mine?
> 嘿，瑞秋，这些烛台不是我的吗？

**Rachel Green:** No-no, I bought those.
> 不，不，我买了那些。

**Monica Geller:** Ohh! Yeah, I forgot.
> 哦！是的，我忘了。

**Rachel Green:** Yeah. (Rachel walks away.)
> 是的。（瑞秋走开了。）

**Monica Geller:** (under her breath) That you’re a liar. (Hides the candlesticks in a drawer.)
> （屏住呼吸）你是个骗子。（把烛台藏在抽屉里。）

**Ross Geller:** (on phone) No-no-no, that’s great! I’ll be there Monday. And thank you again! (Listens) Okay. (Hangs up) (To All) Umm, that was the head of the Paleontology department at N uh, Y, uh U!
> （在电话上）不，不，那太好了！我星期一会到那里。再次感谢你！（听）好的。（挂断）（向所有人）嗯，那是纽约大学古生物学系主任！

**Monica Geller:** Wow! Uh what, did he uh, say?
> 哇！他说了什么？

**Ross Geller:** Well remember that paper I had published last year on sediment flow rate, huh? They loved it.
> 还记得我去年发表的那篇关于泥沙流速的论文吗？他们喜欢它。

**Rachel Green:** Well, who wouldn’t?!
> 嗯，谁不会呢？！

**Ross Geller:** I know! Anyway, they asked me to be a guest lecturer! I mean it’s temporary, but uh, if they like me it could lead to a full time job. How great would that look great on a mailbox, huh? "Professor Geller."
> 我知道！不管怎样，他们要我做客座讲师！我的意思是这是暂时的，但是如果他们喜欢我，这可能会导致一份全职工作。放在邮箱上看起来有多好啊？“盖勒教授。”

**Phoebe Buffay:** Yeah, Professor and Mrs.
> 是的，教授和夫人。

**Rachel Green:** And Mrs.?!
> 那……夫人呢。？！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Oh! Yeah, y’know you and Ross are still married.
> 哦！是的，你知道你和罗斯仍然结婚。

**Rachel Green:** What?!!
> 什么？！！

**Phoebe Buffay:** Just kidding!
> 只是开玩笑！

**Rachel Green:** Ohh! Oh God! (Laughs her way into the living room.)
> 哦！天哪！（笑着走进客厅。）

**Phoebe Buffay:** (To Ross) Saved your ass.
> （对罗斯）救了你的命。

I’ve really gotta start living now! (So she picks up the latest copy of Car and Driver (a U.S. auto magazine), leans back, and starts reading.)

*(They pause to think about it.)*

**All:** Oh that’d be great. Sure!
> 哦，那太好了。当然

> 嗯，我从来没见过一个男人如此认真地盯着一张纸，上面没有裸体的小妞。

(Pauses and darts his eyes between Chandler and Rachel.) (Rachel starts laughing) Each of these theories (glances at Phoebe) can be further subcategorized (glances at Chandler) into…"

**Chandler Bing:** Why don’t you open with a joke?
> 你为什么不开个玩笑呢？

**Ross Geller:** Open with a joke? It’s a university, not a comedy club!
> 用笑话开场？这是大学，不是喜剧俱乐部！

**Chandler Bing:** Wait a minute, hold the phone! You’re not talking about Chuckles University?!
> 等一下，别挂断电话！你不是在说笑大学？！

**Ross Geller:** (gets up) Okay!
> （站起来）好的！

**All:** Ohh! We’re kidding! Oh, we’re kidding!
> 哦！我们在开玩笑！哦，我们在开玩笑！

**Rachel Green:** Ross, hey you know what might make it less boring?
> 罗斯，嘿，你知道什么能让它不那么无聊吗？

**Ross Geller:** Thank you!
> 谢谢！

**Rachel Green:** Some uh, some visual aides.
> 一些，嗯，一些视觉助手。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Oh-ooh-ooh! Y’know what’s a good visual aide?
> 哦，哦，哦！你知道什么是好的视觉助手吗？

**Ross Geller:** Please don’t say naked chicks.
> 请不要说裸女。

**Joey Tribbiani:** Why not?!
> 为什么不呢？！

**Ross Geller:** I-I-I don’t even know why I bother to talk to you guys about it. Y’know what? I’m just gonna do it on my own with no naked chicks.
> 我甚至不知道我为什么要跟你们谈这个。你知道吗？我要自己去做，没有裸体的小妞。
